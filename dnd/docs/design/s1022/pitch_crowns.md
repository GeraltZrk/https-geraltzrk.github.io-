# 《无冕之年》 The Uncrowned Years

Campaign pitch for s1022 (1022 AA). Angle: the political heart of the era. Sources: [wiki: Page], [ck3: path relative to `src/ck3/`]. "Bookmark events" means `localization/english/event_localization/anb_bookmark_events_l_english.yml`.

## 1. Title, logline, stakes

**Title.** 《无冕之年》 / *The Uncrowned Years*

**Logline.** 1 Castanmark 1022. The war is won, the ball is over, and the ink on the Treaty of Anbenncóst is dry enough that somebody has to ride out and watch the oaths being sworn. You are one of those Witnesses. For thirty-one years, from the first morning in the tent-city to the last winter of the White Pestilence, the crowns the war left lying about pass through your hands: a half-elf's, a two-year-old's, an eight-year-old duke's, the hated Black Dragon's, and the one on Trialmount that nobody is allowed to touch. Halfway along you find that the peace was laid over a pit exactly the size of an emperor.

**中文。** 灰烬历1022年卡斯坦月一日。仗打完了，舞会散了，《安本科斯特条约》的墨刚干。得有人骑马去各国，看着大家把誓发完，你就是这么一个监誓人。往后三十一年，战争丢在地上的几顶王冠都要过你的手。半精灵的，两岁孩子的，八岁小公爵的，人人喊打的黑龙的。还有试炼山上谁也不许碰的那一顶。从帐篷城的头一个早晨，到白疫的最后一个冬天，你一路给人定名分。跑到半路你会发现，这份和约底下压着一个坑，大小正好埋一个皇帝。

**World stake.** Your rulings decide whether Castan's mantle is buried, mended or stolen; whether the Pestilence of 1040 finds Cannor's war dead in the ground and its peoples on speaking terms, or finds open graves and a scapegoat with pointed ears; and whether the mages come out of it as wardens or as riders. Added up, they decide whether "Anbennar" (communion) arrives as a union, as a regency of the long-lived over the short-lived, as a leash held by wizards, or not at all.

**Emotional spine.** You make people swear when you are young, then live long enough to meet what the oaths made of them: the duke of eight and the king of two you ruled on in Act 1 are men of twenty-six and twenty in Act 3, and they remember. Beside you an elf stays a boy, a knight goes grey, a harpy drinks herself old and a seer loses her mind one vision at a time.

- Ruben II killed in the lists by Crege Dameris (1020). Ioriel rules for Reán IV (b. 1020), who marries Aucanna Silmuna and dies of the Pestilence in 1050 [wiki: List of Lorentish monarchs].
- Wex: Dyren Ryonard, 8, legitimised bastard of a lapsed kingdom; Butcher's Field (978); Barry Moonbeam, an elf born 958 with Wexonard culture, "the closest thing I have to a father"; the Necropolis of Corseton under the halfling High Priest Folcard; the gifted Crovan Vinerick, once Iacob's ward [ck3: history/characters/anb_wex.txt; ck3: bookmark events (0001); wiki: The Necropolis].

**Castanor**
- Garrec Ebonfrost's chain: the War to End the Dragon, then the search through Bal Mire and the Headman's Wood to a waystation where an old loyalist tests the visitor for possession ("You are not one of his... you are still... you") and taps a hollow tile; burial at Westgate, the North Citadel or Pantheonway [ck3: bookmark events (0100–0127)].
- Castan LIII died of "not lightning, not flame. Just a word" [ibid. 0110]; Nichmer then enthralled "the weak-minds of both armies" [wiki: Timeline]. He could "enchant the magic of others" and came out of the Ravenseers [wiki: Nichmer, the Sorcerer-King; wiki: Order of the Ravenseers].
- The Trials (stone map, Oathstone, the golem that beheads itself and kneels) were "irreparably damaged" and the mountain left "cursed and inaccessible"; the mod gates *Claim the Mantle of Castan*, in Black Dragon and Silver Dragon variants, behind *The Wounds of the Trialmount* [wiki: Trials of Castan; wiki: Castanor; ck3: localization/english/anb_castanor_l_english.yml].
- Clarimonde and Calindal; the Sapphire Eye; Edmund Fairhand, later the death knight Edmund Palehand [wiki: Calindal, the Gleaming Blade; wiki: Order of the Sapphire Eye].
- The harpy Castan, nicknamed "LIV" the day Castan LIII died: dynasty Ebonfrost, bastard, drunkard, claimant to the empire [ck3: history/characters/anb_jarnklo_harpy.txt].
- Iacob the Betrayer, alive as a duke under Anor; Lucan Silurion, half-elf, born 1019 to Urion Starsworn and Alva Ebonfrost; Ulric Coldsteel of Gawed, half Ebonfrost [ck3: history/characters/000_test_characters.txt; ck3: history/characters/anb_alenic.txt].
- The First Scion's War (Escann, c. 1023–24) survives as a name only [wiki: Order of the Sapphire Eye].

**The clock**
- Canrec worked on the plague "since well before the war" and used it to become a lich. First case 3 Castanmark 1040 at Ravenhill: a dead man "rising from the open grave and devouring the priest of Nerat". Souls bound to the Book of Canrec. Cure made at Varlosen by Garion, his apprentices and the Ravenseers from Canrec's own laboratories. Ended in 1053 when the squire Aldresia died killing him [wiki: White Pestilence; wiki: Aldresian Crusade; wiki: Bellowing Soul].
- Elves caught it less, hence pogroms (Alenath, 1042) and the rise of the half-elven houses [wiki: Elf; wiki: Ginnfield].
- The Ravenseers' inner council are wereravens who read the Unkind Prophecy, go mad with age, and hold that "a small act of evil in the present can justify averting a massive disaster" [wiki: Unkind Prophecy].
- Corvuria under sun elves; Jaher away east 1038–1056; Lucian síl Vivin made Protector afterwards; vampires born when crusaders dig under a temple of Surael [wiki: Korbarid; wiki: Jaherian Eastern Campaigns; wiki: Vampire].
- Baseline deaths: Alenn III (1023), Crege and his heirs, Marion's firstborn Arrel, Reán IV (1050), Godrac XI (1053) [wiki: List of Gawedi monarchs; wiki: List of Damerian monarchs].

**What I invent**
1. **The Witnesses of the Treaty (监誓人):** a one-off commission of seal-bearers named by the signatories. In the baseline it lapses and leaves no trace.
2. **The bridle in the Oathstone:** how Castan LIII was held, and what "the Word" was.
3. **Sleepers:** thralls from both armies at Bal Mire who never woke and still carry orders; "I was enthralled" as the legal plea of the age.
4. **Canrec the collector:** he keeps the royal dead and wants the emperor's body; his motive; his seat by Daravan's Folly.
5. **The Ravenseers' two readings** of the white year, and their decision to watch.
6. Act 2 as the content of the First Scion's War; the date and place of the Rose–Moon wedding; the Sun King's seal-keeper at Brasan; Aldresia as a girl at Varlosen in 1040.
7. The player, two companions (Vezina, Valeran) and all ten pre-mades: the canon minor figures of 1022 are rulers or have fates the plot needs fixed, so each pre-made is tied to a canon person or event instead.

## 3. The truth, and when the player learns it

**A. The bridle.** Every Castan swore at the Oathstone, and the oath is binding magic entered freely. Nichmer enchanted one piece of someone else's magic, the oath, and rode the man inside it for thirty-seven years. The Word at Bal Mire was the oath's own forfeit. Clarimonde's stroke cut the working and left the knot: mend the Trials without unpicking it, and the next Castan can be ridden by whoever knows the Word. Iacob and Canrec do.

**B. The collector.** Canrec has spent the peace doing what the reburial of a continent makes easy, taking in the dead. He holds kings and wants the emperor under the tile, because a dead Castan is still inside his oath. His reason: he watched his master hold the living and lose everything to a knock on the head. The dead do not change sides.

**C. The ones who let it come.** The Ravenseers saw the white year the winter the war ended. One reading says kill the Defiler now. The other, held by the elder who trained Nichmer, says every future where the plague is strangled early ends with human lords driving the elves into the sea, and the only futures with peace in them run through an elf and a seeress's daughter making a cure together. He may be right; canon's own road to Anbennar runs that way. So the seers watch a grave at Ravenhill and do nothing.

| End of | The player believes |
|---|---|
| Prologue | The war is over and my job is to carry a seal. One wrong note: somebody acted with nobody home behind the eyes. |
| Act 1 (1022) | Nichmer's thralls did not all wake. Someone aims them at the half-elf king, and the handler's teacher sits in Escann: Iacob. The court mages want a writ to open minds. |
| Act 2 (1024) | The enthralment lived in the Oathstone; a Castan can be ridden; Iacob wanted to be the rider and is dealt with. *False floor:* the tile-keeper recalls an earlier visitor who smelt of myrrh and asked for the body, not the son. |
| Act 3 (1040) | Canrec lives, the plague is his, the elves' resistance is an accident he never planned, and the Ravenseers watched it come. |
| Act 4 (1041) | Why they watched. And Canrec knows the Word: crown a Castan on an unmended mountain and you hand the crusade to the lich. Every king at the wedding wants an emperor anyway. |
| Finale (1053) | Canrec's own account, among dead kings. Who was right about the prophecy is left to the epilogue cards. |

## 5. Outline

Track changes are written `Unburied −2`; the tracks are defined in section 6.

**Prologue — 印 The Seal** (1 Castanmark – Esmarment 1022). Per origin, as in section 4.

The four acts are shared, with openings only some seats have: an Ebonmarck Witness can stand surety for Garrec; an Ibevari one speaks to Ibenion's envoy as kin; the Old Rites can anoint and bury; Garion's Pupils can examine a mind; the Sapphire Eye can call for champions; a dwarf can work the forge of the third Trial.

### Act 1 — 谁的手 Whose Hand
*Anbenncóst, the Moonmount, the Luna, Wexkeep, Corseton. Esmarment–Suren 1022.*

**Question:** can a peace stand when anybody may say "I was enthralled"?

1. The swearing-in. Castan LIV lands in the arena demanding a seat for Castanor; Barry arrives with a hamper.
2. The knife at Marion's table. Masilda examines the veteran, and only another diviner could check her verdict.
3. The Plumwall hearing: Cecilius Prumuris pleads that his father was enthralled.
4. Emil of Verne's peace on the Luna, with a clause against elves.
5. The Moonmount: Aria offers to anoint the Witnesses' verdicts. The queen is with child.
6. Butcher's Field: Folcard's ledger, the body-takers, sixteen graves nobody dug.
7. The regency of Wex: who raises Dyren.
8. The handler in the cellar at Acromton and his paymaster, Vidraldus Acromis, who says a priestess crowned her own grandson over Damerian heads.

**Choices.** The Plumwall plea (accept: `Writ +1`; refuse: `Communion −1`). Emil's clause (strike it: `Communion +1`). Butcher's Field (finish the reburial: `Unburied −2`). The regency (Barry: `Communion +1`; Crovan Vinerick: `Writ +1`; Marion as guardian: `Feud +1`; or the barons). Acromis (expose: `Communion −1`; settle quietly, and he owes you in 1040; trade the scandal for Garion's writ: `Writ +2`).

**Fights.** A tent-city riot at the Elvendocks. Body-takers in the fog. The cellar: an enchanter and two thralls, with a special action from Bal Mire, *hit the thrall on the head*.

Level 4. The handler names his teacher's teacher: "Iacob had it from the mountain."

### Act 2 — 地砖下的皇帝 The Emperor Under the Tile
*Castonath, Ebonmarck, Bal Mire, the Headman's Wood, Trialmount. Bloomsdawn 1023 – Truefrost 1024.*

**Question:** what is owed to a beaten enemy who was not himself, and may anyone be Castan again?

1. Alenn III dies (3 Suren 1023); Ricard of Gawed is the first king crowned under the new rule, and his nephew is half Ebonfrost.
2. Castonath: two kingdoms both called Castanor, and Iacob at Lain's court, helpful and guilty of something.
3. The siege of Ebonmarck, the opening of what chroniclers will call the First Scion's War: certify the War to End the Dragon, or not.
4. Bal Mire, then the waystation: a bowl of stew while the keeper decides whether you are still you.
5. The body, and where it goes.
6. Clarimonde at Bladeskeep, who wants no more Castans and has the sword to say so.
7. Trialmount, cursed and closed to anyone who walks. Castan LIV flies, and lets down a rope. The stone map still works: draw Castanor's borders as they are, and as they SHALL BE.
8. Iacob arrives with masons and a candidate, four-year-old Lucan Silurion, Ebonfrost and elf in one small boy: the Silver Dragon, and a Castan he could ride for sixty years.

**Choices.** The war (certify: Garrec dies or goes to Urviksten; grant a hearing: `Mantle +1`). The body (Garrec's funeral at Westgate `Mantle +1`, the North Citadel or Pantheonway `+2`; a quiet grave `−1`; burn it as the keeper begs: `Mantle −2`, `Unburied −2`; leave it, and Canrec gets him). The mountain (mend and unpick, which needs Alarian Singkeep, the Ravenseers or Iacob: `Mantle +2`, flag `knot_cut`; mend as it is: `+3`; bring the stair down with Clarimonde: `Mantle 0`). Iacob (hang, pardon for service, or hand to Garion: `Writ ±1`).

**Fights.** Werewolves in the Headman's Wood (spear ring or fire circle, as in the mod). The breach at Ebonmarck, from either side. The headless golem of the ninth Trial.

Level 6.

**Time skip, 1024 → 1040.** One scene asks what you did with sixteen years; a short chronicle follows from your flags. Humans are eighteen years older than in the prologue. Barry looks the same.

### Act 3 — 白年 The White Year
*Ravenhill, by sea to Brasan, Wex, Varlosen. Castanmark–Suren 1040.*

**Question:** who is to blame?

1. Folcard's letter: "A man got out of his grave and ate my colleague." The party counts grey hairs.
2. Ravenhill under a sun-elf garrison: the open grave, the first night.
3. Elven soldiers take the fever and get up alive; by Bloomsdawn the Reach says the elves brought it.
4. Wex: Dyren, twenty-six and exactly what your regency made him, issues his edict on the elves.
5. Brasan. Jaher is in Haless; his seal-keeper will admit Cannorian arms to the protectorate only on terms.
6. The rookery: Vezina's order has known for nineteen years.
7. Varlosen: Garion, two hundred this year, needs Canrec's samples and the power to seize bodies and close roads. A squire of fourteen named Aldresia carries his water.
8. The public answer to "why".

**Choices.** The quarantine (hold: `Unburied −1`; open: `+2`). The Sun Court (accept sun-elf command: `Communion +1`; smuggle the crusade in; hand over the Ravenseers). The blame (Canrec, with proof from your Act 1 ledger: `Communion +2`; the elves: `Communion −3`, a quick muster, `Unburied −1`; the Ravenseers exposed: the cure delayed, `Unburied +2`; or shielded, and you share their lie). Plague-law for Varlosen (grant: `Writ +2`, `Unburied −2`; with a term and a lay council: `Writ +1`, `Unburied −1`; refuse: `Unburied +1`).

**Fights.** Ravenhill graveyard (toll the bell to pull the dead off the gate). A pogrom street in Wex, winnable without killing. Wereravens in the rookery.

Level 8.

### Act 4 — 玫瑰与月的婚礼 The Wedding of Rose and Moon
*Viswall, Lorentei, then Trialmount. Teysuren 1040 – Bloomsdawn 1041.*

**Question:** who commands, and under what crown?

1. The Small Country shuts its gates; King Finnic's Viswall boils shrouds in its dye vats; Lorent and Gawed both offer "protection".
2. Lorentei: Ioriel, unchanged, and a son of twenty who has never made a decision she did not prepare. He marries Aucanna Silmuna before every crown of western Cannor.
3. Crege Dameris must attend the wedding of the man whose father he killed.
4. The council in the wedding tent, and four charters for the crusade: an emperor; Ioriel's league ("we will still be here when it is over"); Varlosen's writ; Marion's free muster.
5. Purists at both ends of the table: Enteben's lords, and Ibenion's envoy closing the Larthán line to human refugees.
6. The Word: Canrec has it.
7. A knight rides into the lists with his visor down. He fights like Edmund Fairhand because he is.
8. The second ascent of Trialmount: a coronation or a sealing.

**Choices.** Crege (penance: `Feud −2`; champions: `−1`; let Ioriel have him: `Feud +2`). The charter (emperor: `Mantle +3`; league: Rose Court up; writ: `Writ +3`; free muster: `Communion +2`, `Unburied +1` because it is slow). Ibevar (talk the Larthán line open: `Communion +2`).

**Fights.** The dead come up the river into the wedding quarter (the Ruby dwarves' sluice, if anyone ever thanked them). The lists against Palehand.

Level 10.

**Time skip, 1041 → 1053.** "Twelve winters", a chronicle built from the tracks: who died (Crege's line, Arrel, Reán in 1050), whether Alenath burned in 1042, when the cure left Varlosen.

### Finale — 最后一个冬天 The Last Winter
*Varlosen, Bal Dostan, the drowned south by Daravan's Folly. 1053. Ten scenes.*

1. Muster: Roilsardi who came for land, Lucian síl Vivin, and Aldresia at twenty-seven leading the last column.
2. Child-kings again: Ruben III at seven under Ioriel, Godrac XI at six and coughing.
3. A temple of Surael, and crusaders who want to dig under it.
4. The march; each companion's last conversation.
5. The door: Edmund Palehand.
6. The court of the dead: Arrel II on Canrec's right, and Castan LIII on his left if you left him under the tile.
7. Canrec speaks, and says the Word if there is an emperor to hear it.
8. The price. Canon's is Aldresia. The player, or an emperor who meant the oath, can pay instead; with `Unburied` low, a timely cure and the seers' help, nobody has to.
9. The Book: burn it and free the souls, give it to Garion, or keep the mark.
10. The last crowning: who gets Corvuria.

## 7. Endings

Checked in this order at the last scene.

1. **缰绳 The Bridle** (defensible wrong). A Castan is crowned with the knot intact and `Writ ≥ 7`, or the player keeps the Book's mark. An emperor on a leash held by Garion's pupils, over Garion's objection. The plague ends fastest of all, and the Mages' Peace arrives by force 180 years early.
2. **五十四世 The Fifty-Fourth.** `Mantle ≥ 8`, knot cut, a Castan crowned: Garrec (the Black Dragon), Lucan (the Silver), or the harpy. One banner over the crusade, and Escann whole three centuries before Castellyr. It works, and it rebuilds the thing the war was fought to end.
3. **同席 The Common Table.** `Communion ≥ 8`, `Writ` 4–6, `Feud ≤ 3`, no emperor. The realms and peoples meet yearly at Anbenncóst with nobody in the chair. The hardest to reach. Chroniclers later argue whether the Empire of 1221 was founded or merely named.
4. **各回各家 The Parting** (defensible wrong). `Communion ≤ 2`. The killing stops because the elves go: behind Larthán, into Moonhaven, some back aboard the ships. Marion rules as a half-elf nobody trusts, and "Anbennar" is a word in somebody else's language.
5. **长生者的摄政 The Long Regency** (bittersweet). Ioriel's league took the charter and `Unburied ≥ 5`. The houses left standing have pointed ears. Peace under people who will outlive your grandchildren, with the resentment stored for the Interregnum.
6. **监誓人的账 The Witness's Ledger** (bittersweet; the baseline). None of the above. Thirteen years, Aldresia dead on the lich's floor, the cure from Varlosen, the chronicles as written. What differs is a short list of people who are alive because of you.

**Epilogue cards** by track, companion and origin: the toll, and whether Arrel lived; Alenath 1042; vampires; the Ravenseers' end; Wex as crown or duchy; Ibevar's walls; the Small Country; Castellyr 1339; the Mages' Peace of 1221, which Barry Moonbeam attends aged 263, at last an adult by anyone's count; the Lilac Wars as a quarrel of cousins or a war of strangers.

## 9. Risks

**Canon**
- *The bridle is my reading of thin text.* Canon says only that the Trials' magic was "drained, severed"; the mod's repair decision is a stub. So the mechanism is explained by Iacob and the tile-keeper, both unreliable.
- *Two companions are mod characters with almost no written text.* The harpy's fatherhood is her own claim (the mod names no father) and stays in her mouth; Barry's fate is unwritten.
- *Ioriel and Crege.* Canon calls the lance an accident and leaves Ioriel's hand in later deaths as rumour. The player may offer her Crege; the text never says what she did with the offer.
- *Aldresia may live and the plague may be shorter.* The baseline ending is canon to the letter; every bent outcome is marked on its card as one timeline among several.
- *The Ravenseers as watchers* darkens an order canon treats as allies. Their creed supports it, and they still make the cure.
- *Check before writing:* Act 2 as the First Scion's War, the Sun Court's seal-keeper (unnamed on purpose), Lucan as the Silver Dragon's face, the wedding in 1040, Arrel born in 1022 (canon says only "firstborn", with Lorran in 1024). The Ruby Crown stays off the infant's head; dossier 10 flags its dating.
- *Community.* Dossier 13 rests on search snippets (Reddit is blocked by the session's proxy) and finds little fan material on the CK3 era. It does say fans treat everything before 1444 as fixed, so the baseline stays strict, and that they like monsters as peoples, first-person documents, grudges and absurd institutions played straight: hence a harpy called Castan, a ledger of graves and an elf named Barry.

**Engine**
- *Three periods mean three states for every recurring NPC.* Cap it at five tracks and about fifteen named flags; the two chronicle scenes do most of the reconciling.
- *Aging against free race choice.* Age lines are written against `is.longlived`; only short-lived races get the grey-hair text.
- *Armies.* The engine fights at party scale, so sieges and the crusade are party actions with allies and `specials`; the tracks carry the larger result.
- *Dark-lord drift.* Canrec appears in person once, in the finale. Before that he is ledgers, missing corpses and a smell of myrrh.

## 10. Disagreements with the brief

1. **A second skip.** The lead proposed ending in 1040. I put the finale in 1053: the plague's canonical end lets the baseline be canon exactly, and the blame chosen in Act 3 gets paid for in play. The cost is a human hero past fifty, which suits a setting born from a dynasty game.
2. **Thread (d) as law.** I treat domination less as a mage-hunt than as a legal question, "whose will was their own?", because that is what makes mages kingmakers.
3. **Corvuria is not an origin.** The Ravenseers hold the plot's secret, and playing one from inside gives it away. Dwarves and halflings enter as pre-mades inside Lorent and Wex; if a seat that is neither human nor elven is wanted, Wex could be traded for the Small Country at some cost to the child-duke thread.
