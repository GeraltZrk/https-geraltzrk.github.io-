# 《代天者》 Regents of an Empty Sky

Campaign pitch for s1022 (1022 AA). Angle: the metaphysical mystery.
Citations: `[wiki: Page]`, `[ck3: path]`, `[eu4: path]`, paths relative to each mod root.

---

## 1. Title, logline, stakes

**中文标题**　《代天者》
**English title**　*Regents of an Empty Sky*

**Logline.** Two months after the Grand Ball of Anbenncóst, an old priest of Castellos is knifed in the veterans' camp by a war hero who remembers nothing. His satchel holds a rubbing from the wrecked Trials of Castan. The trail leads up Trialmount, where the Sorcerer-King's last battle broke open what fifty-three emperors kept shut: evidence that Castellos, the god in whose stead every Cannorian king claims to rule, was the elves' own sky-god and died on the Day of Ashen Skies. Nichmer knew, and took the empty throne for his. His last acolyte, the lich Canrec, knows too; his answer climbs out of a grave at Ravenhill in 1040. Over eighteen years the player decides who is told, who may be Castan, whether mages guard or rule, and whether elves and humans still share a table once the dead walk.

**一段话简介.** 安本科斯特的大舞会散了才两个月，老兵营里死了个卡斯特洛斯的老祭司。他怀里揣着一张纸，是从试炼山誓石上拓下来的。顺着这张纸往上爬，山顶埋着一件谁都不想听的事。天下君王都借一位神的名义坐江山；那位神原是精灵的天空之神，灰烬之日那天就死了。术士王比你早知道，所以他要当王。他最后一个学徒坎雷克也知道，他打算等到 1040 年，从渡鸦山的一座坟里把他的答案放出来。往后十八年，有几件事等你拿主意。这话说不说，对谁说。试炼山还让不让人上。法师该看门，还是该当家。精灵和人还坐不坐一张桌子。

**World-scale stake.** What is done with the death of Castellos decides whether the Regent Court, the divine right of every Cannorian crown and the mantle of Castanor outlive the eleventh century, and how much of Cannor outlives the White Pestilence. It also decides whether the elven-human communion later named Anbennar is founded as shared mourning, imposed by mages, or never founded.

**Emotional spine.** Everyone in 1022 rules for someone dead or too young: a mother's crown, a two-year-old king, an emperor under a floor tile, a god. The baby the party takes in after the first murder grows up during the time skip and, in the last act, asks whether what she was raised to believe is true.

## 3. The truth and the reveal schedule

The narrator confirms none of it. Each layer is a document, an object or a witness with a motive.

1. The "newfound god" of 639 BA was the precursor elves' Castellar. The Wayguide, his last priest in Cannor, stayed on the mountain: he was the "Castellos" that Castans spoke with.
2. On 1 Castanmark, 1 AA, Castellar died over Aelantir. The fleeing ships' first watch saw it. In three generations at sea "we saw him fall" became "the gods abandoned us". The Silver Dragon kept vigil five hundred more years, buried the priest, and died in the Dragonwake. Every Castan since reached the top and found nobody. None said so.
3. Castan LII confided in his advisor. Nichmer, refused the Prophecy by his own order, concluded that an empty throne belongs to whoever can hold it. In 1020 he fled to the summit to seize the Trials' magic by enchanting it. That is the Wound.
4. Canrec climbed the wreck, opened the cairn and took the bones. From the priest's ghost he has a transcript in which the god orders the regency ended if he does not return. His own reading: Castanor's dead were promised the White Citadel and nobody is there to open it, so he will hold them until somebody is. The Book is his court and the plague his census, loosed when the moon of the cairn-robbing came round again. He means to carry the Book up the Trials and swear as Castan of the dead.
5. The Ravenseers have foreseen what the telling does (canon's Age of Unraveling) and commit small evils to prevent it.

| End of | What the player believes |
|---|---|
| Prologue | Someone at the Ball was not themselves. A priest died for a sheet of paper. |
| Act 1 | A Ravenseer killed to keep the Council from hearing what lies under the Oathstone. The survivors swore silence. The elves' oldest log says their sky-lord burned on the first day. The mages must be hiding Nichmer's last weapon. |
| Act 2 | It is no weapon. Castellos was Castellar, he is dead, and an elf crowned the first Castan. Canrec lives and holds the only witness. |
| Act 3 | The plague is aimed. It takes souls, the Ravenseers knew the date, and the street's half-truth does Canrec's work. |
| Act 4 | Canrec thinks he is the last loyal Castanorian. The transcript says the god wanted the regency ended, unless a lich forged it. |
| Finale | Nothing further is confirmed. |

## 4. Seats at the table

### Origins

1. **Dameria, the Moon Court** (flag A58). The First Union's own house; Marion does not know what his Marshal and Court Mage saw. *Prologue:* you serve the king's household in the tent-city. Sergeant Ascaric Valeris, decorated at the Ball, stabs a Castellosi petitioner and remembers only going for water. Istralania closes the inquiry in a day. You keep the dead man's satchel.
2. **Lorent, the Rose Regency** (A01). The other winner, ruled by an elf for a two-year-old. *Prologue:* Lorentainé, three days before King Reán's birthday. Roen Arthelis's knights accuse Ioriel of enthralling the council. It is less than they say and more than she admits. You ride east with the embassy that will demand Crege Dameris answer for King Ruben.
3. **Ebonmarck, the Black Dragon** (Z34, Black Castanor). The hated losers, who still call the Enthralled "Castan". *Prologue:* Garrec's hall, encircled. Someone is paying old coin to learn where the emperor fell. You carry Garrec's plea through a cordon of Adeanic zealots and are cut out of an ambush by a harpy who charges "family rate".
4. **Ibevar, the people of the Lunateín** (A32). Elves who fought for a realm of their own and want no gods, least of all human ones. *Prologue:* Vardoced, where the Carillon's bells crack unless a mage holds them. Cannorized kin are stripping the moored Lunateín for timber. Prince Ibenion sends you for its reliquary deck. The sealed log inside opens on the Day of Ashen Skies.
5. **Corvuria, ravens under the Sun** (A59). Occupied country, Nichmer's homeland, and the one seat that starts with a date. *Prologue:* Arca Corvur under Governor Denarion. The Ravenseers quarrel over a shared vision: snow, an open grave at Ravenhill, the third day of a year eighteen winters off. Master Dekinan leaves for the Dameshead "to stop a priest talking". A rival master sends you after him.

### Factions

- **Oathkeepers 守誓人** (Dameria, Lorent, Ebonmarck, Corvuria). The world stands on kept oaths, whoever they were sworn to. Starts with Temples and Black Dragon favour; unlocks Trial by Chivalry and oath options; leans toward burial or a lawful Castan.
- **The Table 同席人** (all five). Silver Band veterans and mixed households. Starts with Moon Court and Elfrealm favour; unlocks mediation and elvish options; leans toward truth told as shared loss.
- **The Watchful 明眼人** (Dameria, Lorent, Ibevar, Corvuria). Pupils of Garion's circle or the Ravenseers. Starts with the Wise and the Ravenseers; unlocks detection and counter-enchantment; leans toward a mage order that keeps the secret.

Custom creation: human, elf (moon or sun), half-elf (no older than 21), dwarf, halfling or gnome, in any class the era allows.

### Companions

- **Castan "LIV" Ebonfrost** (canon), harpy fighter, heir by name to an empire whose second emperor cleansed her kind [wiki: Castan Beastbane]. *Voice:* loud, always counting, calls everyone "little brother". Joins in the Ebonmarck prologue or Act 1. *Loses her:* denying her the right to walk the Trials herself. She is forty-eight after the skip, old for a harpy.
- **Primrose Stillsong**, halfling tollwright of Nerat from the Necropolis, cleric. *Voice:* feeds you, measures you for a coffin by eye, asks who inherits. Joins in Act 1 with the murdered priest's body. *Loses her:* she stays to hold the Necropolis in Act 3 unless the party defends it, and walks if the Book is kept.
- **Ultariel**, log-keeper of the Lunateín, moon elf lore-bard, b. 731. *Voice:* unhurried; "last time" means the ninth century. Joins at the Elvendocks. *Loses her:* whether the First Log is burned or read to the captains is hers to choose; take that from her and she goes.
- **Vezina Eskaudnid**, Korbarid diviner, Dekinan's apprentice. *Voice:* exact, corrects herself aloud, "in theory". Joins when her master is unmasked. *Her decision:* in Act 4 the inner council offers her the eye. Accepting brings the Prophecy and, by the finale, trust in nobody; refusing makes the order hunt her.

**The ward.** Sergeant Valeris's infant daughter is **Aldresia**. Who raises her during the skip (Clarimonde's Order, the Temple, the Watch, or the player) sets her training and loyalties. The baseline is canon's squire.

## 5. Outline

### Prologue (five versions, 5–6 scenes, level 2)

1 Castanmark 1022, the anniversary of the Ruin. A home scene showing what this people gained or lost by the Treaty; an errand that turns up one piece of evidence (satchel, accusation, old coin, log, vision); one easy fight; the road to Damesear.

### Act 1: After the Ball (Anbenncóst, Castanmark–Bloomsdawn 1022; level 4)

*Question:* who is still wearing someone else's will, and why did it kill a priest?

1. The tent-city and the Elvendocks. The Council opens in the Old Colosseum; Castellos' delegates sit at the back.
2. Father Welyam's body, Valeris's blank week, and Primrose, who will not release the corpse until the debt is recorded.
3. Garion and Masilda propose the Watch: a register of enchanters, and leave to look inside a suspect's mind. The player testifies.
4. The Lunateín's hulk: salvagers against Ibevar purists; Ultariel and the First Log.
5. Lorent demands satisfaction from Crege: Trial by Chivalry, or betrothing the baby king to a Silmuna daughter not yet born.
6. Dekinan unmasked in the beast-pits, behind a dozen thralls who are also victims.
7. Istralania, Finnic and Garion admit it: "a grave and a wall", and a promise to say nothing.
8. The vote on kingly avatars; a commission to go east; the baby needs a home.

*Big choices:* how far the Watch may look (Staff); who keeps the Log (Veil, Communion); duel or betrothal (Rose and Moon).
*Fights:* salvage riot on the Lunateín; the pits (special: break shackles instead of killing thralls).

### Act 2: The Emperor Under the Tile (Escann, Halament 1022–Castanmark 1023; level 6)

*Question:* what did the first Castan find, and may anyone go up again?

1. Bal Mire: Brohnfeld's kettle, a melted sword, a scrying bowl.
2. Headman's Wood; the waystation, the loyalist's stew and his test; the body and the Ebonsteel Crown.
3. The War to End the Dragon: break or broker the siege of Ebonmarck. Garrec meets his half-sister.
4. Where to bury a Castan: Westgate, the North Citadel, Pantheonway, or nowhere marked.
5. The broken Trials: nobody answers the cry; the map-desk draws old borders by itself.
6. The golem of Castan VI, on its feet since 1015.
7. The cracked Oathstone; then the summit's ruined temple, the opened cairn, the tally wall, a grave-robber's candle ends.
8. The Summit Pact: seal it, tell the crowns, tell the Council, or give it to the Wise.

*Big choices:* the burial (Mantle); who disarms the golem, since it kneels to them until they die (PC, Castan LIV or nobody); the Pact (Veil, Staff).
*Fights:* werewolves; Adeanic lances at Ebonmarck; the golem (special: disarm).

### Interlude: Seventeen Years (1023–1040; 4 scenes)

Choose two: build the Watch at Oldtower; mend the mountain; serve your crown through the betrothal; raise the ward; hunt Canrec's workshops. One fixed scene: the Tearfall of 1033. Humans go grey. Ultariel does not.

### Act 3: The White Pestilence (Corvuria, the Borders, Gawed; 1040–1042; level 8)

*Question:* who is to blame?

1. Ravenhill, 3 Castanmark 1040. The Ravenseers are already at the graveside.
2. Denarion wants the town burned.
3. The elves mostly stay well. A half-truth about a dead elven god reaches the taverns; who leaked it depends on the Pact.
4. The Necropolis: the corseted dead turn their heads. Primrose's decision.
5. Ibevar shuts Larthán to human refugees.
6. Crege and his children die. Masilda's first draught makes three doses.
7. Alenath, 1042: the mob at the elven quarter.
8. Aldresia, eighteen, takes a vow and a banner; Roilsardi volunteers arrive.

*Big choices:* burn or hold Ravenhill (Pestilence); the three doses (Rose and Moon, Communion); stop the mob with shields, a speech or the Watch's enchantment (Staff, Communion); which account of the cause to back (Blame, Veil).
*Fights:* the graveside; the catacombs; Edmund Palehand on the Alenath road (special: invoke the Sapphire Eye's rite and he must accept single combat).

### Act 4: The Defiler (Varlosen, Brasan, Corvuria, Daravan's Folly; 1043; level 10)

*Question:* what does a loyal man owe a dead king?

1. Varlosen: Garion's hidden fortress needs samples from Canrec's laboratories.
2. Brasan: gardens, sunstones, and the Sun King, who stood on the summit and finds none of this new. His price for legions is that Cannor hears the truth his way.
3. The inner council splits over whether the party may live. Vezina and the eye.
4. The crusade marches. Theodore sína Trinás asks about a tomb under a temple of Surael.
5. The Dreadmire: corpse-barges and the laboratories.
6. The Book's first page: the Wayguide's bones and the transcript.
7. A Bellowing Soul stitched into a siege golem.
8. Canrec has gone north with the Book and Palehand's legion, to swear at New Year, the day the sky emptied. Three weeks are left.

*Big choices:* Jaher's bargain (Veil, Communion); the temple (vampires or none); who gets the cure first (Pestilence, Blame); copy or burn the transcript.
*Fights:* barge ambush; temple steps; the Bellowing colossus (special: break the maker's mark).

### Finale: A Year and a Day (Trialmount, Truefrost 1043 to New Year; 10 scenes)

The Trials, walked in order. (1) Muster at the North Citadel. (2) The cry: reputations decide who answers; Canrec's dead answer him from the plain. (3) The map-desk: draw the borders "as they SHALL BE" (one empire, a league of crowns, elfrealms as equals, a mages' peace). (4) The forge: a gift for the dwarves. (5) The Oathstone: say the oath aloud once and be held to it. (6) The crown of mankind: each companion adds a piece or refuses. (7) Ten jumps round the Eccentric's rock under arrow fire; the golem, kneeling or not. (8) The slope: Palehand, and Aldresia's stand. (9) The summit: Canrec's case, then the fight (specials: read names from the Book to free souls; return the bones to the cairn). (10) Who swears, in whose name, and what the world is told. Whoever swears stays a year and a day, as the first Castan did.

## 7. Endings

1. **The God's Funeral 《给神出殡》.** The truth is read from the Oathstone and the mantle buried with Castan LIII. With Communion ≥ 4, elves and humans keep one mourning-night and "anbennar" is a creed before it is an empire. Below that, the Unraveling starts five hundred years early. *Needs Veil ≥ 4.*
2. **The Long Regency 《守口》** (bittersweet; canon's course). The cairn is rebuilt and the rubbings burned. Canrec goes down as one more necromancer. Epilogues run true: Ioriel's century, the Interregnum, 1221, the Lilac Wars, 1518. Ultariel carries it alone. *Needs Veil ≤ 1 and the Book unmade.*
3. **Castan LIV 《第五十四位》.** Someone swears a new oath, as regent for the people instead of the sky: Garrec, Clarimonde, the harpy, or the player if the golem kneels to them. Escann meets 1424 with an emperor; the Dameshead unites in fear of it. A non-human Castan breaks the Progenitor's "your blood alone", and the epilogue counts the cost. *Needs Mantle ≥ 4 and a living claimant.*
4. **Peace at Wandpoint 《杖下太平》** (a defensible wrong). The Watch takes the mountain and the secret. The plague ends soonest here and no mob burns anything, because mobs are calmed. 1221 comes early, and so does the Black Doctrine. *Needs Staff ≥ 5.*
5. **The White Harbour 《各归各家》** (wrong, and hard to argue with). The truth becomes an accusation, or the elves decide Cannor's graves are not theirs. The fleet gathers at Celmaldor and the elfrealms shut. No pogroms, because no neighbours. Half-elves choose. *Needs Communion ≤ 1.*
6. **Regent of the Dead 《亡者的摄政》** (the other defensible wrong). The player grants Canrec's premise without Canrec, or with him under oath: the Book is kept at the Necropolis as an ark and the dead man the White Walls. Primrose leaves. In 1424 the Greentide breaks on a wall that does not sleep. *Needs the Book kept at the summit.*
7. **The Long Crusade 《十三年》** (defeat at the summit). Canon exactly: war until 1053, Aldresia dies killing him, the Orda takes her name, elves inherit a thinned Cannor.

Epilogue cards read the six tracks, the blame flags, each companion, the ward's upbringing, the vampire flag and the origin's own stake.

## 8. Sample moments

**序章开场（达梅里亚）**

1022 年卡斯坦月 1 日。舞会散了两个月，营地还没散。帐篷从旧城墙一直搭到精灵码头，湾里泊着四艘大船，一艘就有一条街长。你跨过三只空酒桶，找到出事的那顶帐篷。老弓手坐在门口，手上的血没洗，正拿面饼蘸肉汤。

**老弓手**　他们说我捅了个祭司。我只记得出门打水。

**同伴台词（卡斯坦“五十四”）**

**卡斯坦“五十四”**　小兄弟，账要这么算：我爹是五十三，他死那天我在沼泽堡外头喝酒，酒钱还是我垫的。往下数一位就是我。谁有意见，先把那天的酒钱还我。

**检定失败（月火号舱口，说服）**

你报了伊贝尼昂亲王的名号，又补了一句精灵话。守舱的老精灵把你的口音原样学了一遍，学得比你还像你。

## 9. Risks and how to defuse them

- **Revealing "Castellos is dead" five centuries early** is the largest strain. The evidence is local, fragile and readable two ways; the narrator never rules; Father Alric's spells keep working, as the wiki's note on divine magic allows; the baseline ending loses every proof before 1518. The Mural and the sceptre in Aelantir are never shown, leaving them for the later campaigns.
- **The Wayguide.** Canon keeps him a mystery. We take one of the wiki's listed theories and let companions argue the others.
- **The afterlife.** Canrec's claim about the White Citadel touches a secret canon holds until the 1600s. It stays a lich's claim.
- **Canon people on stage.** Marion, Ioriel, Garion, Jaher, Clarimonde and Garrec are patrons with the mod's traits. Aldresia's parentage is ours; the fallback is an unnamed ward who takes the name as a vow. Castan LIV's father is implied by her dynasty, never named. Jaher campaigns in the east from 1038; if he cannot be at Brasan, a regent delivers his terms.
- **Date conflicts** (Seventh Council 1015 or 1022; Trialmount 1020 or 1021; the "exact" nineteen years; Reán IV dying in 1050, not 1041). We follow the mod and the Timeline, make the nineteen years Canrec's reckoning, and treat anything after 1022 as bendable.
- **Engine.** No ageing rule exists: use an `aged` flag, inline conditions, second portraits and `is.longlived`. Ending 3's claimants are text variants inside one ending. Finale allies use `allies` and reputation-keyed `mods`. About 115 scenes in all, so Act 1 must converge by its second scene.
- **New Chinese terms** for the name table: 代天者, 誓石 (Oathstone), 引路人 (Wayguide), 渡鸦先知会 (Ravenseers), 月火号 (Lunateín), 乌霜边区 (Ebonmarck), 守望会 (the Watch), 收殓人 (tollwright).
- **Community checks (dossier 13).** Gods never settle doctrine on stage; elves are newcomers; institutions are grim and individuals funny; the monster's seat is the harpy. Reddit was reachable only as that dossier's search snippets; every anchor above was read in the Fandom dump or the mods.
