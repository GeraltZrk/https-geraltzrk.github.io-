# s1444 pitch — angle "crowns"

## 1. Title, logline, stakes

**中文名**《空王座》（备选《三把空椅子》）　**English** *Regents of the Empty Throne*

**Logline.** On 11 Nerament 1444 Corin and Korgus Dookanson kill each other in a plaza in Lower Castonath. By next morning three seats have nobody safely in them: Castonath's, the Dove Throne (won on a lie) and, though no one knows it yet, the throne of heaven. You are the nobody the victors pick to mind the truce-ground. Over twenty-four years that job takes you from the ruins of Escann to the Imperial Parliament, down to the Wolf Gate of Khugdihr and through the Deepwoods to a carved wall in Aelantir, deciding who is crowned, whether the orcs stay, and whether Cannor is told its king of gods died in year 1.

**一句话。** 1444 年 11 月 11 日，科琳和科尔古斯在卡斯托纳斯的旧集市同归于尽。第二天早上，有三把椅子没人坐得稳：卡斯托纳斯的，鸽座的，还有天上那一把。各家兵团谁也不服谁，就把你推出来看着停战的那张桌子。往后二十四年，你从东坎诺的废墟走到帝国议会，下到玛瑙之门，再穿过深林去阿伦蒂尔看一面墙。谁当王，兽人留不留，诸神之王死了一千四百年这件事说不说出去，都要过你这张桌子。

**World stake.** Your choices decide whether Escann ends the 1460s as a restored Castanor, a holy order's march, a free city of men, orcs and goblins, or the mustering-ground of a second Greentide, and whether the Empire's first peace in ninety-five years survives the truth about the Dove Throne. Above both: every king in Cannor rules as regent for Castellos, and you can prove, fifty years before canon does, that the throne they are regents for is empty.

**Emotional spine.** The people who inherit the morning after are all about twenty (Lothane Bluetusk 19, Gurzan 20, Welyam of Gawed 17, your priest 20), and you grow old with them across the time skips, watching what your handshakes turned into. It opens with a dying soldier asking whether Corin lived and ends with a continent asking the same kind of question: the campaign is about what a kind lie costs, and who gets to tell it.

## 3. The truth and the reveal schedule

There is no mastermind. There are three vacancies, each papered over by a lie somebody thought was kind.

1. **Heaven.** Castellos died containing the Ruin in 1 AA. The Silver Dragon went on serving Castanor for five centuries without orders. After 1020 the temples declared every king an avatar; we read that as the patch over a silence. Castonath's elders have sat on the scroll for 944 years. Their devil-worshipping cousins drew the other conclusion.
2. **The Dove Throne.** Lothane did not win. Bardswood has built a real peace on that sentence.
3. **Castan's mantle.** The mountain's magic is gone; the Trials only ever needed witnesses. Anyone can be Castan if enough people answer the shout.

The Prince of the Blackened Branch knows the first truth and wants it carried home: Castanor burned the World Tree in Castellos's name, and nothing would hurt Castanor's children more.

| End of | The player believes |
|---|---|
| Prologue | The war is over; a god chose a squire and she is dead; the question is who rules. |
| Act 1 | Corin is a goddess by acclamation, the temples are shopping for avatars, and the elders hid that the Silver Dragon served unanswered. Castellos is *silent*. |
| Act 2 | The Emperor's duel is a lie. The elves' abandoned sky god was called Castellar. The answer is in Aelantir, which no ship reaches. |
| Act 3 | The Eldest raised the Veil against "what killed the sky". A fey who hates Castanor is offering the road, which should worry you. |
| Act 4 | You have seen the last panel. A carving is not a corpse, but you believe it, and so will everyone. |
| Finale | Whatever you choose to make true. |

The narrator never confirms a god's death: only scrolls, wounds, carvings and testimony.

## 5. Outline

**Prologue: 号角停了 (11–12 Nerament 1444, Castonath).** Five or six scenes per origin: where you were when the horns stopped; a small fight (looters, a wounded warg, a lynch party); a dying person asks a question you may answer kindly or truly, which starts the hidden counter `lies`; the plaza and the two bodies; Maron ringing a cracked bell on Pantheonway; captains, counts and elders agree on one neutral square and on you to keep it. Level 2.

**Act 1: 第二天早上 (winter 1444 – spring 1445; Castonath, Trialmount, Pixiebury, Gulenhyl).** *Who holds the city, and what happens to the losers?*
1. Two funerals: who speaks over Corin; who gets Korgus (Bonecarvers, the White Walls, the Iron Sceptre's necromancer, fire).
2. Silverfall stairs: a Marcher mob against the Crags goblins.
3. Hire Ruuk; ride to Ghurad and to blind Grama Clouded Eye; meet Arosha.
4. A dead elder in the Upper District; the Morningstar's Passage of Blood; the Sashite scroll.
5. North Citadel: Maron shouts the first Trial. Who answers is your standing made audible. Prince Gryf cannot finish the stone map.
6. Gurzan's moot, 4 Silversight: you attend as kin, guest or hostage.
7. Avatar-shopping, then the Tenth Council: Castellos's priests want you to endorse Carleon, Adean's want Gryf, Dominic has Corin. Maron asks where the King is.
8. **Big choice, the Castonath Compact**: League stewardship, Corintar guardianship, a free city with a goblin ward, or partition (canon). Moves Mantle, Hearth, Heaven; Korgus's body moves Tide.

Fights: the stairs (open the sluice); the Passage, fought unarmed; Dogeater wargs at the White Walls. Level 4.

**Act 2: 笑脸与匕首 (1446–47, then 1452; Arbaran, Esmaria, Anbenncóst, Arca Aldresia; Lorentainé or Gaweton).** *What is the Dove's peace worth, and who pays?*
1. The Compact needs the Emperor's seal and grain; each origin carries a second errand (Rogier's pardon, a clan charter, Asra's loans, Dominic's creed).
2. Freecestir and "Escann Stands"; broken men on the road.
3. An Esmari barge at the Thorned Moon Boat Festival: Aucanna "Ironside" and Vincen síl Cestir's broken spear.
4. Bardswood's offer: recognition for three promises (no Castan; Rogier stays dead; Corin's church under the temples). Then the Broken Lance parley: help him bind Aucanna or stiffen her.
5. Arca Aldresia: Delian's inventory of the dead, and where Skylance's wound was.
6. Elvendocks: a prayer-plank with the old gods scratched out. One reads Castellar.
7. One embassy: gentle Kylian and hard Ruben in Lorentainé, or the Winter King, his Magnate and Cobalt's unpaid wages in Gaweton.
8. **Jubilee, 1452.** A man you know draws on the Empress. **Big choices**: stop the blade or not (the alliance; Dove); the proof: publish, sell to Bardswood, hand the Moon a held knife, or burn (Dove, Moon; Eléanor and Tilda).

Fights: the road (or talk their sergeant down); knives on the barge at the lantern hour; a duel under Threeguard rules. Level 6.

*Skip to 1461. Cards show what the Compact became. The humans have grey in their beards; Tilda has not changed.*

**Act 3: 熊心 (1461–64; Castonath, Khugdihr, Marrhold, Bal Mire, Trialmount).** *Does the tide come again?*
1. Grama is dying and talks to you as one kingmaker to another; Arosha challenges for Clouded Eye.
2. Khugdihr: Jarmun, the charter, drums on the Dwarovrod; an expedition-prep hub.
3. **Big choice, the Wolf Gate**: hold it, collapse it, or sell tolls (Tide; the Deep).
4. Marrhold: the two Gryfs, a marriage table with Kalista Blacktower, and Maugunia of Ording courting griffons against Pixiebury (Mantle).
5. Bal Mire and the end of Khozrugan.
6. Gurzan means to climb Trialmount as his father passed seven trials; Gunnar Ebonfrost lands for the same mountain.
7. The muster: canon's coalition, or yours. **Big choice, Gurzan**: Ozarm'Chadash, chains down the Alen, a charter for the clans, or step aside (Tide, Hearth).
8. Narawen Drennen brings Arun's notes. Ruuk opens the elm box.

Fights: the Gate; the race up Trialmount; Bloodgorger berserkers. Level 8.

**Act 4: 最后一幅画 (in, autumn 1464; out, 1467; Shadow Grove, the Vyr, the Bloodgroves, Oldhaven).** *Now that you know, who else must?*
1. Shadow Grove: Troknar, bark starting on his warriors.
2. The Prince's terms: passage both ways for three winters and a promise to carry home what you find.
3. The Vyr: a bright sky with no sun; stay on the path; the rabbit.

3. Call the temples to Pantheonway, an Eleventh Council fifty years early, or keep silent.

5. Trialmount with your claimant: the shout, the stone map (you draw Escann "as it SHALL BE"), the gift, the Oathstone, the crown of mankind, ten laps of a rock, the golem you must disarm (fight).
6. The ending you have unlocked and choose.

## 7. Endings

1. **Castan 山上有人.** Mantle ≥ 8 and your claimant (Kalista, Gryf, or Rogier's line) finishes the Trials, sworn as regent for a god you kept alive, or for Adean. Castanor returns around Castonath; the Empire arms; clans are vassals or exiles by Hearth.
2. **Sword and Shield 剑与盾.** Truth ≥ 6, Heaven ≥ 7. Pantheonway names Corin. Bluetusk still refuses a crown. The Corinite Proclamation happens in Castonath in 1468, not Ionntrás in 1519, and the right to revolt reaches an Empire whose Emperor is dying.
3. **Nobody Sits 没有人坐.** Truth ≥ 8, Hearth ≥ 6, Mantle ≤ 5. You publish and name no heir. The Compact becomes a charter; the Oathstone oath is recut without gods; the Court comes apart temple by temple, without a war over the succession. Witch-Kings will quote you too. *Bittersweet.*
4. **The Kind Lie 好心的谎.** Truth ≤ 2. The canon course, chosen knowingly: two generations of peace, a priest who never gets his answer, and 1518 waiting. *The defensible wrong choice.*
5. **The Green Mantle 绿披风.** Hearth ≥ 8, Clans high. Arosha Oakbreaker swears on the Oathstone. A Castanor of two peoples; the Marchers who bled at Rottenstep leave it.
6. **Second Tide 第二次潮.** Tide ≥ 8. Black Orcs come through Khugdihr and Castonath is besieged in 1468. The West unites at last, out of fear. An orc who stands with Gurzan has his reasons: every clan that tried peace was sold.
7. **Moon over the Dove 月照鸽座.** Dove ≤ 2, Moon high. Aucanna raises the broken lance, Rogier rides west at sixty-two, the Emperor offers a duel he once faked. Justice for Skylance, and the war everyone was too tired for.

**Epilogue cards** look ahead by track: the 1470 election and the Chastening of Wex; Rogieria and the crown Bluetusk refuses; Khugdihr's queen; the Pioneers in South Castonath; Vertesk's chains or their absence; Laurens síl Place, Varina Escer and the Black Demesne; Endral in 1490; Maron at ninety-six in 1520, relieved or not; Ruuk's "Once was enough."

## 9. Risks, and where I disagree with the brief

**Canon strain**
- *The mural in the 1460s.* The party arrives by a road that cannot be charted, takes rubbings at most, and leaves the temple for its canonical finders. Early publication moves the Corinite schism forward fifty years. That is the campaign's largest permitted bend, and the baseline ending keeps 1518.
- *The Silver Dragon.* An avatar active until 500 for a god dead since year 1 is a contradiction canon leaves open. Do not solve it. Offer two readings (a loyal orphan; simply a dragon who liked us) and let priests fight.
- *The Sashite scroll, Delian's inventory, the prayer-plank* are inventions set into real silences. Each is a document a character can call forged. If a later canon update fills the silence, cut the document and keep the scene.
- *Ruuk* comes from an undated Sapchopper mission chain. We give him a past, not a death; his canon line about getting lost in the Feyrealm reads as our Act 4. His father's clan turns Emerald only decades later: show bark at the edges, no more.
- *Chroniclers* are still a sect inside the Magisterium in 1444 [wiki: Order of Chroniclers]; write them that way.
- *Loose dates*: the Broken Lance and Grama's death are undated; Eilísa dies in 1447 in the EU4 file, at the jubilee on the wiki. Follow the wiki.
- *Dossier 13 (community) did not exist when this was written*, and Reddit is unreachable from here. Fan-sensitive points (Corin and Lothane, Bluetusk refusing crowns, orcs written as people) follow the wiki and mod text and need a pass once that dossier lands.

**Engine strain**
- Five prologues plus four shared acts is about 115 scenes. Variation lives in `if` paragraphs, tagged options and the Table hub (once-options), never in forked acts.
- No armies: every battle is one fight with specials plus two checks.
- An orc in Anbenncóst needs a written reason (Bluetusk's herald, a safe-conduct) and race-tagged options in every Act 2 scene.
- `lies` must be counted from the prologue, or the Bloodgroves scene has nothing to bite on.
- Cap each act at eight speaking names; the rest go to the codex.

**Disagreements**
1. *Not "across the sea".* A ship in the 1460s pre-empts Endral and Venáil. The fey road is canon, unrepeatable, and its price is the time skip.
2. *One gate, not the Dwarovar.* Khugdihr is where the dwarven race and the second Greentide are the same question. More tunnel makes this a dungeon.
3. *The player is never crowned.* The earlier card game let the user rule Escann. Here you own a door on two barrels.
4. *Open at dusk on the 11th* and wake on the 12th: the mod's own text says "yesterday" [eu4: anb_age_of_greentide `greentide.0`].
