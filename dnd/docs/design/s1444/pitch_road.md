# s1444 pitch — angle "the road"

## 1. Title, logline, stakes

**《送神》**（备选《抬棺人》）· ***The Burial Detail***　题记：请神容易送神难。

**Logline.** The morning after Corin and Korgus Dookanson kill each other in Castonath's Old Bazaar, every host in the city lends one pair of hands to a burial detail, and the detail is handed the one corpse nobody can agree on: the Dookanson's. Nobody ever dismisses it. Over twenty-eight years the job takes five strangers around Escann, down the dead rail of the Dwarovar, through the Empire's hollow peace and out by a fey road to the elves' ruined Oldhome, burying what other people would rather keep using: an orc messiah, a ten-thousand-year grudge, a duke's last duel, and finally the king of the gods, who has been dead since year 1 and never had a funeral.

**一句话。** 1444 年 11 月 11 日，科琳和科尔古斯·杜坎森在卡斯托纳斯的旧集市同归于尽。仗打赢了，尸首没人收。各家兵团各出一个人，凑了一支收尸队，头一趟差事就是杜坎森本人：边区兵想把他挂上城门，他儿子想抬回去供着，死灵法师已经开了价。这支队伍后来一直没人下令解散。二十八年里，你们走完东坎诺，顺着矮人的死铁道下到蛇脊山脉底下，混过帝国那点靠不住的太平，再跟着一个地精钻进深林，从妖精的路去了精灵的老家。一路埋的都是别人舍不得埋的：一个兽人的救主，一笔记了几千年的账，一位大公的最后一场决斗。最后一个是诸神之王。他灰元元年就死了，一千四百多年，没人给他办过丧事。

**World stake.** What the detail does with each body decides whether Korgus's son gets a relic for a second Greentide, whether orcs and goblins keep a hearth in Escann, who holds Castonath when the tide returns in 1472, and whether the Empire's first peace in ninety-five years survives the truth about the Dove Throne. Last, it carries home, or leaves on a wall in Aelantir, the proof that every crown in Cannor is held in regency for a dead god, forty-six years before canon prints it, and chooses whether heaven gets an heir by blood, an heir by merit, or a funeral and an empty chair.

**Emotional spine.** Five people stuck with the worst job in a victorious army become the only family any of them has, and every time skip asks who is still at the fire and what the road took. Under it runs one question put to each corpse they carry: do the dead belong to whoever can use them, or are they owed the truth and a grave?

## 3. The truth, and when the player learns it

There is no conspiracy. Cannor runs on its dead, and every power here is using one: Dominic uses Corin's body to make a goddess, Gurzan his father's to make a cause, the Iron Sceptre anyone's for labour, the dwarves six thousand years of them for claims, Lothane III Adénn's to hold a throne, and every king Castellos's to hold a crown. The detail's trade is taking the body away. Revelations come only as documents, objects and testimony; no god speaks.

|---|---|
| Prologue | Two demigods are dead and no dragon came. A boy novice asks you, since you are going past the walls anyway, to find out why. |

| Act 2 (1450) | The orcs' god was an elf with a laboratory, and the same wall lists the elves' own gods, one of them "Castellar, of the Sky". The answer is an elven answer. |
| Act 3 (1461) | Castellar is Castellos; the fleet that fled Oldhome wrote down where his temple stood and never looked back. A throne can sit on a corpse for decades: you have just seen one do it. The sea is shut. The forest is not. |
| Act 4 | He died on the first day of year 1, one arm out, holding the Ruin off the rest of the world. The same day the Eldest spent itself on the Veil, and Corin killed what was left of it. Nobody answers at the wall. Outside it is 1472. |

## 5. Outline

**Prologue (per origin, 5–6 scenes, 11 Nerament 1444, level 2).** Where you stood when they fell; an errand with a check; a small fight (looters, a berserker who has not heard, ghouls); a mercy asked (a goblin still chained, a dying man's wish); the captains' quarrel at dusk; the cart. Ushka is already measuring Korgus's knuckles and Tan holds the lantern. Three other hands share the cart; none finishes Act 1.

**Act 1 — 没人肯埋的兽人 The Orc Nobody Would Bury.** Escann, Nerament 1444 to Silversight 1445. *Who owns the dead?*
1. Night one: Varina offers a chest of crowns, the Marchers want the head on a gate, Gurzan's envoy wants his father, Ushka wants her knives. The truce needs the body out of town.
2. Out through Balgar's Underpass; Chainbreak goblins hiding there; Nixee guides for a struck chain and a share.

## 7. Endings

1. **《送神》 Seeing Him Off** (bittersweet; hardest). Word ≥ 7, a conclave held, Peace ≥ 5, Scale within ±2, Tan told. A funeral on Pantheonway forty-eight years early. The throne is left empty on purpose and kings keep the word "regent". The schism still comes, later and smaller. Nobody thanks you.
2. **《剑上天》 The Sword Enthroned.** Scale ≥ +3, Word ≥ 6. Dominic proclaims Corin heir of heaven in the city where she died. Escann gets one faith, and orcs who kneel to it keep their hearths; the west gets its war of temples a generation early.
3. **《长子》 The Eldest Son.** Scale ≤ −3, Peace ≥ 6. The temples are told first and name Adean quietly. Crowns stay on. The Corintar become a sect, and whoever holds the Dove Throne has heaven's seal on it.
4. **《军令未撤》 The Standing Order** (canon; bittersweet). Word ≤ 2, door sealed. The detail swears and scatters. Endral sails in 1490, the pamphlets come in 1518, Garaele neither confirms nor denies. The price is forty-six more years of Tan's dawn office.
5. **《卡斯坦的披风》 Castan's Mantle** (the defensible wrong choice). Someone climbs what is left of the Trials and swears at the Oathstone: you, Kalista Blacktower, or, with Embers ≥ 8, Gurzan Bear-Heart, since the mountain was open to anyone. The oath forces the truth out. Castonath has order by spring and an empire's appetite within a lifetime.
6. **《白骨有价》 Bones Have a Price** (the other one). Unburied ≥ 8. The city is saved by its dead: Korgus's skeleton holds Southgate against his son, and nobody in Castonath goes hungry that winter. The Iron Sceptre reads your rubbing as the first proof that the chair is free for the taking.
7. **《第四个人选》 The Fourth Candidate** (`divine`). A living god is in the room when the throne is declared empty, and the clerics turn round.

**Epilogue cards** by tracks, companions and origin: Pixiebury, 1467; the crown Bluetusk refuses, 1473; Endral finding a cairn, or a door, in 1490; 1518 with nothing left to print; the Corinite Proclamation pre-empted, hardened or unneeded; the Crimson Deluge, about which nobody proves anything; Esthíl and the Witch-Kings; Unguldavor or Barumand; the Grudgebearers lost in 1493, with or without Belgrak; Garaele in 1803; Tan in 1520; the roll, every name on it.

## 8. Sample moments

**序章开场（出身 E）**
旧集市的石板是灰的，你的手背也该跟着发灰。它没变。杜坎森倒在卖咸鱼的摊子边上，胸口插着半截长剑，那个红头发的人类压着他的左臂。你盯着自己的手看了很久，还是绿的。喊撤的是兽人话，越喊越远。一个边区兵拿矛尖顶住你的后腰，问旁边的人：这个还喘气，算俘虏还是算尸首？

## 9. Risks and disagreements

- **1472, not the 1460s.** I end on the canonical Second Battle of Castonath and pay for it with eleven years lost on the fey road, which the Veil lore and the Prince's trick on Oubblig support. For the 1460s, shrink the toll and let Embers bring the battle forward.
- **Aelantir before 1490.** Reached on foot through the Feyrealm, with no ship, chart or colony; Endral still "rediscovers" it. Seal the door and 1518 stands. Canon does not fix the Mural Temple's site.
- **Canon companions.** Garaele lives to 1803, so she is downed, never dead, and leaves only by choice; the sealed ending explains her canon silence. Belgrak's canon fate is his default.
- **Korgus's body** is my largest invention. The Khak-Bazi option leaves canon untouched: the wiki's cult of Korgus spreads by envoys and river rites and needs no relic.
- **Hul-Jorkad.** In the mod, orc scholars reach the Ducaniel reading later; here it is rubbings and an argument, optional, never narrator-confirmed. "Castellar" on that wall can be cut; the Pastkeeper log carries the same clue.
- **Trialmount.** The Trials are defunct in canon; ending 5 rests on the oath and on who the city accepts, not on revived magic.
- **Gods stay silent.** The proof is a wall, a staff and nobody answering. Clerics' magic keeps working, as the wiki's note on faith allows.
- **Three departures from the brief.** The orc seat is a prisoner of the losing host, so the "three roads" are the player's own Act 1. Tan waits instead of joining: someone has to stay and age. Factions reuse the three estates from the user's earlier card game; rename them by their mottoes if that feels too familiar.
- **Engine load.** Five lands mean five sets of art and enemies, about 112 scenes. Time skips need `is.immortal` text and each act its four god-reactive scenes. The dead-cart camp is the one recurring hub. The cart stays dry, never gory, and selling the Dookanson must cost more than it pays.
- **Three human seats from the west.** If a native seat is wanted, swap C for the Count's League (B55).
