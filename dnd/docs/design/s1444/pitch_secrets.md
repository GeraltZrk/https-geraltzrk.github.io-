# s1444 pitch — the metaphysical mystery

## 1. Title, logline, stakes

**《银龙没有再来》 / *The Silver Dragon Did Not Come***

**Logline（中文）** 1444 年 11 月 11 日，科琳和杜坎森死在卡斯托纳斯的旧集市里。东坎诺的人等了二十年银龙，银龙没来；现在仗打完了，他们想知道为什么。你从一座没了屋顶的神殿出发，上试炼山，下矮人的旧厅，穿过深林里一扇不该开的门，走到精灵老家的一面墙跟前。墙上刻着答案：众神之王一千四百多年前就死了。回来的路上，皇帝、神殿、科琳的信徒、杜坎森的儿子，人人都想要你怀里那张拓片。给谁，不给谁，烧掉，还是抬着它办一场迟了一千四百年的丧事，你说了算。

**Logline (EN)** The morning after Corin dies, a ruined kingdom asks why its god never came. Twenty years later you hold the answer, two generations before the world is meant to find it, and every throne in Cannor wants to decide what happens next.

**World stake.** Who sits on the throne of heaven (Adean by blood, Corin by merit, or no one), and therefore by what right any crown in Cannor is worn, is settled in 1464 instead of 1520, as a funeral or as a war. Tied to it: whether Escann burns in a second Greentide, whether orcs and goblins keep a hearth there, who holds Castonath, and whether the Emperor's peace survives a truth that makes his own lie look small.

**Emotional spine.** A boy priest has said the dawn office every day to a god who never answered, and you are the one who finds out why and decides whether he, and everyone like him, gets told. Corin was given an apotheosis within a year; Castellos has waited fourteen centuries for a funeral, and the campaign is the walk to it.

The key word is in the religion's own name. A *Regent* Court rules for an absent king. Nobody asked how long a regency lasts once the king is dead.

## 2. Canon anchors

- **11 Nerament 1444.** Corin kills Korgus in Castonath and dies: Corinsfall, Dookansfall, Chainbreak [eu4: localisation/anb_age_of_greentide_l_english.yml `greentide.0–.6`; wiki: Corin (person)]. Every orc's skin stops changing colour at that moment [wiki: Orc].

- **Tenth Council, 1445.** "A conclave of adventurer-clerics" under Dominic of Gallowspeak; Agradosian elders arrive with an eight-point prophecy Corin fits [eu4: localisation/anb_events_religion_l_english.yml `corinite.100`; wiki: Cult of Agrados]. Dominic is "more than nine decades" old in 1520, so about twenty now [eu4: `unraveling.6`].
- **Trialmount.** The Trials include an Oathstone with no founder listed, two feuding forest spirits (Castan XIV) and an immortal golem to disarm (Castan VI); their magic was "drained, severed" in 1020 [wiki: Trials of Castan; wiki: Battle of Trialmount].
- **The Silver Dragon**, Castellos' avatar 651 BA–500 AA, once led the lost Castanites out of the Deepwoods and died killing Zaamalot [wiki: Avatar; wiki: Silver Dragon of Castanor].
- **The secret.** The Mural of Castellar (last panel "in a less refined hand": *Castellar, God of the Sky*, *The End*), the Broken Scepter, the image cast on the clouds from Adráil, and an Eleventh Council where Castellos' own voice is "an aged Castellyrian refugee… relieved to finally have an answer… why the Silver Dragon did not come again" [eu4: localisation/anb_age_of_unraveling_l_english.yml `unraveling.1–.6`]. Published 1518, ratified 1520; the temple has "a small supplemental library" [wiki: Castellos]. - **Dookan.** Ducaniel, cornered in the lowest level of Hul-Jorkad, "began preparing a spell, which can be seen in the temple"; brown-orc shamans "have been calling him Dookanil all this time" [eu4: localisation/Flavour_ShatteredCrown_H72_l_english.yml `shatteredcrown.41`; Flavour_Shadowdreamer_H83 `shadowdreamer.26`; wiki: Hold of Hul-Jorkad].
- **Garaele Pastkeeper**, moon elf, b. 1401, cleric of the Dame, field Chronicler in Escann in 1444, in the Serpentspine with the Asra Expedition in 1450, later "allegedly" among the finders of the Mural Temple: "a rumor she refused to confirm nor deny for the rest of her life" [wiki: Garaele Pastkeeper].
- **The road west.** Seasonal Gates join the Deepwoods to Eordand's Domandrod, whose Gladewardens complain that "elves like us have trespassed" [eu4: localisation/anb_deepwoods_l_english.yml `cyranvar.10–.14`]; crossing the Veil can cost years [wiki: Verdant Veil]; the orc sellsword Ruuk Elmscourge tracks gladeways [eu4: same file, `sapchopper.10–.12`]; the winter witch Immarel Winterswrath falls in 1461 [wiki: Witch-King; wiki: Early Modern Eordand Timeline].
- **The Dove Throne's lie.** Erlan Silmuna stabbed Adénn; Lothane III took the credit; "a pair of nameless divination wizards" expose it in 1468 [wiki: Empire of Anbennar; wiki: Lothane III síl Wex].
- **Baseline Escann.** Gurzan's 4 Silversight gatherings and a "new Greentide" broken at the Second Battle of Castonath, 1472 [wiki: Bloodgorger Clan]; Sack of Pixiebury by the Ashen Rose and Marrhold, 1467 [wiki: Ghurad Heartgrinder]; Pioneers take South Castonath, 1479 [wiki: Pioneer's Guild]; Endral reaches Aelantir, 1490 [wiki: Rediscovery of Aelantir].
- **The vultures.** The Men of the Morningstar, a nobles-only cult of Kazraiel, Castellos' cast-off half [wiki: Kazraiel]; "Kazraiel shall rise" is a canon reaction to the news [eu4: `unraveling.5.c`].

**Invented:** Evin of Scepterton (the unnamed 1520 refugee, given a name and a youth); Dakha Clouded Eye; Bimble Lanternwood (who can be one of the two nameless diviners); nine premades; the Castonath Moot of winter 1444; the forest spirits' testimony; a fleetborn cradle-song naming Castellar; a soldiers' shrine to Castellar in Ducaniel's redoubt; the Mural Temple's site (canon gives none); one party slipping through a Gate decades before Cyranvar opens them; the Tenth Council sitting in Castonath (canon: "in Escann").

## 3. The truth and the reveal schedule

**The truth (S1 + S3 joined).** Castellos was Castellar, sky god of the precursor elves. He died on the first day of 1 AA trying to hold back the Ruin. The Ruin was Ducaniel's Wrath, and Ducaniel is the being orcs call Dookan. So the heaven of Cannor has been a regency for a dead king since the calendar began, and the hand that emptied the throne made the people who destroyed his kingdom. The first half shakes every crown. The second can save Escann's orcs (they were a weapon, and owe their maker nothing) or doom them (their god killed ours). The narrator confirms none of it: the player holds a rubbing, a broken staff, some graffiti in a dwarf hold and the word of two trees.

| End of | The player believes | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Prologue | Castellos abandoned Escann. Everyone says so. | A roofless temple; a roll of unanswered petitions. |
| Act 1 | False answer 1: He is angry at Castanor's sins. Private doubt: the silence began on the Day of Ashen Skies, long before those sins. | Fey testimony on Trialmount: the Silver Dragon asked the sky and got nothing either. |
| Act 2 | False answer 2: the gods are alive but were never ours. Castellos was on elven banners 7,500 years ago; Dookan was an elf. | Cradle-song in Anbenncóst; shrine and workroom at Hul-Jorkad. |
| Act 3 | He is dead. He died doing his job. Ducaniel did it. | The Mural, its library, the Sceptre. |
| Act 4 | Last doubt: Corin's road to godhood was written down in advance by people who already worshipped a dead god. Merit, or a script? | Agradosian elders; Dominic's own account. |
| Finale | Whatever the player has decided is true enough to build on. | — |

## 5. Outline

**Prologue (11–13 Nerament 1444, Castonath; 5–6 scenes each).** Where you are when the news lands; one small fight (looters, berserkers fighting on in a lost battle, ghouls at the grave pits); one choice that sets a first reputation; the two bodies in the Old Bazaar; Pantheonway at dawn. Level 2.

**Act 1 — 灰烬里的会议 (Castonath, Trialmount, Pixiebury, Elderlan; Nerament 1444 to summer 1445).** *Who holds Castonath, and why did He not come?*
1. The Castonath Moot in Legion Square: companies, counts and Patricians divide the city gate by gate.
2. Garaele hires you on a Chronicler's Grant to check three Silver Dragon sightings before the temples parade them: a painted wyvern hide, a "miracle" over the Charred Tower staged by Patricians who want it exposed, and an old woman who looked up every day for twenty years and saw nothing.
3. Pixiebury: Ghurad asks for a letter of safe-conduct; Maugunia of Ording's Ashen Rose wants the same orcs as "servants". Dakha arrives as Grama's eyes.
4. The avatar hunt: help or sink Carleon's and Gryf's candidacies.
5. 4 Silversight 1445, Elderlan: Gurzan's first gathering, attended, spied on or broken up.
6. Trialmount: the dead Trials, the two spirits and their feud, the golem, the Oathstone. The spirits remember the silver one asking the sky questions after "the grey day".
7. The Tenth Council. You are a witness. What you say goes into scripture.

*Choices:* who garrisons Pantheonway and the bridges (Castonath, reputations); Heartgrinder's safe-conduct (Hearth +, Embers −, League −); your testimony (Heir toward Corin or Adean; Truth +1 if you speak of the silence).
*Fights:* Deadfang "Death Whisperers" in the Underpass (special: smash the ghost-jars); the golem (special: disarm it and the fight ends).
Level 4. **Skip to 1450.**

**Act 2 — 铅与鸽 (Anbenncóst, then Khugdihr to Hul-Jorkad; 1450–52).** *What is a god made of, and what is a throne made of?*
1. Elvendocks: in a mansion built on a beached arkship, Garaele's fleetborn great-aunt hums a cradle-song with a name the Fleet stopped saying at sea. The Magisterium seals the hymnal next morning.
2. Bimble is assigned as your escort and says plainly that she is a spy.
3. Jon Bardswood reads your notes and sees at once what "regent" means if the king is dead. He offers the Throne's seal and purse for exclusivity. The Emperor is seen once, across a garden whose tulip beds have been replaced.
4. A Damerian asks Bimble whether a palace wall remembers.
5. East with the Asra Expedition: Khugdihr, Darkscale kobolds; Garaele finds her parents' last notebook.
6. The dead railway: Shattered Crown Old-Dookanists call Korgus a false prophet to Dakha's face; the lost Grudgebearer camp of Belgrak Cavedweller.
7. Hul-Jorkad's lowest level: the workroom, the spell on the temple wall, and a soldiers' shrine with a one-armed god holding a sceptre. Garaele reads one name. Dakha reads the other.

*Choices:* Bardswood's bargain (Peace +, Throne +, but the Throne now knows); Khugdihr for the charter, the kobold tenants or a remnant claim (Holds, Hearth); what leaves Hul-Jorkad: copies, the tablets, or rubble (Embers ±, Clans, Dakha).
*Fights:* a Darkscale trap gallery (special: set off their own acid mines); Ironweave Skitterers in the Jorkad sewers; a Shattered Crown warband at the temple door (special: an Ozarm'Chadash, if an orc stands for the party).
Level 6.

**Act 3 — 冬之门 (Oldhaven, the Deepwoods, Eordand, the Ynn, the Mural Temple; 1452, then 1461–63).** *Is He dead, and what does knowing cost?*
1. The burnt World Tree at Oldhaven; Arun Drennen, who helped kill the Eldest, and Ruuk Elmscourge guide you down the Vyrs.
2. The Winter Gate. The toll is time. You walk for an afternoon.
3. Snow in the Domandrod. Garaele counts the moons three times: 1461. Gladewardens arrest the elves for trespass and have no word for the rest of you.
4. The last winter of Immarel's Red Winter: get through her lines or help Jahainar Truespear break them.
5. The Old Man of the Archives sells the War of Two for something that would otherwise be forgotten.
6. South down the Ynn: crop-eared river knights, a funeral barge, a toll bridge, a lord who takes Evin for a priest of the River.
7. The Mural Temple: the hall, the last panel, the library that names the destroyer.
8. A ruinborn shrine, a stone tabernacle, a six-foot white staff with its gem burst.

*Choices:* the Old Man's price (a memory, a spell, or go without the second half of the truth); the wall: rub it, read it, deface the name, reseal the door (Truth ceiling; Garaele, Evin); the Sceptre: carry it, leave it, give it to the River; the way home: the Autumn Gate, quietly, or a Caamas hull across the sea, which opens Aelantir twenty-seven years early (Peace −, Holds +).
*Fights:* Immarel's thralls on a frozen lake (special: break the ice); Silent Watchers in the mural hall (special: put out the lamps).
Level 8. **The skip is the Gate: nine years. You come home in 1463.**

**Act 4 — 回来的人 (Escann and the Empire, 1463–64).** *Who gets the truth?*
1. Homecoming: Carleon is seventy-two, Dominic is forty with a church, and your origin shows you what happened without you.
2. Five suitors: Dominic and Lothane Bluetusk (proclaim Corin now); envoys of Ruben VI and Adean's priests (name the son, in council, quietly); Bardswood, ninety and in a hurry (seal it); the Chroniclers (print it); a Patrician of the Morningstar (sell it, or be robbed).
3. Evin is asked to preach on Dragonsight.
4. Pixiebury: Ashen Rose and Marrodic riders are already probing toward the sack canon dates to 1467. Warn Ghurad, turn the knights, or ride with them.
5. 4 Silversight 1464, Elderlan: Gurzan Bear-Heart, one eye, half an arm. Dakha speaks.
6. Bimble is summoned to cast the palace scene.
7. The Morningstar move on the Sceptre; a chase through the Underpass.

*Choices:* who gets the first copy; Pixiebury; Dakha's words; the divination (expose Lothane, bury it, or trade it to him for a free hand in heaven). All six tracks move.
*Fights:* Pixiebury's wall, from either side; Blackguards in the Underpass (special: flood a channel).
Level 10.

**Finale — 十一月十一日 (Castonath, 11 Nerament 1464, twenty years to the day; 10 scenes).** Every power is in the city for Corinfall. The procession; Evin's temple, still roofless; a council that convenes or collapses; Gurzan at Southgate if Embers are high (canon's Second Battle of Castonath, eight years early); the climb to the Oathstone, where someone swears the old oath or nobody does; the last thing you do with the staff. *Fights:* Southgate, or the Morningstar's last attempt; a closing confrontation a high check can settle without blood.

## 7. Endings

1. **《神的葬礼》 A Funeral for a God** (bittersweet; hardest). Truth ≥ 7, Peace ≥ 5, Castonath ≥ 6, Succession within ±2, Sceptre carried, Evin present. A council sits on Pantheonway fifty-six years early, agrees He is dead, and on purpose agrees on nothing else. The Court keeps its name: regents carry on for a king they have now buried. The schism still comes, later and smaller. Evin keeps a tomb.
2. **《剑与盾》 Sword and Shield.** Truth ≥ 6, Succession ≥ +3. Dominic proclaims Corin heir of heaven with the staff in his hands. Escann gets an answer and Castonath a half-orc protector; the west gets the War of the Elensbridge while Lothane III is still alive to lose it.
3. **《长子》 The Eldest Son.** Succession ≤ −3, Peace ≥ 6. The temples are told first and name Adean in good order. Crowns stay on. The Corintar become a cult, the right to revolt is denied before it is written, and a liar keeps the Dove Throne with heaven's seal on it.
4. **《五十四年》 Fifty-Four Years** (the defensible wrong choice; canon). Truth ≤ 2, door resealed. Escann could not have borne it in 1464, and you may be right. Endral sails in 1490, the mural is printed in 1518, Garaele never confirms or denies. The cost is every dawn office Evin says in between.
5. **《空椅子》 The Empty Chair.** Truth ≥ 8 with no council (Castonath or Peace too low), or chosen by the Crownless. Bimble's friends put the last panel on the clouds over the Dameshead. No heir. Moots and bhailines quote it; so, later, do the Witch-Kings. Castonath is a commune that argues every night and has an orc quarter.
6. **《杜坎之日》 Dookansday.** Embers ≥ 8 and not defused at Southgate, or chosen by a Heartgrinder player. "He slew the Sky-King" goes from fire to fire and the clans take the city they once kept as a hold. Gurzan's reason is plain: "They will sell our children in Vertesk either way." The west finally marches, as it never did in 1424.

**Epilogue cards** (by tracks, companions, origin): Pixiebury 1467; the divination and Ghurad's last summer, 1468; the Second Battle of Castonath, 1472, or its absence; the crown Lothane Bluetusk refuses, 1473; Endral, 1490, or a Caamas ship already rotting in a Lorentish harbour; the Corinite Proclamation, 1519, pre-empted or never needed; the Crimson Deluge, about which nobody proves anything; the Black Demesne quoting your rubbing; Evin in 1520.

## 8. Sample moments

**老萨满**　杜坎森死了。

没人问他怎么知道的。一条沟三十个兽人，都在看自己的手。

埃文把那截游行用的权杖头擦了第三遍，擦完拿布包好，塞回铺盖卷里。

你刚说到“两位各让一步”，左边那棵橡树就笑了，笑得掉了一地橡子。

**战斗开场（试炼山石像）**
石像从坑里站起来，膝盖往下掉碎石。它比北要塞的门还高，右手一把没开刃的铁剑，左手摊着，等人把兵器放上去。一千多年里放上去的兵器都在它脚边，锈成了一堆。它拿剑敲了三下地。没人放。

**结局段落（《五十四年》）**
1520 年，第十一次泛神会议。卡斯特洛斯的席位上坐着个老祭司，东坎诺口音。别人为继承的事吵了三个礼拜，他一直在打盹。散会那天有人问他，神死了，他怎么不难过。

**埃文**　我难过过了。比你们早了大半辈子。

他把那截擦了一辈子的权杖头留在桌上，自己拄着拐走了。

## 9. Disagreements and risks

**Where I depart from the brief.**
- *"Across the sea."* I go through the wood and leave the sea as a choice on the way home. The Gate is on foot, it is canon, it makes the time skip happen to the party, and it keeps 1490 intact unless the player breaks it.
- *Skips "1444, 1450s, 1460s."* Kept, but the second is nine years taken at the Gate. The party comes home nine years younger than the calendar says, to people who aged without them. It is also where the baseline shows: Act 4 opens on what canon did in their absence.
- *Origins.* Count's League replaces the Lorentish and Damerian candidates: the plot's question belongs to Castellyr. Lorent arrives as Adean's party in Act 4; the Sons of Dameria and the heirs to the Moon gather around the divination.

**Canon strain.**
- *Two generations early, and before Endral.* Defused by the Gate, by keeping the party tiny, and by Ending 4 restoring canon exactly. Garaele's canon silence is the hinge.
- *Two canon secrets joined.* "Ducaniel's Wrath" and "Dookanil" are both in the mod, but nobody in canon holds them together this early. Keep both as documents, keep the shamans split as in `shatteredcrown.41`, and never let the narrator rule.
- *Never confirm a godswar* [wiki: Crimson Deluge]. Divine magic comes "through faith rather than being granted by objectively existing deities" [wiki: Magic], so clerics keep their spells after the Mural, a player cleric of Castellos included, and nobody explains why.
- *Invented evidence* (cradle-song, Hul-Jorkad shrine, the spirits) is testimony or graffiti, deniable by any elf who says the names only sound alike.
- *Canon people.* Garaele and Aucan have recorded later lives: they can leave, never die. Dominic, Lothane Bluetusk, Bardswood, Gurzan and Ghurad keep their baseline fates as the default epilogue.
- *Fan-sensitive points:* Corin and Lothane never acted on it; Corin was timid off the field; goblins are people with doorknob problems, not comic relief; precursors are stone and magic, never machines; no portals except thin places; no instant healing by spell.
- *Unsettled sources:* where in Castonath the duel happened; the level of Hul-Jorkad (fourth on the wiki, seventh in the mod). Write "the Old Bazaar" and "the lowest level" and flag both in the bible.
- *No community dossier (13) existed when this was written,* and Reddit is outside the allowed network. Fan expectations here come from the Fandom wiki dump and the mod's event text; re-check against dossier 13.

**Engine strain.**
- About 115 scenes. Origin and faction variation inside the acts must be conditional paragraphs and tagged options, not forks.
- The lost years need a block of conditional effects at the top of Act 4 that drifts each track toward canon unless Acts 1–2 set flags against it. It is the most error-prone data in the campaign: write and test it first.
- Six endings from six tracks risks ties. Fixed priority: Dookansday, Fifty-Four Years, Funeral, Sword and Shield, Eldest Son, with The Empty Chair as the fallthrough.
