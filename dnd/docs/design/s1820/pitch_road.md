Campaign pitch for `s1820` (start: 1 Castanmark 1820, first day of the Empyreal Age). Angle: an adventuring company's long road.

## 1. Title, logline, stake, spine

**Working title.** 《长路》 / *The Long Road*. 副题：迟到者公司的最后一份合同 (the Latecomers' last contract).

**Logline.** On New Year's Day 1820 a Busilari scholar rows out of a whirlpool with a fairy in a jam jar, and the oldest, poorest adventuring company in Anbenncóst is hired to mind the jar. Over sixteen years the firm takes four more jobs: escorting six civil-service wizards to make the wheat grow in a republic that shot its wizards; surveying a railway across the last kingdom of mages; recovering "strayed property" in a slave port; and finding the door in the southern sea that every flag wants to put its name on. Halfway down the road the Company works out that every report it ever filed went to one reader, its silent majority partner: a three-thousand-year-old bronze dragon in a Republican general's uniform, taking inventory of the world's last magic. At the far end a fey court will sign one treaty with one party, and it chooses the firm that never sold the jar.

**一段话简介。** 1820 年元旦，一个布西拉尔学者划着小船从大漩涡里钻出来，怀里抱着几盆怪草，还有一只装在果酱罐里的仙灵。当天晚上，安本科斯特最老、最穷的一家冒险公司接了一单活：看罐子。往后十六年，这家公司又接了四单。护送六个公务员法师，去给一个枪毙过法师的共和国催麦子；替银行勘一条铁路，要穿过最后一个巫师王国；在蓄奴的港口替人找“走失的财产”；最后是南方海上那扇门，谁都想在上头挂自己的门牌。活干到一半你们才弄明白，每一份报告都寄给了同一个人：公司的匿名大股东，一头披着共和国将军制服、活了三千年的青铜龙。他在盘点这世上还剩多少魔法。路的尽头，门那边的仙灵只肯跟一家签约，就是当年没把罐子卖掉的那一家。签什么，你定。

**World stake.** Where the next century's magic comes from now that the comet is dead: a rationed commons, a chartered import from the planes, a crown's trust, one dragon's hoard, a knock on Heaven's gate, or nowhere. That choice settles who is counted as a person and who as a source, and with it the Republic's promises, Escann's master, the Concord's chains, the machines' bills of sale and the name on the door.

**Emotional spine.** A firm of people nobody else would hire becomes a family by doing jobs for money, and then has to decide what it will not do for money. You grow old on this road; the dwarf and the fairy do not, and the ledger records all of it.

## 3. The truth, and when the player learns it

**Rule for writers:** the narrator never rules on metaphysics. Every layer arrives as a ledger, a report, a measurement or a dragon's table talk, and the companions disagree about it.

1. **The comet is dead** and nobody will say so in print until 1838. *(canon)*
2. **Every well still open is somebody.** Blood in Escann, cores and chained hands in the Trollsbay, the fey whose very air is damestear. The Company finds one per contract. *(canon parts; the pattern is the plot)*
3. **One well refills: belief.** The Church has taught since its First Debate that celestial power rises and falls with faith, and Fractalines have been charting the Fragment's hums against church attendance ever since, which is one reason nobody is shown the Fragment. In an age of presses and plebiscites belief can be manufactured, and Republic, Crown, Church and Company are all doing it. *(canon doctrine; invented notebook)*
4. **The Partner.** Aakhet is at the end of a dragon's span. Zerat was the first time in three thousand years that something hurt, and he blamed the cannon. He joined the people who were killing magic to find out how long he had, bought a failing adventuring firm as generals do, and has used it to prospect for a source large enough for one ascension. He will not look at the answer in front of him: he is still breathing because an empire he walked out on lights lamps to him every dusk. *(invented motive; the narrator never confirms the lamps)*
5. **The jar.** Do-Not-Shake was not caught. Her clan sent her to be found, to learn whether the people about to arrive keep their word. *(invented)*
6. **Left open:** whether there is a One God; what is behind the gate; whether Torrieth lives.

## 5. Outline

**Two devices recur in every act.** *The meeting* (团务会): a hub scene before each big choice where the four state their votes; you may overrule as captain, the dissenters remember, and three overrules mean the last meeting is held without you. *The report* (报告): each act closes on a telegram to the Partner, and you choose what goes in it: everything, an edited version, or a lie. Reports move 龙账 and let him act on the world between acts.

**Prologue — per origin, 5–6 scenes, Anbenncóst, level 2.** Where the New Year finds you → what ends the old life → the notice in the *Tribune* → one small fight on the way (Bilge footpads, a debt collector's bullies, a scrap dealer who wants Asset 14) → the office above a chandler's on Forgeway: half-worn brass plate, Etta, the book. You swear a clause. The firm has had no captain since 1811 and the articles require one to sign anything over a hundred crowns, so the newest member is elected, because nobody else wants the liability. A Tellumite crier is shouting about a whirlpool. First job, tonight: the Chroniclers need guards for a jar.

### Act 1 · 收成 (Harvest) — Anbenncóst and Esmaria, Suren–Tearfall 1820. Level 4.
*Who gets fed, when a republic that shot its wizards needs them to bring in the bread?*

1. The Chroniclers' hall. The specimen speaks, to correct the label. Gratto Silcalas has bid for her; a cheque from "our partner" outbids him and she rides with you, on loan.
2. The contract: the War Ministry, on General Deshird's recommendation, hires the firm to escort the Third Harvest Circuit, six state transmuters and a damestear ration wagon, through Esmaria before the referendum. Guilhem is the senior caster.
3. Barge and branch line. Fields that glow at dusk after a casting; Verdancy soil that grows nothing without one; farmers who hate needing it; a pamphleteer with "For Esmaria to unite, Anbennar must divide."
4. The telegram: skip the four districts that polled wrong. One is Guilhem's.
5. Night raid on the ration wagon. They are former war-mages, and they want the crystal, not the coin.
6. Konwell: Whiterose agents offering the Circuit triple wages to cross to Lorent. Lorent-origin players are handed the purse.
7. Referendum day. A brick, a shot from nowhere, a Peacekeeper line that is Burâ's old company.
8. Tearfall night on the barge roof. Nineteen meteors. Do-Not-Shake's light needs the lamp turned down to see. The first report.

**Choices:** the skip-list (obey, cast in secret, read it to the dock, or put it to the meeting) → 白鸽 ±1, 余泪 ±1, Republic rep. The square (stand between, get the mages out, or hold the Ministry's line) → 白鸽 ±2 and the `volleys` flag. The first report → 龙账.
**Fights:** the towpath ambush (specials: cut the barge loose; open the jar); the square (specials: ring the temple bell; talk your old sergeant down, Burâ has advantage).
**Skip:** three years.

### Act 2 · 路权 (Right of Way) — Escann: the Ancardian frontier, Castonath, the railhead below Khugdihr, 1823. Level 6.
*What is a mage worth, and to whom?*

1. The Asra Bank and a League–dwarven syndicate hire the firm to survey and guard the Castonath Line. One state on the route bans engines, the other bans wizards.
2. The navvy camp: Ungulan orcs, half-orcs, Jazhkredu goblins, a Corinite chapel in a boxcar, wages three weeks late.
3. The Ancardian post, where a sentry tests travellers with a lump of raw damestear and watches whose eyes follow it. Oto of Moreced wants the line for troop trains.
4. Castonath: no gaslight, eight towers, a plaque where Corin fell. Yamina Gracosael, ninety-three, receives you standing.
5. The bleeding-rooms. Ancardia taxes its registered mages a pint a month; the Demesne sells its apprentices' blood to pay its mercenaries. Each has proof about the other. The line would carry it west by the tank-car.
6. A Floodborn girl of twelve, due for the rite, and the vacant seat.
7. The strike, and the National Guard sent to end it.
8. A Dûr-Vazhatun astronomer on the works train with a box of plates he cannot get published. The share register in Etta's trunk: the Partner bought into the blood trade nine days after your last report.

**Choices:** the route (through Castonath: 余泪 −1, Chartered +2, the Demesne opened to industry; round by Ancardia: Escann unified by bullets, Crown and Wand −2; no line: the bank fails, the firm's word suffers). The blood ledgers (publish, sell, burn, or report them) → 枷锁 ±2, 龙账. The strike → Unowned rep, Burâ.
**Fights:** the roundhouse (Guard and a steam traction engine, `noAwe`; specials: uncouple, vent the boiler); the bleeding-room under the old Dragonforge (a Blood Baron's guards and a Royal Wizard's apprentice in mid-Drowning; special: get her breathing, which ends the fight).
**Skip:** five years.

### Act 3 · 货单 (Bill of Lading) — Aelantir: Port Isobel, Marlliande, the levee road, Ynnsmouth, 1828. Level 8.
*Who is cargo?*

1. Two contracts for one job. A Marlliande consortium hires the famous old firm to recover runaway orcs and "defective" gin-automata. Archprovost Undercliffe wants the same people walked north to Ynnsmouth, where two flags mean no warrant runs.
2. Customs at Port Isobel wants Burâ's papers and a bill of sale for Asset 14.
3. Dinner on the estate. The host is charming, does not eat, and signs in a beautiful hand.
4. Asset 14 wakes: eight years beside a fairy whose breath is damestear. (If you are Asset 14, the gin-units wake because you spoke to them at machine speed.) 783-A12 speaks for the machines on the Line.
5. The Fractaline's notebook: thirty years of Fragment hums plotted against attendance. Undercliffe has never shown it to his flock.
6. Burâ's raid.
7. The levee, the catchers, the dogs.
8. Ynnsmouth. A Boek court, a Sarda customs house, and a guest from upriver in a good coat who says you smell of bronze. Varlengeilt is three hundred and eighty-four, worshipped, and in excellent health. He explains over fish why the old ones who are not prayed to grow tired, and suggests you ask your partner how he sleeps.

**Choices:** which contract you are fulfilling (planters, the Line, or both by fraud) → 枷锁 ∓3, Concord rep, the firm's word. Asset 14: strike it from "equipment" and enter it as a member, by vote → 枷锁, Etta. The raid. Ynnsmouth as free soil → tilts the Concord–Sarda crisis.
**Fights:** the levee (catchers, hounds; specials: open the sluice; the machines hold the line); the gin-house (a militia with a war-pattern unit still obeying, `noAwe`; special: read out its serial number and ask what day it is).
**Skip:** seven years.

### Act 4 · 门牌 (The Nameplate) — Port Jaher, the Straits of Tef, Karshyr, Aramoole, Síl Berkhen's Land, 1835. Level 10.
*Whose door is it?*

1. Everyone wants to hire you. Busilar has the frequencies, the CLSTC a draft charter, the Rectorate a ship, Lorent a fleet under an admiral born in 1449 who remembers when adventurers mattered.
2. Port Jaher. Álvar has horns now. Do-Not-Shake asks to be introduced.
3. Audience aboard the Lorentish flagship. Castíne, and the other voice. "I am the only delegate here with a personal interest."
4. Karshyr: tour graduates are ministers; the Guild has noticed that a Spire hums when the jar goes past.
5. Aramoole at dusk. Ten thousand lamps to an empty throne. Janebisa Ohithird asks, politely, whether you have seen Him.
6. The Partner in person. He has resigned his commission. He does not fly here: Khatalashya's guns are across the lake.
7. Six drafts on the table and the longest meeting.
8. The fork, the mast, the maelstrom.

**Choices:** tell Karshyr what the Spires are (界门 +2 for the doorstep peoples), sell it, or keep it. Castíne or Ioriel: whom you address decides the `crown` flag. The Partner: expose him to Hakhepra, to the Republic, to Khatalashya's gunners, or keep his secret → 龙账 ∓3. The frequency: bought, given, stolen, or sung.
**Fights:** the vault (special: a wrong note opens a maelstrom the size of a washtub); a Khatalashyan boarding party in the strait (special: turn the Stone Lion's lamp).

### Finale · 彼岸 (The Far Shore) — a sea in the Feyrealm; you come home on 1 Castanmark 1836. 8–12 scenes.

A sea where magic is weather and the jar fogs from the inside. Do-Not-Shake at full light, and her clan, who address her by rank. The Court of the Slack Tide will treat with one party, the one that carried its envoy fourteen years without selling her; if she is lost, it treats with no one and only the endings of force remain. The drafts are read. Then bronze wings over the water for the first time since Zerat: a boss fight (`noAwe`), or a negotiation with a creature who has never once agreed he lost. Beyond the court, if you choose to go and someone will pilot, the dark Celestial shallows: orphaned angels asking for news, a boat hauled up at a door with no handle, and 168 years of tally marks that stop without a body. Knock or do not. The last meeting. One signature. At the harbour of Torapriaka the clerk tells you the year.

## 7. Endings

1. **《特许状》 The Charter** (canon's course; the fallback). The sea goes to the companies. Aerated damestear arrives by the shipload, the lamps never dim, and the Feyrealm gets its own Trollsbay. Rabon Deshird retires. *Needs the Chartered's draft, or nothing better.*
2. **《公海》 The Common Sea.** No flag and no charter. Tolls are paid in what the fey count as money: favours, festival days, true names. Damestear comes by quota, and a clause forbids any signatory to draw magic from a person. Slower lamps, forty years of lawsuits, an enforcement problem nobody has solved. *Needs Do-Not-Shake, 彼岸 ≥ 6, 枷锁 ≤ 3, 龙账 ≤ 5, two powers at rep ≥ 4.*
3. **《红宝石和约》 The Ruby Peace** (wrong, for good reasons). Black damestear is banned by treaty and the Wand holds the sea in trust. A long, tidy, hereditary peace; mages are safe and so, mostly, is everyone they like. Castíne's constitution or the Ioriellan Empire, by the `crown` flag. *Needs Crown and Wand ≥ 6 and Guilhem or a Lorent origin.*
4. **《关门》 The Shut Door** (bittersweet). The fork goes into the strait and the door closes behind one fairy. Magic gutters out by the 1880s. Nobody is fuel, and a baker's son can always say no to a wizard. The last live damestear is reserved by statute for cores if 枷锁 is low; if not, the machines lie down one by one. *Player's choice at the sea; needs the Chartered ≤ 3 or the vault emptied.*
5. **《他的帝国》 His Empire** (the other defensible wrong). Aakhet signs. He goes home to Aramoole a god who answers letters and holds the world's magic as a hoard: capricious, vain, and, unlike a board of directors, possible to shame in a newspaper. *Needs 龙账 ≥ 8, or your signature on his paper.*
6. **《敲门》 The Knock** (bittersweet). The gate does not open, but the knock is heard on this side, and every lost celestial within hearing turns toward it. What comes back with you is a convoy of orphans who need lodgings, and Torrieth's tally book. Arcane magic still fades. Faith does not, and the century gets saints with work permits and a humbler Church. *Needs the Church ≥ 5 and a pilot.*
7. **《长明灯》 The Standing Lamp** (divine only; §10).

**Epilogue cards** key on 白鸽 (Dove / Artheta / United No More); 枷锁 (Concord abolition, Mechanim Abolition or Slavery Enforced, Asset 14's line in the ledger); the Escann route; `crown`; Karshyr (a republic with a door, or the 334th empire's province); each companion; origin (a pension, a Queen's letter, a debt marked paid, a deleted asset number, a thesis); and, wherever he lives, a paragraph from the 1851 interview: "A minor investment."

## 9. Risks

- **A canon dragon as patron.** Canon fixes that he is a general in 1820, vanishes, and talks in 1851. *Defuse:* the firm is private and minor, as he is; the baseline returns him to canon's course; the lamps are never confirmed as what keeps him alive.
- **"Pixie" against "fairy".** *Defuse:* the wiki is written by Chroniclers and distinguishes the two; the mislabel is an in-world joke, and nobody in the game calls it settled.
- **The Awakening is canonically the 1870s.** *Defuse:* one unit wakes for a stated local cause; a wider waking happens only if the player spreads it. The mod's own trigger is "critical mass".
- **The Spires are "unknown in 1820", aerated damestear a late-century discovery.** *Defuse:* the Company learns both early and privately; publication is a choice that moves 界门.
- **Heaven.** *Defuse:* the gate never opens on screen, Torrieth is a boat and a tally, and three readings survive every ending.
- **Slavery.** The fugitives must not be scenery for the machines. *Defuse:* Act 3's main choice binds both; an Undercliff orc is a playable seat and Burâ carries the argument.
- **Blood.** Canon gives the technique and the term; the two state trades are invented. *Defuse:* keep them small, deniable, and on paper.
- **Lorent is seen from outside.** *Defuse:* a Lorent origin, a recurring Whiterose rival, the flagship audience, an epilogue pair.
- **Engine load.** Three new race entries (mechanim, lizardfolk, fairy as companion only); fallback: a League organiser replaces origin 5 and the fairy fights as a sorcerer of race "fey". The meeting and the report are ordinary hub scenes with flags. Asset 14 is the player or an NPC, so its scenes need paired text.
- **Terms to fix in the name table:** 迟到者公司, 别摇, 团务会, 盾条／印条／灯条, 余泪, 龙账, 彼岸, 平潮庭 (Court of the Slack Tide).

## 10. 神格线：the "ascend" cheat in this campaign

The cheat menu can make the hero a god (immortal, invulnerable, every check passes) or only immortal, at any moment, and take it away again (CONTENT_GUIDE §10). This campaign is about what the century believes in, and its hidden patron has spent three thousand years failing to become what the player becomes with one click. Five rules for writers:

- **A god is still on the payroll.** The articles have a clause for it. One share, no rise.
- **Belief arrives, and it is not yours to steer.** Whatever you do in public has a shrine, a collection box and a schism by the next act. Time skips make this visible.
- **You cannot lose a fight, so someone else can.** Every set-piece puts a third party at risk: the transmuters, the girl in the Drowning, the twenty-three in the cane.
- **The fey have a rule for you.** Under the Accord no power of that size may act directly against another. At the court a god may not sign as a mortal party, so the others must sign for you or without you.
- **The Partner takes it personally.** He calls on you in Act 1, uniform pressed, to ask how it was done. He never believes the answer.

**Time skips** (`is.immortal`): Etta recalculates your pension twice and gives up; Guilhem's hair goes from brown to iron to white across three office scenes while your entry in the book keeps the same handwriting; Burâ asks whether you will still know her name in a hundred years, and wants a number.

**Divine ending — 《长明灯》 The Standing Lamp** (`divine: true`). The court will not sign with a god, so it does the other thing the fey do with powers: it recognises you. You become the well. Lamps are lit to you at dusk on four continents, the magic that answers them runs through your hands, and the Company amends its articles to add one partner who draws no wage. You find out what Aakhet has known since Zerat: when you stop turning up, the lamps start going out.

**Epilogue cards.** `is.god`: (1) the Rectorate's verdict runs to three volumes and concludes "an aspect, pending review"; the Corinite Greaves of Escann vote you "companion of Corin" seven to five; the Small Country puts you on a tobacco tin. ## 11. Disagreements with the brief

- **Escann, not the Dwarovar.** A kingdom of wizards beside a republic that outlaws them is the magic deficit with a border post on it, and players of the 1444 campaign get to see Castonath again. The holds appear as a railhead, an astronomer and Etta.
- **Sarhal, not Haless, and a lizardfolk origin in place of the League.** The Spires and Hakhepra's empty throne are where the planar thread and the patron meet. The League stays in the story as capital.
- **Heaven is the last mile, not the destination.** The other two pitches end at the gate. On a road the weight belongs on the treaty and on who carried what to get there.
- **End on 1 Castanmark 1836.** Sixteen years to the day, and the date the mod itself once started on.
