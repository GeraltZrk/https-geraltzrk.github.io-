Campaign pitch for `s1820` (start: 1 Castanmark 1820, first day of the Empyreal Age). Angle: the metaphysical mystery.

## 1. Title, logline, stake, spine

**Working title.** 《泪尽》 / *The Last Tearfall*. 泪落 is the house word for Tearfall; this is the year the tears run out.

**Logline.** On the first morning of 1820 a Busilari scholar rows back out of a whirlpool with a pixie in a jar, and in a factory on Anbenncóst's Forgeway an automaton puts down its spanner and asks what day it is. Across eighteen years — the Bilge, an observatory under the Serpentspine, a slave port on the Trollsbay, a sea on no chart — the player audits the one account nobody in the age of steam wants opened: magic is a single finite substance, souls are made of it, the comet that paid it in broke up in 1810, and the black bullets that founded the Republic pay it out. When the sums are done the gate of Heaven is still locked, and republic, crown, company, church and machine are all on the step arguing about whether to knock.

**一段话简介。** 1820 年的头一天，一个布西拉尔学者划着小船从大漩涡里回来了，罐子里装着一只小妖精。同一天早上，安本科斯特铸炉街的一台机械人放下扳手，问今天星期几。往后十八年，你从贫民窟查到蛇脊山底下的天文台，又从巨魔湾的奴隶码头查到一片海图上没有的海。查出来的是一笔谁都不想认的账：魔法就这么多，魂也是拿它做的。往里存的那颗彗星 1810 年碎了。往外支的，正是共和国立国用的黑子弹。账算清了，天国的门还锁着。共和国、王冠、公司、教会和机器全挤在门口，吵该不该敲。敲不敲，谁去敲，你定。

**World stake.** Whether magic outlives the century, and as what: a ration book, a company share, a mage's birthright, a flood, or nothing. Whether the Blackpowder Republic, Lorent's crown, the automata and the dead themselves come out of the settlement as owners, as persons, or as fuel.

**Emotional spine.** Everyone you travel with is made of the thing the world is running short of: a war-mage with nullifier in her shoulder, a nun who has waited a century and a half for one voice, a gnome who wants her name on the patent, a machine that has just found out it is somebody. The proof that they are people is the same sheet of paper that puts a price on them.

## 3. The truth, and when the player learns it

**Rule for writers:** the narrator never rules on metaphysics. Each layer arrives as a document, an instrument reading or a witness, and two characters may read it differently.

1. The inflow stopped in 1810. *(canon)*
2. Magic loops; nullification is the one leak. *(canon)*
3. A soul is the same substance held in a shape. A core makes one. Every automaton has had one since assembly; "awakening" is it getting the controls back. *(canon: "regain")*
4. **The Double Entry** *(invented link)*: what nullification pushes out presses back at every rupture. Cores stir first. The oldest rupture, the Fragment, is going black. The Republic's bullets are waking the Republic's machines and silencing the Church's God.
5. Souls burn, and a woken core burns longer than the crystal it was cut from. *(canon principle, invented use)*
6. The Fragment is a core: one end of a line into locked Heaven. It has always said "they". Since 1666 one voice is Torrieth's. *(invented; Stamh's canon heresy, sixty years early)*
7. By the voices' own account the gate was barred from inside by the dead, after the first great theft. Heaven is the largest store of the substance there is. Open it and the deficit ends for a thousand years; the dead become a mining district. *(invented; testimony only)*

**Left open:** whether there is or was a One God; who built the four-pillared chamber; whether the voices are people, a chorus or a recording.

## 5. Outline

**Prologue — per origin, 5–6 scenes, level 2.** One morning seen from five places: a wrong thing → a superior who wants it filed → one small fight (scrap crew, damestear footpads, tunnel vermin, night riders) → whom you tell (sets faction standing) → the road to Anbenncóst.

### Act 1 · 行情 (The Price) — Anbenncóst and Aranthíl, 1820. Level 4.
*Is the damestear coming back, and who already knows it isn't?*

1. The pixie on show at the Chroniclers' campus; the board at the Tael jumps a fifth before noon; Gratto Silcalas is buying.
2. Forgeway orders "defective" units scrapped. Mayor Fadlun's union cannot decide whether a machine may hold a card.
3. The Asra Bank job: war-mages take the damestear and leave the coin. Lianne is their captain.
4. Druznar's plates arrive. A junior Chronicler wants promotion, Delda Waggleknack a front page, the Censor the box.
5. An Esmari referendum rally; a Peacekeeper volley drops a ward-mage. What a black round does to a spell, up close.
6. Aranthíl will not show the Fragment. Narwen is set to mind you and lets slip the count: eleven hums this year, forty in 1809.
7. Tearfall night on the tower roof: 214 meteors where the 1810 almanac has thousands.

**Choice:** print the plates, sell them to Silcalas, or surrender them to Reán síl Tirufeld → 见报 ±2, 白鸽 ±1, 泪雨 ±1 (panic nullification or hoarding).
**Fights:** the bank lobby (specials: trip the Korashi dust sprinklers; shut the vault on them); the Forgeway yard (scrap crew and a punch-card siege automaton, `noAwe`).
**Skip:** two years.

### Act 2 · 复式账 (Double Entry) — the dwarven rail and Dûr-Vazhatun, 1822–23. Level 6.
*Where does it go when it is spent, and what is a core?*

1. Down the line past the old junction at Er-Natvir: lamp-damestear rationed by the carriage, refugees from the Obsidian war on the platforms.
2. The Setzunkhûpa and its queue; Druznar, two comets old, with a first tremor of Astral Terror; traditionalists against Tilda's gadgets.
3. Evidence comes home: the kobold's Amadian samples run prismatic; the Mwalim's ritual shows a wave leaving, then nothing.
4. The bench: a spent crystal, a black round, a dead core, and 783, on the Gauge. The needle swings the same way for a round going black and a core waking.
5. The night after. 783 asks to see its number again. Lianne is first to say what a furnace would be. A Konwell student, Jahan síl Bennon, takes notes.
6. Speakstone dispatches: a walk-out at Forgeway; Lorent bans Korashi within its borders. Somebody in Lorentainé can add.
7. The gallery of the hold's two hundred hauler automata.

**Choices:** (a) the patent: file it open in the hall of records, sell it, or burn it → 魂炉, 见报, Tilda. (b) Run the Gauge backwards and wake the gallery, fifty years early, or seal it → 觉醒 +3 or 0.
**Fights:** a hired Makata company on the night train (special: uncouple the car); the siege automaton that wakes afraid in the mirror gallery (`noAwe`; 783 can talk it down at machine speed).
**Skip:** five years.

### Act 3 · 魂炉 (The Furnace) — Port Isobel, Marlliande, Vincenton, Noruin, 1828. Level 8.
*Who gets burned?*

1. Port Isobel never sleeps, and its lamps are not burning damestear.
2. The Vincenton engine: a planters' and railway consortium runs a mill on woken cores bought as scrap from three continents.
3. A Marlliande estate where chained orcs and woken gin-units hide each other, while free dock labour wants every machine smashed ("Shared Struggle" against "Laborer's Despair").
4. Archprovost Undercliffe wants the Gauge in court. If a core has a soul, his flock must look at what else it owns.
5. The empty chamber: four pillars, a pedestal that still moves the needle, Torrieth's tally scratched under the word "they".
6. Bernal II dies. A transcript of the Fragment's last hum leaves the conclave. Narwen knows the voice.
7. The Deposition.
8. Fire at the engine house.

**Choices:** (a) the judgment: cores only; cores and orcs in one ruling (the Concord splits, planters arm); or license the engine and keep the lights on → 觉醒, 魂炉, Concord. (b) Leak the dimming to the electors (an Erudite High Rector who opens the vault) or keep Narwen's confidence (a Zealot who fits out a ship for salvation) → 见报, 界门.
**Fights:** the engine house (boss: a four-armed war pattern on a screaming core; specials: pull the governor, vent the boiler); night riders on the levee road.
**Skip:** six years.

### Act 4 · 开会 (The Conference) — Anbenncóst, Port Jaher, Sil Berkhen's Island, the Root, 1834. Level 10.
*Who speaks for the world when it knocks?*

1. Home after six years. Reán drinks more; Fadlun is sixty-three; Lianne is grey; 783 has the same dent.
2. The Tearfall Conference in the Old Colosseum: Republic, Lorent, League, Hierarchy, Rectorate, Busilar, Triarchy, a Jaddari observer ("you are dividing a dead man's purse"), and a mechanim delegation nobody knows where to seat.
3. Ramsgate against a ban: "My rounds are the only reason a baker's son can say no to a wizard." Silcalas for the charter: "Tell me which ward of St. Alaria's you would like dark first."
4. A private audience. Castíne, resolute or shattered by earlier acts; Ioriel's offer in either mouth: a Third Mages' Peace. "I am the only delegate with a personal interest."
5. Outside, the referendum crowd. Three volleys. The Breaking Point.
6. Port Jaher: the frequency vault, by treaty, purchase or burglary; Álvar, horns just showing, as pilot.
7. Sil Berkhen's Island: a lizardfolk spire on the headland hums at the fork's pitch. Nobody has noticed before.
8. Through the maelstrom: a sea where magic is weather, and a Company siphon ship already at anchor.

**Choices:** (a) the Convention: Ban, Ration, Charter, or none → 泪雨, 界门. (b) The Republic: honour the parliaments, shoot, or let go → 白鸽. (c) Whose ship: Church, Company, Republic, Lorent's navy, or the Combination's (a mechanim crew need not breathe).
**Fights:** the Colosseum riot (specials: fire hoses; talk down a Peacekeeper line); the vault (special: strike a wrong note and open a puddle-sized maelstrom); boarding the siphon ship.

### Finale · 敲门 (Knocking) — beyond the world; you come out in Tearfall 1838. 8–12 scenes.

Weeks aboard are four years outside. The speakstone backlog reads out what the tracks did without you; Druznar's paper is out, on canon's date. Then the Celestial shallows, where angels ask for news of home; the last suburb of the Crumbling City, where Red Reaper clerks keep the queue; the gate, with no handle on this side; the Fragment, if you brought it, as the bell-pull. Torrieth speaks through the door, and each companion reads her differently. Narwen: it is God, in voices we can bear. Tilda: a relay with good manners. 783: "(Certain.) It is people. I know what a box with people in it sounds like."

**Final fight by path:** the Company's engine-ram at the gate; the Red Reaper Auditor, if you open against the voices' terms; Lianne, if she turned. All `noAwe`.

## 7. Endings

1. **《配给本》 The Ration Book** (bittersweet). The Convention caps nullification, meters every crystal and writes cores down as persons with a ration. Magic lasts the century as a utility, with queues. The gate stays shut and the dead keep waiting. *Needs 见报 ≥ 7, 魂炉 ≤ 3, the Ration text.*
2. **《上市》 The Flotation** (canon's course, forty years early). The planes open under charter. Aerated damestear by the shipload; the lamps never dim; the Feyrealm gets its own Trollsbay; the Awakening arrives into a world that has already priced cores. *Needs 界门 ≥ 7 via the Companies; also the fallback.*
3. **《静世纪》 The Quiet Century** (the defensible wrong choice). Nobody gives up the bullets. Magic gutters out by the 1870s. No Witch-King rises again; a baker's son can always say no. The automata lie down one by one, Ioriel stops mid-sentence and Castíne rules alone at last, old sea-walls lose their wards. *Needs 泪雨 ≤ 2, no Convention.*
4. **《第三次法师和约》 The Third Mages' Peace** (wrong, for good reasons). The crowns ban Korashi and licensed mages hold the reserve in trust. A long peace, tidy accounts, mechanim as "wards of the Wand", the Dove dead or a client. *Needs Crown and Wand ≥ 6, the Ban text, Ioriel's offer taken.*
5. **《开门》 The Opened Gate.** The living knock and the door gives. Magic comes back wild for a season; every core wakes in one night; the celestials go home; the dead walk out as claimants with opinions about their grandchildren's republic. Forced with the ram, the same flood arrives as a mining concession, and the cards say so. *Needs the Fragment or the ram at the gate.*
6. **《守门人》 The Doorkeeper** (bittersweet). A treaty through the keyhole: the gate stays shut, the queue in the Crumbling City begins to move, the living forswear the furnace, cores are persons by treaty. Magic declines slowly, on purpose. Someone stays as the living's resident at the door — Narwen, or you — relieving Torrieth after a hundred and seventy-two years. *Needs 见报 ≥ 6, 魂炉 ≤ 2, 觉醒 ≥ 6, one companion left.*
7. **《新泪》 The New Tear** (divine only; §10).

**Epilogue cards** key on 白鸽 (Dove / Artheta / United No More); 觉醒 (abolition and 783's *Upon a rainy day*, or Slavery Enforced); Lorent (the Castíne Constitution or the Ioriellan Empire); 协约; each companion; origin.

## 9. Risks

- **Answering what canon keeps open** (the Fragment, the One God, Heaven). *Defuse:* the testimony rule in §3; Heaven's interior is never shown; three readings survive every ending.
- **The Awakening is canonically 1870s–80s.** *Defuse:* 1820 has a few isolated cases; a mass awakening happens only by the player's choice, and the baseline keeps canon's date. Canon itself ties the peak to exploitation of the Empyreal.
- **The Soul Engine** is infernal technology on the wiki, with no Prime-Material date. *Defuse:* one crude prototype the player can end.
- **Torrieth** is "status unknown". *Defuse:* a voice behind a door, identified only by Narwen's ear.
- **Real rulers on stage.** *Defuse:* keep them to their template traits; nothing before 1820 changes; Bernal II dies in canon's year.
- **Slavery.** The machine plot must not upstage the enslaved orcs. *Defuse:* Act 3's main choice binds both cases in one judgment; Ohdaka is a playable seat.
- **Engine load.** Six tracks is the ceiling, and the Conference has many outcomes. *Defuse:* one hub scene that writes three flags and reads tracks; headlines (`cls: 'letter'`) carry the off-screen world. The mechanim race needs a class filter: no spellcasting, monks allowed (canon lets them use chi).
- **Terms.** Aranthíl 阿兰蒂尔 and Aelantir 阿伦蒂尔 differ by one character. 魂秤, 魂炉, 复式账, 度量会, 特许行, 互助社 are coinages for the name table.

## 10. 神格线：the "ascend" cheat in this campaign

The cheat menu can make the hero a god (immortal, invulnerable, every check passes) or only immortal, at any moment, and take it away again (CONTENT_GUIDE §10). This plot has a specific use for that: everything in it is on a meter, and a god is the one entry that does not balance, a rupture that pays in. Four rules:

- **Power is not proof.** A god can read a core without the Gauge, but a god's word is not evidence; the Chroniclers will not print it and a court will not take it. 见报 never moves on divine say-so. The god still needs plates, witnesses and friends.
- **Every miracle has a market.** Whatever a god does for free reprices something, and the bill arrives within the scene.
- **You cannot lose a fight, so someone else can.** Each set-piece puts a third party at risk (the tellers, the twelve cores in the firebox, the crowd in front of the volley); specials decide who else walks out.
- **1820 has a procedure for living gods:** measure, value, convene. The Rectorate files you as "a gifted mage" or "an aspect of the One God"; Fractalines bring instruments; Hakhepra sends envoys to see whether you are Aakhet, and Rabon Deshird pays a professional call; Varlengeilt writes with a trade offer; the Red Reapers open a file on anyone who has stopped dying.

## 11. Disagreements with the brief

- **No Escann act.** The comet evidence lives in Dûr-Vazhatun, so Act 2 goes under the mountains; the Demesne and Ancardia appear only as Conference delegates.
- **No separate orc or goblin origin.** The orc seat sits inside the Concord origin, where the law makes it sharpest.
- **The Awakening is a consequence, not a given.** Canon dates it fifty years on; letting the player bring it forward is the stronger version of the same thread.
- **End in 1838.** Eighteen years lets the canonical paper land, lets the humans go grey beside an elf and a machine, and gives the Republic time to break or hold.
