# Dossier 01 — The world at 1022 AA (CK3 mod start) and its road to 1444

Scope: the elven Landing, the War of the Sorcerer-King, the map and tensions of 1 Castanmark 1022, and the consequences that run forward to 1444.

**Source legend.** `[wiki: Title]` = Fandom dump page. `[ck3: path]` = file relative to `src/ck3/`. "ck3 ibid." in tables repeats the previous CK3 file. Where wiki and mod disagree I follow the mod and list the conflict in §7.

**Dates.** The mod writes year.month.day. Months of the Reformed Castanorian Calendar: 1 Castanmark, 2 Esmarment, 3 Bloomsdawn, 4 Silversight, 5 Halament, 6 Suren, 7 Teysuren, 8 Yshdament, 9 Bloomsend, 10 Tearfall, 11 Nerament, 12 Truefrost. ## 1. Overview

On 1 Castanmark 1022 Cannor is fourteen months past the death of Nichmer the Sorcerer-King and two months past the Treaty of Anbenncóst (31 Tearfall 1021) that carved up his empire [wiki: Treaty of Anbenncóst]. The mod's single bookmark is titled "A New Cannor": "winds of change are blowing in the future lands of the Empire of Anbennar, as men and elves intermingle. New peoples arise, along with new conflicts as the old borders are redrawn" [ck3: localization/english/bookmark/bookmark_dameshead_1022_l_english.yml].

Three facts define the moment:

1. **Castanor is gone and nobody may replace it.** The human empire of Escann, founded in 639 BA and so more than sixteen centuries old (though for long stretches after 1 AA it was not one united polity until Ebonfrost reunified it), ended with its last Castan murdered at Bal Mire (1015) and the Trials of Castan wrecked in the Battle of Trialmount (1020): the Trials were "irreparably damaged … magic drained, severed, or otherwise rendered useless" and Trialmount itself left "cursed and inaccessible", so no one can lawfully become emperor again [wiki: Trials of Castan; wiki: Castanor; wiki: Timeline]. Its heartland is split between rival kingdoms that both call themselves Castanor ("Cast" and "Anor" are later chroniclers' labels) [wiki: Chivalric Escann].
2. **The elves have been in Cannor for twenty-one years.** They are war heroes, kingmakers, and a tiny minority. The first half-elf, Marion Silmuna, is twenty and has just become King of Dameria [ck3: history/characters/anb_damerian_carnetori.txt]. "Elvenization" of the nobility is beginning [wiki: Timeline].
3. **The victors are young, bereaved and already quarrelling.** Lorent's king is three days short of his second birthday; Wex is held by a legitimised bastard of eight; Gawed's king (49) has seventeen months to live (he dies 3 Suren 1023); the Sorcerer-King's acolyte Canrec the Defiler, believed dead, is a lich quietly breeding the plague that will kill much of Cannor in 1040 [ck3: history/characters/000_test_characters.txt; ck3: history/characters/anb_wex.txt; ck3: history/characters/anb_alenic.txt; wiki: White Pestilence].

Tone note for writers: the mod treats this as a post-war settlement drama (legitimacy, mixed marriages, veterans, grudges), not a dark-lord story. The dark lord is already dead.

## 2. Facts

### 2.1 Deep background a 1022 character would know

- **Castanor** was founded in 639 BA by Castan the Progenitor at Castonath, with the dwarf Balgar the Builder raising the White Walls and the citadels (the "Bal" forts: Bal Vertesk 593 BA, Bal Dostan 603 BA, Bal Vroren 546 BA, Bal Hyl 466 BA, Bal Mire 457 BA) [wiki: Timeline; ck3: localization/english/anb_buildings_l_english.yml]. Each emperor took the name Castan after passing the Trials of Castan on Trialmount [wiki: Trials of Castan]. Of the citadels, Bal Hyl is the fortress now called **Wexkeep** and Bal Vertesk is the **Black Tower of Vertesk**; the mod's building text has Bal Mire "razed by the dragon Zaamalot the Black during the Dragonwake" and Bal Vroren's refugees frozen by Elkaesel in 472 AA (both start as "Ruins" buildings), although the Ebonfrost event text describes Bal Mire's white stone as still "standing strong" and garrisoned (see §7) [ck3: localization/english/anb_buildings_l_english.yml].
- **The Trials themselves** (useful if anyone climbs Trialmount): candidates set out from the North Citadel; each trial was added by a different Castan. Listed on the wiki: a rally-cry loud enough for the North Citadel garrison to answer (Castan XIII the Quiet); drawing Castanor's borders "as they currently are, and then as they SHALL BE" on a cold stone map-desk (Castan V the Great); forging a gift for the dwarves at a magically preserved forge (Castan III Dwarf-friend); the Oathstone, carved with every Castan's oath; adding a stone to the "crown of mankind" (Castan IV Realmbuilder); reconciling two forest spirits (Castan XIV); befriending a wyvern (Castan XXXVI Dragonfriend); jumping round a rock ten times (Castan XXII the Eccentric); defeating an immortal golem (Castan VI Giantsbane); hunting a beast and crafting something from it (Castan II Beastbane). The oath begins "I pledge to defend my land and my people to the end of my days, but will not bring unjust strife unto the world" [wiki: Trials of Castan].
- **The Dragonwake (470–500 AA)** is five centuries in the past, not part of the 1022–1444 story. Zaamalot the Black razed Damenath (474), ending the Damerian Republic; Nimrith the Red took the dwarven hold of Khugdihr and burned Damescrown; kobolds poured out of the Dragonheights and destroyed the Gnomish Hierarchy; the Alenic "Great Host" of Godrac the Invader founded Gawed and drove the Wexonards, Marrodics and Morbani south [wiki: Dragonwake]. Live consequences in 1022: kobolds hold the Dragon Coast, Nimrith still sits in Khugdihr (slain only in 1103), Elkaesal the White sleeps in the Titan's Rest kept under by skald-song [wiki: Dragonwake; ck3: localization/english/religion/anb_religion_dragon_cult_l_english.yml].
- **Era of Black Ice (768–978).** Gerudian Reavers raided and settled Cannor: Deranne (Derhilde Reaverqueen, 861), the Kingdom of the Pearl (Henrik Divenscourge, 879), Reveria (Askel Iron-Neck, 821), Tef (891) [wiki: Reavers; wiki: Era of Black Ice; wiki: Kingdom of Reveria]. (The Timeline page instead starts the era in 798.) In 865 the reaver Gunnar Ebonfrost killed the emperor (Castan XLVIII), passed the Trials and became Castan XLIX. His house made the Trials dynastic, though only in practice after his non-Gerudian successor Castan L was deposed within two years; their Gerudian-descended followers are the **Black Castanorians** [wiki: House of Ebonfrost; ck3: history/characters/anb_castans.txt; ck3: localization/english/anb_major_decisions_l_english.yml].

### 2.2 The Landing

- The **Remnant Fleet** fled Aelantir on the Day of Ashen Skies and wandered for a thousand years (the Great Wander). It was a city afloat: everything from fishing boats to "Migrant Ships" 150–400 m long, wooden, some with Precursor-steel hulls [wiki: Remnant Fleet].
- **The Great Storm** (1000.2.10) destroyed over half the fleet, including the Migrant Ships Norande and Saraea. The navigator **Munas Moonsinger** (b. 828 on the Lunateín, "Moonfire") and his brother Varilor drifted seven days before the Estádha ("Lady Luck", captain Istralania Warsinger) found them. Munas then sang to the moon and the moon sang back [wiki: Munas Moonsinger; wiki: Remnant Fleet].
- **Itinerary.** 23 Tearfall 1000 the fleet reaches the fog-bound island later called Venáil and learns of the war from Lencori fishermen; the captains hold council. 1 Nerament most follow Munas; **8 Nerament 1000 (Lansday)** they land by night at Damucen on the Isle of Damesear, where a single woman in white waits on the shore: **Auci "Eightborn" Dameris**, Queen of Dameria and avatar of the moon goddess the Dame. In a hungry winter the Damerians share food and hearths [wiki: Remnant Fleet; wiki: Munas Moonsinger; wiki: Lansday].
- **Three splits.** (a) The Sundancer fleet under **Jaher** of the Diranhriá stays at Venáil, is visited by "the Phoenix", sails 12 Suren 1001 and lands at Brasan in Bulwar on 21 Suren: the future Sun Elves. (b) **Maldorian the Navigator** of the Galsheáh ("Whitespear") refuses the war and founds Celmaldor in the Alenic Reach (1002). (c) Some crews refuse land altogether and become Sea Elves [wiki: Remnant Fleet; wiki: Principality of Celmaldor].
- **The First Union.** Munas and Auci marry on the Moonmount: "Ours, and our people's fate is now bound in matrimony and blood." Munas urges elves to "cannorize" their names; Varilor Cymlan becomes Varilor Bluetongue. The elves who follow form the **Silver Band** (1000–1013) [wiki: Munas Moonsinger; wiki: Silver Band].
- Not all elders went willingly: **Ibenion ta'Lunateín** (captain of the Lunateín) and **Ianren the Rider** (captain of the Árthíl) agreed to fight only in exchange for true elven realms [wiki: Elfrealm of Ibevar].

### 2.3 The War of the Sorcerer-King (978–1020)

**The enemy.** **Nichmer** (b. 940, Korbarid, once of Corvuria's Order of the Ravenseers) was an advisor to the imperial court. The mod has him kill Castan LII "the Kind" on 978.1.5 (death reason "possessed") and enthral the successor, **Castan LIII "the Enthralled"** (Ebonfrost, b. 955). Castan's first wife Clothilde killed herself that year; his second, Onna, was executed in 1004 for growing suspicious [ck3: history/characters/anb_castans.txt; ck3: history/characters/anb_escanni.txt; wiki: Order of the Ravenseers]. Nichmer's signature art was enchantment so strong he could "enchant the magic of others", seizing a rival's spell after it was cast [wiki: Nichmer, the Sorcerer-King]. He took the title Sorcerer-King only in 1015.

**Chronology** [wiki: War of the Sorcerer-King unless noted]:

- **978, 27 Silversight — Butcher's Field Massacre.** Elrac the Butcher's legion slaughters Wexonard nobles and peasants gathered for a peace meeting with the Milcori [wiki: Butcher's Field Massacre; ck3: localization/english/anb_game_concepts_l_english.yml]. Invasion of the Borders 978–979; the Wexhills (around Wexkeep, the old citadel Bal Hyl) hold out via hidden supply tunnels [wiki: War of the Sorcerer-King], but in the mod "Wexkeep is re-sieged and their authority is lost" on 990.10.30 and the Wexonard crown stands vacant from then on [ck3: history/titles/anb_k_wex.txt].
- **979** Dameria, Esmaria and Verne ally: the future **League of Free Realms**.
- **980–986** Invasion of Gawed: Vertesk, recently conquered by Gawed, invites Castanor in. King Godrac X is slain (mod: 986.5.23 at the Battle of the Golden Plains, killed by Caylen Longlance); the Vertesk Dominion is created. Adshaw joins Black Castanor after the Burning of Aldtempel [wiki: War of the Sorcerer-King; wiki: Blue Reachmen Wars of Independence; wiki: List of Gawedi monarchs; ck3: history/characters/anb_alenic.txt].
- **980–981 Third Tall War.** "With Gawed being invaded, Lorent also declares war to conquer the Gawedi parts of the Small Kingdom" [wiki: War of the Sorcerer-King]. The later allies began the war by preying on each other.
- **988–996** Invasion of the Eastern Dameshead. **994, 12 Bloomsdawn:** King Arrel II "the Betrayed" of Dameria dies when vassals of the **Plumwall Conspiracy** (in the mod led by the "Betrayer Duke" Devianus Prumuris of Plumwall, see §4), promised titles and riches, cut off his army and change sides; his body is taken by Nichmer's acolytes. His daughter Auci, 17, is elected queen [wiki: List of Damerian monarchs; ck3: history/characters/anb_damerian_carnetori.txt].
- **992–1000** Invasion of Lencenor; Viswall falls (993–994). Lorent is never conquered.
- **1000** The Landing. Garion defeats Iacob at Lorentei. **1001** Second Battle of Ruby Pass; a mass execution on Nichmer's order (12 Castanmark 1001) kills Lorent's crown prince Rewan the Younger among others [ck3: history/characters/anb_lorenti.txt]; Mafalda assassinated; Bayvic revolts in the Reach.
- **1003–1007** Liberation of the Eastern Dameshead (Relief of Wexkeep 23 Silversight 1006; the Black Castanorian warlord **Drosten River-Reaver** changes sides months later [wiki: Drosten River-Reaver]). **1007–1008** Busilar and Tef; **1011** Corvuria, by Jaher.
- **1013 — Battle of Damesteeth.** The Castanorian armada from Vertesk is beaten by Varilor Bluetongue's fleet; Munas's ship is "obliterated by the raw magics" of Venac and his body never found (mod date 1013.10.3) [wiki: Munas Moonsinger].
- **1014** Storming of Vertesk (16 Esmarment): besides Venac, the dead include Gawed's prince Alenn the Younger (killed by Venac) and, in the mod, Duke Lothane Ryonard of the Wexhills and his brother Welham, father of the bastard Dyren [ck3: history/characters/anb_alenic.txt; ck3: history/characters/anb_wex.txt]. Liberation of Gawed 1014–1015. **Battle of Morban Flats** (mod 1014.7.14): the last League defeat. The armies camped fourteen days without fighting until a skirmish between water parties started it; then a pincer by **Caylen Longlance** (cavalry) and **Caradaig Ebonfrost** (infantry and overall command; Castan LIII's firstborn and "clear successor", killed by a stray arrow). Caylen forbids pursuit and takes the captured Marion Silmuna as a pupil; Marion was twelve by the mod's history dates (b. 1001.8.19), thirteen only by the bookmark's 1001.2.19 [ck3: localization/english/anb_game_concepts_l_english.yml; wiki: Battle of Morban Flats; wiki: War of the Sorcerer-King; wiki: Caradaig Ebonfrost].
- **1014, 30 Truefrost – 1015, 12 Castanmark — Siege and Battle of Calascandar.** The Castanorians march south to break into Esmaria (Caylen advises against it and withdraws to Bal Mire). King Alenn "the Older" rallies the army beaten at Morban Flats, joins the defenders and surprises the besiegers, most of whom surrender [wiki: War of the Sorcerer-King].
- **1015 — Battle of Bal Mire** (mod 1015.12.14). A blow to the head breaks Castan LIII's enthralment; he orders surrender. Nichmer kills him with a single spoken word ("not lightning, not flame. Just a word") and burns the legionaries who rush to their emperor [ck3: localization/english/event_localization/anb_bookmark_events_l_english.yml]. The Timeline adds that he then proclaimed himself Sorcerer-King, "enthralling the weak-minds of both armies to his will" [wiki: Timeline]. Caylen Longlance and Iacob raise **Free Castanor**.
- **1016–1019** Liberation of Castanor. **Spring 1017, Battle of Hornwood:** a downpour ruins the League's archers and cavalry; Commander **Liandiel Speardancer** withdraws into the Deepwoods, crosses the Verdant Veil and vanishes from the world: the origin of the Wood Elves [wiki: Wood Elf].
- **1019, 7 Silversight — Surrender of Castonath.** The patricians under Ottrac Mascabir open the gates [wiki: Ottrac Mascabir].
- **1020 — Tourney of Grand Victory** at the foot of Trialmount, two weeks, to choose who climbs. King Ruben of Lorent is killed in a joust by Crege Dameris (accident; Ioriel never forgives it). Urion Starsworn defeats King Rocair of Verne [wiki: Tourney of Grand Victory; ck3: history/characters/anb_lorenti.txt].
- **1020 — Battle of Trialmount** (mod 31 Tearfall). The Trialmount Company climbs through the forbidden Trials. Dead: Caylen Longlance, Urion Starsworn (dying so Istralania could live), Korvin Lorentis, Serondal Mageshield, Alaria the Lifehand, the gnome Melbin the Mirror, Dalyon the Woodsman, Ser Devan the Righteous, the dwarf Sevrund the Exiled. Survivors: **Clarimonde of Oldhaven** (Caylen's squire, who found the lost blade Calindal and killed Nichmer), Jaher, Garion (lost an arm and a leg), Finnic Shadowhand, Melba the Mirror, Istralania. Marion Silmuna and his friend Ottrac the Wexonard left midway on news of Auci's death; the news was carried up the mountain by Crege Dameris, who had withdrawn from the Tourney in shame after killing Ruben and was not part of the Company [wiki: Battle of Trialmount]. (In the mod Auci dies a year later, 3 Tearfall 1021: see §7.)
- **1021, 31 Tearfall — Treaty and Grand Ball of Anbenncóst.** Borders redrawn; "many half-elves were conceived that day" [wiki: Timeline; wiki: Treaty of Anbenncóst]. (The war page's table dates the treaty 31.08.1021; the mod's title files date most grants to 1021.10.31. I follow the treaty page and the mod.)

**Treaty terms known** [wiki: Treaty of Anbenncóst; wiki: Halfling; wiki: Elfrealm of Moonhaven]: the Disputed Lands between Alenics and Escanni go to elves (Arbaran north, Ibevar in the Forlorn Vale); halfling petty kingdoms are restored; Saltfort goes to the Knights of the Saltmarch (granted by King Rocair V of Verne); Teagan of Teagansfield is granted Teagansfield; the League's camps and settlements around the bay are consolidated into the city of Anbenncóst [wiki: Anbenncóst]; elfrealm rulers are styled *prince*, deliberately below kings; and because Castellos's priests backed Nichmer and the Ebonfrosts, **any Regent Court priest may now crown a king** (previously only Castellosi could). The wiki's own list of edicts is headed "TODO: TBD", so this list is incomplete, not exhaustive: writers may add minor grants but should not contradict these.

### 2.5 Religion in 1022

The mod's "Cannorian Pantheon" is "rather than an organized religion … a combination of many cultural and folkloric gods that instead share an overall mythos"; "Regent Court" as a unified church is still forming [ck3: localization/english/religion/anb_religion_cannorian_pantheon_l_english.yml]. Faiths and their fault lines:

- **Castanorian Pantheon** (Escann): "all gods equally worshipped, as long as all gods follow the rightful ruler of all: Castellos." Discredited by association: Castellosi priests crowned and supported the Ebonfrosts and Nichmer. Former centre of worship: Trialmount [wiki: Castellos; wiki: Treaty of Anbenncóst].
- **Temple of the Highest Moon** (mod key `cult_of_the_dame`; Dameria): the Dame, once a harvest goddess, now also knowledge, magic and tolerance, "and her partner Munas". Seat on the Moonmount; ranks Librant, Luminary, High Luminary. Auci was "the eighth incarnation of the Dame". **Silversea Temple** (West Dameshead, seat at the Windtower) and **Luna Damish** (Vernmen) are autonomous branches [wiki: The Dame; wiki: Silversea Temple; ck3: localization/english/anb_nicknames_l_english.yml].
- **Court of Adean** (Lorent's west, Verne's "Emulant Cults", Adenica): chivalry, duty, tournaments; "de facto god of war ever since Agrados' fall". Caylen Longlance is held to have been Adean's avatar (mod trait `anb_avatar_adean`; recognised by the Seventh Council) [wiki: Avatar; ck3: history/characters/000_test_characters.txt]. The **Adeanic Vow** ranks a knight page → Earnest Squire → Resplendent Knight → Favoured Banneret → Champion ("the Spear Incarnate", name inlaid in steel on temple walls); disgrace means a broken spear and the name scratched out [ck3: localization/english/event_localization/decisions/anb_adeanic_knight_decisions_l_english.yml].
- **Whiterose Court** (Lorent): Adean and Minara together; "brave, honest and victorious on the battlefield to then feast, celebrate and enjoy life." **House of Minara** in the Bay of Wines.
- **Cult of Esmaryal / Ryala** (Esmaria), **Cult of Ara** (Crownsmen; priests are "notarites"), **Cult of Balgar** (dwarves, Escann), **Cult of Falah** (the Reach), **Northern Temple** (Gawed), **Small Temple** (halflings: hearth, farming, food), **Border Cults** (Wex; priests are "tollwrights"; great temple of Nerat at the **Necropolis** of Corseton, whose High Priest Folcard is a halfling; **corset burial** stiffens the corpse for the journey to Nerat) [ck3: localization/english/anb_decisions_l_english.yml; wiki: The Necropolis].
- **Pre-Castanorian survivals:** Eidoueni (Sormanni; hero-god Artanos), Moors Cult, Ansuwir among Old Alenics (werewolves as blessed of Ariadas; the heathen Duke Petran Balgard under Gawed), Korbarid Dragon Cult, Cult of Nimrith, Skaldhyrric Faith [wiki: Eidoueni Pantheon; wiki: Ansuwir Pantheon].
- **Cults of Agrados** in the Greatwoods and northern Escann: the dead war-god as flawed father of humanity; bonfires, blood offerings, masked storytellers, and a prophecy of an orphan saviour who dies, returns and leads "a host of warriors from many origins" (fulfilled by Corin in 1444). The mod notes the cult re-emerging in Agrador "following the fall of Castanor" [wiki: Cult of Agrados; ck3: localization/english/religion/anb_religion_infernal_cult_l_english.yml].
- **Elven Forebears:** the Fleet "abandoned their gods for allowing … the Day of Ashen Skies" and revere ancestors through attuned heirlooms. Cannorized elves (Munas's kin, Istralania, Ianren) have already adopted human gods; Ibevar, Venáil, Celmaldor have not [ck3: localization/english/religion/anb_religion_elven_l_english.yml].
- **Seventh Pantheonic Council** (1022 on the Timeline and Avatar pages, 1015 on the Councils page) deifies Munas as god of navigation and music, the first elf in the Regent Court, championed by the Damish priest Darin of Bisan and his *Moonsinger Codex*; Drosten River-Reaver was an early supporter. The same council recognises Caylen Longlance as an avatar of Adean. It also debated **Alaria the Lifehand**: many wanted her declared an avatar of Esmaryal or a new goddess beside Munas, but she had refused to worship any Cannorian god ("the Ruin proved that no gods were deserving of praise") and the council would not give a human goddess an elven avatar, ruling that if she was an avatar it was of a god outside the Court [wiki: Pantheonic Councils; wiki: Avatar; wiki: Drosten River-Reaver].

### 2.6 Peoples in 1022

- **Elves.** Lifespan c. 400; child until 100, elder at 300; one elven birth per seven human [wiki: Elf; ck3: localization/english/event_localization/childhood/anb_coming_of_age_event_l_english.yml]. Coloured hair marks old Precursor nobility. Purism exists on every side: the mod has traits for elven, human, half-elf, dwarven, gnomish and halfling purists, plus "racial legitimacy" laws under which vassals may claim a title held by the "wrong" race [ck3: localization/english/anb_traits_l_english.yml; ck3: localization/english/anb_racial_legitimacy_laws_l_english.yml].
- **Half-elves.** A handful of children and teenagers; recessive, often indistinguishable but for slightly pointed ears. A racially impossible child is an "Obvious Bastard" event [wiki: Half-elf; ck3: localization/english/event_localization/anb_racial_events_l_english.yml].
- **Dwarves.** Surface remnants: Ruby dwarves (Rubyhold), Silver dwarves (Silverforge under Dhaine Silverhammer, allied to Dameria since 588 though the mod files it under Esmaria; their architect Heharrûk is building Ibenion's capital at rediscovered Vardoced), Stone dwarves of Escann (who mourn Castanor and blame the elves for breaking it). The Serpentspine holds are mostly lost; the wiki's list of states existing during the war names Segbandal, Krakdhûmvror, Arg-Ôrdstun, Ovdal Lodhum, Ovdal Kanzad and Ovdal-az-Ân in the mountains, plus Ovdal Tûngr in Bulwar [wiki: War of the Sorcerer-King; wiki: Silver Dwarf; wiki: Countries during the War of the Sorcerer-King].
- **Gnomes.** Refugees of the Hierarchy (Nimscodd, Iochand, city gnomes); already among the first elf-trained mages (the twins Melba and Melbin the Mirror).
- **Halflings.** "Gregarious and diminutive natives of the Small Country … long subjects of other races"; briefly sovereign again. Viswall is the "City of Colors" (dyeworks) [ck3: localization/english/anb_traits_l_english.yml; wiki: Viswall].
- **Kobolds.** Masters of the Dragon Coast since 470; ancestors-as-dragons faith (Malliath, Tayekan).
- **Harpies.** Always female. Extinct in Cannor proper since the Dragonwake except the Járnklo "valkyries" of the Titan's Rest; queendoms in Bulwar [wiki: Harpy; wiki: Western Harpy Migrations].
- **Orcs and goblins.** Underground, in warbands, fighting dwarven remnants. Known to Cannor only from a raid c. 30 AA and a few since: a half-legend. Their settled "Age of Heroes" begins in the 1100s; the Greentide is four centuries away [wiki: Orc]. The mod leaves the orc holds and the Deepwoods unpopulated [ck3: history/titles/anb_k_khugdihr.txt; ck3: history/titles/anb_k_deepwoods.txt].
- **Gnolls** survive in Businor's hills and Bulwar's deserts; **werewolves** haunt the Greatwoods and "have not forgotten their hate for the sons of Castanor" [ck3: localization/english/event_localization/anb_bookmark_events_l_english.yml].

### 2.7 Magic after the Sorcerer-King

- Before the Day of Ashen Skies magic was an unknowable force wielded by few, worked through rituals, reagents and invocation of spirits or gods; after 1 AA it became easier to harness but stayed rare. What the Fleet added in 1000 was "the magical bloodlines of elvenkind and the elven style of casting arcane magic" [wiki: Magic]. Healing by a single spell is impossible in this setting; Alaria was the lone exception and nobody has replicated her.
- **The Wise Council:** Garion the Wise and his first apprentices, founders of the eight schools: Garion (Transmutation), **Masilda the Diviner** (Divination; Mafalda the Mad's daughter, Garion's lover, bearer of Augur's Blood), Korvin Lorentis (Evocation, dead), the Mirror twins (Illusion), Serondal Mageshield (Abjuration, dead), **Alarian Singkeep** (Enchantment; took a fort by singing its garrison to sleep on the harp Swansong), Alaria the Lifehand ("School of Life", dead). With Alaria died the only known instant healing; her school is henceforth called Necromancy [wiki: Wise Council; wiki: Magic; ck3: localization/english/artifacts/anb_artifacts_l_english.yml].
- There is **no Magisterium yet** (the mages' body forms secretly in the Interregnum and founds the Empire in 1221) [wiki: Magisterium]. Each court has a **Court Mage** councillor [ck3: localization/english/anb_game_concepts_l_english.yml].
- **Forbidden magic** in the mod is exactly Nichmer's crime: *Compel* and *Dominate* schemes that seize a mind, a secret that if exposed brands the caster "Evil Mage", and a story cycle for the victim ("A Mind in Shackles … a faint voice whispering to me") ending in "Reclaim your Free Will" [ck3: localization/english/anb_spells_l_english.yml; ck3: common/story_cycles/anb_story_cycles/anb_story_cycle_break_domination.txt].
- **Damestear** (meteoric magical ore, falling most in Tearfall) is traded as powder and can be ingested for power [wiki: Damestear; ck3: localization/english/anb_spells_l_english.yml].
- Planetouched (fire, water, earth, wind), lycanthropes, vampires and liches all exist as mod traits [ck3: localization/english/anb_traits_l_english.yml].

### 2.8 What the CK3 mod dramatises

- **Bookmark characters:** Marion Silmuna ("Son of an Avatar and a god … now wears the crown of his mother") and, as a landless adventurer, **Narawen the Wanderer** with "Narawen's Band" [ck3: common/bookmarks/bookmarks/00_bookmarks.txt]. Other adventurer bands at start: the agate dwarf Norgrin Carum's **Mosstone Company**; the Akalšesi assassin-mage Zarin "Narašnur"; and the **Ebonwings** of a Járnklo harpy named Castan, nicknamed "LIV", bastard of House Ebonfrost with claims on Ebonmarck and the Empire of Castanor [ck3: history/titles/01_anb_laamp_titles.txt; ck3: history/characters/anb_jarnklo_harpy.txt; ck3: localization/english/anb_titles_l_english.yml].
- **"The Last of the Ebonfrosts"** (the most developed chain). Garrec starts with crushed legitimacy and a "shunned dynasty", attacked by "Reachmen warbands, Adeanic zealots, even your own Black Castanorian kin-traitors" in the **War to End the Dragon** (lose: Garrec is executed, Ebonmarck partitioned, Urvic exiled toward Urviksten). Win: "The Black Dragon lives", a Kingdom of Ebonmarck and a *Black Dragon Reconquest* casus belli. Then **"Into the Silence of Castan's Fall"**: the search for Castan LIII's body via the Adenican plains, Bal Mire (a one-eyed legionary with a melted sword; one-legged Captain Brohnfeld; a shrine of Nerat with a scrying bowl), the Headman's Wood (werewolf night attack) and a ruined waystation where an old loyalist keeps the emperor under a hollow tile. Rewards: the Ebonsteel Crown of Castanor and a state funeral at Westgate, the North Citadel or Pantheonway [ck3: localization/english/event_localization/anb_bookmark_events_l_english.yml; ck3: localization/english/anb_wars_l_english.yml].
- **Castanor decisions:** *Unite Inner Castanor* (historically the peaceful union as Castellyr, 1339); *Claim the Mantle of Castan* in three flavours (generic, "Empire of the Black Dragon", "Empire of the Silver Dragon"), gated on *The Wounds of the Trialmount*: "Nichmer's trail of destruction across the Trialmount has been righted" [ck3: localization/english/anb_castanor_l_english.yml].
- **Blademarches:** succession by drawing Calindal (be found worthy or go blind); the **Trial of the Blade** inflicted on prisoners and pretenders; failed kings appoint a Bladesteward; the blinded may join the Blind Masters [ck3: localization/english/event_localization/anb_blademarches_succession_l_english.yml; ck3: localization/english/event_localization/anb_blademarches_trial_by_blade_l_english.yml].
- **War of Blue Reachmen Independence** against Adshaw, where a Black Castanorian general offers Rylen his sword for land [ck3: localization/english/event_localization/anb_reachmen_independence_l_english.yml].
- **Other major decisions** (each a named grievance): Shatter the Rose (Enteben's revenge on Lorent, optionally restoring the High Kingdom of Lencenor); Reclaim what Dameria Stole (Carneter); Old Damerian Resurgence (expel Damerian and Moon Elvish lords; capital question Eararcen vs Damucen); Consolidate Arbaran; Restore the Havoric Kingdom; Break the Gawedi Yoke (Vertesk); The Moormen Kneel; Drive out the Reavers; Scour the Legacy of Divenscourge; Reunite the Vernid Kingdoms; Reconquer the Eskumdinas (Korbarids against the sun elves); Formalize our Dominion over Cannor (Siloriel/"Ioriellan"); Found the Stonescepter Court [ck3: localization/english/anb_major_decisions_l_english.yml].
- **Systems:** elvenization as a cultural innovation requiring an elf or half-elf ruler, spouse or councillor; "Marked by Destiny" for canon-critical figures; wyvern-riding (mount an enchanted juvenile and it obeys, or "throws you off and eats you"); skaldic recitation [ck3: localization/english/anb_elvenization_l_english.yml; ck3: localization/english/anb_core_l_english.yml; ck3: localization/english/event_localization/decisions/anb_wyvernrider_decisions_l_english.yml].
- Much mod text is marked TODO/WIP; treat mechanics as intent, not finished canon.

### 2.9 Beyond Cannor

- **Bulwar.** **Jaher ta'Diranhriá** (b. 791) landed at Brasan in 1001, allied with the Akal of Zanšap, drove out the gnoll Red Xhaz and crushed the harpy matriarchy of Siadan by 1006, and was crowned; the Sun Cult proclaimed him **Surael Reborn** (third after King Aga and Barid szel-Sur). His captains hold governorships (Amarien, Eledas, Vulzin "the Red", Uyel, Veharia, Gelmonias). Outside his empire in the mod: Akalšes (Darius, "Way of Kings"), Azka-Sur, the harpy queen Hassi Lisinyau, gnoll packs of Zhavekal. The Copper dwarves of Ovdal Tûngr are blockaded until 1162. Jaher carries the Precursor-steel spear Dinătoldir. His eastern campaigns begin in 1038 [wiki: Sunrise War; wiki: Jaher ta'Diranhriá; ck3: localization/english/anb_game_concepts_l_english.yml; ck3: localization/english/artifacts/anb_artifacts_l_english.yml].
- **Kheterata.** King Aahtep II died 3 Truefrost 1021; the new king Rhasmor (b. 8 Nerament 1021, holder from the day Aahtep died) is a weeks-old half-elf whose real father is not the king. His mother Nitet, Aahtep's niece and wife, follows the Cult of the Dame [ck3: history/characters/anb_kheterata.txt; ck3: history/titles/anb_k_kheterata.txt].
- **Gerudia.** Bjarnrík under the 18-year-old Gunnarr Bjarnsson (coronation sword of ebonsteel); the Skaldskola; trolls north of the Dalr. Skalds sing "Dirge of the Deep", "Gjalund and the Giantslayers", "The Old Winter Lullaby" to keep the White Dragon asleep; the Elkaesal cult keeps ritual silence and murders skalds [ck3: localization/english/anb_decisions_l_english.yml; ck3: localization/english/religion/anb_religion_dragon_cult_l_english.yml].
- **Deepwoods.** A fey-touched forest behind the Verdant Veil since 1 AA; in 1022 Liandiel's lost army entered five outside-years ago, camped at what becomes Ciranmyna, and is meeting fey and forest goblins. Time runs differently inside: the elves live "over a thousand years" there while only about 400 pass outside (roughly 2.5 to 1), so more than five years have passed for them. Their mages fail for decades to break the Veil; canonically they have no contact with the outside world until orcs cross the Veil during the Greentide and Corin's party lifts it in 1443. Liandiel (also spelled Liandel) dies inside "a little under two hundred years after" Hornwood by their count [wiki: Deepwoods; wiki: Wood Elf; wiki: Timeline].
- **Serpentspine.** Aul-Dwarov has been dying since c. 6000 BA; Marrhold's tunnels were collapsed long ago, "an act of mercy on Escann" in one later theory [wiki: Last Days of Dwarovar; wiki: Kingdom of Marrhold].

## 4. Nations and factions as playable sides

- **Dameria (the Moonlit Kingdom).** Wants: to turn a war camp into a capital, bind elves and humans, keep Carneter close. Fears: Old Damerian nobles (Acromis) who resent elven lords, and the disgraced house of the Plumwall conspirators. These were not left unpunished: the "Betrayer Duke" Devianus Prumuris of Plumwall and his wife Lutana (an Acromis, aunt of Vidraldus) both died by suicide on 20 Tearfall 1003; their son Cecilius (b. 985; gregarious, callous, arrogant) "married a Damerian loyalist" of Wesdam to survive, and the Treaty of Anbenncóst took "the duchy and most of land" from the family and gave it to Marion. Cecilius still holds the county of Plumwall as Marion's vassal in 1022 [ck3: history/characters/anb_damerian_carnetori.txt; ck3: history/titles/anb_k_dameria.txt]. Friends: Carneter, Moonhaven, Istralore, Silverforge.
- **Lorent under Ioriel.** Wants: Siloriel dominion from the Redglades. Hates: Crege Dameris; Entebenic human-purists; Gawed (old rival for the Small Country).
- **The Black Dragon (Ebonmarck).** Wants: survival, the emperor's body, then reconquest. Hated by nearly everyone; quietly related to the Silurions, the Coldsteels of Gawed, and a harpy pretender.
- **The Sapphire Eye and the Castanorian successors.** Cast and Anor want legitimacy without a Castan; the Order (Sapphirewatch on Lake Silvermere) wants disputes settled by Trial by Chivalry [wiki: Order of the Sapphire Eye].
- **The Blademarches.** Legitimacy rests on a sword that blinds the unworthy.
- **Elven purists (Ibevar, Venáil, Celmaldor) vs Cannorizers (Silver Band veterans).** The elven quarrel over whether to remain a people apart.
- **Gawed and the north.** Wants: Arbaran, Vertesk, the Moors, Eaglecrest. Hates: Black Castanorians, Lorent, soon elves.
- **Blue Reachman cities and Celmaldor** vs Adshaw.
- **Wex.** A child duke, a murdered kingdom, a people who remember Butcher's Field.
- **Small Country kingdoms.** Freedom on sufferance between Lorent and Gawed.
- **Rubyhold / Silverforge / Stone dwarves.** Three different verdicts on the elves.
- **The Phoenix Empire in Cannor** (Corvuria, Busilar) and the Korbarid resistance.
- **Hidden hands:** Canrec's lich-cult, the Ravenseers (wereraven inner council), Agradosian and Infernal cults, forbidden enchanters.

## 5. Plot threads with canon basis

Each thread starts from attested material, but the dramatic questions ("who is still enthralled?", "can the army be rescued?") are the dossier author's proposals, not canon. Where a thread would contradict the fixed timeline it is marked.

1. **The Emperor Under the Tile.** Trace Castan LIII's body from the field of Bal Mire to the ruined waystation beyond the Headman's Wood where an old loyalist hid it (it is not at Bal Mire itself), and decide where he is buried (Westgate, North Citadel, Pantheonway) and what that makes his sons. Bury an empire or restart it.
2. **The Wounds of Trialmount.** The Trials are broken, so no Castan can exist. Whoever repairs them reopens the imperial question for Garrec, Clarimonde, Cast, Anor, and a harpy calling herself Castan LIV. Echo: Castellyr, and every later "restore Castanor" dream.
3. **The Lich's Eighteen Years.** Canrec is alive and patient: the first plague case is 3 Castanmark 1040, eighteen years after the 1022 start (the wiki calls it "exactly 19 years after the end of the War"). (That wiki phrase fits none of the war's end dates given elsewhere, 1020 for Trialmount or late 1021 for the treaty.) A 1022 party can find his laboratories and the Book of Canrec early; success or failure sets the scale of the White Pestilence and of elven supremacy after it. Canon outcome to respect: the plague happens, Aldresia dies killing Canrec, and the cure is made at Varlosen from "samples and research gained from Canrec's laboratories".
4. **A Mind in Shackles.** Domination magic did not die with Nichmer. Who at the Grand Ball is still enthralled, and by which student? Hunting it risks inventing the mage-police that becomes the Magisterium.
5. **The First Union on trial.** Marion's crown, Old Damerian resentment, the dispossessed Prumuris of Plumwall and their Acromis kin (see §4), purist elves and human purists: a court campaign over whether "communion" becomes Anbennar or a pogrom.
6. **The Rose and the Moon, first blood.** Ruben's death in the lists is the seed of the Siloriel–Silmuna feud that ends in the Lilac Wars.
7. **The Lost Army in the Deepwoods.** Liandiel Speardancer's host walked out of the world in 1017; kin, deserters and the fey all want something from a rescue. Canon limit: no rescue succeeds; the Veil holds until 1443 and time inside runs about 2.5 times faster (§2.9), so a story can reach the Veil, bargain with fey or lose people to it, but should not bring the army home.
8. **War to End the Dragon / Reach in revolt.** Northern Escann and the Alenic Reach as a mercenary sandbox with a hard deadline (Coldmarket, 1024).
9. **Seventh Council.** Deifying an elf, recognising a dead ex-enemy general as Adean's avatar and refusing the same honour to the healer Alaria, months after the Treaty stripped Castellos's priests of their coronation monopoly (the monopoly ended by treaty, not by the council).
10. **The Sun King's offer.** Jaher holds Corvuria and recruits Cannor's unemployed heroes for the east (the Eastern Campaigns proper begin in 1038; recruiting in 1022 is the author's extrapolation).

## 7. Uncertainties and source conflicts

- **Dragonwake placement.** The task brief lists it between 1022 and 1444; canon is 470–500 AA [wiki: Dragonwake; wiki: Timeline].
- **Trialmount date.** Mod: 1020.10.31 in most character files but 1020.8.31 in Finnic's entry; wiki war page: Tourney 16.08.1020, battle 31.08.1020. The Sapphire Eye page says Urion died "in 1021". I use 31 Tearfall 1020.
- **Great Storm:** wiki 1000.2.10; the mod kills Munas's parents in it on 1000.10.15. **Bal Mire:** wiki 06.12.1015; mod 1015.12.14. **Morban Flats:** wiki 14.05.1014; mod 1014.7.14; the wiki casualty list puts Godrac Coldsteel's death in 1015.
- **Who killed Munas.** War page: Canrec. Munas, Acolyte and Venac pages and the mod: Venac.
- **Auci.** Death: 3 Tearfall 1020 (her page), 1021 (Avatar page), 1021.10.3 (mod). Her page gives reign 980–1020 and father Galien; the monarch list and mod give reign from 994 and parents Arrel II and Aria. Marion's accession shifts accordingly.
- **Marion.** Born 1001.8.19 (history) vs 1001.2.19 (bookmark). Wife Elantia (mod, and the wiki's Carnetori monarch list) vs Elemia síl Sorncóst (wiki's Damerian monarch list): use Elantia. Accession 1020 (several wiki pages) vs 1021 (mod, Damerian list).
- **Treaty date.** Treaty page: 31 Tearfall 1021. War page table: 31.08.1021. Mod title files: grants dated 1021.10.31 (Cast and Anor's crowns 1020.10.1 under the same comment).
- **Era of Black Ice.** 768–978 on its own page; the Timeline says it begins in 798.
- **Bal Mire's condition** (ruin vs garrisoned fortress) and **Bal Vertesk's date** (593 BA in Timeline and mod; 443 BA on "Balgar's Wonders").
- **Crege's accession.** Wiki Carnetori list: crowned at 16, reign 1014–1050. Mod: Auci holds Carneter until 1021.10.3.
- **Who ruled Sormannon in 1000.** Wiki (Remnant Fleet): King Alcuin. Mod: Alcuin b. 995, king only after murdering Petran in 1019.
- **Corvuria.** Wiki: liberated 1011. Mod: held by Jaher's governor Denarion from 1008.6.1.

- **Lorent.** Wiki monarch list gives Ruben II lifespan 1000–1020 but reign to 1022 and Reán IV from 1022; mod makes Reán king from 1020.10.27. Maugun's page names "Rewan VIII".
- **Ianren.** Mod: holds Arbaran from 1015, Cult of Ara. Wiki: reign 1022–1170, Elven Forebears. **Redglades:** founded 1003 (wiki) vs held from 1018 (mod).
- **Verne.** The mod already shows three kingdoms on 1022.1.1; the wiki dates the break-up after Emil's 1022 treaty. The king at the Tourney is "Rocair V" on two pages, "Rocair IV" on Verne's; in the mod the King of Verne from 989 to 1021 is Emil I, and his brother Rocair appears only as holder for one day in 1021 and then as King of Wyvernmark. Safest reading: Rocair fought at the Tourney as a Vernid prince and claimant.
- **Alaria's school.** "Master of Conjuration" on the Battle of Trialmount page; founder of the "School of Life", later Necromancy, on the Magic and Wise Council pages (where Conjuration's founder is left blank).
- **Spelling.** Gelmonias (mod) vs Kelmonias (wiki) Dalzuir; Liandiel vs Liandel Speardancer; Bilfiz vs Bilfix the Short; Lunateín vs Luneteín.
- **Later chronology.** Wars of Rule start 1244 or 1253; Reán V dies 1364, 1374 or 1376; Vincen dies 1421 or 1422; Lothane III elected 1422 or 1423; Third Lilac War from 1400 or 1407; Damesear falls 1442 or 1443; Magisterium "established 1284" vs founded with the Empire; Gawed refounded 1015 or 1016.
