# 02 — Western & Central Cannor at the EU4 start (11 Nerament/November 1444 AA)

Scope: the Empire of Anbennar and its neighbours — Lorent, Gawed, the Small Country, the Dragon Coast, Businor and the Divenhal — in the eighteen months after the Lilac Wars ended.

Citation key: `[wiki: Page]` = Fandom dump; `[eu4: path]` = EU4 mod file under `src/eu4/`. Where they disagree I say which I followed (section 8). Chinese names are given only where found in the listed Chinese sources (section 7).

---

## 1. Overview

On 11 November 1444 western Cannor is a continent catching its breath. The Lilac Wars (丁香战争), ninety-five years of dynastic war between House Silmuna (西尔穆纳) of Dameria and House Siloriel of Lorent, ended on 11 Silversight (April) 1443 when Grand Duke Adénn "Skylance" Silmuna was cut down in the Imperial Palace at Anbenncóst and the city was sacked by the Wexonard army [wiki: Lilac Wars; wiki: Invasion of the Damesear; eu4: localisation/Flavour_Wex_A30_l_english.yml `flavor_wex.1`]. The winners are tired, guilty and broke: Lorent "won" but most of its knightly order is dead and its Wine Lords are in open defiance; Wex holds the Dove Throne but only "with the begrudging support of our Lorentish allies of convenience" [eu4: localisation/Flavour_Wex_A30_l_english.yml `A30_lothane_triumph_desc`]. The Grand Duchy of Dameria has been abolished and partitioned; the Grand Duchy of Esmaria collapsed a lifetime earlier; of the "Three Grand Duchies" only Wex remains [wiki: Grand Duchy of Wex].

Everything else follows from that vacuum. Fields lie fallow, disbanded soldiers turn bandit, noble lines have "catastrophic generational losses", and in the east the Greentide (since 1424) pours refugees into the Empire [eu4: localisation/anb_lilac_wars_l_english.yml; wiki: Lothane III síl Wex]. The start date is itself the day the Greentide's back is broken: Corin (b. 23 Bloomsend 1420) dies on 11 Nerament 1444 killing Korgus Dookanson at the Battle of Castonath, far to the east [wiki: Corin (person); wiki: Corin; wiki: Timeline] — so on the start date itself western Cannor is still living with the Greentide as an open wound (how fast the news travels west is not stated in the corpus). A teenaged king in Gawed (crowned at fifteen in 1443), a twenty-one-year-old duchess in Istralore, a six-year-old Silmuna marquis in Wesdam and a 240-year-old gnome Hierarch on Nimscodd are all waiting to see whether Emperor Lothane III can make "Wex must rule" stick.

Calendar note for writers: years are AA; months are Castanmark, Esmarment, Bloomsdawn, Silversight, Halament, Suren, Teysuren, Yshdament, Bloomsend, Tearfall, Nerament, Truefrost (Jan–Dec); weekdays Newday, Intirsday, Middlesday, Akursday, Faladay, Cedesday, Sorrowday (Cedesday and Sorrowday are rest days) [wiki: Calendar]. The EU4 start is 11 Nerament 1444.

## 2. Facts by sub-topic

### 2.1 The Lilac Wars (1348–1443)

**Name.** Either from a purple-pink evening sky over Bigwheat in the Small Country that made two armies stop fighting to look, or simply Damerian blue mixed with Lorentish red [wiki: Lilac Wars]. Elves call it "the Lilac Ballet"; the surface dwarves of Silverforge and Rubyhold call it "the Flower-Grudge" [eu4: localisation/anb_lilac_wars_l_english.yml `lilac_wars.46`, `.47`; eu4: events/anb_lilac_wars.txt (event .47 fires for A73 Silverforge and A20 Rubyhold)].

**Cause — the Imperial Crisis.** When Emperor Lothane II Silmuna died in 1343 the obvious heir, Grand Duke Aldres III of Dameria, was twenty, "preferred whoring and hunting", and happily let his grand-uncle Grand Magister Vincen "Palemantle" Silmuna push his five-year-old daughter Riannón onto the throne. Palemantle had had her declared an Avatar of the Dame; many magisters saw a return to mage rule. Only Duke-Elector Alec III síl na Trisfer of Pearlsedge protested and was publicly ridiculed by Aldres. On 14 Yshdament 1348 the electors met while Palemantle was away at Aranthíl and voted to depose her (only Bofur IV of Silverforge called it treason). The child locked herself in the throne room; with the city rioting she cast a vast enchantment that turned the mob into her puppets. Her bodyguard and kinsman Ardan Silmuna killed her and her advisers at the cost of his own life. Palemantle died in 1350 "falling down the stairs" of Aranthíl Tower [wiki: First Lilac War; wiki: Vincen Palemantle; wiki: Empire of Anbennar].

**The foreign emperor.** With the Silmuna name poisoned, the humiliated Elector of Pearlsedge whipped votes (Beepeck and the Grand Magister among them) for King Reán V "the White Fox" Siloriel of Lorent — an albino schemer — while the rest split between Grand Duke Otau of Esmaria and Aldres III [wiki: First Lilac War; wiki: Reán V Siloriel]. The Empire revolted at once.

**First Lilac War (1348–1376).**
- 1348–49: an imperial fleet out of Neckcliffe blockades the Bay of Wines; Reán is turned back and has to return to his procession fleet in a rowboat.
- 1 Bloomsdawn 1350: Reán marches ~40,000 men on Neckcliffe, quotes a Damerian Republic play, prays to Nerat and crosses the border. His marshal is the half-elf Vaceran "Leallord" síl Ionnidar.
- 1352: the leaderless Imperial Navy shatters; Reán lands on Damesear, routs the drunk Aldres III at the First Battle of Anbenncóst and is crowned (12 Castanmark 1353). Aldres's son Vincen vanishes — smuggled by a servant to Damescrown and then to Gawed, raised in the household of the Gawedi lord Edmund Brasslance.
- 1360: a seventeen-year-old rides into Damescrown square at the head of a mercenary army, removes his helmet and proclaims: "I am Vincen Silmuna, son of Aldres, brother of Riannón, grandson of Emperor Lothane…" At Wexkeep he proves himself with his father's signet ring and Silmuna silver hair, and founds the **Moon Party** (月党) with Alvar III síl Verne, Artus I síl Wex and Aucan II Silistra. Reán's faction was already being called the **Rose Party** in letters.
- 1361–64: War of Esmari Succession; Vincen ends it by declaring Esmarainé a Free City so no one can reunite Esmaria.
- 8 Yshdament 1365: after a feint via leaked letters, the Moon Fleet lands near Auraire under a full moon; Anbenncóst throws out its Lorentish garrison and opens its gates.
- 1370: Battle of Módgate — won only by the sacrifice of Brasslance and his Gawedi veterans.
- 13 Silversight 1376: after a shouting match with his son Kylian, Reán is found with his throat slit. Kylian V opens Lorentainé's gates, gives Vincen his sister Eilís Siloriel in marriage, and Vincen is elected Emperor by five of seven votes: "Never again will the Emperors of Anbennar be puppets or tyrants."
[wiki: First Lilac War; wiki: Lilac Wars]

**Second Lilac War (1381–1394).** Kylian V died of sickness in 1378; his only child Queen Reánna III (a partying disaster with one bastard, Emile, and a reputedly impotent husband, Delian síl Kyliande) died in 1381 without a trueborn heir [wiki: Lilac Wars; wiki: List of Lorentish monarchs]; by Lorent's absolute cognatic law her aunt Eilís — Vincen's wife — was heir. Vaceran stormed the council and crowned Eilís's ten-year-old half-brother Laurens IV; young Nicolas of Madelaire challenged him and died (duel or a broken neck against the table, depending on which party tells it). Madelaire, Deranne, Crovania, Rubenaire and Eilísin swore to "the True Queen, Eilís Siloriel", who kept a rival court at Rubenaire. Key beats: the two-year siege of Lorentainé, which the boy-king refused to leave; the **Miracle of Rósionn** (1385), where Marquis Willam VII of Wesdam died covering the Moon retreat — the root of Wesdam's grudge; Vincen's **chevauchées** through the Small Country from 1386; the elf Finorian Bluetongue hiring the Hillthrone gnolls against Busilar; **Horsegrave** (1392), won by the young Otó síl Wex and Ricain "the Brash" Silistra, where Vaceran was captured (dead six months later); the **Burning of Bigwheat** (1393) and a Cannor-wide famine; Laurens IV's **Pacification of Deranne** (House Steedspear exterminated; halfling Dustin Smallknight knighted). The **Ording Compromise** and Treaty of Beepeck left Laurens king but made Vincen's son Adénn his heir — cutting Eilís out behind her back and wrecking the marriage. At the peace-tourney of Enteben Adénn entered as the "Knight of the Bluerose", unhorsed Emile the Bastard and earned the name **Skylance**. Laurens responded by renewing Lorent's friendship with the dwarves of Rubyhold, who armed the Rose Knights in rosemetal [wiki: Second Lilac War].

**Third Lilac War (1407–1421/22).** A two-front invasion of Lorent from Deranne and the West Dameshead nearly won it for the Silmunas. The turning point was the **Treachery (Betrayal) of Wesdam**, 1417: Wesdamerian forces fell on the resting Imperial army; Grand Duke Otó IV of Wex died shielding Adénn; only a quarter of the host escaped through Neckcliffe. Otó's sixteen-year-old son Lothane inherited Wex and a grudge. In 1419 the merchants of the Bay of Wines seceded as the League of Winebay. Vincen "the Old Owl" died of old age on 17 Teysuren 1421 [wiki: Lilac Wars; wiki: Lothane III síl Wex; wiki: League of Winebay; wiki: Vincen "the Old Owl" Silmuna].

**Fourth Lilac War (1422–1443).**
- **Battle of the Moon Council**, 5 Bloomsdawn 1422: the night before the election Adénn's host, arriving for the vote, is ambushed by Lothane's men and hidden Lorentish troops. **Correction:** Lothane's gnomish tutor Jon Bardswood designed only the plan for Lothane to *contest the election* (counting on Pearlsedge, Beepeck and Damescrown opposing another Silmuna); the bloodbath was "some unknown move" by Lothane himself, and it "horrified and enraged the gnome", who nonetheless stayed loyal ("if this many eggs were already broken, then we had better make an omelet worthy of Castellos"). Laurens IV of Lorent and Marion of Wesdam are the ones the wiki names as its alleged orchestrators [wiki: Jon Bardswood; wiki: Lothane III síl Wex; wiki: Lilac Wars]. Moon dead include Adénn's sons Reán and Maurise, his sister Varina, Duke-Crossguard Kylian II Silmuna, Devan III síl Eilísin, Marquis Alveru II síl Arannen, Ricain "the Brash", Duke Marion II síl Verne and Tomar síl Deranne; Rose dead include Marion I Silmuna-Wesdam, Varil VI Silcalas of Arbaran and Brayden síl Wex. Adénn escapes; the remaining electors crown Lothane [wiki: Battle of the Moon Council; wiki: Jon Bardswood].
- 1422: Laurens IV and Marion of Wesdam "die of horrible sicknesses, allegedly for their orchestrating of the Battle of the Moon Council" (source conflict: the battle's own casualty list has Marion I Silmuna-Wesdam killed *in* the battle — see section 8); Kylian VI succeeds; Lothane marries Eilísa Siloriel, a match arranged by Bardswood [wiki: Lilac Wars; wiki: Jon Bardswood].
- 1424: the Greentide erupts; both the Magisterium and Orda Aldresia forbid intervention; half the Aldresian knights (mostly the younger ones) desert east as the Sword Covenant under Galinda síl na Eán — the "Covenblad(ic) Schism" [wiki: Orda Aldresia; wiki: Delian síl Arannen; eu4: history/countries/B19 - Sword Covenant.txt].
- 1430 Rubenaire defects to Lorent; 1439 Deranne surrenders; by 1441 the Wine Lords have capitulated.
- 1442–43: Lorentish and Wexonard armies (with the Black Hounds mercenary company) land on Damesear. After a month's defence of the Imperial Palace, Lothane breaches the walls with his own magic on a full-moon night. In the throne room Adénn, last man standing, duels Lothane to a standstill until his cousin **Erlan Silmuna of Wesdam** stabs him in the back; Lothane then kills Erlan with a last surge of magic and claims the victory as his own (the truth surfaces only in 1468 through divination). Asked by the priests to stop the sack, Lothane, cleaning his greatsword beside the bodies, says only "Wex alone rules"; the sack lasts three days by one account, months by another [wiki: Adénn Skylance; wiki: Lothane III síl Wex; eu4: localisation/Flavour_Wex_A30_l_english.yml].

**Outcome.** Nominal Lorentish/Rose victory: Siloriel keeps Lorent, Wex takes the Dove Throne (鸽座), Dameria is dissolved. Eastern Damesear (Varivar/Eargate, Acromton, Plumwall) goes to Wex; the west and north of the isle to the Magisterium in exchange for accepting Wex's seizures; Anbenncóst becomes an autonomous free city [wiki: Isle of Damesear; wiki: Grand Magister Crovan; eu4: localisation/Flavour_Wex_A30_l_english.yml `flavor_wex.12–14`]. Surviving Silmunas: the boy Laurens III Silmuna-Wesdam (Rose-aligned, b. 1438) and Adénn's third son **Rogier "the Exiled"** (b. 1406), who faked his death, marched his loyalists through Arbaran to the Marcher Lords and founded the **Sons of Dameria** in Escann; his sister Eilís "the Blue" was lost in Castellyr and her half-orc son Lothane Bluetusk becomes Rogier's heir [wiki: House Silmuna; wiki: Rogier the Exiled]. Lothane offered the remaining Silmuna loyalists safe passage through the Empire if they went into exile in Escann, while putting a bounty on Rogier's head [wiki: Rogier the Exiled]. Adénn's wife (Rogier's mother) was Calassa síl Deranne, daughter of Duke Tomar — the blood tie behind Deranne's Moon loyalty [wiki: Adénn Skylance; wiki: Lilac Wars; wiki: Second Lilac War]. Orda Aldresia held out in Arca Aldresia until summer 1444 [wiki: Delian síl Arannen]. Lorent's wiki says the war ended "with the Welyamite peace, pushed by Welyam III" [wiki: Kingdom of Lorent] — **[unverified / do not dramatise as written]**: Welyam was a fifteen-year-old prince when Anbenncóst fell (11 Silversight 1443) and was only crowned on 3 Tearfall 1443 [wiki: Welyam III Gerwick]; the same Lorent page later uses "Welyamite peace" for the Lorent–Gawed détente that peaks at the 1473 Tourney of Horsegarden. Treat it as a later label for the long northern peace, not as a treaty Welyam brokered in 1443.

**Who stood where in 1444** (EU4 start flags): *Rose* — Lorent, Wex, Redglades, Wesdam, Sorncóst, Madelaire, Great Ording, Kyliande, Ionnidar, Venáil, Neckcliffe, Pearlsedge, Beepeck, Damescrown, Vertesk, Uelaire, Arbaran, Aranmas, Telgeir, Estallen, Cestirande, Sugamber, Toarnen, Ibevar, Busilar, Reveria. *Moon* — Deranne, Eilísin, Rubenaire, Verne, Istralore, Moonhaven, Silverforge, Anbenncóst, Bisan, Konwell, Bennon, Leslinpár, Esmarainé, Arannen, Celliande, Asheniande, Eborthíl [eu4: history/countries/*.txt `lilac_wars_rose_party` / `lilac_wars_moon_party`]. The lists above are not exhaustive; the same flags also mark, for the Rose: Rubyhold, Terr, Crothan, Menibor, Gisden, Seinathil, Deamoine, Varaine and Southroy (tag-file spellings, diacritics not checked); for the Moon: Orda Aldresia, Galéinn, Silvelar, Bluehart, Gabmorionn, Themarenn, the Brinkmarches and Deshak (Eborthíl's Akasi crown) [same files, checked by grep].

### 2.2 The Empire of Anbennar: constitution

- **Name and founding.** *Anbennar* is Elven for "communion/unity". Founded 1221 at the Grand Summit of Aranthíl, when a cabal of court mages forced peace on the Interregnum warlords — the **Mages' Peace**. The founding mage was Delian Whitecloak. Early rulers were Mage Emperors chosen inside the Magisterium; the Wars of Rule (1253–1284 on the war's own page and the Timeline, beginning when the nobles stormed Aranthíl and killed Mage Emperor Mathos I "the Idle"; the Empire and Mages' Peace pages say 1244) and the Treaty of Aranthíl / Concordat of Rule split the office into a noble Emperor (who must still show magical ability) and a Grand Magister as chief adviser [wiki: Wars of Rule; wiki: Timeline; wiki: Empire of Anbennar; wiki: Mages' Peace; wiki: List of Anbennarian Emperors].
- **Eligibility.** A noble with magical ability; since the elf Aldarion Elventhrone the **Elder Pact** caps long-lived emperors at fifty years [wiki: Empire of Anbennar; wiki: Elder Pact].
- **Seven Electors**, each representing a constituency: Istralore (nobles of the East Dameshead; also hereditary Marshals of the Empire), Pearlsedge (West Dameshead), Beepeck (halflings), Silverforge (dwarves), Moonhaven (elves), Damescrown (merchants, and the nobles of the Damescrown region), the Magisterium (magic-users) [wiki: Empire of Anbennar; eu4: history/countries `elector = yes`].
- **Emperor in 1444:** Lothane III síl Wex, Grand Duke Lothane VI of Wex (b. 4 Bloomsend 1401), a "powerful mage" (evocation, abjuration, enchantment, some conjuration) and Wexonard greatsword fighter; consort Eilísa Siloriel (b. 1413, daughter of Laurens IV); heir Caylen (b. 1430), gentle and rumoured simple; a second trueborn child, the clever and strong-willed Nara síl Wex, later betrothed (1451) to Dustin síl Verne [eu4: history/countries/A30 - Wex.txt; wiki: Lothane III síl Wex; wiki: Alvar IV "the Shining" síl Verne].
- **Colour Courts:** nine noble-run circuits for law, tax and defence — Blue (Damerian lands, Damesear, Luna cities), Red (Verne), Purple (Wex, Bisan, Sugamber), White (Pearlsedge, nominally Roilsard), Gray (the Borders), Orange (Esmaria), Yellow (Damescrown and Arbaran), Black (Vertesk and Uelaire), Green (Beepeck) [wiki: Empire of Anbennar].
- **Imperial Parliament / Diet** meets in Anbenncóst; new emperors must go there to receive fealty [wiki: Anbenncóst; eu4: localisation/Flavour_Dameria_A58_l_english.yml]. The mod's reform ladder is renamed in-setting: Call for Reforms, the **Dove Tax**, Emperor's Will, Enforce Realm Peace, Imperial Adventurer Companies, Permanent Parliament, Embrace Erelas Reforms, Revoke the Privilegia, Abolish the Elections, Unify Anbennar [eu4: localisation `emperor_*_title`].
- **Bhailines** — guild-run city governments named from Moon Elven *bhail*, "community"; Damescrown's claims descent from the Damerian Republic, which is why it is an elector [wiki: Bhailines].
- **The Magisterium (魔法师议会).** Regulates all magic in the Empire; seat at Old Damenath/the artificial isle of Adráil; Grand Magister elected by an inner council of the heads of the eight schools. Districts are "towers": Adráil, Aranthíl, Oldtower (Verne), Vertesk's Black Tower, Windtower (Wesdam), the awkward Konwell (Esmaria), Varlosen (Arannen), plus Lorincrag (Busilar) and Jurith (Eborthíl). By imperial law every noble house is sent a magister as adviser, tutor and court mage. Its soldiers, the **Magister-Militant**, are non-mages bound by a geas oath. Grand Magister since 1419: **Crovan of Vinerick**, a Wexonard, elected at 33, widely suspected of complicity in the Moon Council; his house's rise against the traditionalist Silgarions is "the Mages' Strife" [wiki: Magisterium; wiki: Mages' Peace; wiki: Grand Magister Crovan; eu4: history/countries/A85 - Magisterium.txt; eu4: localisation `A85_magister_militant_desc`].
- **Orda Aldresia (阿德蕾西娅骑士团).** Arcane knights (sword as spell-focus, the art of *Diranbe*), the Emperor's bodyguard and the Magisterium's rival. Oath: "I shall hold no lands, accept no titles, take no partner and bear no children…". Grandmaster Delian síl Arannen, Adénn's boyhood friend, bent the knee in summer 1444, took up guard duty that autumn and left again within weeks "upon seeing the tulip beds of the palace replaced" [wiki: Orda Aldresia; wiki: Delian síl Arannen].
- **Anbenncóst (安本科斯特), "city of the world's desire".** Formerly Damucen; the elven Remnant Fleet landed here in 1000 AA (Lansday), and the Elven name Anbenncóst had replaced Damucen by the time the War of the Sorcerer-King ended (c. 1020) — it was not renamed on the day of the landing. Districts: Oldtown (Old Colosseum, Damerian Forum, Temple of Ara/Aranite Guildhall), Marionside (Castle Dameris, Silverhall with the Grand Ballroom of the Silver Moon — destroyed in the sack — the Tael city hall with its astronomical clock, Court Street), Elvendocks (floating mansions built on beached arkships; Prince's Park, Elfcastle), Newport, the Imperial District and Palace, Dovemarket, Forgeway (dwarven smiths), the Temple District, Towerhill with the "twin academies" (Imperial Academy and Silmuna School), and the Bilge, a slum covering "at least a quarter" of the city (EU4; the wiki says nearly a third by the 19th century) where "many times, city authorities have disappeared". In 1444 the Lord-Mayor is **Awen of Earshore**, a militia leader installed to restore order after the occupation, never originally elected [wiki: Anbenncóst; eu4: history/countries/A38 - Anbenncost.txt; eu4: localisation/Flavour_Anbenncost_A38_l_english.yml].
- **Other free cities:** Damescrown (Lord-Elector Aldres the Gold; the Grand Exchange, the Intertidal Banking Clan, the Calasanni Trade Company "CLSTC", the "Barques of Damescrown"); Beepeck; Vertesk ("Highriver", old Castanorian citadel; Arnos Tordan); Esmarainé (Nara Seinpírártharn); Tellum (ruled by a Council of Criers; Teagan síl na Tíl); Giberd (Imperial gnomes) [wiki: Free City of Damescrown; wiki: Free City of Vertesk; eu4: history/countries].

### 2.3 Wex (威克斯) and "Wex must rule"

An Alenic people expelled from the Alen by the Gawedi in 470, the Wexonards sacked Damescrown, seized the Castanorian citadel Bal Hyl (now **Wexkeep**) and have lived ever since as "an Alenic surrounded by a sea of Anbennarians"; they call Godrac the Invader "Godrac the Grasper" [wiki: Wexonard; eu4: localisation `flavor_wex.3.d`]. The duchy runs on the Ironhills (iron, steel, now coal), dairy and wool, and on mercenary service. Its army is infantry: Wexspears (shielded spear blocks), Axeguards, plate-armoured Greatswords, early arquebuses; the **Wexonard School of Fencing** at Threeguard teaches the greatsword "Art of Fighting" [wiki: Grand Duchy of Wex].

Lothane's creed, in the mod's words: "if Wex does not rule, Wex shall not be." His adviser **Jon Bardswood** (Ion obb Bardsild, Imperial gnome, b. 1373) is a realist political writer — "wise counsels… must necessarily be due to the prudence of the prince" [eu4: localisation `flavor_wex.3.d`, `A30_court_of_the_grand_duke_desc`; wiki: Jon Bardswood]. Private life: from 1438 an affair with **Cela síl Plumwall** (brown curls streaked purple and red, smelling of Diohúis plum brandy) produced a bastard, Óto; exposed in 1442 with the Duke of Cestirande's backing, it froze his marriage and will eventually cost him the Lorentish alliance [wiki: Lothane III síl Wex; eu4: localisation `flavor_wex.9`].

### 2.4 Lorent (洛伦特), "the Rose Kingdom"

- **Shape of the realm.** Founded 900 by Lorevarn II from Redwood, Enteben and Redfort; capital **Lorentainé** on the Bloodwine, famed for high red walls. The Ruby Crown was forged at Rubyhold for Lorenan the Great. House Siloriel descends from the elf Ioriel Redrose and Ruben II (m. 1018); rumour holds Ioriel's soul is bound in the crown. Law: absolute cognatic succession — ruling queens, children take the mother's name [wiki: Kingdom of Lorent; wiki: List of Lorentish monarchs; eu4: localisation `A01_ruling_queens_of_lorent_desc`].
- **1444 court.** King **Kylian VI** (b. 4 Nerament 1410), half-elf, "a gentle man with no military inclinations… horrified when he heard of the Sacking of Anbenncóst". Queen **Laurenne síl Kyliande** is Duchess of Kyliande in her own right; his mother **Lisbet síl Ionnidar** still rules Ionnidar (her house holds the hereditary custodianship of Lorenan's tomb) with Kylian as heir; heir **Ruben** (b. 1427, "conqueror"). Half-brother **Maurise "Realmkeeper"** was Laurens IV's chancellor. The crown starts with the privilege "Entrenched Lorentish Nobility" [eu4: history/countries/A01 - Lorent.txt, B68 - Kyliande.txt, B72 - Ionnidar.txt; wiki: List of Lorentish monarchs; wiki: Kylian VI Siloriel].
- **Problems.** The Knights of the Rose are mostly dead; Lorentainé is damaged; the **Wine Lords** — Rubenaire (Rión V), Eilísin (Reánna II, aged 13), Madelaire (held with Wesdam) — "refuse to bow"; the League of Winebay under Wineport's Énri the Taster and its Council of Seventy Chalices is independent; **Deranne** under the cruel young Adénn III síl Deranne plots revenge; Sorncóst under the perfectionist Petran III is loyal but proud [eu4: localisation/Flavour_Lorent_A01_l_english.yml; eu4: history/countries; wiki: Kingdom of Lorent].
- **Peoples.** High Lorentish (elvenised, flamboyant, masters of flattery and tactful put-downs); Low Lorentish (Entebenic horse-folk, laconic, honest; the Ording Workhorse); Sorncósti (least elvenised, red-dyed clothes, everyone can appraise a vintage, river-goddess Sorbodua); Derannic (reaver-descended, House of the Seaflower, Westport, the magical Daromsforge); Roilsardi ("thorns… always returning") [wiki: High Lorentish; wiki: Low Lorentish; wiki: Sorncósti; wiki: Duchy of Deranne; eu4: localisation/Flavour_Deranne_A02_l_english.yml].
- **The Redglades (红林).** Ioriel's reward: an elfrealm in the old Redwood, crimson-leaved and fey-haunted by four powers — Odrach the fairy dragon, Idrissa the nymph who takes bad luck and breaks hearts, Sonnider the treant, and Cimagen the dryad of rot who reads the future in fallen leaves. Ruled in 1444 by Elthariel Gladeguard (b. 1199). Gawedi folktales fear the wood; a Gawedi mission is literally "Burning the Redglades" [wiki: Elfrealm of the Redglades; eu4: history/countries/A03 - Redglades.txt; eu4: localisation/Flavour_Gawed_A13_l_english.yml].
- **Wine.** Rubenaire Red (earthy, herbal; perfected by the elf Filendor Gladeguard), Saloren Blue ("Damerian wine"), Roilsard, Toarnen Black, Redglade Fíonca (strawberry nose), Red Lineage (the colour of Lorenan's blood), Pearlsedge's Pearl Vintage white. Bottles are imported from Anbennar; the glassworks are at Bottlepoint [wiki: Wine; eu4: localisation/Flavour_Lorent_A01_l_english.yml; eu4: localisation/Flavour_Pearlsedge_A11_l_english.yml].

### 2.5 Gawed (加韦德) and the Alenic north

- **The Northern Eagle** (Alenic eagle on black; capital Gaweton, 加韦顿). Founded by Godrac the Invader in the Dragonwake; 90% human; worships chiefly Castellos, Esmaryal, Falah, Nerat and Adean. Not elvenised and proud of it: law is Alenic oath-custom — an oath-breaker or kin-betrayer chooses "banishment or death". For a lord this arrives as **"the Black Hound"**: a letter under a black dog-shaped seal offering execution, or a month to settle his affairs before exile (not to be confused with the Black Hounds mercenary company of the Sack) [wiki: Gawedi]. Every three households owe one equipped soldier ("hosts unceasing"). Great Lords descend from Godrac's companions (Oudescker, Gerwick, Baldfather; also Brasslance, Ramsgate). Nobles wear knee-length wool coats under furs and a cap, mail at court; jewellery is iron, copper and pewter; **silver was illegal** except as weapons against the werewolves of the Greatwoods, though great lords carry a silver dagger [wiki: Kingdom of Gawed; wiki: Gawedi; eu4: localisation `A13_*_desc`].
- **1444.** King **Welyam III Gerwick**, "the Winter King", b. 1427, bookish and averse to violence, crowned 3 Tearfall 1443 after his father Crown Prince Jon died at Rottenstep (4 Yshdament 1443) and his grandfather Edmund II — the brute who destroyed Viswall's Rainbow Hall and was slowly poisoned by its fumes (debilitated from 1425 and bedridden in his last years, with Jon ruling as regent) — died of grief. Jon was beheaded by Korgus Dookanson himself and the royal sword Talon was lost; his body came home in late Yshdament. Welyam's uncle Prince Henric offered to take the crown in his place and was refused ("The whole realm mourns, uncle, but they do not forget their duty either"); Henric stays at court for decades [wiki: Battle of Rottenstep; wiki: List of Gawedi monarchs; wiki: Welyam III Gerwick]. Quote on the war's end: "To deny a man your table and hearth is as good as killing him… We've never forgotten how to be humane to one another — how to be human. Maybe they have." On 23 Truefrost 1443 the Reachman merchant **Derek "Deland"** arrived at court and became indispensable; the king's mother Alanna Cockerwall will storm the keep with 1,500 Moormen in 1445 to oust him and be stabbed at a feast a month later. Derek's niece **Karina Deland** (sable hair, Reachman furs, iron jewellery) is the future queen. The Cobalt Company, unpaid for Rottenstep, turns anti-monarchist [wiki: Welyam III Gerwick; wiki: List of Gawedi monarchs; wiki: Battle of Rottenstep; wiki: Cobalt Company; eu4: localisation `flavor_gawed.4`, `.35`].
- **Neighbours.** Westmoors (Moor Lords; Devan III Cottersea), Adshaw (Old Alenics; Rycan IV), the Alenic Reach cities (Bayvic is, centuries later, a republic known for its city goblins — "run by goblins" is not supported [wiki: Order of Chroniclers; wiki: Ux Limptooth]), Bjarnrík across the Giant's Grave Sea, Vertesk (old capital, permanent rival) and Arbaran, the Empire's shield [eu4: history/countries; eu4: localisation/Flavour_Gawed_A13_l_english.yml].

### 2.6 The Small Country (小人国) and halflings

- Cannor's breadbasket between the Portroy, the Dragonhills, the Redglades and the Ruby Mountains; rivers Widderoy, Burroy, Middanroy. Once the Gnomish Hierarchy's "Small Province" (hence the name), then the Small Kingdom (478–979). In 1444 it is split almost exactly in half: **Redfoots** under Lorent (red for the rose), **Bluefoots** under Gawed [wiki: Small Country (Region); eu4: localisation/Flavour_SmallCountry_A97_l_english.yml].
- **Viswall**, "City of Color", was the last free halfling state; King Callam síl Vis's **Viswall Rebellion** (1421–23) was crushed by Lorent and Gawed acting together (50,000 halfling dead), the Rainbow Hall destroyed, and the city partitioned into North Viswall (Darian Longfellow) and South Viswall (Arnol the Fat) [wiki: Viswall Rebellion; wiki: Free City of Viswall; eu4: history/countries].
- **Beepeck (比佩克)**, Elector of the halflings: salt from the Saltmarsh, glass, pigs; hexagonal cement "hivestacks"; a river navy of engineer-sailors, the "Seabees"; Begga's "Cathedral of the Last Crust". Lord-Elector **Devan the Old Bee** (b. 1387; scholar, entrepreneur, embezzler). Its politics: "generosity is sweetest when it serves our ends" — it buries rival halfling petitions in imperial backlogs and refused to help Viswall. It wants halfling-majority Orston from Exwes [wiki: Free City of Beepeck; eu4: localisation `A12_*_desc`; wiki: Empire of Anbennar].
- **Halfling life.** Up to seven meals a day; bare, groomed, sometimes beaded foot-hair (nobles wear powdered foot-wigs); "house stacking". Sub-peoples: Hillfoot, Moonfoot, round Ciderfoots (Butterburn, Appleton), martial Royfoots, lean Oakfoots of the Approach, tiny hirsute Beefoots, Visfoots [wiki: Halfling]. Hero of the hour: Ser **Dustin Smallknight** (b. 1373), knighted by Laurens IV, now leading the Small Fellows adventurers in Escann and credited with choosing the ground at Rottenstep [wiki: Second Lilac War; wiki: Battle of Rottenstep; eu4: history/countries/B10 - Small Fellows.txt].

### 2.7 The Dragon Coast (巨龙海岸): gnomes, kobolds, reavers

- **Gnomes.** The First Gnomish Hierarchy (capital Oddansbay, "eclipsing even Castonath") fell in 470 when Tayekan the Blue woke and the kobolds poured from the Dragonheights. Survivors: the **Nimscodd Hierarchy** (尼姆斯科德) — Cliff Gnomes on a damestear-mining island; religion *The Thought*; the Island Fleet, the Nimscodd Engineering Academy where artificery was first revealed, and "the Eyes of Dr. T.J. Ecklebozz" (spyglass sentries) — and the merchant **Portnamm Hierarchy** (波特纳姆; Creek Gnomes, theatre, red caps; Hierarch Pordolt Somberwazey). The gnome-ruled Kingdom of Iochand (伊奥尚; crowned with a theatre prop) was destroyed by Reveria in 1264; its tag is revivable. Nimscodd retook the Gnomish Pass in the 1390s with Gawedi help and has a truce with Gawed at start [wiki: Dragon Coast; wiki: Nimscodd Hierarchy; wiki: Portnamm Hierarchy; wiki: Kingdom of Iochand; eu4: history/countries/A06 - Nimscodd.txt, A13 - Gawed.txt].
- **Hierarch Mordibam Quintain** (b. 1204, in office since 1401) "takes power right after the gnomes take the Pass… Then, he just… waits", has "weirdly blue eyes", skin that looks teal and scaled in certain lights, and "talked about dragons and kobolds like he knew us personally". Kobold soldiers whisper he is **Tayekan the Blue** in gnome form; a late Kobildzan mission shows Tayekan shifting "between that of the kobold, the gnome" [eu4: localisation/Flavour_Kobildzan_Z38_l_english.yml `Z38_a_malignant_whisper_desc`, `Z38_eclipsing_a_dragon_desc`; wiki: Dragonwake].
- **Kobolds.** Three *Viraxia* by scale colour — Redscale (Levaz Sozax; herders and trap-ambushers who scream "Nimrith's battlecry"), Bluescale (Tayekar Crozamor; lore-keepers in the caverns), Greenscale (Dervaszix Korlak; sailors squatting in ruined Coddorran) — with smaller units called *trivu* and *zendia*; dragon-priests and "trapseers". The **Kobold Dragon Cult** teaches that when the world's greatest hoard is gathered in the Dragonheights (the Hoard at Soxun Kobildzex), Malliath, father of dragons, will wake. Afterlife schools: Kamaheriotza (Sky Death — ancestors as stars), Soxunheriotza (Deep Death), Axzoheriotzak (Many Deaths), Urzaxanheriotza (sea). Kobolds lay eggs, are full-grown by 10–12 (adult at 15), and fight with spike pits, "acidic landmines" and drake-fire; their villages hold barely a hundred each [wiki: Kobold; wiki: Kobold Dragon Cult; eu4: localisation/Flavour_Kobildzan_Z38_l_english.yml; eu4: localisation/Flavour_EOA_l_english.yml `flavor_empire.22`].
- **Reveria (瑞维利亚).** Reaver kingdom ("Revrlund", founded 821 by Askel Iron-Neck), still Gerudian in identity; king Lothane III síl Rever is a navigator, drunkard and embezzler. Portnamm joke: "Four Reverians walk into a bar. The bartender walks out." [wiki: Kingdom of Reveria; eu4: history/countries/A05 - Reveria.txt; eu4: localisation/Flavour_Iochand_A80_l_english.yml]
- *Aakhet the Bronze* is a Kheteratan dragon and does not belong to this region's cults [wiki: Dragonwake].

### 2.8 Verne, Pearlsedge, Businor and the Divenhal

- **Verne (弗恩).** Duchy at the south-east corner of the Dameshead, successor of the wyvern-riding Kingdom of Verne founded by Armoc Vernid. The riding secrets were destroyed by Daras III Vernid in 1207; by the Lilac Wars wyverns are near extinct in the Khenak (hunted for their venom, "the ultimate solvent of anything organic", which Hillthrone gnolls smear on weapons). Riders needed a hatchling enchanted to see them as kin; a rider who lost his nerve was eaten. Duke **Alvar IV** (b. 3 Esmarment 1411) grew up at the mountain fort Arca Lar under court mage **Tristan síl Vartras** and guard-captain **Alisa** (who cut a gnoll in two with a Wexonard greatsword to save him); he has no magic, many languages, argued as a boy against joining the war, and inherited when both Duke Arman V and his own father died beside Adénn. He was a regent of Verne from 1426 and its effective administrator through the war; Arman V's sickly son died in 1440, which made Alvar heir. Wife Ariana síl na Bronzewing (m. 1432); daughters Alara (b. 1434) and Cela (b. 1436, magically gifted) and son Dustin; a cold father. He opened Verne to Escanni refugees and will later tame the first wyvern in generations (egg found 1465; presented to his nobles in luminous bronze armour on 2 Castanmark 1468; Cela dies in the binding in 1467; the beast escapes when Tristan dies in 1474). Vernmen are hard-working, moustachioed and adventure-mad; "Vernman bronze" (which outsiders *erroneously* call red-brass) makes the best cannon; the twin river-cities are Verne and Heartspier on the Corteum. **Correction:** the Knights of the Saltmarch are *not* Verne's order in 1444 — they were founded under the old Kingdom of Verne, but their Grandmaster became the sovereign Duke of **Galéinn** (salt-mining duchy of Vernman culture under Adrien IV síl Galéinn, b. 1412; former Damerian vassal, Moon party, independent after Dameria's fall and quarrelling with Cestirande over Thanallen) [wiki: Duchy of Galéinn; eu4: history/countries/A84 - Galeinn.txt]. [wiki: Duchy of Verne; wiki: Wyvern; wiki: Alvar IV "the Shining" síl Verne; eu4: localisation/Flavour_Verne_A33_l_english.yml; eu4: localisation `A33_the_red_brass_guns_desc`].
- **Pearlsedge (珍珠边地).** Founded 879 by the reaver Henrik Divenscourge on half the Tretunic Kingdom; Verne's thousand-year rival ("a people who take for their banner the pale white of the sickbed"). Elvenised nobles under House síl na Trísfer over conservative commoners. Customs: the **life-pearl** (the first pearl you collect yourself; spouses exchange them); warriors shave their heads — "Bald Heads Glistening in the Sun"; Munas Moonsinger is their sea-god. Duke-Elector **Caylen V** (b. 1423; silver-tongued) is married to Castina Silcalas of Arbaran; son Tomás born Esmarment 1444. He will be emperor in 1470 as the "third option" [wiki: Duchy of Pearlsedge; wiki: Pearlsedger; eu4: history/countries/A11 - Pearlsedge.txt; wiki: Empire of Anbennar].
- **Busilar (布西拉尔)**, "Kingdom of the Stone Lion", capital Hapainé, House Silnara since 1224; old Kheteratan colony (Busirat), with West Sarhaly settlers who brought the games Oware and Yoté, griot music and the belief that the brave are reborn as lions. Fought for the Rose; its Lion Armada was destroyed by Verne and Eborthíl at the **Battle of the Busilari Straits** ("the Slaughter of the Lion"). King **Marcante I** is a half-elf approaching eighty, married to Cecille Siloriel; heir is grandson Ardano "the Mariner"; second son Ostán has left for Escann with the Stalwart Band [wiki: Kingdom of Busilar; wiki: List of Busilari Monarchs; wiki: Battle of Busilari Straits].
- **Eborthíl (埃博蒂尔).** Island kingdom on Tef between West and East Divenhal; plutocratic monarchy under **Delian II Silebor**, who also took the crown of Deshak in Akasik when gnolls wiped out its royal house. Moon ally and Verne's friend; aims to toll every ship "from Anbenncóst to Brasan" [wiki: Kingdom of Eborthíl; eu4: localisation/Flavour_Eborthil_A47_l_english.yml].
- **The Divenhal (迪文哈尔海).** West half stormy and raided by gnoll "Dog Corsairs" (Viakkoc) for slaves; east half mirror-calm. The Hillthrone gnoll queendom of Xanzerbexis holds the Khenak [wiki: Divenhal Sea; wiki: Businor].

### 2.9 The middle of the Empire

- **Arbaran (阿巴兰)**, the Northmarches, capital Freecestir (*cestir* = campground), mustering-ground of the Free Realms and now the refugee road from Escann. An elfrealm under Ianren the Rider until 1170, then House Silcalas (heirs of Calasandur, whose "Three Castles" include Calascandar) and its trading company. Arbarani are mixed-blood, green-eyed horsemen (Arbarani destriers; the green-plumed Knights Magnificent) who also eat horsemeat, a Crodamic relic. Marquis **Ricard I Silcalas**; Rose party; rival Damescrown, whose CLSTC branch it schemes to buy out [wiki: Marquisate of Arbaran; wiki: Arbarani; eu4: localisation/Flavour_Arbaran_A46_l_english.yml].
- **Istralore (伊斯特拉洛尔)**, capital Cilséinn; named for Istralania Warsinger, whose ship Estádha ("Lady Luck") first followed Munas; home of the Dalemines and the all-women **Istralari Banshees**. Dameria's "first and most loyal vassal" and now its self-appointed heir. Duchess **Aucanna I "Ironside" Silistra** (b. 1423) was Adénn's squire, sent home in the first months of 1443 before the spring campaign; her father Maurise III and eldest brother died defending him; her other brother Adénn Silistra is crippled (half his body crushed under his horse) and was passed over for her. Granddaughter of Ricain "the Brash"; mother Marian Dunteris is alive and counsels caution; consort Ricain Silistra (b. 1420) [wiki: Aucanna Silistra; eu4: history/countries/A45 - Istralore.txt]. Stern, rule-bound, impetuous. Her enemy is **Vincen II síl Cestir** of Cestirande, the turncoat who coined "Brokenlance" and will bait her with a broken spear [wiki: Duchy of Istralore; wiki: Aucanna Silistra; eu4: localisation/Flavour_Istralore_A45_l_english.yml].
- **Arannen (阿兰嫩)**, "Domain of the Salt Lion", eastern march in the Borders founded by Roilsardi crusaders after the White Pestilence; salt, castles, later lace. Marquis **Ostán IV** (b. 1417) succeeded Alveru II, killed at the Moon Council; the house surrendered to Lothane in 1443 while its second son Delian led the Aldresians [wiki: Marquisate of Arannen; wiki: The Borders; eu4: history/countries/A72 - Arannen.txt].
- **Esmaria (埃斯玛里亚).** River-country of the goddess Esmaryal; no overlord since the Grand Duchy died; rivers are borders and highways; nobles and commoners spend months on **river-party barges**. Esmari are "smiles and daggers" (Lothane's phrase), vain, hedonist, good at business; women wear open-shouldered gowns, men cloaks and high boots. After the war comes the **Thorned Moon Boat Festival**, a fashion "battle" where old Moon and Rose houses meet again. **Bennon (本农)** is the serious exception: hunting hills, heavy knights, a Museum of Virtue; Duke **Aldres III** fought for the Moon while his cousin Ser **Frederic síl Bennon** — Corin's instructor — fought for the Rose and died at Rottenstep. **Leslinpár (莱斯琳帕尔)**: parks, Leslinwaffles, riverboats, the board game "Cones of Leslinpár", the Day of Five Thousand Candles. **Estallen**: Duke Lucian IV is a powerful mage; his son Raubin will be a one-year emperor murdered at a Lansday feast. **Silverforge (银锻炉)**: clan-republic of the Silver Dwarves under Foldan III Silverhammer [wiki: Esmaria; wiki: Esmari; wiki: Duchy of Bennon; wiki: Duchy of Leslinpár; wiki: Empire of Anbennar; eu4: localisation/anb_lilac_wars_l_english.yml `lilac_wars.42`; eu4: history/countries].
- **Elfrealms.** Moonhaven (月港; elector; Prince Varion "the Young" Bluetongue, b. 1203; owns Port Munas; staunchly Damerian), isolationist royal **Ibevar** (Ibenion II ta'Lunateín; sided with the Rose) [wiki: Elfrealm of Moonhaven; wiki: Elfrealm of Ibevar; eu4: history/countries].

### 2.10 Magic at court

Magic is a legal qualification for the throne and a household office: by imperial law each noble house hosts a magister. The mod models three "mage organisations" — **Magisterium** (most imperial princes, plus Wex, Verne, Anbenncóst), autonomous **Guilds** (Lorent, the Wine Lords) and **State** mages (Gawed's "Cobalt Circle"; also Nimscodd). Orda Aldresia is its own tag (A77: Moon-flagged, capital province Rotwall, ruler Delian síl Arannen since 1400) with a fourth, unique organisation, `estate_mages_organization_orda_aldresia` [eu4: localisation/anb_estate_mages_l_english.yml; eu4: history/countries; eu4: history/countries/A77 - Orda Aldresia.txt]. Schools: the Imperial Academy, the Silmuna School of the Arts (founded 1024; "Freeform Style", wands and rings) and the Konwell Institute; a graduate traditionally had to add something new to magic [wiki: Cannorian Magical Education; wiki: Silmuna School of the Arts]. Enchantment is the feared school (Riannón; Ettalinde the Wretched's mind-controlled massacre at Esmarwic). Gawedi distrust magic outright. Squires of the Aldresians and Magisterium apprentices brawl with "fireballs, gunshots, and a dozen dead peasants" [wiki: Kingdom of Esmaria; wiki: Gawedi; eu4: localisation/Flavour_EOA_l_english.yml `flavor_empire.6`]. Damestear — meteoric "raw magic" that falls in Tearfall — is the strategic reagent [eu4: localisation `A85_access_to_damestear_desc`; wiki: Calendar].

### 2.11 The Regent Court (摄政王庭) as lived religion

A pantheon organised like a feudal court: Castellos is king of the gods and mortal kings rule as his regents. People keep one patron and honour the rest. Core court: Castellos (silver dragon with sceptre), the Dame (moon and stars; harvest, magic, knowledge), Esmaryal (hearth), Falah (hunt), Nerat (ruby scales; death, law, medicine), Adean (lance in a circle; chivalry), Ryala (love), Balgar the Builder, Ara (coin and horseshoe), Munas Moonsinger (travel, music; deified 1015), Nathalyne (thieves), Begga Goodfingers (charity; a Beepeck beggar-girl born 1216), Minara (half-eaten apple; joy and lust). Minor figures include Belouina and Dercanos, twin yeomen of the royal cellars, and Wip, god of jesters [wiki: Regent Court]. **Corin is not yet a goddess in Nerament 1444**: she is canonised by the tenth Pantheonic Council in 1445 [wiki: Regent Court].

Regional texture: Damerians and mages follow the Dame (Temple of the Highest Moon on Moonmount; ranks Librant, Luminary, High Luminary); Lencenor is Adean's country (Adeanscour, Highcour in Lorentainé, Vinterscour in Wineport; the Spearway of spear-shrines) with Minara's head temple, "the Bosom", at Minar, to which Sorncóst ships its finest grapes each year under a mage's preservation; Esmari honour Esmaryal, Ryala and Minara; Ryalan priestesses officiate noble weddings across the Empire, and Ryala's Festival of Vows and Beauty Celebration fill the calendar; Beepeck is Ara's and Begga's [wiki: The Dame; wiki: Adean; wiki: Minaran Temple; wiki: Kingdom of Lorent; eu4: localisation/anb_cannor_religious_flavour_l_english.yml; eu4: localisation/Flavour_EOA_l_english.yml `flavor_empire.27–28`].

### 2.12 Adventurers and sellswords

The war left "a generation's worth of sellswords"; the Marcher Lords in Escann absorb some, the rest turn bandit [wiki: Lothane III síl Wex]. Anbenncóst is the retirement home of famous adventurers and the headquarters of mercenary captains; Forgeway sells "the finest weapons that crowns can buy" [wiki: Anbenncóst; eu4: localisation/Flavour_Anbenncost_A38_l_english.yml]. The mod's adventurer regimes are **Chronicler Grants** (the Order of Chroniclers in Anbenncóst's Temple District pays for first-hand information; Anbenncóst and the Magisterium start with it), **Licensed Adventurers**, and unregulated **Land of Adventure**; its "Adventurers Wanted!" incidents include thieves' guilds with calling cards [eu4: localisation/anb_estates_l_english.yml `estate_adventurers_organization_*`; eu4: localisation/anb_estate_organization_l_english.yml; eu4: localisation/anb_adventurers_wanted_l_english.yml; wiki: Order of Chroniclers]. Named companies of the moment: Sons of Dameria, Small Fellows, Sword Covenant, Stalwart Band, Verne's Brave Brothers, the Cobalt Company, the Black Hounds, Esmari bandit-companies under landless cadets [wiki: Rogier the Exiled; wiki: Duchy of Verne; wiki: Esmari; wiki: Cobalt Company].

## 4. Nations & factions as playable sides

- **The Wexonard court** — wants a hereditary-in-practice Dove Throne, loyal electors and Dameria digested. Hates Istralore, Moonhaven, Gawed; distrusts Lorent.
- **Lorent's crown** — wants obedient Wine Lords, the Winebay, all halflings and a second try at the Empire. Hates Gawed, Deranne, any Silmuna revival. Internal split: gentle Kylian against hard Ruben; crown against the Seventy Chalices.
- **The Moon remnant** — Istralore, Moonhaven, Silverforge, Verne, Bennon, Arannen, Eborthíl, the Aldresians, the Varivari elves, the Sons of Dameria. Want vengeance, restoration, or simply dignity. Fault-line: which heir — Rogier, Laurens of Wesdam, or Istralore itself.
- **The pragmatic Rose princes** — Pearlsedge, Damescrown, Beepeck, Arbaran, Cestirande: rewarded, nervous, already hedging against Wex.
- **The Magisterium vs Orda Aldresia** — Vinerick politickers against Silgarion traditionalists; humiliated knights guarding the man who killed their prince.
- **Gawed** — a reforming boy-king against his Great Lords, with the Magnates as a rising third force; outwardly peaceful, with claims on Vertesk, Damescrown and the Small Country.
- **Halflings** — Beepeck's selfish elector, partitioned Viswall's underground, loyalist Appleton and Pearview, Smallknight's veterans.
- **Nimscodd and the kobold clans** — reconquest against survival; either can be the sympathetic side.
- **Free Anbenncóst** — militias, guilds, the Bilge and refugees between a Wexonard garrison across the isle and the mages to the north.
- **Southern sea powers** — Verne and Eborthíl against Pearlsedge and Busilar; gnoll corsairs against everyone.

## 5. Plot threads with canon basis

1. **The lie in the throne room.** Lothane did not beat Adénn; Erlan stabbed him and Lothane murdered Erlan. Divination exposes it in 1468 — a party could find the truth earlier [wiki: Adénn Skylance; wiki: Lothane III síl Wex].
2. **Three heirs to the Moon.** Rogier with a bounty and a half-orc heir; a six-year-old raised by the people who betrayed his kin; Aucanna, who thinks Istralore is the true heir. Later, a Damerian tries to kill Laurens while he tours Lorent "for his family's betrayal"; the trauma drives his lifelong work of reconciling Dameria's successors (and the Sons of Dameria), which the wiki says plants the seeds of the Shadowmoon Conspiracy "generations later" [wiki: Empire of Anbennar; eu4: anb_lilac_wars `lilac_wars.32`, `.33`].
3. **Rose and Dove.** The Wex–Lorent alliance rests on one cold marriage and one hidden bastard; if it breaks, Lorent marches on the West Dameshead and the Emperor answers with a painting of the Lorentish king strangled by thorns [eu4: Flavour_Lorent `flavor_lorent.5–15`].
4. **The Eagle looms.** Gawed's king wants peace; his lords and Magnates may not. Canon end-states range from the Burning of the Redglades (1514) to a bloodied eagle delivered to Gaweton [wiki: Kingdom of Lorent; eu4: Flavour_Gawed `flavor_gawed.24–34`].
5. **Electoral Transfer Wars.** Lothane tries to move the elven vote from Moonhaven to Ibevar and the eastern vote from Istralore to Cestirande — a constitutional coup the party can fight in the Parliament and the field [eu4: Flavour_Wex `flavor_wex.4–7`].
6. **The dragon in the Hierarch's chair.** Is Mordibam Quintain Tayekan the Blue, steering both gnomes and kobolds? The Hoard, Malliath's awakening and the Dragon Coast war hang on it [eu4: Flavour_Kobildzan; wiki: Dragonwake].
7. **The Crown that thinks.** Ioriel Redrose's mind sleeps in the Ruby Crown and can take the wearer [eu4: Flavour_Lorent `flavor_lorent.1`; wiki: List of Lorentish monarchs (Ruben VIII)].
8. **Free halflings.** Viswall's agents "bide their time" after 1423; Beepeck sabotages rivals; a famine lever over all Cannor [wiki: Free City of Viswall; eu4: anb_lilac_wars `lilac_wars.48`].
9. **The broken peace-keepers.** The Aldresian schism (Sword Covenant), an imperial-bodyguard plot to kill Lothane, the Mages' Strife, Kantin's peasant rising in Arbaran [wiki: Empire of Anbennar; wiki: Delian síl Arannen].
10. **Wings over the Khenak.** Verne's hunt for the last wyvern eggs, with gnolls, alchemists and Pearlsedge in the way [eu4: Flavour_Verne; wiki: Wyvern].

## 8. Uncertainties and source conflicts

- **Date of the fall of Anbenncóst.** 11 Silversight 1443 in [wiki: Invasion of the Damesear; wiki: Adénn Skylance] and EU4 (`death_date = 1443.4.11`; `flavor_wex.1` "in 1443"). But [wiki: Lothane III síl Wex] and [wiki: Anbenncóst] say the 1444 campaign season. **Followed EU4: 1443.** Sack length: three days (wiki) vs "several months" of looting (EU4) — reconcilable as a formal sack plus a long occupation.
- **Lothane's election:** 1422 (emperor list, EU4 flag) vs 1423 [wiki: Timeline]. **Vincen's death:** 17 Teysuren 1421 vs 1422 in the Lilac Wars chronology.
- **War dates:** Second Lilac War ends 1394 (Treaty of Beepeck) in its own article, 1400 in the Lilac Wars lede and Delian's page; Third begins 1407 or 1409. [wiki: Empire of Anbennar] carries an older outline (Reán "slain in battle" 1364; Third from 1400) that the dedicated war pages supersede.
- **Lorentish succession:** wiki has Kylian VI king 1422–1449, heir Ruben VI b. 1426; EU4 dates the monarch block 1442.3.17 and calls the heir "Ruben VII" b. 1427. I followed the wiki for reign dates and treat the numeral as unsettled. Eilísa is called both Laurens IV's daughter and Kylian VI's sister (consistent); her EU4 death date is 1447, while the wiki has her killed at Lothane's thirtieth-year celebration (c. 1452).
- **Gawed:** EU4 has Welyam III reigning from 1435; the wiki's 1443 accession is detailed and consistent with Rottenstep, so I followed it. The Viswall Rebellion infobox names "James III Gerwick" and Kylian VI as leaders in 1421–23, contradicting the king lists (Edmund II; Laurens IV).
- **Rubenaire:** wiki says it defected to Lorent in 1430; EU4 flags Rubenaire and Eilísin as Moon and Madelaire as Rose at start. Treat the Wine Lords as defeated ex-Moon houses of doubtful loyalty.
- **Deranne/Wine Lords surrender:** 1439 vs 1441. **Gnomish Pass:** 1396 [wiki] vs 1399 [eu4]. **Deshak union:** 1430 [wiki] vs gnoll invasion of 1442 [eu4].
- **Names:** Marquis of Arannen is Ostán IV in EU4, "Austen/Austyn IV" on the wiki; Alvar IV's son Dustin is b. 1430 (EU4) or 1439 (wiki); Aucan II/III Silistra numbering varies; Anbenncóst's leader is Lord-Mayor Awen of Earshore in EU4 but "Steward Andras" in the wiki infobox (era unclear).
- **Anachronism risks:** the "Whiterose Court" of Adean and Minara is a post-Corinite development; the Order of Chroniclers' independence and the printing boom are later, though EU4 gives Anbenncóst "Chronicler Grants" at start; Halesstown, half-orcs and kobold migrants in Anbenncóst are later events; the wyvern "revival" is two events, not a conflict — Alvar IV tames a single wyvern (1468–1474), and Alvar V (grandson of Alvar IV's son Dustin VI) re-establishes the wyvern-riders in 1504 [wiki: Alvar IV "the Shining" síl Verne; wiki: Duchy of Verne]. In Nerament 1444 nobody in Verne rides a wyvern.
- **Wars of Rule dates:** 1253–1284 [wiki: Wars of Rule; wiki: Timeline] vs 1244–1284 / "41 years from 1244" [wiki: Mages' Peace; wiki: Empire of Anbennar]. The war's own page, with its dated list of Mage Emperors (Mathos the Idle 1240–1253), is the more detailed; **followed 1253**.
- **Marion I Silmuna-Wesdam:** listed among the Rose dead *at* the Battle of the Moon Council [wiki: Battle of the Moon Council] but also said to "die of horrible sicknesses" with Laurens IV later in 1422 [wiki: Lilac Wars]. Unresolved; safest wording is "dead by the end of 1422".
- **Who stood in the throne room:** [wiki: Adénn Skylance] has Aldresian knights "led by their Grandmaster" dying with Adénn; [wiki: Delian síl Arannen] has Grandmaster Delian sent away beforehand to raise a relief force, surviving to submit in 1444. **Followed Delian's page** (EU4 has Delian síl Arannen, b. 1378, as ruler of the Orda Aldresia tag from 1400 and alive at the start [eu4: history/countries/A77 - Orda Aldresia.txt]).
- **Rogier's knighting:** [wiki: Rogier the Exiled] says he squired for "Alvar IV, the Duke of Verne" and was knighted by him in 1427 — but Alvar (b. 1411, five years Rogier's junior) only became duke in 1443 and was a regent from 1426. Don't lean on that detail.
- **Other EU4/wiki mismatches:** Aucanna's accession 1436 (EU4) vs 1443 (wiki); Marcante I b. 1366 (EU4) vs 1367 (wiki), reign from 1367 (EU4 placeholder) vs 1389 (wiki); Silverhall "destroyed during the Sack of Anbenncóst of 1444" [wiki: Anbenncóst] repeats the 1443/1444 split noted above; the Third Lilac War's Betrayal of Wesdam is 1417 on most pages but "almost immediately" after a 1409 start on Delian's page.
- **Electors:** besides the seven, the EU4 file `B99 - Aranthil.txt` also carries `elector = yes`; I did not establish what that tag is at start and list only the seven the wiki names.
- **Thin or missing pages:** Fourth Lilac War sub-sections are placeholders; no page for Erlan Silmuna, the Black Hounds, the Damish Temple's organisation or the Cobalt Circle (the last is only a code comment). Moon Council location and Reveria's "Rose" flag are not explained in prose.
- **Not verified here:** who holds which exact provinces on Damesear in the 1444 setup (province history files were not in the corpus); the Mordibam–Tayekan identity is presented in the mod as rumour, never confirmed in the text I read.

### B. Errors corrected in the text

1. **Jon Bardswood did not design the Moon Council ambush** (section 2.1 and the character table called him its designer/architect). [wiki: Jon Bardswood] says he planned only for Lothane to contest the election, that the massacre "was not what Jon Bardswood had intended by any means" and "horrified and enraged" him; [wiki: Lilac Wars] names Laurens IV and Marion of Wesdam as the alleged orchestrators. Rewritten in both places.
2. **Wars of Rule dated 1244–1284.** The war's own page and the Timeline give 1253–1284 (the 1244 figure survives on two other pages). Changed to 1253 and the conflict recorded in section 8.
3. **Knights of the Saltmarch given to Verne.** They are Galéinn's order — their Grandmaster became the Duke of Galéinn [wiki: Duchy of Galéinn]. Removed from Verne; short Galéinn note added.
4. **Invented quotation** "this silence will last a while", attributed to `lilac_wars.22`. The event does not contain it. Replaced with the event's actual words.
5. **Reánna III "died childless"** — she had a bastard, Emile; the sources say "without trueborn children". Reworded; Kylian V's death (1378) and her husband added.
6. **Damescrown's electoral constituency** is "the merchants, as well as the nobles of the Damescrown region", not "the northern cities". Fixed.
7. **Ruben and the wine barrels** — he humiliated Wineport's Council of Seventy Chalices, not "the Wine Lords" (who are the dukes of Rubenaire, Eilísin and Madelaire). Fixed in the character table.
8. **Anbenncóst's renaming** — the fleet landed in 1000 AA, but the name replaced Damucen only by the end of the War of the Sorcerer-King. Reworded.
9. **"Its knightly order is dead"** (overview) — the mod says "the majority" of the Knights of the Rose died. Softened to match section 2.4.
10. **"Vernman bronze (red-brass)"** — the mod says it is "erroneously called Red-Brass". Reworded.
11. **Shadowmoon Conspiracy "grows from Laurens's guilt"** — the source ties it to the assassination attempt on Laurens and his later reconciliation work. Reworded.
12. **Wyvern revival listed as a source conflict** — it is not: Alvar IV tamed one wyvern (1468–74), Alvar V restored the riders in 1504. Section 8 corrected.
13. **Citation path** for the adventurer organisations: the strings are in `anb_estates_l_english.yml`, not `anb_estate_organization_l_english.yml`. Added.
