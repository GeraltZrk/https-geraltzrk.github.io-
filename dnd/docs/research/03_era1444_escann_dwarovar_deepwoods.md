# 03 — Escann, the Greentide, the Dwarovar, the Deepwoods and the North at the EU4 start (11 Nerament 1444 AA) and in the century after

Scope: eastern Cannor the morning after Corin and Korgus Dookanson killed each other in Castonath; the mountains the orcs came out of; the forest they got lost in; and the frozen north that had its own, greyer invasion.

## 1. Overview

For four centuries Escann (东坎诺) was "Chivalric Escann": the successor kingdoms of fallen Castanor settled quarrels by joust and champion under the Trial by Chivalry, refereed by the Order of the Sapphire Eye from Sapphirewatch on Lake Silvermere [wiki: Chivalric Escann; wiki: Order of the Sapphire Eye]. In 1424 the last western dwarf hold, Khugdihr, fell to Gurukk Siegebreaker, and through its broken gate Korgus Dookanson (科尔古斯·杜坎森) led the united orc clans of the Serpentspine (蛇脊山脉) onto the surface "in their millions" [eu4: localisation/anb_dwarovar_startup_l_english.yml; eu4: localisation/anb_startup_screen_l_english.yml `string_start_escann_adventurer`]. Twenty years later every Escanni kingdom but Marrhold was gone. The west, busy with the Lilac Wars, looked east only in 1443; the volunteers who marched were called the Marcher Lords. They fought the Greentide (绿潮) to a bloody halt at Rottenstep (4 Yshdament 1443), a dead squire named Corin (科琳) got up from the mud as the avatar of the disgraced war-god Agrados, and on 11 Nerament 1444, in Lower Castonath, she killed Korgus and died of it [wiki: Battle of Rottenstep; wiki: Corin (person); eu4: localisation/anb_age_of_greentide_l_english.yml `greentide.0`].

The EU4 mod starts the next morning. Nobody rules Escann. Eighteen adventurer companies (by the mod's count of company tags) camp in ruins; ten orc clans and seven freed goblin tribes hold what they took; one human king sits in a dwarf hold with griffons; a league of surviving counts holds Oldhaven. Behind them the Serpentspine is suddenly half empty, and dwarves whose clans have lived among humans for generations are raising loans to go home. In the Deepwoods (深林) the magic wall that trapped everything inside for fourteen centuries fell about a year ago, in 1443, when Corin killed the Eldest [wiki: Verdant Veil; wiki: The Eldest]. In the north the grey-skinned orcs who refused Korgus rule a human kingdom and have a half-orc heir.

Everything that follows — the Corinite faith, the adventurer kingdoms, the witch-kings, the reclaimed holds, orcish kingdoms with human subjects — grows out of this one morning. The wiki calls it the "1444 Divergence": after this date the canon history is one timeline among those the games can produce [wiki: Countries after the 1444 Divergence; wiki: Timeline].

## 2. Facts by sub-topic

### 2.1 Orcs before Korgus, and what "Dookanson" means

- **Origin.** Orcs appeared in 6000 BA in the fourth level of the dwarf hold Hul-Jorkad ("Jorkad Hall", whence "orc"), made by the Precursor elf general Ducaniel from goblins, ogres and his own remaining elven soldiers while dwarven armies besieged him. They slaughtered the garrison and spread; orcish religion remembers Ducaniel as the chained creator **Dookan** [wiki: Orc; wiki: Hold of Hul-Jorkad; wiki: Pre-Orcish Dwarovar].
- **Old Dookan vs Great Dookan.** *Old Dookan*: the dwarves imprisoned our god; we exist to free him and punish them. *Great Dookan* (Korgus's creed): Dookan fought for Castellos in the Godswar, was cheated of a homeland for his people and jailed by *all* the gods, so the Regent Court and every work of Balgar the Builder must be destroyed. Symbol: a chained fist. Holy ground: any battlefield, and Jorkad Hall. No afterlife — the "orcish soul" reincarnates if you die well; tricksters, cowards and suicides are unmade. Shamans work "spirit magic" (divination/enchantment) and read omens by self-mutilation [wiki: Great Dookan; wiki: Old Dookan; eu4: localisation/anb_events_dookan_l_english.yml]. Orcs call Dookan's prison *Dookan's Cage*; it is the dwarves who call it the Heart of Doom [wiki: Great Dookan]. The doctrine that worthy non-orcs and half-orcs can carry an "orcish soul" was introduced late in the Greentide by the shaman Dhuram Clouded Eye [wiki: Great Dookan].
- **Dookan's Call.** A recessive magical failsafe that makes some orcs hear a voice, see visions and gain huge strength; the wiki treats this as the mechanism behind "Dookansons" [wiki: Orc].
- **The Seven Trials of the Dookanson** (commonly accepted version): draw the blood of the Sporeweaver in its sanctum; slay a Great Foe; pry the secrets from Zokkorna, Eldest of the Deepcrows, and keep your mind; forge unity beyond one tribe; destroy those whose faith faltered; descend to commune with the orcish ancestors; enter Hul-Jorkad and return with the Mark of Dookan. Korgus is the only orc ever to complete all seven [wiki: Trials of the Dookanson; wiki: Korgus Dookanson].
- **The Age of Two.** By the late 1300s the western Serpentspine was split between two orc powers, each with a Dookanson claimant: **Skullhowl**, a shaman-state ruled by a lich called the Voice of Dookan from Verkal Kozenad, and **Graycrown**, a state of smiths and disciplined soldiers whose champion was Dugorik Dragonbreaker. A clanless orc, Korgus, was recognised by the blind seer-chieftain **Grama Clouded Eye**, completed the trials, shattered the Voice's skull with his hands, beat Dugorik in a duel that was also a catechism ("What is the ultimate purpose of every orc?") and took both hosts [wiki: Orc]. Clans that refused him — under Zurtin Stonejaw and Brasûr Frozenmaw — were exiled north and became the Gray Orcs (2.9) [wiki: Orc; wiki: Great Clan of Frozenmaw].
- **Korgus himself.** Born 12 Truefrost 1400 by the mod; no weapon but his fists; sigil a scarred, aging orc with bound fists. Zealous, charismatic, clever; dogmatic, ruthless, incapable of stopping. He wanted to save "the orcish people, not his people" [eu4: history B01; wiki: Korgus Dookanson]. Mod epigraph, the "Castellyr Address": "The strong take from the weak. I have slain armies, destroyed kingdoms… we will destroy all the kingdoms of men and free Dookan from his prison!" [eu4: localisation/anb_startup_screen_l_english.yml `string_start_greentide_clans`].
- **Goblins.** When the clans mustered, cave goblins either fled (the Exodus Goblins who poured into Bahar/Bulwar) or were dragged along as slaves and arrow-fodder [eu4: localisation/anb_dwarovar_startup_l_english.yml `string_start_cave_goblin_description`; wiki: Goblin].
- **Orc customs writers can use.** *Ozarm'Chadash*, the duel for honour by which anyone may take anything, including a chieftaincy; loser leaves or dies, "orcish history has no interest in losers". Pillage is honest, guile is not ("an orc owns only what he can fight for"). Those who die out of battle are cowards, fed to pigs. Scars are extended into ritual patterns. Bone trophies; bones were once currency. Wargs are the mount [wiki: Orc; eu4: localisation/Group_Flavour_Orcs_l_english.yml]. Until Korgus died orc skin changed colour within days to match terrain (black underground, green in Escann, grey in the north); the ability vanished from every orc in the world at the moment of his death [wiki: Orc; wiki: Green Orc].

### 2.2 The Greentide, 1424–1444

1. **1424 — Khugdihr falls**, "the Wolf Gate", home of the Agate Dwarves and birthplace of Balgar the Builder; refugees scatter over Cannor [wiki: Dwarven Holds; wiki: Hold of Khugdihr]. It was a nine-year siege, not a storm: Korgus camped at the gate in 1415, handed command to Gurukk around 1420 and went back into the depths to raise more warbands; Gurukk held a third of the hold by 1423 and broke it in 1424, earned the name "Siegebreaker" from Korgus, and sent carts of dead dwarves through Escann, hauled by captives, with the message "This is simply a prelude for what is to come. For you, your realms, and your gods." [wiki: Hold of Khugdihr]. The human orc-hunter Gerin Orcrend, arm broken, watches Gurukk's assault from the caverns [wiki: Gerin Orcrend]. The tide first "emerged" on a 4 Silversight, year not stated [eu4: localisation/anb_powers_and_ideas_l_english.yml `B30_cult_of_korgus_desc`].
2. **Castellyr first.** The kingdom that held Castonath and the White Walls collapsed almost at once — "its white walls worthless against this threat from a new direction" [eu4: localisation/anb_startup_screen_l_english.yml]. The Balgarsmiths scuttled the Dragonforge rather than let Korgus use it [wiki: Hammerhome]. Eswall burned in 1424, orphaning the small child Corina [wiki: Corin (person)].
3. **The Blademarches** were "one of the last countries to fall" [wiki: Blademarches] (the mod's clan start text narrates their fall straight after Castellyr's, so do not hang a precise order on it): at Bladeskeep the orc warchief Varok brought his hammer down on the king's raised sword **Calindal, the Gleaming Blade**; both sword and king shattered "in a blinding burst of light" and Korgus renamed the clan Bladebreaker [eu4: localisation/anb_powers_and_ideas_l_english.yml `B25_breakers_of_calindal_desc`; wiki: Blademarches]. Background: the kingdom had been refounded after 1021 by Clarimonde of Oldhaven, the squire who found the lost Calindal on Trialmount and killed the Sorcerer-King with it; after the Greentide the Order of the Blade Stewards recovered the shards and reforged the sword in secret [wiki: Blademarches; wiki: Stalbór].
4. **Adenica**, "birthplace of knights", was broken after the Rotcleaver shaman Zien Steelshaper fixed a curved blade and hammer to a pole to reach men on horseback [eu4: same file `B23_architects_of_the_adenner_destruction_desc`].
5. **Marrhold** evacuated its valleys into its hold; **Corvuria** held at Arca Corvur (1439) and Bal Dostan with night raids by its Black Knights [wiki: Kingdom of Marrhold; wiki: Asra Expedition; wiki: Battle of Rottenstep].
6. **1439 — the lost army.** A host under Troknar Silentblade tried to turn Bal Dostan through the Deepwoods and vanished behind the Verdant Veil (2.8) [wiki: Greentide].
7. **Farraneán**, the half-elven kingdom, fell last (it and Adenica "still stood" in 1439 and collapsed between about 1440 and 1443 [wiki: Asra Expedition]); by 1443 the Vanguard could only break out through Gawed's unfortified border along the Oudescker [wiki: Battle of Rottenstep].

Consequences inside Escann: temples razed in a deliberate erasure of the Regent Court; famine because orcs knew nothing of surface farming; survivors enslaved by orcs or used as "fodder" by the last kingdoms; a generation of half-orcs born from 1424 onward [wiki: Greentide; wiki: Half-orc].

### 2.3 Rottenstep — "the Last Battle of Chivalry" (Sorrowday, 4 Yshdament 1443)

- **Who.** Crown Prince Jon Gerwick of Gawed (his lords refused to muster for "a piffling thing in Escann", so he paid from the family purse) and Ricard I Silcalas, Marquis of Arbaran; about 30,000 against about 60,000 [wiki: Battle of Rottenstep].
- **Ground.** A dry, muddy bed of the Oudescker near Mossford, its west bank a stair of rotted fallen trees. Chosen by the halfling Dustin Smallknight and the gnome engineer Pondo Fussbolt: stakes and buried pots in the mud, scaffolds for Damerian longbows and dwarven guns, a prefabricated gnomish bridge for the cavalry [same].
- **The break.** After two hours of failed charges Korgus feigned retreat while Grama Clouded Eye's shamans cast as one. Twelve knights of the Orda Aldresia were enchanted into believing the orcs had routed and charged; the wall opened; "one army of 30,000 became twenty armies each doing different things". Ricard formed a shield-ring; Galinda síl na Eán pulled her knights (and her niece) back; the mage-knight Laurens síl Place went berserk; Captain Hector of Alenfield of the Cobalt Company died covering his men; Rogier Silmuna's Sons of Dameria saved the left [same].
- **The charge.** It was Frederic síl Bennon who, after a hurried word with Prince Jon and the knight-commanders, ordered the mounted reserve to remount and cross the bridge, reasoning that killing the now-visible Dookanson would still shatter the tide. The Lorentish knights struck first; Prince Jon was pulled from his horse and beheaded by Korgus, and the Gawedi royal sword **Talon** was lost. Frederic and his brother Aldres ("the Bennon Brothers") hit second and were separated in the press. Grom Dogeater on his warg Karlur killed the Lorentish commander Leon síl Toarnaire, struck down Frederic síl Bennon and cut through the shield of his squire Corin; the dying Frederic killed Grom [same; eu4: localisation/anb_powers_and_ideas_l_english.yml `B28_bared_fangs_desc`]. Three of "Korgus' Ten" — Brasul, Snafu, Drosh — died [wiki: Battle of Rottenstep].
- **After.** Korgus withdrew to Castonath. That night Ghurad Heartgrinder wept in front of the commanders, accused Korgus of feeling nothing, lost an Ozarm'Chadash to him, limped out alive and marched his clan south "swearing that not one more of his clan would be slain for the cause of the Greentide" [eu4: same file `B21_ghurads_rottenstep_renunciation_desc`]. King Edmund II "the Ill" died on hearing of his son's death; the crown passed to Jon's 16-year-old son Welyam III (Edmund's grandson; spelled "Weylam" on the battle page), who refused to pay his father's mercenaries [wiki: Battle of Rottenstep; wiki: Cobalt Company]. The Marcher army split into companies: some to finish Korgus ("Marcher Lords" proper), some simply to loot [wiki: Marcher Lords].

### 2.4 Corin, her Circle, and Castonath

- **Corin.** Born Corina, 23 Bloomsend 1420, in the forest of Canreced by Eswall; her mother is thought to have been a hidden priestess of Agrados. Adopted by the wandering knight Frederic síl Bennon, renamed Corin and raised as his "bastard son" so she could squire; timid off the field. Red-haired. Died at Rottenstep; about three hours later a column like "a blade of red flames" lifted her body, black mist rose from the closing wounds, the frogs and the approaching ghouls went silent. She said she had marched through a void among ghostly soldiers and that **Nerat**, god of death, gave her Agrados's power to save Cannor and redeem him. Powers: wounds close in moments, inhuman strength, an aura of hope [wiki: Corin (person); eu4: localisation/anb_startup_screen_l_english.yml].
- **The road.** The stragglers' **Squire's Alliance** tried to rush Castonath, lost on the north shore of Silvermere and fled across the lake behind orc lines [wiki: Marcher Lords]. The Neratic priest Dominic of Gallowspeak stalked her for days and stabbed for the heart; the wound closed while he watched (that is the wiki's telling; in the mod's own event Corin's instincts hold off his surprise attack, he joins the Circle at sword-point still meaning to kill her, and is only won over by knowing her — see section 8). They hauled the half-orc Lothane out of an execution pit with a pulley rigged by the halfling Winston Foolfoot. At Oldhaven the elder lords of the **Count's League** imprisoned Lothane for his birth (the wiki has them trying to arrest and execute Carleon as well); the younger counts under Carleon Blacktower couped them with Corin's help [wiki: Corin (person); eu4: localisation/Flavour_CountsLeague_B55_l_english.yml `cl_bonds_bluetusk_desc`].
- **The Veil.** Seeking the fey, Corin crashed the Conclave of Elynáile, was nearly arrested, and with the exiled wood-elf mage Arun Drennen and the goblin warlock Oubblig killed **the Eldest**, the feral husk of the archfey who had raised the Verdant Veil (翠绿帷幕) within days of the Day of Ashen Skies — the year-zero of the BA/AA calendar, some fourteen centuries earlier — and had held it up ever since. The kill is dated 1443. Arun died buying Corin her opening, and Oubblig was flung into the Feyrealm and lost to the party (his own story continues in 1434; see section 3). Wood elves and the Groundhawk goblins followed her out [wiki: Corin (person); wiki: The Eldest; wiki: Verdant Veil].
- **Castonath.** A two-front convergence — Marcher Lords from the west, Corin and Lothane from the south — and a week of street fighting. In Lower Castonath Korgus downed Lothane; Corin stepped between with her shield, took Korgus's off-hand blow, and ran him through with her longsword. The gnome Iacobb Creekwhistle's rifles and portable cannon had cleared her path; he watched through a sniper's eye [wiki: Corin (person); wiki: Castonath; eu4: localisation/corins_circle_l_english.yml `corins_circle.8.d`]. Orcs call the day Dookansfall, humans Corinsfall, goblins Chainbreak [eu4: localisation/anb_age_of_greentide_l_english.yml `greentide.0`, `.1`, `.6`].
- **Deification.** The Tenth Pantheonic Council (1445), organised by Dominic "out of a collection of adventurer-clerics", made her goddess of war in Agrados's seat [wiki: Pantheonic Councils; eu4: localisation/corins_circle_l_english.yml `corins_circle.12.d`].

### 2.5 "Adventurers" — what the word means here

- **A social fact.** Parties form guilds, guilds answer to companies; "it's not about who you are but what you've achieved". After Rottenstep the Lilac Wars' unemployed — knights without lords, Moon Party exiles, mercenaries nobody paid — are the only organised humans in Escann [eu4: localisation/anb_adventurers_l_english.yml `promote_adventurer_unity_desc`; eu4: localisation/anb_adventurer_missions_l_english.yml].
- **A government.** A company is led by a Captain, has no dynasty, may not declare war without cause, cannot make ordinary alliances and instead joins defensive "federations" of at most three; its war goal is the "Reclamation of Escann". It camps on "tribal land" and settles outward [eu4: localisation/anb_adventurers_l_english.yml]. Its internal parties are the **Marchers** (边区派: warriors and the vengeful, "the defeat of all orckind"), **Pioneers** (先锋派: settlers and returning Escanni) and **Fortune-Seekers** (淘金者: merchants and opportunists) [eu4: localisation/anb_factions_l_english.yml]. The generic mission tree is a recipe for becoming a state: restore the farmlands, forge your own swords, mint "Our Own Crowns", open a tax office, urbanise the capital; the "supply train" of hunters, whores and returned refugees becomes the citizenry [eu4: localisation/anb_adventurer_missions_l_english.yml].
- **"Adventurers Wanted!"** Literally a crier's call ("Tellumite criers sounded the call: adventurers wanted for a second Asra Expedition!" [wiki: Asra Expedition]) and the mod's name for a notice-board problem in a province: Thieves' Guild, Bandits (up to a "Bandit King Fortress"), Werewolves, Trolls ("Troll Country"), Goblins, Haunted House ("House of Evil"), Ghouls, Orcs (raiding party → warband → migratory clan), Wyverns, Satyrs ("A Constant Party"), Wendigo, Zombies, Vampires, Eldritch Cultists ("Town of Mysteries"), Hags. Each festers through three tiers unless adventurers — better with "Generous Quest Rewards" — deal with it; a monster that crosses a border is a casus belli for "Monster Attack Reparations"; a tolerant realm can turn an encounter into resident citizens; sometimes the hired party "ran away with the stolen goods" [eu4: localisation/anb_adventurers_wanted_l_english.yml].

### 2.7 Castonath, Castanor, Castellos and the Trials; Marrhold

- **Castonath** (卡斯托纳斯), "Castan's Town by the River": carved stone in imitation of a dwarf hold by Balgar the Builder from 636 BA. *North Castonath*: the Imperial Quarter and its **Charred Tower** (blackened by the dragon Zaamalot), the Upper District of cliffside patrician manors, **Pantheonway**. *South Castonath*: **Dragonforge Hill** (a foundry shaped like a dragon's head, fed by Trialmount's magma), the five-storey tenements of **the Crags**, **Legion Square**. *Lower Castonath*: the slum below the **Silverfall** ("Shitsfall"), the **Old Bazaar**. Lower Castonath is where Corin and Korgus died; the wiki names the district only, not the exact spot, so placing the duel in the Old Bazaar itself would be invention. Underneath, Balgar's unmapped **Underpass**. Its **Patricians**, a "quasi-mercantile, quasi-noble" caste of Castanorian descent, outlast every ruler [wiki: Castonath; eu4: localisation/anb_patricians_estate_l_english.yml `estate_castonath_patricians_desc`]. Orcs treated it as the first "hold" they kept rather than looted [eu4: localisation/EscanniOrcsandGoblinsevents_l_english.yml `escanntribal.3`].
- **Castanor** (卡斯塔诺): humanity's first empire (from 639 BA), the common tongue, the Legions (I Dragonflame, founded by Castan the Progenitor, onward), the White Walls ringing Inner Castanor. Its emperors were all "Castan"; it burned the Oldwoods and the fey World Tree at Oldhaven; it ended as "Black Castanor" under the Gerudian Ebonfrost dynasty and the Sorcerer-King Nichmer (dead 1021) [wiki: Castanor; wiki: Castanorian Legions; wiki: White Walls of Castanor; wiki: Ashentree Fey].
- **The Trials of Castan (卡斯坦试炼).** Anyone could climb Trialmount from the North Citadel; each emperor added a test: shout so the garrison answers (Castan XIII the Quiet); draw Castanor's borders "as they are, and then as they SHALL BE" on a cold stone map (Castan V the Great); forge a gift for the dwarves (III Dwarf-friend); swear at the **Oathstone**; add a stone to the "crown of mankind" (IV Realmbuilder); reconcile two forest spirits (XIV); befriend a wyvern (XXXVI Dragonfriend); jump around a rock ten times (XXII the Eccentric); disarm an immortal golem (VI Giantsbane); hunt a beast and craft from it (II Beastbane). Oath: "I pledge to defend my land and my people to the end of my days, but will not bring unjust strife unto the world…". The Ebonfrosts made them hereditary; the battle that killed Nichmer drained the mountain. In the mod a restored Castanor revives them, and failures "do not always return alive" [wiki: Trials of Castan; eu4: localisation/Flavour_Castanor_B32_l_english.yml `castanortrials.12.d`].
- **Castellos**, head of the Regent Court, patron of Castanor, whose avatar was the Silver Dragon. The Mural of Castellar (published 1518) shows him dying to blunt the Day of Ashen Skies; the Eleventh Council (1520) names his son Adean successor; the Corintar proclaim Corin instead (1519) [wiki: Castellos; wiki: Escann].
- **Marrhold** (马尔霍德; dwarven *Ânumdihr*, "Lonely Door"): an Alenic tribe that trekked east in the Dragonwake, moved into an abandoned dwarf hold whose tunnel to the Dwarovar had been collapsed, and tamed griffons (Ulric "the Griffon Prince", son of King Marr). Short, stocky, long-haired "like a griffon's mane"; shepherds and miners in wool. In the Greentide: "Not my problem." King **Gryf VII "the Old"** (b. 1375, craven) sat in the hold; Prince **Gryf "the Young"** (b. 1424) flew the Order of the Griffon with Corin to Castonath and lost many riders. Their first missions are *Revenge* and *Justice* against an orc warlord wearing a cloak of griffon feathers. The only kingdom left, they despise "greenskin barbarians" and "upstart adventurers" alike [wiki: Kingdom of Marrhold; wiki: Marrodic; eu4: history B36; eu4: localisation/Flavour_Marrhold_B36_l_english.yml].

### 2.8 The Deepwoods

- **Place.** What Castanor's Second Great Cleansing left of the continent-wide Oldwoods. Nine groves: Mountain, Lake (Silent Lake), Bone (bone-rich soil, "Bone-hoarders", apparitions), Thorn (closest to the Feyrealm), Hunter's (megafauna the size of hovels), Arrow (limestone caverns), Flower (tropical, nearly everything poisonous), River (gentle, the only silver), Shadow (black-leaved canopy, home of the Ashentree fey). Travel only by roads (*Vyrs*) or the four *Gladeways* through the Feyrealm; step off and you may come out in Yanshen, or seventy-five years ago [wiki: Deepwoods; wiki: Verdant Veil].
- **Wood elves.** A Moon Elf army under Liandiel Speardancer retreating from the Battle of Hornwood (1017) crossed the Veil; a thousand years passed inside for four hundred outside. Regiments became clans (*salla*) led by a *ceann/ceanna*; you give your salla before your name; lifespan about 250 years. Their "Fey Court" is etiquette, not worship: Seelie (joy, cruelty with a smile) and Unseelie (discipline, terror of being forgotten) [wiki: Wood Elf; wiki: Fey Court]. Curse: "Ashen boughs!" [wiki: Ashentree Fey].
- **The Whirlwind (*Thyr Gaethneán*), 1428–1444.** Raiders in 1428; the Bloody Tusks in 1433 (who fled into the trees and came out 75 years earlier); then in autumn 1439 the host of **Blackmoon, Cursed Howl and Silentblade**, lured in by an Ashentree archfey, the **Prince of the Blackened Branch**, glamoured as an orc under the name Drakmar Mancleaver [wiki: Silentblade Clan]. "Oberon's Flight" — the stag-archer Oberon Fealgarn baiting the horde from grove to grove; the sack of Imyhain; Gordak killing his own chief Bolk in disgust; the Blackened Prince dominating Troknar's mind and delivering Salla Ghúl to slaughter. Three sallas were broken: Ghúl was all but annihilated in the Shadow Grove when the Ashentree fey turned on it, while Scotha and Ahain rejected the Pact and had their remnant armies scattered, enslaved (by Cursed Howl in the Flower Grove) or bound to the exile Selussa Ghúl; all three exist as tags in the mod but have no ruler in 1444 [eu4: history I31, I32, I34]. It was the Seelie archfey the **Prince of Fools** (Oubblig's patron) who turned Troknar's intended duel with Gordak into a revel and spirited Cursed Howl away. **Narawen Drennen** called the Conclave of Elynáile in 1442; after more than a year of argument the **Verdant Pact** of Myna, Elyn, Cenág, Drennen and Fealgarn was the result [wiki: Greentide Invasion of Deepwoods; wiki: Verdant Pact]. For the orcs inside, "only mere five years had passed" outside but "decades" within [eu4: localisation/anb_age_of_greentide_l_english.yml `greentide.4`].
- **1444 sides** [eu4: history I26–I44, U12, B82]. *Elves:* Salla Elyn (艾琳氏族; Cinrthir, b. 1243, at Elynáile), Myna (Bricena, at the mountain-woven citadel Ciranmyna), Drennen (Narawen, b. 1410), Cenág (Alara; Bone Grove gallows humour), Fealgarn (Oberon, b. 1302), Ayeth (Irvah, broken by his consort's death). *Orcs:* Blackmoon (Relgakk, who has ordered every goblin killed on sight), Cursed Howl (honour-bound Gordak, Flower Grove), Silentblade (Troknar, a puppet in the Shadow Grove; son Ruuk has fled), Beasteater (Vord), Sapchopper (Khagor), Blightseed (Bosk; the clan was founded during Oberon's Flight by the shaman Og Blightseed, whose roots snared the stag-archers), Silvertusk (Kharag, formerly of the Bloody Tusks). *Forest goblins* (yellow-eyed, older than the elves, heirs of a vanished "Nameless Kingdom"): Brambleskinner (Ghuk), Oubbligschild (Nerlisk pobnek-Oubblig — but note its 1444 capital in the history file is Aldwigard in Ourdia, *outside* the forest: this is the part of Oubblig's band that slipped through the weakened Veil around 1434–35 and fell on Ourdia during the Decades of Devastation, even though the goblin start text still counts it as holding part of "his diminished realm" [eu4: history U12; wiki: Verdant Veil; wiki: Oubblig; wiki: Decades of Devastation]), Spiked Log (Cleesb, sheltering runaway slaves), Verminhusk (Carg, steered by the exiled elf mage Selussa Ghúl), Petalmasher (Vox) [eu4: localisation/anb_startup_screen_l_english.yml; wiki: The Nameless Kingdom]. Goblins split between Pactseekers (fey warlocks) and "the Shaman Old Ways".
- **Mod storylines.** *Cyranvar* (西兰瓦尔) — the Pact's clans elect a ruler and unify, later opening "Seasonal Gates" to another forest. *The Emerald Rise / War of the Black Trees* — the Ashentree Pact, who want humanity punished for the World Tree, turn Silentblade into bark-skinned **Emerald Orcs** (Tugund-Darakh, "clan of a thousand trees") about seventy years after 1439; elves and orcs must ally to stop them. *The World Tree* can be regrown — or burned again. Orcs may drift from Dookan to **Bulgu Orazan**, ancestor-songs of heroes like Og Beardbreaker and Grima the Shadow. Flavour: the lake-race that crowns the year's **Moonsinger** with moon lilies; the goblins' once-in-a-lifetime **Yellow Lights Festival** of firefly lanterns; the orc matron Boar-Wife's vision brew [eu4: localisation/anb_deepwoods_l_english.yml; eu4: localisation/anb_age_of_greentide_l_english.yml `greentide.5`; wiki: Silentblade Clan; wiki: Cyranvar].

### 2.9 Gerudia and the Reach

- **Shape.** North of the Giant's Grave Sea: the Dalr valley and the Olavish fjords (Gerudians, the Skaldhyrric Faith); south of it the Alenic Reach of small rich cities; beyond, troll country and "Far Gerudia" [wiki: Gerudia].
- **Giants and trolls.** Frost giants under King Gerud ruled an ice age from palaces of pure ice until the last dragons melted them (the sea is their grave). Jormir Once-Thawed and his fratricide brother Kranmas revived the realm (570–357 BA); Castan VI Giantsbane exterminated them and chained Kranmas to rebuild Bal Vroren. Their servants remain: **Obrtrol**, the fjord-troll chiefdom under matriarch **Svala "the Crusher" Hardrock** in the giants' fortress Murtrhol, which enslaves captives in mountain mines; forest trolls beyond Titan's Rest (Digoveda). Trolls regenerate, settle disputes by *Zeinbrött* (first to rip off the other's arm; the arm is handed back as courtesy) and are raised by a "den mother" [wiki: Giants; wiki: Kingdom of Bjarnrík; wiki: Obrtrol; wiki: Troll; eu4: history Z17].
- **Faith.** Skald-priests recite magical Tales; the greatest duty is the Old Winter Lullaby that keeps the dragon **Elkaesal the White** asleep (she — the wiki's faith page and the mod's Vængheim tales make the dragon female — wrecked Gerudia in the Era of Frost, 470–491, and was sung down by the One-Armed Skald). Temples keep public libraries; literacy is prestige. Seat: Skaldol/Skaldskola [wiki: Skaldhyrric Faith].
- **Bjarnrík** (比亚恩国), "the Bear of the North": first Gerudian kingdom, founded by the warrior-queen Bera with southern loot on a southern model. Konungr **Sven II Bjarnsson** (b. 1405), queen Grethe Sidaett, heir Lars; restive vassals Sidaett and the reaver-port Revrhavn (Jansen the Tall), which still raids though the crown has abolished reaving. Missions: End the Troll Menace, Reunion of the Bears, "Thaw the Maw", reclaim the reaver kingdoms of the Era of Black Ice (768–978) in Reveria, Deranne, Pearlsedge [eu4: history Z08, Z10; eu4: localisation/anb_startup_screen_l_english.yml `string_start_Bjarnrik`; eu4: localisation/Flavour_Bjarnrik_Z08_l_english.yml; wiki: Reavers].
- **Urviksten** (乌尔维克斯滕): cradle of the Ebonfrosts, the reaver house that seized Castanor and made the Trials hereditary. Jarl **Karl II Ebonfrost** (b. 1392; strict, cruel), heir Gunnar; black-sailed fleets, "Pale Banners", black pines, the giants' forge, ebonsteel. Beat Naugsvol in a war just before the Graytide; missions run from *Conquer Olavlund* to *Surmount the Trialmount* and *Resurgence of Castanor* [eu4: history Z15; eu4: localisation/anb_startup_screen_l_english.yml `string_start_Urviksten`; eu4: localisation/Flavour_Urviksten_Z15_l_english.yml; wiki: Jarldom of Urviksten].
- **The Graytide.** **Brasûr Frozenmaw** (b. 1399), whose father Korgus killed, led the exiled Old-Dookan clans out of the Northern Pass, took **Bal Vroren** by surprise in 1420, subdued Vrorenmarch lord by lord, was crowned its king in 1428 and married the last princess, **Frida Vrorensson**, a devotee of Esmaryal who preaches under his roof. Their half-orc son **Marosh** (b. 1428, kind-hearted, a cleric) fought in Corin's Circle and stays with the Corintar as a convert. Brasûr fights green orcs as heretics (in 1442 he even marched into Escann with human retainers hoping to kill Korgus himself and avenge his father, but Corin got there first [wiki: Kingdom of Grombar]) and means his orcs to build cities "without any reliance on pillaged dwarven tools". The ice-sword Shaman-u-Yakh is **not** in his hand in 1444: it belongs to a later mod event chain in which a veiled woman calling herself the Ice Smith forges it from a claw of the dragon Elkaesal and vanishes, leaving robes full of snow [eu4: localisation/Flavour_Frozenmaw_Z18_l_english.yml `flavor_frozenmaw.21`–`.22`]. Reachmen plot around the Lady Magda and wait for **Crovis Adshaw**, Frida's nephew, to land and restore the kingdom. In wiki canon he does invade, in the 1450s, and is cheered as a saviour at first; then his unpaid mercenaries sack the very human towns that opened their gates, the Reach lords send their militia to Brasûr, and the joint orc-human army destroys him at the castle of Eskerborg — the war that reconciles the two peoples. Brasûr reigns as Gray King 1428–1469; Marosh 1469–1481 (he visits Ionntrás in disguise in 1475 and wins a public alliance with Lothane and the Corintar, is killed in an Ozarm'Chadash by the orc Thrur, and is succeeded by his son Gunk when the human and half-orc lords refuse to accept the duel) [wiki: Kingdom of Grombar]. Rival grey clans: Broken Jaw (Jayuk), Doombringer (Mogdur). Successor state: **Grombar** [wiki: Kingdom of Grombar; eu4: history Z18; eu4: localisation/Flavour_Frozenmaw_Z18_l_english.yml; eu4: localisation/anb_age_of_greentide_l_english.yml `greentide.2`].
- **The Reach.** **Celmaldor**, an elven principality (Prince-elect Taelar Icewind, b. 1103) holding the March of **Serpentgard**, guard of the Frozen Pass; merchant republics Bayvic (Valeran Crownswright), Coldmarket, Everwharf, Reachspier, Dinesck, Envermarck; and **Adshaw**, a fallen Old Alenic kingdom under **Rycan IV, "the Viper of Rycastle"**, who has seeded the coast with spies to avenge the Blue Reachmen Wars of Independence [eu4: history Z21–Z33; eu4: localisation/anb_startup_screen_l_english.yml `string_start_adshaw`; wiki: Alenic Reach]. Also the harpy matriarchy **Vængheim** (Reginleif Vebjornsdottir) [eu4: history Z88].

### 2.10 The Dwarovar

- **Aul-Dwarov** (众矮人之国), "All-Dwarves-Home": one empire from Escann to Yanshen under a High King at **Amldihr** wearing the seven-gemmed **Dwarovkron**, advised by the assembly of hold-kings, the **Amlharaz**; fed from the terrace farms of the Serpent's Vale. Each hold had a trade: Er-Natvir rails and post; Mithradhûm mithril; Verkal Gulan gold and the mint; Orlghelovar glass; Shazstundihr marble; Ovdal Kanzad cannon; Hul-az-Krakazol ale; Dûr-Vazhatun astronomy; Hul-Jorkad plumbing and lead; Khugdihr bookkeeping. Holds are cities stacked in levels; none ever dug below the fourth [wiki: Aul-Dwarov; wiki: Dwarven Holds; wiki: Hold of Amldihr].
- **The rail (Dwarovrod).** Rails of constantly electrified **dagrite**, a self-repairing metal, with mobile rail-foundries; the main line ran 83 segments from Khugdihr to Verkal Dromak, with branches (Dagrinrod north, Argrod through the Serpentreach, Natvirod to Marrhold, Seghrod). Er-Natvir once delivered a siege order "three minutes before noon" with "not one grain of wheat missing" [wiki: Dwarovar Railway; wiki: Hold of Er-Natvir].
- **The fall.** Orcs out of Hul-Jorkad (6000 BA) took Er-Natvir (5934 BA) and Amldihr (5888 BA); the rail was declared defunct in 5876 BA and with it communication, armies and food — the origin of dwarven isolationism. King Murnir IX "the Vengeful" collapsed **Ovdal Asra** on himself and the orcs to shield Khugdihr. The "Last Days of the Dwarovar" lasted six thousand years [wiki: Black Orc; wiki: Dwarovar Railway; wiki: Hold of Khugdihr; wiki: Last Days of Dwarovar]. Later lights: the **Segbandal** alliance of the Middle Dwarovar under Seghdihr (hero: Krummul Orcslayer, with the bone greatsword *Skycleaver*) and the eastern **Jade Empire** of Grônstunad, broken by hobgoblins [wiki: Krummul Orcslayer; wiki: Dwarven Holds].
- **1444: three kinds of dwarf.**
  1. **Remnant holds** — awake behind sealed gates, "novel research replaced by endless debates", forbidden to step into the dark; the mod's epigraph is Arg-Ôrdstun law: "No Diamond Dwarf, in any part large enough to bleed… may leave the Hold." They must complete a "Remnant Awakening" before they can expand [eu4: localisation/anb_dwarovar_startup_l_english.yml]. *Krakdhûmvror* (冰霜锻炉; quartz dwarves, descendants of exiles and criminals, motto "Skill over Blood", ice-steel; First Councillor Garlim Frozenforge); *Arg-Ôrdstun* (diamond; Queen Ardeginn XXII Whiteforged, "sinner, reformist"); *Ovdal Lodhum* (爱之居所, "the Hold where Love Lives": wine, poetry, midwives, paired glow-gems — ruled since 1430 by the **human** Gerin Orcrend, with dwarf queen Dwalra Garnetpick and adopted heir Balgar); *Seghdihr* (citrine; Storkhumlir XVIII az-Segh, b. 1282); *Ovdal Kanzad* (火炮城, the Cannonhold; amber dwarves; Kragarun LXIII Powderkeg, b. 1262, drunkard); *Verkal Gulan* (金堡; Protector Urothr of the Gate) [eu4: history H76–H79, I02, F47; wiki pages of each hold; wiki: Gerin Orcrend].
  2. **Adventurer expeditions** — surface dwarves of Silverforge and Rubyhold. *Second Asra Expedition* (阿斯拉探险队; Jarmun Asra, chartered by the Asra Bank: "profits must take precedence over prestige or any idea of 'Dwarven glory'"; found only a goblin tribe in Khugdihr in early 1444; refounds it in 1475 under Queen Erin Asra); *Company of Duran Blueshield* (contract signed in a halfling's house in Thomsbridge with relatives, adventurers and a wizard); *Ruby Company* (Kûrgûm Gemseeker, hunting the Ruby of the Dwarovkron); *Forgemaster Union* (Khuznoli Hammerfall and one shard of mithril); *Blackbeard Cartel* (Dwosgraeg, who "had never felt so alive" as in a shield-wall in the dark); later *Began's Expedition*, *Mithril-Arm Cartel*, *Axebellow Cartel* (Amethyst dwarves who lost the key to Hul-az-Krakazol while drunk) and the *Company of the Grudgebearers* (justice-for-hire from Rubyhold, joined by Belgrak Cavedweller of Corin's Circle; its 1450 expedition was "ill-fated" and Belgrak was never heard from again) [eu4: localisation/anb_diggy_diggy_hole_l_english.yml `diggy_spawnable.*`; wiki: Asra Expedition; wiki: Corin's Circle; eu4: history H68–H71, H95–H98]. A "Dwarovar Claimer" surge lasts until 1494 — the canon window of the land-rush [eu4: localisation/anb_dwarovar_startup_l_english.yml].
  3. **Squatters and warbands.** *Shattered Crown* orcs in Amldihr (Ghonor Bordu), Old Dookanists who call Korgus "the false prophet who blamed the humans for the sins of the dwarves"; *Siegebreaker* (Gurukk, breaker of Khugdihr; rumoured to have been sent back by Korgus for reinforcements); *Roadwarrior* (Skoth "Chiefcleaver"); *Masked Butcher* (Vrirrig Beardbutcher); *Shadowdreamer* (Zog, on a "dream crusade" toward Gor Ozumbrog); *Skewered Drake* (Gashak Gorrath). The **Darkscale kobolds** (Zorën), who ruled Khugdihr around the hoard of the dragon Nimrith the Red until adventurers killed him in 1103; the cave trolls of **Grizehûd** under young Lagok "Friend-Seeker"; the Mire Maw ogres of the flooded Serpent's Vale [eu4: history H72–H93, Z55, M01, J32; eu4: localisation Flavour_ShatteredCrown_H72, Flavour_Mountainshark_H84; eu4: localisation/anb_startup_screen_l_english.yml `string_start_darkscale`, `string_start_grizehud`].

Down there: seven holds around the capital Hargûn Ruzazir, joined by magma canals crossed in adamantine barges whose blood-runes are fed by slaves; a council of clans Shadowhammer (return and revenge) and Darkstone (conquer the depths), the priesthood, the runesmiths and five Legions. Neighbours: pale **Deep Goblins** of ruined Deepcities, **minotaurs** in labyrinth-citadels, **Kantharos** fungus-folk who pilot dead **mantle whales** as town-ships; mantle serpents; zirakant. Decaying mantle beasts exhale **Halanna's Breath**, the gas that lets glowing **Serpentbloom** grow through the whole Dwarovar. In the mod a hold that digs through a "strange calcite layer" of unknown runes triggers the **Obsidian Invasion**; in wiki canon the Obsidian Legion first surfaced at Verkal Skomdihr [wiki: Pre-Orcish Dwarovar; wiki: Obsidian Empire; wiki: Primeval Serpentdepths; wiki: Obsidian Legion; eu4: localisation/anb_diggy_diggy_hole_l_english.yml `diggy_fallen.*`].
- **Hul-Jorkad.** Lead dwarves, aqueducts, bathhouses, a lake; young warriors hunted sewer-spiders as a rite. Briefly retaken by the Segbandal (4520 BA), lost again in 2681 BA to a warhost "looking for their lost god". Orcs' holiest site and the seventh Trial; in the mod a refoundable hold and a target of the Skewered Drake [wiki: Hold of Hul-Jorkad; wiki: Krummul Orcslayer; eu4: localisation/Flavour_Skewered_Drake_H73_l_english.yml].

## 4. Nations & Factions (candidate playable sides)

- **The Corintar** — finish Corin's work without becoming what it fought; torn between Marcher vengeance and Lothane's mercy. Later: abolition of orc slavery, an oath of "righteous violence", crusades. Enemies: Bladebreakers, slavers, eventually Esthíl [eu4: localisation/Flavour_Corintar_B02_l_english.yml].
- **An adventurer company** — wants land, pay, a name; hates whoever exiled it (Cobalt→Gawed; Sons of Dameria→Wex; Raven Banner→Corvuria's "true rulers"; Ashen Rose→Lorent; Iron Sceptre→the Magisterium; Sword Covenant→the Aldresian council).
- **Legitimists** (Count's League, Marrhold, Farrani heirs) — to them adventurers are squatters in someone's house.
- **An orc clan, three roads** — coexist (Heartgrinder→Barumand); follow the stronger god (Arosha, Unguldavor; "For Corin!" [eu4: localisation/Group_Flavour_Orcs_l_english.yml `orc_flavor.7`]); or a second Greentide (Gurzan; Khozrugan's "Second Dookanson").
- **Freed goblins** — never a chain again; orcs want them back, humans want them gone.
- **A remnant hold** vs **a dwarven expedition** — the same dream from opposite ends: ancestral right versus bank charter. **Squatters** (Shattered Crown, Railskulker, Darkscale) are the people who actually live there now.
- **Verdant Pact elves**, **Deepwoods orc clans**, **forest goblins**; behind them the **Ashentree Pact**.
- **Bjarnrík**, **Urviksten**, **Frozenmaw/Grombar**, **Reachmen restorationists**, **Obrtrol**.
- Late antagonists: **Obsidian Legion**, **Emerald Orcs**, **Black Demesne**, a restored (or Black) **Castanor**.

## 5. Plot Threads with canon basis

1. **The morning after.** A week of street fighting has just ended; two demigods lie dead in Lower Castonath; the Patricians are counting who is left; and the armies that won are about to become rivals. Who holds Castonath? (The Pioneers do not retake South Castonath until 1479.) [wiki: Castonath; wiki: Pioneer's Guild]
2. **Lothane's inheritance.** A half-orc Silmuna leading a holy order, courted by the Count's League, opposed by Marchers, writing to an uncle who wants Dameria back, and to Arosha, whose alliance bloc will fight his. Offered a crown and refusing it [wiki: Lothane Bluetusk; wiki: Corin's Circle].
3. **The second Greentide that almost was.** Gurzan Korgusson gathers chiefs each 4 Silversight; Aâdga's "might makes right"; Dûsh's nihilism; against them Ghurad, Arosha, and adventurers who cannot agree [eu4 ideas `B30_cult_of_korgus_desc`; wiki: Escann].
4. **Is she a god or a devil's avatar?** Agrados is a disgraced god; Dominic thought her infernal; the Tenth Council is contested; in 1518 Castellos is revealed dead and Escann finally has an explanation for its suffering. The seed of every later religious war [wiki: Corin (person); wiki: Escann; wiki: Castellos].
5. **Who may climb Trialmount?** Counts, adventurers, an Ebonfrost jarl from the north, an orc king — anyone who takes the North Citadel can attempt ten ancient trials. A king who failed (Elecast of the Blade, 660) lost his life and his kingdom's sword on the mountain [wiki: Trials of Castan; wiki: Blademarches; eu4: Flavour_Urviksten_Z15 "Surmount the Trialmount"].
6. **The race for the Dwarovar (1444–1494).** Bank-chartered expeditions, awakening remnants, orc and goblin squatters, seven lost gems, a dead railway, and something that dug upward "from the other side". The Asra charter's clause against "Dwarven glory" versus the men who signed it [wiki: Asra Expedition; eu4: anb_great_projects `dd_wyltramvar_desc`].
7. **Dig too deep.** The calcite layer, the Runefather's legions, blood-runes, magma barges — a civil war of dwarves six thousand years deferred, with a real argument about who betrayed whom [wiki: Pre-Orcish Dwarovar; eu4: `diggy_fallen.*`].
8. **The forest's long game.** The Verdant Pact's fragile unity; orc clans stranded for subjective decades; goblins freeing slaves; and an archfey wearing a chieftain like a glove, breeding Emerald Orcs to avenge a tree burned in 580 BA. Time travel is canon here [wiki: Greentide Invasion of Deepwoods; wiki: Silentblade Clan].
9. **The Gray King's house.** An orc conqueror who hates Korgus, a captive queen who converts his court, a half-orc heir who prayed beside Corin, a human pretender landing to cheering crowds (whose mercenaries, in wiki canon, end by sacking the towns that cheered them and are crushed at Eskerborg in the 1450s), and an Ebonfrost watching from the north [eu4: Flavour_Frozenmaw_Z18; wiki: Kingdom of Grombar].
10. **Good done by evil means.** Laurens síl Place's mind-shredding, Varina's skeleton labour, Maugunia's "servants", Lucian's "immortality", orc slaves down the Alen to Vertesk — the adventurer age curdling into the Age of Witch-Kings [eu4: corins_circle `.2`, `.13`; wiki: Corintar Order].

## 6. Everyday & sensory detail

- **Rottenstep the morning after:** the riverbed runs again, with blood; flies, corvids and vultures; priests burying, ghouls and necromancers "prowling the riverbed the coming years". The poem *Weeping Oudescker*: "Oh river, humble river, who are you weeping for?" [wiki: Battle of Rottenstep]

- **The north:** wind "tearing the snow" off the mountains; the creak of longships when the sea freezes; forges burning day and night at Urviksten; a child handed a beet-roll in a dockside crowd and lifted onto the pretender Crovis's horse; Reachmen's "intimate tavern social circles" closed to their orc rulers; the poem *The Eagle and the Orc* [eu4: localisation/anb_startup_screen_l_english.yml; eu4: localisation/Flavour_Frozenmaw_Z18_l_english.yml; wiki: The Eagle and the Orc].

## 8. Uncertainties and source conflicts

1. **Day of the duel.** `greentide.0` and [wiki: Corin (person)] say 11 Nerament 1444; the startup text in the same mod says "yesterday, the 10th of Nerament" (the game opens on the 11th); [wiki: Corin's Circle] says 11 Truefrost. I followed 11 Nerament for the deaths; whether the player's first morning is the 11th or 12th is open.
2. **Dugorik Dragonbreaker.** [wiki: Orc] makes him Korgus's defeated rival-turned-follower; [wiki: Gerin Orcrend] and [wiki: Gem of Asra] have Gerin kill him in Ovdal Asra in 1410; [wiki: Korgus Dookanson] (developer notes) calls him Korgus's blood brother. Unreconciled; no mod text settles it.
3. **Rottenstep losses:** the battle page gives ~10,000 and ~18,000; [wiki: Escann] says "upwards to thirty thousand souls". Both pages call it nominal victory/stalemate.
4. **Who holds Castonath on day one** is not stated in the files available (the corpus has no province history). The Greentide tag's capital is North Castonath, but that tag is not a starting country; treat the city as contested ruin.
5. **Spellings:** Jergan (history file) / Jergen (intro event) / Jherg (wiki) of Corveld; Daemir (mod) / Daenmir (wiki) Brighteyes; Zorën (history) / Zorn (startup text) Darkscale; Khagor (mod) / Khagur (wiki) Sapchopper; Vloki Halvtarsson (mod) / Halftansson (wiki); "Iron Hammers" in game text though the file is named "Silver Hammers". I used the mod's in-game forms.
6. **Iacobb Creekwhistle's unit:** "Sparkdrive Rifles" [wiki] vs "Stormwater Rifles" [eu4]. **Laurens síl Place:** a Tellumite Orda Aldresia mage-knight of the "Order of the Hallowed Will" [eu4; wiki: Corin's Circle, marked deprecated] vs "last surviving Knight Brother of the Sapphire Eye" [wiki: Sword Covenant]. Followed the mod.
7. **Sword Covenant camp:** Victor's Path in the mod, Carlanhal on the wiki; Raven Banner's intro speaks of settling Vernham, which is the Stalwart Band's capital.
8. **Graytide dates:** wiki — Bal Vroren taken 1420, coronation 1428; the mod's event says the capital fell "overnight" in 1420 but Brasûr's reign is entered from 1414 and Marosh born 1428. Urviksten's prior war is "Black Pine war… 1410" in the mod text and "Black Frost War (1417–1420)" on the wiki. The Orcish Kingdom of Vrorenmarch ends in 1483 [wiki: Great Clan of Frozenmaw] or 1505 [wiki: Kingdom of Grombar].
9. **Castellos revelation:** mural "leaked" 1511 and council "convened in 1518" [wiki: Escann] vs published 1518, council 1520 [wiki: Castellos; wiki: Timeline]. Shadowmoon Conspiracy: "mid 1500s" vs 1573.
10. **Everything after 1444 is one timeline.** The post-1444 dates in 2.11 are the wiki's "Jahanic" canon; the mod lets any of them fail to happen. Many wiki sections for this region are explicit stubs ("Chronicler's note… being reorganized"), notably the fall-of-Escann chronology and the Battle of Castonath, which has no page of its own.
11. **Company camps:** the "camp" column is each tag's capital in the mod's history file; the Company of the Thorn (Tederfremh) and Freeflower Vanguard (Freetower) have low-numbered capitals that may lie in their homelands rather than Escann, and the wiki places both in "Western Castanor". Unverified without the map data.
12. **Not verified here:** the internal layout of Marrhold hold; Korgus's "Ten" beyond the three named dead and Aâdga, Varok and Grom; the names of the Greentide-era kings of Castellyr, Adenica and the Blademarches (none found in the files read).
13. **How Dominic was won over** (added by the fact-check). [wiki: Corin (person)] has him stab her and watch the wound close; [eu4: localisation/corins_circle_l_english.yml `corins_circle.5.d`] has her hold off the ambush, take him into the Circle "at sword-point", and has him convinced only gradually ("she laid bare her soul"). Pick one; do not blend.
14. **Cobalt captaincy and Gunther's age.** Hector of Alenfield is "Captain of the Cobalt Company" on [wiki: Battle of Rottenstep]; [wiki: Cobalt Company] says the band was "formed by Captain Gunther of Mossford" and makes him 72 in 1470 (born c. 1398); the mod gives 1387.
