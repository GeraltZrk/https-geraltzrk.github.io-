## 1. Overview

Bulwar (布勒瓦尔) — in its own tongue Surakeš, "Land of the Sun" — is the river-plain subcontinent that bridges Cannor, the Salahad and Rahen. Cannorians named it after one city, the seat of the old Castanorian "Bulwar Accord" [wiki: Bulwar]. It is the oldest human civilisation on Halann and has almost never ruled itself: genies, God-kings, chariot nomads, harpies, gnolls, Castanor, Dameria, Kheterata, a bronze dragon, and since 1001 AA the Sun Elves have all sat on it [eu4: localisation/anb_startup_screen_l_english.yml].

In November 1444 the elven order is visibly dying. The six Sun Elf "Phoenix Estates" that inherited Jaher's Phoenix Empire (凤凰帝国) have spent three centuries on the Cinder Wars and shimmering palaces; drought (1413–18), a queen-killing war (1417–20), human revolts, a goblin exodus out of the Serpentspine and two gnoll warlords have gutted them. In the west of the plain Tluukt "Cleaver-of-Realms" holds the Šad Sur and the walls-less city of Bulwar; in the east Zokka "Devourer-of-Suns" has maimed the king of Sareyand, killed the last elven king of Azka-Sur and takes tribute from the Sun Cult's oldest temple. Beyond him, in the Far Salahad, waits the Desert Legion of Jaddar Jexiszuir, 283 years old, alleged son of the Burned Empress, prophet of a creed — the Jadd (贾德教) — that says any creature, gnoll and harpy included, can carry Surael's Light [wiki: Age of Cinders; wiki: Jaddar; eu4: history/countries/F46 - Jaddari.txt].

West across the desert, Kheterata (赫特拉塔), "the First Kingdom of Humanity", shattered in 1439 when the gnoll Grizaka "Empress-of-Gnollkind" took the gold city of Golkora. Its cat-headed demigods, the Khet, lounge on pleasure barges while the high priest of Nirat rebels, a bronze dragon resurfaces among the southern generals, corsair gnolls enslave Akasik and Sun Elves from Elizna walk into the port of Koroshesh [wiki: Kingdom of Kheterata; eu4: localisation/anb_startup_screen_l_english.yml].

The century that follows is the "Age of the Dancing Sun": Jaddar kills Zokka in single combat, converts his gnolls, swallows the elven kingdoms after the Tragedy of Samartal (1491), and proclaims the Jadd Empire (贾德帝国) in 1562, dying in 1563. On the Sorrow the gnolls themselves become the Stewards and, eventually, Khetists (Konolkhatep 哥诺勒哈特普). In Bahar the goblins of Marblehead end as the cosmopolitan Aqatbar Overclan (大氏族) [wiki: Jadd Empire; wiki: Pyreclaw Gnoll; wiki: Aqatbar Overclan].

## 2. Facts by sub-topic

### 2.1 Land and peoples of Bulwar

- Five regions: Bahar (巴哈尔; wooded north-west, the only timber of the east Divenhal), the Harpy Hills (鹰身女妖山; Šad Našratu, from the Šad Kuz to Lake Jorkad), Bulwar Proper (the Suran plain from Brasan to Sareyand plus the Šad Sur, "Mountains of the Sun"), Far Bulwar (upper Suran, Lake Naza) and the Far Salahad (远地萨拉哈德) [wiki: Bulwar].
- Two rivers, Suran and Buranun, are in myth Surael's wives; they join at Medurubar. The old canals linking them at Bulwar city are derelict. Since the Day of Ashen Skies half the Drolas peninsula and the Kuzaram lowlands lie under the "Gulf/Bay of Glass" [wiki: Bulwar; wiki: Bulwar Proper; wiki: Paradise Garden].
- Gates: the Ourdian pass to Cannor, Invader's Pass and Harpy Pass to the Forbidden Plains, the Davarhal pass over the Paravimvata range to Rahen [wiki: Bulwar].
- Human peoples: Bahari; Gelkar hill-shepherds (female-led after millennia beside harpies); Kuzarami (who still honour the moon-goddess Kuza); Brasanni of the delta city-states; Zanites (扎尼特) of the middle Suran and Naza — the prestige court culture, players of Mussakal (polo); Surani of the pious upper Suran; Šadnatu mountaineers; Mašnsih desert nomads; Maqeti freedmen on the Gulf of Rahen; Mišari half-elves [wiki: Bulwari; wiki: Zanite].
- Nobility (Barkabti) has no titles and no legal privilege; old houses carry names in szal-/szel- ("blood of"), e.g. szal-Fazil. Elven landlords are the Ralkadezim, "King's Companions", whose estates are worked by paid farmers and who ride as heavy cavalry [wiki: Bulwari; wiki: Phoenix Estate].
- Trade: Raheni dyes and Halessi porcelain westward through Brasan ("Port of Emperors") and Anzabad; silk (the secret stolen by Zanbari spies), cotton, Gelkar wool, Šad Sur gold and copper, incense from Eduz-Vacyn made in monasteries where old Sun Elves retire, coffee from Azka-Sur and Kufu; red Azanerdu glass of Kalib, kasiyaru spice of Zanšap, the wines Jaherian Sunlight (Varamhar) and Azkapaš (Re'uyel) [wiki: Bulwar].

### 2.3 The Sun Cult: doctrine, clergy, practice

- Name: Sar-Arukanaš, "Devotion to the Light". Henotheist and dualist: Surael (Bulwari: Surakel; "Zurel" to the Mengi) is the only living god; the Malevolent Dark is "the voice in the Dark". Their war is the Struggle [wiki: Bulwari Sun Cult; wiki: Surael].
- Six universal tenets: serve only Surakel, all other gods are dead; banish the darkness from your heart; keep the light of your children; speak no lies; do not abandon hope, the sun will come again; let shine the light of joy [wiki: Bulwari Sun Cult].
- Ilu ("divinity") lives in every thinking being as inner Light and grows or shrinks with conduct; one who lets it gutter becomes an Ilugalu, a "divineless demon". Evil is conduct, never race — the hinge on which the Jadd turns [wiki: Bulwari Sun Cult].
- Order = civilisation = gardening. Bulwar is Kirimahsur, the Garden of Surael; outside is the Dark's domain. Every city keeps walled Paradise Gardens; every house a family plot [wiki: Kirimahsur; wiki: Paradise Garden].
- Gumar ("Chosen/exalted"): once any pious person; since the Council of Brasan (1201) the Sun Elves as a people, heirs to Jaher's mission — the New Sun Cult (新日崇拜). Old Sun Cult (旧日崇拜) sects reject this and hide; the Jadd extends Chosenhood to anyone who fights the Dark [wiki: Bulwari Sun Cult; wiki: Sun Elf].
- Surael Reborn: three recognised — King Aga, Barid szel-Sur, Jaher. Zanlib's purist sect expects a being with the god's full power [wiki: Bulwari Sun Cult].
- Powers: the Erbatraši (Four Wise Ones) — Sarnagir the Phoenix, Lahmaš elder of the lamassu on Mount Lazzaward, the dead djinn Brasan, and Amašuri of the island Annail. The Erbuturgal (Four Great Demons) — Šaksu/Xhazob (the corrupted nature-goddess), Irhungal "the Whisperer" (corrupted god of magic, immortal shapeshifter with a black staff), the sea-monster Wanamur, and the wind-demon Nikšaru. The Thirteen Pure Dead (Ešeral Mitellu) include Pinnagar, god of cheesemaking, and Kiraši, goddess of brewing; each temple keeps their statuettes in a Hall of Mourning [wiki: Bulwari Sun Cult; wiki: Irhungal].
- Bayšigal, "whisperer demons", are bodiless tempters that possess only humans and only in Bulwar; only the Kašipti (exorcist-priests) can cast them out [wiki: Bayšigal].
- Clergy (Namensar): a Kašra (high priest) and a Šasig (administrator) run each temple-city of schools, granaries and workshops. The Gabarišin, dream-interpreters, are an all-female office (men who join live as women); rulers sleep in the House of Dreams to be read [wiki: Bulwari Sun Cult].
- Rites: burnt offerings from one's own garden — white smoke good, black smoke a sign of Darkness; New Year at the summer solstice, when the whole city cleans streets and pens together while priests sprinkle water, and elven incense turns the white fire rainbow-coloured; Umaslu, "Day of the Sheep", at the winter solstice (strict piety and spiced mutton); Umašaru, "Day of the Lion", at the Bloomsdawn equinox (licensed revelry the clergy frown on) [wiki: Bulwari Sun Cult; eu4: localisation/Flavour_Bulwar_l_english.yml].
- The Three Temples of the Suran: Eduz-Rakadagš in Bulwar (red Azanerdu glass and moving mirrors make flames dance on the walls), Eduz-Vacyn (埃杜兹瓦金; oldest, a temple-state), and Azka-Sur's Eduz-Meduru [wiki: Three Temples of the Suran; eu4: localisation/Flavour_Sareyand_F25_l_english.yml].
- Afterlife: the Silent Garden, a shadow-plane Bulwar of apathetic shades around the old djinn capital Ilu-Abbašlam [wiki: Silent Garden].
- Institutions: the Order of Judges (wandering lawmen, seat Azkabar since 557 BA, who also sign marriage contracts); the Exemplars (Jaherian temple-guard turned monster-hunters and magi-watchers, headquartered on Elizna) [wiki: Order of Judges; wiki: Order of Exemplars].

### 2.4 Magic in Bulwar

Bulwari Magušim descends from djinn Wish-magic: spells are long, legalistic and exact — stronger and cheaper than Cannorian casting, slower and less flexible. Magi are classed by the four Knowings: Light (Ereš-sar), Inscription (Ereš-malthar, written in lamassu cuneiform), Order, and Binding (Ereš-dirkešir). Wishblades (Menumšar) are bronze-looking tools covered in lapis-blue Šedu script that work the Wish for anyone, spark or not; perhaps fifty to a hundred survive. Since Jexis human magic is banned or temple-controlled: the Wishcrafters of Mount Lazzaward are secluded, the szal-Fazil hide in the buried city Ulmišbar, rogue magi were jailed at Azkabar. Elven armies field "sky platforms", flying chariots piloted by a mage. In 1446 Karodir I of Varamhar founds the Eka-Idulnazzar (University of Ulmiš Idiqlat) at Mahabar and lifts the general ban [wiki: Magic in Bulwar; wiki: Wishblade; wiki: Eka-Idulnazzar; wiki: Decades of Devastation; eu4: localisation/Flavour_Kumarkand_F30_l_english.yml].

### 2.5 The elven "Cinder Realms" in 1444

- **Sareyand (萨雷扬德)**, "the Ashen Phoenix". King Eledas III Sarelzuir (b. 1252) lost his left arm to Zokka — who reportedly ate it — and the upper Suran with it. The harshest elven supremacists; Ralkadezim manors around Lake Naza; the half-ruined Ash Palace; the Ash Legion; monks of the "Way of the Blazing Sun" who marry Light-magic to Xianjie chi. Mission arc: recover the plain, "No More Gnolls", humble "the Rogue Legion", prove themselves "True Heirs of Jexis" [eu4: history/countries/F25 - Sareyand.txt; eu4: localisation/Flavour_Sareyand_F25_l_english.yml; wiki: Age of Cinders].
- **Irrliam (伊尔利亚姆)**, "the Golden Phoenix", capital Medurubar. Taelarios I Irrliazuir (b. 1064), architect of the New Sun Cult, is back on the throne at 380 as regent for his grandson Thelrion (b. 1419), the only other survivor of the Slaughter of Medurubar. Overlord of Zanšap and "protector of Brasan". Every Irrliam mission opens with a verse of the Dinu Sar canon [eu4: history/countries/F37 - Irrliam.txt; eu4: localisation/Flavour_Irrliam_F37_l_english.yml; wiki: War of the Ember Queens].
- **Elizna (埃利兹纳)**, "Masters of the Sea", an island the Khetarch gave Jaher for "every grain of sand on this beach". King Oloris I Vulzinzuir, "friend to all", rules Brasanni and Kheteratans; his mother Zelaria's syncretism and the sack of Medurubar made Elizna a pariah. Holds the Exemplars' headquarters and the migrant ship Diranhriá; has just occupied Koroshesh. Missions: human auxiliaries under elven officers, the Zornartakel naval base, depose the Crodamos [eu4: localisation/Flavour_Elizna_U05_l_english.yml; wiki: Kingdom of Elizna; wiki: Sun Elf].
- **Birzartanšes (比尔萨坦舍斯)**, capital Birsartansbar on the Bay of Mirrors. Queen Keladora I (b. 1391) has just deposed her father Arantir; reforms the army and examinations; fights the rebel Dartaxes. She dies at Samartal in 1491 [eu4: history/countries/F21 - Birzartanses.txt; wiki: Decades of Devastation; wiki: Tragedy of Samartal].
- **Varamhar (瓦拉姆哈尔)**, "the White Phoenix", capital Traz Buranun: roofed cities against harpy raids, the White Phoenix Guard. King Karodir I Varamzuir (b. 1210), a proud wizard, was tortured in a magic duel by Tluukt and lost Bulwar city. His secret "Project Divinity" aims at "the creation of a god" by the "Sunmaker" procedure [eu4: localisation/Flavour_Varamhar_F42_l_english.yml; wiki: Tluukt].
- **Azka-Evran (阿兹卡埃乌兰)**: the rump of Baharkand under the bastard Deggarion I Evranzuir, wall-guard officer; destroyed by Marblehead in 1453 [wiki: Kingdom of Azka-Evran].
- **Azka-Sur (阿兹卡苏尔)**, the "Fortress of the Sun" beside the Citrine dwarves of Seghdihr (南域之门). Its last elven king, Erlian Surzuir, died in 1443 shielding refugees from Hašr; a human Akal, Mardint szel-Sur, now rules a single valley. Coffee, elephants of Davharral, a long Raheni imprint [eu4: history/countries/U20 - Azka-Sur.txt; wiki: Akalate of Azka-Sur; eu4: localisation/anb_startup_screen_l_english.yml].

### 2.6 Cities, rebels, goblins and dwarves

- **Republic of Bulwar** (F39): ruled by the Twelve Families (Bilul-Yulun, Dikuras, Alukatran, Ruqašah, Gillu-gameš, Aharmandas, Fula, szal-Lekad, Belatis, szal-Givar, Kastalukast, Huš-Nekar); head of state in 1444 Mardin Bilul-Yulun. Sacked by Tluukt c. 1439, walls razed, the Twelve made to kneel before her host. In 1452 Bulwar, Zanbar and Harklum beat her at Eduz-Buranun and form the Naqtazan, the River Alliance [wiki: Twelve of Bulwar; wiki: Tluukt; eu4: history/countries/F39 - Bulwar.txt].
- **Brasan (布拉桑)**: merchant republic under a Rabsukal, Irrliam's protectorate; sugar and glass [wiki: Republic of Brasan; wiki: Age of Cinders]. **Drolakand**: a pirate republic on the Drolas [eu4: history/countries/F32 - Drolakand.txt].
- **Eduz-Vacyn**: temple-state under Kašra Zaid, tributary to Zokka. **Hašr** (哈舍尔): occupied by Zokka at game start [wiki: Eduz-Vacyn; zh-timeline: country-identity-eu4.json].
- **Azkabar (阿兹卡巴尔)**: seat of the Judges and of an infamous prison; its tree follows a hidden "Eclipse Cult" that plans obsidian "Black Walls" around the Garden [eu4: localisation/Flavour_Azkabar_F35_l_english.yml].
- **Dartaxâgerdim (达塔克斯格丁姆)**: Akal Dartaxes szel-Farramaz (1401–1484), "Hammer of the Elves", ex-general and victor of Habqez, first independent human king in Bahar since Jaher. Allied to the Green Helmets (Šebhuliam), an Old Sun sect whose doctrine of Šanqu ("prudence") permits lying about one's faith and whose youths murder officials in green helmets. - **Re-Uyel (雷乌耶尔)**: "officers' republic" of lesser nobles since 1419–20, slavery banned; **Crathánor (克拉萨诺)** and **Ourdia**: Regent Court enclaves ("no representation without taxation") [wiki: Decades of Devastation; eu4: localisation/anb_startup_screen_l_english.yml].
- **Exodus goblins (流亡地精)**, fleeing Korgus Dookanson's orcs: Marblehead (大理石首; "the Great Looters", holding Aqatbar and its temple-library; Clanboss Idrarseff, who wants to break elves and men, and his brother Perzuk, who admires human custom and will overthrow him), Greysheep (灰羊; Clanboss Guklo; ride their Big Grey Sheep), Mountainhugger (拥山者), Landshark (陆鲨). Perzuk's "Proclamation of Aqatbar" unites the clans; the Overclan is proclaimed in 1552 and adopts the Jadd in 1561 [wiki: Decades of Devastation; wiki: Marblehead Clan; wiki: Greysheep Clan; wiki: Aqatbar Overclan; eu4: history/countries/U15 - Marble Head Clan.txt].
- **Dwarves**: Ovdal Tûngr (港口城, "Hold Harbour"), the only naval hold, Copper dwarves (黄铜矮人), creditors of half the Divenhal, once blockaded by Jaher; Seghdihr, the "South Gate", Citrine dwarves, old patrons of the Surani (ruler Storkhumlir XVIII az-Segh); Verkal Gulan (金堡, the "Golden Citadel") behind its Three Golden Gates, taken by the Jaddari in 1462 and later Jadd by faith [wiki: Hold of Ovdal Tûngr; wiki: Hold of Seghdihr; wiki: Hold of Verkal Gulan; eu4: history/countries/H78 - Seghdihr.txt].
- **Kobolds**: I found no kobold polity in or facing Bulwar in the corpus (see section 8).

### 2.7 Gnolls and the Xhazobkult

- Gnolls (豺狼人): hyena-folk, 1.9–2.3 m, usually matriarchal, short-lived mostly from violence. *Krah* means "slaver" (after the first great slaver, 1152 BA); caravan roads across the desert are *krahways* [wiki: Gnoll; wiki: Krah].
- Xhazobkult (哈佐布秘教): decentralised demon-bargaining. A Xhazob is a soul-hungry demon; a Xhazobine (female) or Xhazobain (male) is a gnoll fused with one after a mass sacrifice, and her holy war is a Xhaz. Rites use pyres of green "felflame"/hellfire. Boons ("Invocations") of Hellfire, Chains, Blood, Gold and Eyes. Seven great Xhaz are recorded; the wiki counts Zokka as a "Xhazobain-to-be" [wiki: Xhazobkult; wiki: Xhaz].
- Sandfang (沙牙豺狼人) are the desert poor and zealots; Suncrown (日冠豺狼人) are bulwarised Sun Cultists whose "Way of the Red Flame" answers Xhazob's green flame; in 1444 most Suncrown live in Akalšes [wiki: Sandfang Gnoll; wiki: Suncrown Gnoll].
- **Zokka Devourer-of-Suns** (佐迦; b. 1407; capital Ilzin Zokka at the Harra Oasis). A male pack lord of fanatical authority and demonic strength. His own event text: born staring at "the First Sun", he has hungered ever since to grow tall enough to pluck and eat it; Jaddar is "another sun" to be devoured. Tributaries: Hašr, Eduz-Vacyn, the slaver "Kog sisters" Khazet and Zykari Gifter-of-Chains on the old Maqeti coast. His mission tree is narrated by his bookish, doubting son, who admits "I am not a good Xhazobkultist. I am not a good gnoll" and who in canon surrenders, converts and is remembered as Zokka Devourer-of-Darkness. If Zokka wins, the tree runs to burning the Phoenix, becoming "the seventh Xhazobain", seeking a planar entity in the Shadow Swamp and "piercing the Hells" [eu4: localisation/Flavour_Bulwar_l_english.yml; eu4: localisation/Flavour_Zokka_F29_l_english.yml; wiki: Age of Cinders].
- **Tluukt Cleaver-of-Realms** (得露革得; b. 1391; Pack Mistress since 1413; capital Ekyrigrax, the captured fortress Ekluzagnu). A witch who began as "Fishcleaver"; lured Akal Bavar III of Zanšap to his death in the crags (1434), starved out the bandit-lords of Ekyrigrax, duelled Karodir. A state-builder: the standing "Skull-Cross Ravagers", codified gnollish (the "Gaz Reforms"), preachers of Eduz-Vacyn turned to her use ("Krah Maga"), a succession rite of her own design in which her matriarchs eat her corpse. Dies in her sleep 25 Yshdament 1463; her heir Aaryz proclaims the "Tluuktraak Akalate"; the last defenders die floor by floor in 1507 [wiki: Tluukt; eu4: localisation/Flavour_Tluukt_F28_l_english.yml].

### 2.8 The Jaddari and the Jadd

- Origins: Phoenix legionaries stranded after Jaher's and Jexis's deaths settled the Far Salahad among the Mašnsih and the Elaiénna harpies, becoming Desert Elves (沙漠精灵). Their tribe keeps legion structure; almost everyone rides and fights. Capital: Ebbušubtu, the "Mountain of Clear Sight", a djinn palace renamed Mukdaious Malas, "Palace of Hidden Waters", fed by wish-made springs [wiki: Desert Elf; wiki: Ebbušubtu].
- Jaddar Jexiszuir (贾达尔; b. 12 Nerament 1161) was raised by the commander Erelessa, came down from the mountain in summer 1333 after nine years' seclusion over the writings of the exiled Kašra Basir, and preaches three tenets: Be the Light; Fight the War on Halann; All creatures can be the Light. In 1444 the legion is "tribal" under a "desert legion" government, with Mašnsih and Siadúnan harpy culture accepted [wiki: Jaddar; wiki: Age of Cinders; wiki: The Jadd; eu4: history/countries/F46 - Jaddari.txt].
- Household: harpy wife Jasiéne Elaiennó (b. 1403), matriarch of Elaiénna (伊莱耶娜); their daughter Jaddarésa, an archer and the legion's diplomat; elven son and heir Andrellion (b. 1315); daughter Erelessa, later governor of Bulwar; grandson Jahid, half-elf, whose father died slowing Zokka's charge [eu4: localisation/Flavour_Jaddari_F46_l_english.yml; eu4: history/countries/F49 - Elaienna.txt].
- Allies: the Sihrušam, "Sand Banner", the pact of the seven Mašnsih (马什恩希赫) tribes — szal-Adad (hospitable, largest), szal-Zaid (first human believers), szal-Ardeth (wardens of Amussu's Grave), szal-Mehran (guides), szal-Yazkur (gnoll-hunters), szal-Attalu (the "Sand-demon tribe" of the port Edešukeru; masters of the martial art Ahu Alaktu, which Jaddar alone among outsiders learned and used to kill Zokka), szal-Fazil (mages of Ulmišbar, not Jadd) [wiki: Mašnsih; wiki: Ahu Alaktu].

### 2.9 Harpies

All-female, egg-laying, taking mates of other races and coming to resemble their fathers' kind (Elaiénna harpies look elven); 60–100 years; a syrinx instead of vocal cords lets them sing two notes at once — the root of their "mountain voice" war-song and Ištarun song-magic. Units: flock, matriarchy, queendom. Faith: the Hunt (狩猎教), godless and pragmatic, taught through epic songs kept by each flock's Riaveltu; it does not proselytise (*mantalqo*) but borrows freely [wiki: Harpy; wiki: The Hunt].

- Harpy Hills, 1444: Ayarallen (阿娅拉伦; Matriarch Itara/Ištara I Ayarato, claiming Firanya's blood), Nanšalen (南莎伦; Sun Cult; holders of Invader's Pass, old allies of Akalšes; red irises mark the line of Nanša the Farseer), Hytiranyalen (希蒂兰雅伦; the scholarly flock, Matriarch Saero I). Harpylen (哈比伦) is the queendom they can re-forge, with its roost-capital Heunthume [eu4: localisation/anb_startup_screen_l_english.yml; wiki: Nanšalen Matriarchy; eu4: localisation/Flavour_Harpylen_l_english.yml].
- Far Salahad: Siádan (锡亚丹; Matriarch Astárta Siadunó; keeps the Crest of Ištara), Mulén (穆兰; the enchantress Kiríja Muló, heir to Múla Swift-Sparrow's creed of persuasion), and Jadd-following Elaiénna. With Jaddari help Elaiénna absorbs the other two in 1451–53 [wiki: Siádan Matriarchy; wiki: Mulén Matriarchy].

### 2.11 Kheterata and the Mother's Sorrow

- The Mother's Sorrow runs from Lake Nirakhet to a delta on the Divenhal (迪文哈尔海). Clear from Esmarment to Halament; in Teysuren it floods red-brown — "the blood tears of Nirakhet", who wept herself to death for her son Elikhet. Newborns are bathed in it; people fast and cast offerings in flood season. Hundreds of genies are said to be bound beneath it. Papyrus boats; a bureaucracy of surveyors re-allots land after each flood, so every civil war is also a famine [wiki: Mother's Sorrow].
- The Khet: human bodies, cat heads, lifespans to 800 years. Inside the lands the Sorrow waters they simply *will* things — sandstorms, floods, darkening the sun — and are nearly unkillable (only the First Xhazobine and Aakhet have slain one). They cannot leave, feel pain only by choice, need no food but demand delicacies, and are lazy: one "surrendered all of Kheterata as they were tired of hearing all the war reports". The Khetarch (in 1444 Baqtkhet II "the Liberator", since 1136) appoints the mortal king, the "Steward of the Sorrow"; four elders form the Eldkhet [wiki: Khet; wiki: Khetarch; wiki: Khetism].
- Khetism is law, not faith: the Mariat, drawn from Elikhet's decrees, studied in the Grand Library of Koroshesh. Elikhet lies in his pyramid; his mask is the symbol of his power. Sects: Elikhetist orthodoxy (埃利赫特宗); Nirakhetism (尼拉赫特宗; prayerful, the mother who mourns the dead; faith of peasants, slaves and Nzeri harpies); Aakhetism (阿赫特宗; the dragon as Elikhet reborn; faith of the Ahati); the rationalist Deltaaw of the Delta gnomes [wiki: Khetism; wiki: Nirakhetism; wiki: Aakhetism].
- Magic is a state monopoly: anyone with talent is taken, by force if need be, to the Black Academy in the Tower of Muhaqaar on Lake Nirakhet; refusal scales from flogging to enslavement or death. Elikhet's ruling: magic born in you is holy, magic stolen (as from djinn) is the worst sin — "Brasan the Liar" drove the Bulwari mad [wiki: Magic in Kheterata; wiki: Khetism].
- The Ahati: commoner warrior-brotherhoods living by *minisris* ("manliness": devotion to the people, virtue, ending misrule). They have assassinated unjust kings, then disembowelled themselves in atonement. In the south (Irsmahap) they were also the merchant guilds [wiki: Ahati].
- 1444: King Lykidas II Crodamos (b. 1362; the dynasty descends from Sedoric, an Alenic mercenary-king of Akasik invited in 488). High Priest Nahab "the Defiant" of Nirat (尼拉特) converts to Nirakhetism and marches on Kheterat with the Ahati commander Dajasht ("Nirat's Defiance"). South of Golkora sits Gnollakaz (格诺拉迦兹): Grizaka of the Pyreclaw (燔爪豺狼人), camp-capital Ilzin Grizaka, who keeps human administrators and runs gold mines of legendary cruelty. Upriver splinters: Aramoole (阿拉莫莱), an Aakhetist stratocracy where the dragon Aakhet revealed himself among the generals; Duwarkani (杜瓦尔卡尼), where Kenmu Menkaunswt married the Nzeri harpy queen Safana Duwaran and preaches that "Nirakhet weeps for them all equally" (both are assassinated by an anti-mixing zealot; Aramoole annexes it in 1458); the mage-theocracy of Muhaqaar; Samsumbat, a "republic of free peoples" [eu4: history/countries/U01 - Kheterata.txt; eu4: localisation/Kheterata_civil_war_l_english.yml; wiki: Pyreclaw Gnoll; wiki: Aakhet the Bronze; wiki: Lordship of Duwarkani].
- Outcome: Grizaka is named Steward, then walks into the Great Pyramid "to defile and devour the gods' corpses" and never returns; her daughter Izhrukt finishes the conquest; after the "Dance of Demons" between the sisters Kyztzehr (Khetist) and Xukret (Xhazobkultist), Kyztzehr proclaims Konolkhatep as "Guardian-of-the-Sorrow". Pyreclaws settle quarrels by Gremrabo, the "Insult War" — which Kheteratan towns have used to talk their way out of a raid [wiki: Pyreclaw Gnoll].

### 2.12 The Salahad, Akasik and the Gulf of Rahen

- The Salahadesi Desert (萨拉哈德) was djinn country until the Day of Ashen Skies. In its east lives Kuršagal, the Sand Demon: a brown dragon 350–400 m long, never seen in human form, who beat Aakhet in 488 and whom the Mašnsih call Abi, "Grandfather"; caravans still vanish with "a low, booming roar" and no tracks. Wonders: the Quartz Cothon, a circular white-marble djinn harbour hidden in the cliffs; buried Ulmišbar; Amussu's Grave, which the szal-Ardeth will go to war to keep sealed [wiki: Sand Demon of the Salahad; wiki: Quartz Cothon; eu4: localisation/Flavour_Bulwar_l_english.yml].
- Akasik (阿卡西克), "land of storms and silver": Mother Akasik (阿卡西克母亲) is the mountain range itself in four aspects — Justice, Resilience, Compassion, Reciprocity — one presiding for twelve years, changed at the Manifesting of Aspects on 20 Suren with water-splashing, dance and poetry. Ancient half-djinn Storm-Queens (Ayar, Sayir, Isila). Since 1437 the Seascourge (海灾豺狼人) corsairs of Viakkoc (维亚哥吉; Viakkoc XI "Scourge-of-All-Seas", capital Kokerrat) have destroyed Khasa (哈萨), burned Stonewing harpies for demonflame and made merchant Ekha (埃哈) a tributary that hands over its criminals as sacrifices. Deshak (德沙克) survives by crowning Delian II Silebor of Eborthíl [wiki: Mother Akasik; wiki: Storm-Queens; wiki: Viakkoc's Grand Corsair Kingdom; wiki: Kingdom of Deshak; eu4: localisation/anb_startup_screen_l_english.yml].
- West Salahad Windhowlers (风嗥豺狼人): Brrtekuh "Splitter-of-Sands" (brain-injured by the last palace guard of Deshak; her pack follows Kvangahga and insists "we are not demons"); Irkorzik, the "Sand Wind" raised by exiled shamans in 1443; Tzilekal, which seizes the Fangaulan capital Sigilan and styles itself a "Zuultanate" [wiki: Windhowler Gnoll; eu4: localisation/Flavour_Brrtekuh_U10_l_english.yml].
- Kvangahga (革万加之道): the smith-goddess Kvanga bound savage spirits into hyenas and gave them the capacity for virtue; everything is reforged, including one's dead, who are eaten in honour. Haraagtseda (哈拉格得塞达) under Rozherg "Hammer-of-Kvanga" is an artificer league at Kvangraak [wiki: Kvangahga; eu4: localisation/Flavour_Bulwar_l_english.yml].
- The Maqeti (马格特) of Dasmatuš (达斯马图什): descendants of Maqet's slave revolt; an Old Sun sect for which slavery is the worst sin; mainland lost in 1432; island fleet of "chainbreakers" and the marines Maqet Gumar; Akal Idras szal-Maqet. ### 2.13 Wider Sarhal (阳洲), in proportion

Zerat (泽拉特), seat of the 332nd, is collapsing before Sykar I Riverclaw of Rayaz, the naga's chosen. The crocodilian Ashhana fight a hag-laid Blood Curse by making art — Zaradyrra (美善智慧). Lizardfolk cannot say m, p or b, shed skin socially in the baths, and find humans "round and sweaty" [wiki: Lizardfolk; wiki: Naga; wiki: Anzalkatsa; wiki: Zaradyrra; eu4: localisation/Flavour_Zerat_L69_l_english.yml; eu4: history/countries/L95 - Rayaz.txt].

Ruled by a coven of three hags — Hireet Smoketusk ("belonging"), Ilfanill Gloomweaver ("secret knowledge"), Komina Riftwhisper ("catharsis") — through swamp trolls (沼泽巨魔) who cannot lie outright or break the letter of an oath, and who war "to cause suffering by winning battles". Exports: night-blossom black dye, the necrotising smoke hattiatia, the drug-flower Last Wish [wiki: Shadow Swamp; wiki: Coven of Yezel Mora; wiki: Swamp Trolls; eu4: localisation/Flavour_Yezel_Mora_S70_l_english.yml].

## 4. Nations and factions as playable sides

- **Jaddari Legion (贾达尔军团)** — wants every people inside the Light and Jexis's inheritance (to Anbenncóst, by its own missions). Hates the Xhazobkult, slavery, elven supremacy. Internal fault lines: elf/harpy/Mašnsih/convert-gnoll; who succeeds a 400-year-old prophet.
- **Sareyand / Irrliam / Elizna / Birzartanšes / Varamhar** — each claims to be the true Phoenix heir. They hate the gnolls, the "rogue legion" and one another (Irrliam–Elizna above all). A player here fights decline: human resentment, empty treasuries, long-lived elves who cannot replace losses.
- **Zokka's Pack** — apocalypse as policy; allied by treaty with Tluukt to partition the Suran. **Tluukt's Pack** — a gnoll state that wants to be a real kingdom, not a Xhaz.
- **Bulwari humans** — the Twelve Families and the Naqtazan (civic liberation); Dartaxâgerdim (Old Sun, anti-elven, pro-magi); Re-Uyel (republican); Eduz-Vacyn (the temple itself); Kumarkand and Akalšes (Zanite revival; Akalšes shelters Suncrown gnolls).
- **Monsters of the north** — Harpylen's matriarchies; Marblehead/Overclan goblins; Copper dwarves of Ovdal Tûngr.
- **Kheterata's heirs** — Crodamos loyalists; Nirat's priests; Gnollakaz; Aramoole and its dragon; Elizna in the delta; Samsumbat's republicans. Whoever pleases the Khet rules.
- **Akasik** — Viakkoc's corsairs versus Ekhan and Khasani resistance, Deshak/Eborthíl.
- **South** — Maqeti abolitionists versus the Kog slavers; Kvangahga artificer gnolls; further off, Rayaz versus Zerat, and the hags' swamp against everyone.

## 5. Plot threads with canon basis

1. **The War of the Eastern Sands.** Zokka versus Jaddar for "who rules the eastern Salahad"; the mod scripts both outcomes — if Jaddar falls, the legion shelters with Elaiénna. A party could decide the duel's conditions (Ahu Alaktu training, a blinding sun, a son in two minds) [eu4: Flavour_Bulwar zokka_jaddari_war; wiki: Ahu Alaktu].
2. **Who is Jaddar's mother?** Jexis by an unknown elf; Jexis by her half-brother Elizar; Erelessa; an orphan look-alike; a virgin birth (its author was burned by Lightbringers); a dragon's child. Evidence lies in Elizna's archives, the ruined Ash Palace and elven memories [wiki: Jaddar].
3. **Convert or die.** The Jadd's promise to gnolls tested on real people: Zokka's son, Hašri survivors, Maqeti who cannot bear gnoll allies, Sandfangs who convert "in name only" [eu4: Flavour_Jaddari_F46; wiki: Tluukt].
4. **Project Divinity.** A mage-king builds a god; Azkabar's Eclipse agents learn of it; the Phoenix and the Whisperer both have reasons to care [eu4: Flavour_Varamhar_F42; eu4: Flavour_Azkabar_F35].
5. **Samartal, 1491.** A masquerade-summit that could have saved the elven kingdoms, and the bomb that ends it; a campaign can aim to stop it, cause it or survive it [wiki: Tragedy of Samartal].
6. **The Mandate of the Cats.** Priest, king, gnoll empress, elven admiral and dragon all court bored demigods; then what lies in Elikhet's pyramid, where Grizaka vanished [wiki: Khet; wiki: Pyreclaw Gnoll].
7. **The Garden's walls.** Old Sun assassins in green helmets, Exemplars hunting magi, Judges on the road, a temple-state paying tribute to a demon-worshipper — a city-intrigue campaign in Bulwar, Eduz-Vacyn or Aqatbar [wiki: Green Helmets; wiki: Order of Exemplars].
8. **Chains.** Krahways, the Kog sisters' plantations, Ekha's sacrificial criminals, Pyreclaw gold mines, Maqeti chainbreakers, the Jadd's later "Abolish Slavery" mission [wiki: Akalate of Dasmazar; eu4: Flavour_Jaddari_F46].
9. **Things under the sand.** Amussu's Grave, Ulmišbar, the Quartz Cothon, Wishblades, genies under the Sorrow, the Sand Demon [wiki: Mašnsih; wiki: Quartz Cothon; wiki: Mother's Sorrow].
10. **Exodus.** As the Jaddari close in, the Exemplars steal away on Jaher's own ship to a new world — a last-days-of-the-old-order story [wiki: Sun Elf].

## 6. Everyday and sensory detail; what a Cannorian finds strange

- **All the gods are dead and one of them is the sun.** Bulwari regard the Regent Court as worship of corpses — "the Dame... perished millennia ago" [eu4: Flavour_Jaddari_F46]. A Cannorian's "avatar" is their Surael Reborn, and they resent the comparison [wiki: Bulwari Sun Cult].
- **Lying is sacrilege, sadness a wound.** Neighbours are expected to cheer the gloomy. An Old Sun believer hiding his faith is in real theological trouble; trolls of the far swamp cannot lie at all [wiki: Bulwari Sun Cult; wiki: Green Helmets; wiki: Swamp Trolls].
- **Elves as a priestly caste.** Old Sun Elves spend their last century healing free at Eduz-szel-Ninezim or blending incense; young ones hold masked balls. Elven artisans take human apprentices as a religious duty; in Sareyand the same theology excuses plantations [wiki: Sun Elf].
- **Monsters as citizens.** Suncrown gnoll merchants, harpy envoys "dressed in finely spun cloth and jewelry of gold", goblins running a library-city, a marble dwarf statue (the Bannerman of Hatred) that walks twelve steps each summer solstice and tears apart anyone who blocks it [eu4: Flavour_Jaddari_F46; wiki: Bannerman of Hatred].
- **Marriage is a contract** signed by a Judge, dissolvable, with land kept separate; polygyny and polyandry are both known, adultery is the taboo [wiki: Bulwari].
- **Food and drink:** lentils, ram and beer as temple offerings; spiced mutton on the Day of the Sheep; coffee before prayer and debate; blue-veined Lawaššar cheese (legend: a shepherd forgot his lunch chasing a harpy) and white, holed Eskuz of Kuzaram, made to resemble the moon; the proverb "Never trust an EaNasir", after a family of cheese-merchants turned swindlers; in Kheterata, meat cured in honey (Hamkhinanp Mudu) [eu4: Flavour_Irrliam_F37; eu4: Flavour_Bulwar; wiki: Hamkhinanp Mudu].
- **Sport and spectacle:** the Great Avamezan Horse Race, the biggest gambling day in Bulwar; Gelkar goat-shearing contests every four years; Mussakal; the Phoenix alighting on Brasan's walls while a queue of pilgrims crosses the whole city [eu4: Flavour_Bulwar].
- **Tavern arguments:** Kuzaram versus Gelkalis ("I don't get kidnapped by harpies, he does"); whether the Buranun wine or Re'uyel's is better; whether Jaddar is a bastard, a prophet or both; whether gnolls can be saved; Surael versus Surakel as the god's name (elves and foreigners say the first, Bulwari the second) [eu4: Flavour_Bulwar; wiki: Surael].
- **Sights:** red-glass temple light; walled gardens in eight wedges round a pond; magic carpets and sky platforms overhead; cuneiform; green pyre-light on the desert horizon; the Sorrow running blood-brown in Teysuren; Khet pleasure barges ruining irrigation by whim; harpy war-song carrying for miles [wiki: Paradise Garden; wiki: Mother's Sorrow; wiki: The Hunt].
- **Courtesy that can kill:** desert hospitality is sacred among the Mašnsih; gnolls may accept a contest of insults instead of a raid; never offer a Khet bad news twice [wiki: Bulwari Sun Cult; wiki: Pyreclaw Gnoll; wiki: Khet].

## 8. Uncertainties and source conflicts

- **Elizna's ruler.** Mod history: Oloris I on the throne since 1384 (born 1369). Mod startup text: he succeeded his mother in 1440. Wiki: Zelaria reigned 1201–1422 and led the War of the Ember Queens (1417–20). I use Oloris as king in 1444 and Zelaria as the wartime queen; the accession year is unsettled [eu4: history/countries/U05 - Elizna.txt; eu4: anb_startup_screen; wiki: Zelaria Vulzinzuir].
- **Dates around Zokka.** First attack on Sareyand: 1426 [wiki: Age of Cinders] or 1429 [wiki: Sandfang Gnoll]; the mod has him pack lord only from 1435. Battle of the Sun's Providence: 1446 (wiki) or 1445 (zh-timeline). The maimed king is Eledas III in the wiki and mod history but "Erelas" in one Sareyand mission text.
- **Tluukt's rise.** Šad Sur unified "by 1434" [wiki: Age of Cinders] versus Ekyrigrax falling c. 1438 and the march on Bulwar in 1439 [wiki: Tluukt]. I follow the Tluukt page.
- **Jaddar's leadership.** Legion "established 1247" [wiki: Jaddari Legion]; ruler from 1310 [eu4]; faith founded 1333 [wiki: Age of Cinders]. Death at 402 in 1563 (wiki) while a mod event says he came down the mountain "over a century ago".
- **Harpy conquests.** Siádan and Mulén absorbed in 1453 [wiki: Siádan Matriarchy; wiki: Age of Cinders] or by 1451 [wiki: Mulén Matriarchy; zh-timeline].
- **Harpylen.** The wiki has Mudam unite the Harpy Hills into Harpylen in 1342 and treats a "Harpy Queendom" as an actor in the 1450s; the mod starts with separate matriarchies and Harpylen only as a formable. The Ayarallen matriarch is "Itara" in history and "Ištara" in the startup text.
- **Marblehead.** One page credits Clanboss Jenthib with killing Kaltan III in 1432 and taking Aqatbar; another has Idrarseff take it in 1439 and execute Kaltan. The mod makes Idrarseff boss in 1444 and Jenthib the Landshark boss [wiki: Marblehead Clan; wiki: Decades of Devastation; eu4: history/countries/U11 - Landshark Clan.txt].
- **End of Kheterata.** "Conquered by Gnollakaz in 1470" [wiki: Kingdom of Kheterata] versus conquest finished "by the 1500s" under Izhrukt and Konolkhatep proclaimed only after the Dance of Demons [wiki: Pyreclaw Gnoll]. The zh-timeline reconciles them: 1470 is the fall of Kheterat and the grant of the Stewardship.
- **Viakkoc numerals.** Grizaka kills "Viakkoc IX" in 1442 [wiki: Pyreclaw Gnoll] or "Viakkoc X" [wiki: Windhowler Gnoll; eu4 startup]; the 1444 ruler is Viakkoc XI in the mod. Ekha's head is Sayir Imkamsa in the mod, Moduman II Akanras on the wiki.
- **Kheterata's founding** is "c. 3000 BA" (infobox), "over four thousand years ago" and river-creation "c. 2700 BA"; the First Xhazobine's bargain is dated 666 BA and 654 BA on different pages.
- **Jaddari conquest dates after 1491** differ by a year or two between the wiki (Sareyand 1493–94) and the zh-timeline (1492; Irrliam's fall and Thelrion's death 1503; Elizna's surrender 1516). Treat exact years as soft.
- **Not covered by the corpus or unverified.** No kobold state near Bulwar was found; the "Proclamation of Bulwar" and "Partition of Kalib" are named but have no page; who planted the Samartal device beyond "Old Sun Cult fanatics" is unstated; several wiki pages (Age of Cinders after 1302, Phoenix Empire, The Jadd) carry "outdated lore" or "work in progress" banners. EU4 diplomatic relations at start were not in the corpus, so overlord/tributary links here rest on wiki infoboxes and startup text.
- **Out of scope but present.** CK3 has `history/titles/anb_k_bulwar.txt`, `anb_k_bahar.txt`, `anb_k_kheterata.txt` for 1022 (Jaher's lifetime); Vic3 has the Jaddanzar Empire, Nahana Jadd, Overclan, Konolkhatep and Rezankand for 1836. I did not mine them.
