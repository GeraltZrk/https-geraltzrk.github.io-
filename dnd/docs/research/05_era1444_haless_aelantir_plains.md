# 05 — Haless, the Forbidden Plains, Aelantir and the far continents (1444 AA and the EU4 era)

## 1. Overview

In November 1444 the three far theatres are at very different points of their story.

- **Haless (东洲)** is thirteen years into the hobgoblin breakout. **The Command (命令秩序)** under Grand Marshal **Moguwon Wolfborn** has destroyed three of the Ruin Kingdoms of Shamakhad (沙摩朅) and is about to face its first revolt in Sir (1445). South of it the harimari-ruled **Dhenijanraj (提尼禅罗阇)** is rich, caste-bound and governed by an idle Raja and his vizier; east of it the eunuch republics and warlord kingdoms of **Yanshen (烟州)** quarrel while **Bianfang (边方)** under **Xinzhu Long** tries to conquer them all; in the misty hills of **Xianjie (仙界)** the martial temples of the Xiaken are leaderless in everything but name. Under all of it sits a truth almost nobody in Haless accepts (Jaher said the temples were elven-built and was hated for it; it is only proven during the Rending): the **High Temples** are a Precursor machine suppressing the **Great Spirits** — and the Oni of **Azjakuma (鬼居)**, who see the temples as "a font of knowledge to corrupt", already know how to siphon spirits.
- **The Forbidden Plains (禁忌平原)** have just survived the **Great Incursion** (1433–1440), "the largest and most destructive invasion since Neagol's" (Naegol's horde was broken at Bokuzqayla in 564 BA) [wiki: Triunic Timeline; wiki: Centaur]. The humans of the lakes ratified the **Lake Federation** in 1441; the centaur caehnate has shattered; the ogre king **Ziltagh Gravelhide** took the throne of **Maghargma** in 1440 and is dismantling the Great Clans.
- **Aelantir (埃兰提尔)** is still unknown to Cannor (rediscovery: 1490, Endral the Explorer). Its Ruinborn civilisations are in full motion on their own terms: the opening phase of the **Wars of the Two Heroes** in Taychend (泰琴德) — **Erankar the Defiant** uniting the north while **Laskaris the Conqueror** conquers the south; the two do not actually meet in battle until 1452 [wiki: Wars of Two Heroes]; **Alaran's War** (1443–44) to restore the Ynnic Empire; fey-driven season wars in Eordand (奥尔丹德). After 1490 come Venáil's "Age of Reclamation", the Empire of **Aelnar (埃纳尔)**, the Trollsbay republics, the Jaherian Exemplars in Taychend, and the discovery of the **God Fragment** (1585) that founds the Ravelian Church.

Three corrections to the task brief, from the sources:
1. The **Larankarha Empire** is not on the Forbidden Plains. It is a Taychendi Ruinborn warlord-empire in South Aelantir (capital Urvand, 1434–1521) [wiki: Larankarha Empire].
2. The **"Rending"** has two unrelated referents. The **Rending of Realms** (c. 1751–1783) is a Halessi spirit catastrophe [wiki: Rending of Realms]; the mod's `gfx/event_pictures/rending/` folder is Great-Spirit portraits for it [eu4: gfx/event_pictures/rending]. Aelnar's civil war is the **Rianvisa**; "Rending Earth / Rending Spirits" are also stage names of the Day of Ashen Skies [wiki: Day of Ashen Skies].
3. The **"Triarchy"** named in Halessi texts ("intervention by the Hierarchy and Triarchy") is the **Harafic (Harafe) Triarchy** (three artificer cities in Haraf, Aelantir), not a centaur state: the Tianlou page links the phrase explicitly to `[[Harafe Triarchy|Triarchy]]` and `[[Gnomish Hierarchy]]`, both of which helped Tianlou repel Lingyuk during the Rending [wiki: Tianlou]. Centaurs organise as clans → ordsiwyn → caehnates [wiki: Centaur; wiki: Harafic Triarchy].

## 2. Facts by sub-topic

### 2A. HALESS

#### A1. Shape of the continent
Haless ("Eastern Land" in Old Castanorian) is the largest continent of Halann, walled from Cannor and Bulwar by the Serpentspine and the Paravimvata. Five regions: **Rahen (罗祆)** in the south-west; **Yanshen (烟州, "Land of Mist")** in the centre-east; **Vimdatrong (帆达陇)** in the south (misty hills of Xianjie, river lands of Bomdan 茫檀, plains of Thidinkai 盖邦, Lupulan rainforest, Ringlet Isles); **Gozengun (高峡国)** in the north-east with the sealed valley of **Nomsyulhan (嫩许兰)**; and the Forbidden Plains [wiki: Haless; zh names]. Rahen itself divides into Shamakhad ("Land of Ruin", home of the Ruin Kingdoms), Rakhadesh (罗佉提湿, "Land of Small Kingdoms"), Dhujat (度阇多, "Land of the Elephants"), the Rahen Coast with the Ascension Jungle, and the Marutha desert. Two rivers matter: the **Kharunyana** (Rahen's eastern border; Sramaya, Khiraspid, Sarisung on its banks) and the **Dhenbasana** (Dhenijansar, seat of the Raj) [wiki: Rahen].

Races: humans everywhere; harimari across the old Harimraj lands; hobgoblins from the Jade Mines; Oni (Horned Ogres) in the Demon Hills (魔岭); Goldscale kobolds in northern Yanshen; Feng/P'ezarang harpies around the Phokhao range; Hill Trolls in Gozengun; Brown Orcs and cave goblins brought out by the Command; dwarves (Verkal Ozovar 东乡堡, and exiles from Grôzumdihr led by "the Exilarch"); a thin scatter of **Sunrise Elves** left from Jaher's legions in Azkare, Arawkelin and the Porcelain Cities [wiki: Haless; wiki: Rahen].

#### A2. Deep history that the 1444 world stands on
- **Precursors and the temple grid.** Wiki working notes (flagged "to write better") give: Precursors enter Haless c. 7800 BA to study the Spirit Realm; they exterminate the Hill Giant kingdoms; the "Pillar of the Earth" is built in Nomsyulhan (completed c. 7400 BA; the Nomsyulhan page describes a planet-portal set in a dead Titan's jaw and fed by the spirit grid — I take these to be the same structure) and the High Temple network grows from a hub at **Tughayasa** to 31+ complexes; **Tianlou**'s complex (c. 6800 BA) suppresses the dragon-spirit Yanhe; the last, Tapumakor, c. 6650 BA; in 6350 BA the temples are used to power the **Kiamoa**, the storm that seals Insyaa from the world [wiki: High Temples; wiki: Nomsyulhan]. Each temple has a **heart** of pure damestear; remove it and the ward dies. Three cornerstones hold the "Spirit Grid": Smaraya (Sramaya), Tughayasa, Tianlou. Tianlou's heart-door is a dragon statue with nine gem slots keyed to the temples of Damaogong, Jiantsiang, Lanjincun, Yanzhong, Zyugok, Lamwun, Jianxusi and Shanyutian [wiki: High Temples]. (The wiki names only these eight for nine slots; its list of Precursor temple symbols has a ninth, **Luoyip**, which is probably the missing one — inference, not stated.)
- **Great Spirits.** Fourteen named beings on the Great Spirits page (thirteen entries, the twins sharing one; the Mystic Accord page adds Semat Tidakhui and Hujan and sorts them into four pantheons of five), said — in the in-world theory of the scholar Edward Alen Gerwicksson — to be the animated body-parts of an Astral entity that punched through into the Empyreal; they make Haless "awash with chi" and make the Spirit Realm a soul-trap for anyone who dies there. Sealed, they have been "pushing and shoving from the other side" ever since. Named: Hokuma (black mist, earth/vengeance, drained by the Oni), Yanhe (spectral dragon, "Father of Kings"), Kharunyana (white catfish), Phokhao (peacock), Hengdu Bowu (frog, patron of solitary Xianjie warriors), Cinulau (whale), Lupulan (monitor lizard), Dhenbasana (elephant, a trickster-innovator who appears at court as a vanishing advisor), Marutha (lion of the desert), Gehet Ketine (scorpion), the twins Hukai (black swan) and Telebei (white turtle), Huszien (nine-tailed fox, mind shattered "six thousand years ago"), Tughayasa (paper-talisman eyes, patron of oracles) [wiki: Great Spirits; wiki: Mystic Accord; the phrase "awash with Chi" is from wiki: Chi].
- **Human antiquity.** Oracle Kings of Tughayasa (1650–1177 BA); Nadimraj (c. 1200 BA, first human empire of Haless); Rahenraj (c. 1000–200 BA). In Yanshen: the mythic Emperor Yan of Longcheng, the Sage Kingdoms, eunuch administrators from c. 380 BA, and the kingdom of Kohai, whose immortality experiment ended with its lake flash-boiling on the Day of Ashen Skies [wiki: Shamakhad; wiki: Halessi Timeline; wiki: Yanshen].
- **Harimar.** Tigers of the Ascension Peninsula became people on the Day of Ashen Skies (the hobgoblin name for that day is the **Godloss**). Harimar, a second-generation harimari, united them at 25 (35 AA), founded the Harimraj (50 AA), conquered every land that held High Temples, raised the Yansheni eunuchs to run it, and walked into the Tianlou temple and never came out [wiki: Harimari; wiki: High Temples; wiki: Halessi Timeline]. (Dates for the disappearance conflict; see §7.)
- **Jaher.** The Phoenix Empire's sun elves conquered much of Haless in the 11th century, ruled from Tianlou (Hall of the Rising Sun), were stopped at Thidinkai by the triple alliance of **Baihon Xinh (眉洪青)** at Fort Taibo, and left after Jaher was killed in Tianlou in 1127 by a Yanszin League assassin and his son Jaerel was also assassinated. Jaher was the first to say aloud that the temples were elven-built; Halessi have distrusted elves since [wiki: Haless; wiki: Yanshen; wiki: Tianlou; wiki: High Temples; wiki: Sunrise Empire].

#### A3. Rahen at 1444: the Second Harimraj
The **Dhenijanraj** (Second Harimraj, 1312–1562) is ruled from the Golden Palace of Dhenijansar; the throne is the **Lotus Throne**, the dynasty "of the Lotus Claw". Founded by **Ramapalar I "the Reunifier"**, who created the **Prabhi** (tax-collecting local lords) and eight **Senapti** (marcher lords owing soldiers) [wiki: Dhenijanraj]. Senapti at the start of the Vizierate wars: Rabhidaraj, Babhagama, Endhuvi, Parusad Bhola, Pasiragha, Pordhatti, Rabaghekur, Tiltaghar.

State of play in 1444:
- Raja **Indranayar I "the Idle"** (mod: born 1359, heir Indrapalar born 1436; "has reigned for 23 years" at the 1444 start, i.e. since c. 1421) delegates everything; he inherited from his heirless brother Vijayswanath I [eu4: history/countries/R51 - Dhenijanraj.txt; eu4: localisation/anb_startup_screen_l_english.yml `string_start_Dhenijanraj`; wiki: Dhenijanraj].
- The office of **Grand Vizier** (formalised by the Edict of 1396) was hereditary in **Amtujsaat** ("of the Golden Paw") until 1438, when Indranayar gave it to young **Manava of the Bloody Claw**, Senapti of **Rabhidaraj**. Amtujsaat has not forgiven this; the mod makes the two houses historical rivals [wiki: Dhenijanraj; eu4: history/countries/R52 - Rabhidaraj.txt, R41 - Amtujsaat.txt]. In the mod's 1444 files the vizier is **"Manava II of the Bloody Claw"** (born 1409, scripted death 1448; heir Narayar, born 1411) and Amtujsaat is ruled by **Jayapalar IV of the Golden Paw** (born 1377) [same files].
- **Bhuvauri (部缚乌离, "Free Land")**: in 1355 Sarhali plantation slaves of the Kharunyana delta rose under three leaders, took the great port of Sramaya and beat the Raj's army at Tanyatta. It is the Raj's standing humiliation — but **it is not an abolitionist state**. The wiki calls it "a country ruled by slaves who owned slaves"; the mod's start text calls it "the slave state of Bhuvauri" and opens with the jibe "'Free Land.' What mockery of freedom and language." Three families ("fictive kin") share power from Sramaya and all agree that "perpetuating slavery is paramount to their power": the rebels-turned-rulers kept trading in people and founded the **Adasa**, a military-bureaucratic caste of enslaved children raised to be loyal only to the state. Its mission texts speak of "dominance over the Raheni side of the slave trade" and name **Ishudhen** as the republic's own "crown jewel". Recent history: in 1443 Bhuvauri attacked its old rival **Bim Lau** and took the citadel of Bieng Phang, then the **Great Isudhen (Ishudhen) Typhoon** killed over 300,000 and wrecked its fleets — the mod starts it with heavy war exhaustion and a "distrustful populace". 1444 ruler in the mod: Barid szel-Azka, a general of Bulwari (Surani) culture (since 1443) [wiki: Grand Republic of Bhuvauri; wiki: Dhenijanraj; wiki: Guru Suhan; eu4: localisation/anb_startup_screen_l_english.yml `string_start_Bhuvauri`; eu4: localisation/Flavour_Bhuvauri_R38_l_english.yml; eu4: history/countries/R38 - Bhuvauri.txt]. (Dates: revolt begins 1355 per wiki Dhenijanraj and the mod; the Bhuvauri page gives independence in 1357.)
- The **Smothering Sickness** (dated 1414 in the mod's start text) killed about a fifth of Rahen, including Raja Amanapurna II and his son Golpalar II, who reigned 18 days [wiki: Dhenijanraj; eu4: anb_startup_screen `string_start_Dhenijanraj`].
- **Sarisung (娑梨孙伽)**, "the Heart of Haless" at the Kharunyana–Telkhram confluence, is a decayed great city run by gangs (mod: ruler "Haripant the Sly", gang-control republic) [eu4: history/countries/R21 - Sarisung.txt; eu4: localisation/Flavour_TheCommand_R62_l_english.yml].
- The **Oracle of Tughayasa (都伽耶娑)** still sits on its mountain (mod: theocracy under Devaraya Kamadeva) [eu4: history/countries/R05 - Tughayasa.txt].

What follows in canon: **Guru Suhan** (a harimari from Guhe in Yanshen, "a cat in a hat") preaches against slavery, patriarchy and rigid caste from the **Hall of Endless Debate**; it was in Sramaya, hearing from the storyteller Ajeeva how Bhuvauri's promise "had faded into the realities of slavery all too quickly", that he turned against slavery. His **Suhan's Praxis** splits the lords (timing conflict: his infobox dates the Praxis from 1450, the Dhenijanraj page has it spreading "amidst the chaos" of the First War of the Vizierate, 1481–87); the three **Wars of the Vizierate** (1481–1505) pit Rabhidaraj's "Greens" against Amtujsaat's traditionalist "Golden Banner"; **Indira of the Bloody Claw**, Manava's niece, becomes the first female Grand Vizier (1478) and dies leading her army at Janandana (1481); the Iron Stripes dynasty takes the throne; the Command invades at Pordhatti (1507); **Hemantsen I** and Rani **Jira of the Enlightened Star** reform the state (Sashtabari Codex) and beat the Command in the Second and Third Dhujat Wars; Rabhidaraj deserts the line at the First Battle of Ghamakrit (1562), Hemantsen dies, and the Jadd Empire (贾德帝国) takes most of Rahen [wiki: Dhenijanraj; wiki: Guru Suhan; wiki: The Command].

#### A4. The Adesi (Raheni caste system)
Properly **Adesi**; in force from the 1400s BA to the Jadd conquest of 1562. Two axes [wiki: Raheni Caste System]:
- **Prikarti** ("nature"): three strata — **Idisari** (upper: administrators, priests, warriors), **Sarstayri** (middle: merchants, many artisans), **Hitiyari** (lower: labourers). The state sees people only at this level (census, tax, levy).
- **Kija** ("toil"): thousands of groups defined by profession + birthplace + extended family + religious school, each with its own dress, diet, greetings and rituals, policed from below by village and tribal courts.
- Members of one Prikarti are **Samanaprikarti** ("equal-natured"): a king is only "first among equals", and pride beyond that is the stock justification for deposing rulers. Other strata are **Bhinnaprikarti**.
- **Keeping Kija** is a moral duty: bad observance damages one's own **svayatma** and the community's **tamatma**, which is blamed for war, famine and plague — hence expulsions and mob violence against the non-conforming. The world-balance is **Visvatma**; the moral way is the **Nitikamarja**.
- **Fluidity**: a child can be apprenticed and *adopted* into a master's kija; an adult needs a sponsor. A farmer who shines in the levy may be adopted into a captain's household and become Idisari — but he *ceases to be a farmer*; no farmer kija has ever been raised. Rigid states bind sons to the father's kija, or at least **Deviai** ("in god": within the same High God's domain). After 1136 the Golden Palace tradition calcified everything, and harimari over-representation among the Idisari hardened into a belief in harimari supremacy, strongest in the south. (The mod's Rahen start text puts the origin earlier and more bluntly: Harimar's son, in the **Edict of Sardiphadin**, reformed the caste system "placing their people at the top of this hierarchy, with humans often constrained to the middle and lower castes" [eu4: anb_startup_screen `string_start_Rahen`].)
- Levies are raised kija by kija because each has its own rites — "a dizzying number of religious obligations on a given day".

#### A5. Harimari
Bipedal tiger-folk, 1.9–2.3 m (males), ~70-year lifespan, yellow eyes with a green-yellow night-shine; rare white, golden or black coats. Retractable claws and an opposable dewclaw (good with pen and sword, bad at throwing); a roar that carries 3 km; strong swimmers; poor heat endurance, so nobles summer in the mountains. Scent is fashion: perfumes and musks matter as much as clothes. **Obligate carnivores** (a week without meat brings weakness, then hallucinations); they cannot taste sweetness, love umami and heavy spice, digest starch badly (brassicas and amaranth are the base of a meal) and cannot take milk, though ghee and yoghurt marinades are fine. Consequence: much of Rahen's meat is reserved for harimari and Raheni humans are largely vegetarian. Cultures: **Royal** (Dhenbasana rulers), **Primal** (Ascension jungle), **Serene** (Yanshen), **Roaming** (Thidinkai; Vandipha in Sarhal) [wiki: Harimari].

#### A6. Hobgoblins, Godlost and the Command
**People.** Red-orange skin, yellow eyes, long hair worn in buns, taller than Halessi humans. Their epic is the taking of **Grônstunad**, capital of the dwarven Jade Empire, in 2419 BA; the Mage-Chief Shakuin "the Iron Spider" (920s BA); wars with the goblin Kringar Bloodscrawl; the **Great Mage Crisis** (c. 440s–580s AA) in which Oni-taught mages led them to ruin [wiki: Hobgoblin; wiki: Jade Empire].

**Godlost.** After the Godloss the hobgoblins concluded the gods had abandoned them and their shamans had failed. Godlost is an atheist civic creed — "unity by strength, unity by family, unity by discipline, unity by order" — borrowing from High Philosophy. Disobedience is sin; all magic-users are "Shamans" to be killed, enslaved or chained [wiki: Godlost; wiki: The Command; eu4: localisation religion string `godlost`].

**The Command.** Three clan-commands confederated in **1392** — the mod's start text calls it the "Confederation of Commands, signed in blood in 1392", and wiki *The Command* and *Haless* agree; only the wiki *Godlost* stub says 1372 — as the **Great Command**: **Wolf** (狼令; logistics, planning, occupation government), **Boar** (猪令; rank and file, vanguard, discipline), **Lion** (狮令; generalship, diplomacy, rhetoric, officer education). The flag shows wolf, boar and lion. The **Grand Marshal** rotates between them (formalised by Jonozuke Lionborn after 1475); disputes are settled by duel, usually non-lethal. The mod: "there are hundreds of fortifications, a dozen major hobgoblin cities, three great warcamps. But there is only one War Room" [wiki: The Command; eu4: localisation/Flavour_TheCommand_R62_l_english.yml]. The War Room's hobgoblin name is the **Kenu-Ike**, "Settlement of Swords" (an event text also renders it "Sword-Home"); only marshals may enter it. The mod's epigraph: "The War Room is in agreement. The Great Campaign shall now commence." — Moguwon, "on conclusion of the Kenu-Ike held 1430". The Ruin Kingdoms' defensive league is called the "withering **Alliance of the Heroic Realms**" [eu4: localisation/anb_startup_screen_l_english.yml `string_start_TheCommand`; eu4: Flavour_TheCommand_R62].

**Slave states.** Goblin clans **Stolen Gem** and **Blackstep** work the Jade Mines; orc clans **Thunderfist** (雷拳) and **Bloodsong** (血歌) march as auxiliaries for near-suicidal assaults and then garrison the conquered. Soldiers eat **serpentbloom**, a bioluminescent cave fungus farmed by slaves. Later: half-orc craftsmen, and the unified goblin **Jade March** (翠玉边区, 1557) [wiki: The Command; eu4: Flavour_TheCommand_R62].

**Mages.** Chained in **korashi** (black damestear) bought from, then tortured out of, the Oni. The mod adds **the Faceless**: half-human "camp-children" mages in korashi-studded lamellar masks who undergo "public death" and are sent to chain other mages [eu4: Flavour_TheCommand_R62 `R62_the_faceless_desc`].

**The Invasion of Shamakhad (1431–1438)** [wiki: Hobgoblin Invasion of Shamakhad]: Ghupada Bend (1431); Sarilavhan stormed in weeks (1432) and handed to collaborators, the **Gerunanin**; Moguwon splits the commands — Wolf east (Banapada, Ghatasak), Boar south under **Kitokan Boarborn** (siege of Nakara), Lion west under **Barugi Lionborn** (Marukhad Sands). A relief army of Sir with 14,000 Bianfang troops forces a reunion at Khasardul; at **Ghilapur Crossing** (1434) Bianfang crosses first and abandons Sir, which falls after a five-month siege (1435); mercenaries and Xia adventurers are crushed at Anakhadakana (1437); cease-fire 22 Esmarment 1438.

**1444 start in the mod**: Moguwon Wolfborn (born 8 Feb 1396; 5/5/6; conqueror, tactical genius, reformist), capital Sarilavhan, magic banned, "Bloodsong advance guard" and "Thunderfist enforcers" active, rivals **Jiangliusi** and **Dhenijanraj** [eu4: history/countries/R62 - The Command.txt].

**Canon course after 1444** [wiki: The Command]: Revolt of the North in Sir (1445–47, backed by Bianfang and the Ruin Kingdoms) → **Xiadao Campaign** (Shanyutian; Liusibao besieged 1450–52; guerrilla war to 1461) → Shamakhad Campaign (1459–66; the great **ramp of Sarnihanpur**) → Moguwon dies 1475 → Reconstruction; the first **Wuhyun** (humans adopting hobgoblin ways: Azepyanunin, Kintonan, Ikaniwagain) → Azjakuma subjugated (1489–92) → First Dhujat War (1507–08, Elephant Gorge) → Sarisung (1510–11) → **Tiger Command** (虎令) and **Elephant Command** (象令, 1518) → Bianfang Campaign (1521–34) → Second/Third Dhujat Wars lost to Hemantsen → **Dragon Command** (龙令, 1540, war camp Chatzin) → **Ten Reforms** (1569) → Great Dhujat War (siege of Khiraspid 1578–80; "Charge of Phoenix and Tiger") → the century-long **Watering of Rahen** against the Jadd → Tianlou falls (1658) → Dragon's Peace → Rending (1751) → Oni return "a thousand severed heads" (1768) → **Great Insubordination** (1783–1806) → **Faiko's Folly** (1809) → dissolution, 1835, into Rinonungungen and warlords. A black-wolf-headed figure, believed to be Moguwon returned, is said to maul would-be Grand Marshals (1809–34).

Voice sample (Wuhyun creed): "do not lie, do not be insincere, do not be obsequious… be on good terms with comrades… with a strong sense of duty" — Chandra Malhotra, *On Honour* [eu4: Flavour_TheCommand_R62 `R62_the_wuhyun_desc`].

#### A7. Yanshen at 1444
Flat river country around the **Yanhe** and Lake Tianhu, governed largely by **eunuchs**, the administrative caste Harimar empowered [wiki: Eunuchs; wiki: Yanshen].
- **Tianlou (天楼)**: eunuch republic at the Yanhe's mouth around the largest High Temple in Haless; "Jewel of Yanshen", "Desire of Conquerors", "Gate of Smoke"; called cursed because both Harimar and Jaher ended there. The **Treasure Fleet** from Zurkanrek comes down the coast every ten years (first voyage 21 AA; the Tianlou page has it arriving in Tianlou that year, the Treasure Fleet and Haless pages say it only reached Tianlou c. 100 AA); after Tianlou expelled Zurkanreki-descended merchants in 872 and banned the fleets, the exiles founded the rival port **Beikdugang**, which celebrates the fleet while Tianlou stays "wary" of it but trades [wiki: Tianlou; wiki: Treasure Fleet]. Mod 1444 ruler of the eunuch republic: Sheng Ren (since 1440); historical rival Beikdugang [eu4: history/countries/Y19 - Tianlou.txt].
- **Bianfang**: fortress-kingdom of Upper Yanshen (江右), "one ruler, one law, one land". **Xinzhu Long**, "the White Dragon of the West" (born 1403), executed his own command staff at 20, burned the Zyujyut forests (1427), crowned himself in 1430, forced Xiyun's kings to be castrated and call him "uncle", and took Lamwun and Szicheng by betraying an ally (1439). His troops chant "Storm! Storm! Storm!" behind crossbows and cannon. He is the only Yan power that fought the Command — and abandoned Sir. Bianfang falls in 1534 [wiki: Kingdom of Bianfang; eu4: history/countries/Y09 - Bianfang.txt].
- **Jiangdu (江都) vs Lanjinhui (蓝巾会)**: the stratocracy of Jiangdu lost its countryside to the peasants' **Blue Scarf Rebellion** (cease-fire 1434); only the kobold state **Balrijin (金陵)** recognises the peasant republic. Balrijin is a federation of Goldscale clans under a Sage Council and Great Sage, worshipping the vanished gold dragon **Balris**, "He Who Shall Return" (arrived c. 500 AA). Yan folk treat goldscales as luck charms [wiki: Sage Council of Balrijin; wiki: Goldscale Kobold; eu4: localisation/Flavour_OneXia_Y99_l_english.yml]. Mod 1444 rulers: Lanjinhui — **Lan Wanxian** (born 1406, "peasant revolt leader", in office 1440); Jiangdu — the general **Qing Jun** (born 1422) [eu4: history/countries/Y05 - Lanjinhui.txt, Y13 - Jiangdu.txt].
- **Yinquan (隐泉)**: republic that took in survivors of boiled Kohai (苦海) and stays friendly to spirits; in the Rending it revives the fox-spirit Huszien and becomes **Lingyuk (灵域, "Spirit Domain")** [wiki: Rending of Realms; eu4: history/countries/Y26 - Yinquan.txt]. Its 1444 ruler in the mod is **Holi Leng** (female, born 1421, a mage; flagged `immortal_ruler`, with a "Huli Jing" fox-spirit patron) — the wiki's Yinquan stub also lists Holi Leng as ruler. Note that a small eunuch republic called **Kohai** still exists on the old lakebed in the 1444 mod (tag Y27, ruler Ding Gao) as Yinquan's "historical friend" [eu4: history/countries/Y26 - Yinquan.txt, Y27 - Kohai.txt; wiki: Republic of Yinquan].
- Also: the Vermillion Throne of Zyujyut (朱樾), the Eternal Council of Jinqiu (禁丘), Fortress of Hubao (虎堡), the Yanszin League (烟心) around Yanzhong.

#### A8. Xianjie and the Xia
The **Xiaken** are the temple-sects of Xianjie, each a small state: Jiangliusi (江流寺), Jianxusi ("Temple of the Sword"), Shanyutian, Fengzhaobu, Wudikmen, Yumaomen, Vidvakhoka, Jilin, Aoshan and more [wiki: Xiaken]. The leading sect holds the office of **Wulin / First Master**, seated in the House of Meditation at **Xiadao** ("central point of the universe"); in 1444 it is Jiangliusi under Master **Gao of the School of Flowing Water** [eu4: history/countries/Y28 - Jiangliusi.txt; eu4: Flavour_OneXia_Y99]. Schools are named "Way/School/Path of…": Flowing Water, Piercing Spear, Crouching Tiger, Drunken Fist, Gentle Fist, Deep Fog, Looming Shadow, Blazing Sun, Golden Core, Heavenly Armor, Cresting Phoenix, Formlessness, and the **Order of the Keepers** [eu4: localisation `xia_path_*`]. The Keepers guard **Kongren, the Void's Edge**, a Precursor spellblade the Oni coated in black damestear so that it cuts spells; a claimant must pass the Trial of Balance and learn the **Still Ocean Technique** [wiki: Kongren, the Void's Edge; eu4: Flavour_OneXia_Y99]. Unification makes **One Xia (一侠)** and ultimately the "Xianxia — an Immortal Xia… that will topple every tyrant".

In canon the Xia lose: the Command breaks them at Shanyutian "for lack of coordination as an army", Moguwon proclaims himself "rightful leader of the Xiaken by martial victory" (1452), and the south fights a decade of guerrilla war [wiki: The Command]. The hobgoblins "lost more than one band of Nosunin to mysterious forces" before grasping that chi is not shaman-magic [eu4: Flavour_TheCommand_R62 `R62_the_nature_of_chi_desc`].

#### A9. Chi, the Paths and damestear
- **Chi** is a real energy leaking from the Spirit Realm; every living thing holds some. Unlike arcane magic (Yan: *mofa/fa*; needs an inborn spark, an aristocratic privilege), chi is **endogenous and open to anyone** through breath, meditation and martial training; farmers know minor techniques. Outside Haless it is "like learning to swim in a stream", but a true master loses little by travelling [wiki: Chi].
- **Righteous Path** is the folk umbrella (sleep indoors and bury properly to keep your chi). **Accretive Path** (Yanshen, Xianjie): chi is yours; grow it to break reincarnation. **Transmutative Path** (Vimdatrong, Kai and Gon): chi is the world's will; perfect your role and be reborn exactly as yourself. **Devouring / Lefthand Path** (Oni, jiangshi): take it — "son eats father and student eats teacher". **Mystic Accord** (Lupulan, the old faith): spirits and people are equals; four pantheons Hebat Daga/Linga/Mahar/Tana. **High Philosophy** (*Darsanasara*): emulate the High Gods carved in the temples; debate is sacred; chi is *atma* [wiki: Righteous Path; Accretive Path; Transmutative Path; Devouring Path; Mystic Accord; High Philosophy].
- **Damestear** (Oni *teimarji*, Yan *lingshik*, elsewhere *tamphora*) is chi's "yang": powdered, it is a dangerous performance drug; **korashi** is the "yin" that makes outward chi and magic inert but cannot touch internal techniques. Chi-drain victims show something like depression, then die [wiki: Chi].

#### A10. The Oni of Azjakuma
Horned Ogres: 3 m, red skin, black or purple hair, glowing eyes; ascetic for giantkin because they feed on chi. Descended from Stone Ogres who failed a revolt in Nomsyulhan (wiki: c. 7325–7250 BA) and hid in **Shirgrii** — fortress-temple-libraries on the peaks. "Our Shirgrii have stood thousands of years and they shall stand for thousands more." They drained the spirit Hokuma for centuries, invented korashi, founded the Devouring Path, and hold archives said to include "the origin of monsters". Government: *Tagharoghi*; the ruler's title in the mod is **Rashenbir**. The mod's start text: society is organised around **four Shirgrii**, "home to castes of wise scholars, fierce warriors, cunning rogues and wealthy tyrants"; the 1444 Rashenbir **Tiraga Aromo** (born 1372, ruling since 1425) is "a proud traditionalist from Shinukhorchi, the oldest and greatest Shirgrii" who has turned the Oni inward, while his "ambitious heir" **Akira Surgoshi** (born 1415) resents the isolation; to the east the Shuvuushudi tribes have united under a warlord known only as the **Faceless Queen**; and the Oni consider striking "an accord" with the Command to buy time [eu4: localisation/anb_startup_screen_l_english.yml `string_start_Azjakuma`; eu4: history/countries/Y01 - Azjakuma.txt]. In 1433 Moguwon opened talks; in 1435 Oni envoys came down to negotiate peace and trade. Subjugated 1489–92; later made **stewards of every High Temple the Command conquers** and used the post to corrupt the hearts — half the cause of the Rending [wiki: Azjakuma; wiki: Horned Ogre; wiki: Hobgoblin Invasion of Shamakhad; wiki: Rending of Realms; eu4: history/countries/Y01 - Azjakuma.txt].

#### A11. Vimdatrong and the elves who stayed
**Baihon Xinh**, the Tamran triple alliance (Hon Sai, Mo Hoang, Nagon) that stopped Jaher, exists under Hoáng **Sang Caoban** (the wiki text writes "Caoban Sang"; born 1363 in the mod) until the War for the Mantle of Baihon Xinh (1449). He succeeded to Hon Sai in 1385, re-proclaimed the empire in 1400, lost six years in the Lupulan and lost the port of Cay Cam Sinh to Yansheni pirates. Then (the wiki gives no years; the mod still has him alive at the 1444 start, aged 81, with an heir Mahin flagged a "babbling buffoon" — so treat what follows as happening between 1444 and 1449) his three sons plotted to split the realm, he ordered them executed, and — heirless — had his mystics ritually kill him so that he returned as a vengeful ghost, the **"Ghost Emperor"** (in Hon Sai, the "Driven Emperor"). His youngest son Caoban Dinh escaped and raised Mo Hoang against "the abomination"; the ghost won the war but began to wither, lingering until Baihon Xinh was re-proclaimed after the anti-piracy war against Pinghoi (early 16th century), when the Visal dynasty took over [wiki: Empire of Baihon Xinh; eu4: history/countries/Y97 - Baihon Xinh.txt]. The mod's own start text for Baihon Xinh ("Alliance of the Three-Soul Soldier") adds dates: the Lupulan tribes revolted against the vassal king of Jaya Raya in 1433; "the Yan pirate queen **Ping**" took Cay Cam Sinh "in a fortnight" in 1439; and in 1444 Sang is still mortal — "there are rumors his sons plan to divide up his empire… If only there were a way to surpass the limits of mortality". (Azkare's start text, by contrast, already speaks of the Tamran "in the midst of a brutal civil war which transcends the boundaries of mortality" — the mod is not consistent on whether the ghost war has begun by November 1444.) [eu4: anb_startup_screen `string_start_baihon_xinh`, `string_start_Azkare`]. The victory over Jaher's legions (1080) was won by **Lemang Ca** of Hon Sai at the battle of Taibo, with dwarven artillery; the mod names the invading commander as Jaher's son "Elizar the Blooded". The **Bom** build tower-forts against prowling spirits and revere ancestors over nature spirits; their necropolis-city **Bim Lau** was raised by Sri Ang Rang, the first *Ascharapouk*. **Azkare (阿兹卡雷)**, a Kai township, is ruled by the sunrise elf **Hiderion Tonanzuir** (born 1369), who "had faithfully served several generations of Azkare lords" before being **elected** lord in 1438 of this, "the smallest of the four [Kai] townships". He reformed the state and in 1441 his new **Shining Lancers** — elite elven cavalry tracing their roots to Jaher's legion, based in the old Jaherian **Citadel of the Dawn** — beat the combined armies of Khao Elnak and Lo Ngoen at the **Battle of Lektonmai** [eu4: anb_startup_screen `string_start_Azkare`; eu4: history/countries/Y86 - Azkare.txt]. He dreams of a **Sunrise Empire (日升帝国)** uniting Haless under elven arbiters with a Sunrise Convocation — Chroniclers call it ahistorical. **Arawkelin (鸦老克林)** is a half-elven plutocracy that rebuilt its temples [wiki: Empire of Baihon Xinh; wiki: Bomdan; wiki: Sunrise Empire; wiki: Great Spirits; eu4: history/countries/Y86 - Azkare.txt, Y65 - Arawkelin.txt].

#### A12. The Rending of Realms (c. 1751–1783)

Smaraya falls in the **Typhoon of Broken Vases** (1699/1700); Tianlou's heart is taken by a party hired by a gambler's son during the Shengdao peace talks; Tughayasa then breaks. Great Spirits manifest; the Yanhe flood of 1759 kills over four million; Mystic Accord peoples make pacts, others raise **spirit-hunters** [wiki: Rending of Realms; wiki: High Temples; wiki: The Command]. ### 2B. THE FORBIDDEN PLAINS

#### B1. Land and long history
A steppe subcontinent north of the Serpentspine, reached from Cannor only by the frozen Northern Pass and from Bulwar by the Harpy and Invaders' Passes. **No spirits, no High Temples, never under elven rule.** Sub-regions: **Yarikhoi** (the lake peninsula), **Ogre Valley** (食人魔山谷), Nuzurbokh, Serpent Gift, the forest Yyl Möitsä. Lakes Kodarve (salt), **Yukelqur** (largest freshwater lake on Halann) and Zernuuk are the goddesses Kodave, Yukel and Enuuk [wiki: Forbidden Plains].
Eras: a demon, the **Fleshmaw**, sealed underground by Fire Giants who siphoned it; Fire Giant terraforming fails c. 6000 BA and their ogres eat them; human tribes; the **Centaur Exodus** (587–574 BA) drives humans onto Yarikhoi; human resettlement after the Great Incursion. "Mawcursed" still appear [wiki: Forbidden Plains].

#### B2. Centaurs
Fey-made in the Oldwoods of Escann (creator or patron: the Archfey Rohibos); declared Spawns of Agrados in 600 BA and purged by Castan II Beastbane. They **cannot be ridden** (an involuntary, violent reaction to weight on the spine) and pull carts instead; two hearts, two sets of lungs; childbirth is the commonest cause of death for women. God: **Irdaeos** (Shirdos), sky, wind and movement — harming a bird is taboo, sedentary work is stigmatised, clans dig geoglyphs for the sky to read; paradise is **Ik Magthaal**. Sects: Maslekhuga, Irdukhuga, Malukhuga, and the militarist Filustkhuga (death in battle is the only way in). Shamans work fire, lightning, wind and ice. Politics: clan-head → **ordsikar** of an **ordsiwyn** → **caehn** of a **caehnate**, all elected for life [wiki: Centaur]. Earlier trade roads: the **Iron Chain** (ogre iron to the lakes — "really a Honey Road") and the **Amber Road** to Bulwar (ended 713).
The **Great Incursion** (1433–1440): a caehn of the **Wild Hunt (狂猎)** used the harshest winter in Triunic memory; "more than a dozen" shamans froze the lakes into ice bridges. Metsamic **Jolgik Ut** agents assassinated the shamans one by one (last in 1439); the trapped hordes were slaughtered. The caehn's name is not given on the wiki; a mod idea text speaks of surpassing "the legacy of Dakarag of the Wild Hunt, and perhaps even of Naegol himself", so **Dakarag** is probably he — [unverified] inference, do not state as fact [eu4: localisation/anb_forbidden_lands_l_english.yml `CENTAUR_valorous_caehnate_desc`]. In 1444 the Wild Hunt is led by Khaimalk III Roaringstorm and "tens of thousands, possibly hundreds of thousands" are dead [wiki: Centaur; eu4: localisation/anb_forbidden_lands_l_english.yml `centaur.200.d`; eu4: history/countries/I78 - Wild Hunt.txt].

#### B3. The Lake Federation (later Kalsyto 湖地联邦)
Three peoples — **Khamgunai** (first settlers, 680 BA), **Metsamic** (taiga refugees in fortified cities), **Zabatlari** (former steppe warlords, now merchants) — in dozens of city-republics. Ratified 1441 at **Haraigizenhi Bulrek**, the treaty city famous for the drink *haraigh*. Each people had its own lake-goddess before Kalyin: Zabatlari — **Yukel** (trade; the central, largest lake; mother of the other two), Metsamic — **Kodave** (the warrior, in a steel-and-mail helmet; the western lake), Khamgunai — **Enuuk** (manufacture, white-veiled; the eastern lake) [wiki: Lake Federation; eu4: anb_forbidden_plains_startup `string_start_religion_triunic`]. Founding trauma: the **Tyrant Queen** (Xāravalāna N'pōtsoit, born Xānātha; the mod's start text has her rise in 274 BA, her wiki page gives a reign of 294–250 BA; she killed her brother and the rest of her family to take the throne), a mage-queen who sealed kidnapped souls into homunculi in search of immortality; nobles and mages sold out the people, commoners burned the **City of Tyranny** (no body was found, so she is the lakes' bogeyman; survivors founded Narkel and its mage-watching **Onyxguard**), and mages have been distrusted ever since ("1500 years" on the Lake Federation page, "2000" on hers). Hence: "there will never be kings on Yarikhoi." [wiki: Lake Federation; wiki: Tyrant Queen; wiki: Forbidden Plains; eu4: localisation/anb_forbidden_plains_startup_l_english.yml].
The "Era of Crises" (1441–1625): War of the Three Goddesses (1449–58, over an iron deal between goddesses); **Rebellion of Frosted Glass** (Qarshtuluu 双玉邦 frees itself from expansionist Vahjevgiiv 万仞岌, 1470–74); Ultakal Crisis (the only gold mine and mint, 1490–96); **Revelation of Kalyin** (1525–47: the three goddesses are faces of one); **the Seiche** (tsunami of 1559); **Arcane Ponderance** (1572–76: mages may gather again under the **Onyxguard** of Narkel); Qarshtuluu Constitution and the Republic of Kalsyto, 1625. Later: **Orachav** frontier settlers, contact with Grombar, and the isolationist **Egdüqon**.
Economy: no good iron, superb soil; rye "is president"; **Qarshtuluu glass**; cyclops-forged artefacts; the Treasure Fleet sells mystique.

#### B4. Ogres of Maghargma (苍空之父)
Fathide ogres of the Ogre Valley; ruler title **Mengur'm**, "Lord of All Ogres"; faith **Iskerag Vulshor** (the mod and the country infobox call the religion **Feast of the Gods**; souls stagnate without constant consumption). **Ziltagh Gravelhide** (throne 1440 per the wiki and the mod's start text, after the centaur invasion "slew Maghargma's ruler Borigg Gravelhide in battle"; the mod's history file dates his reign from 1 Jan 1438; born 1410, heir Chike, a "drunkard") — "a massive ogre, who crushed centaurs whole with a single blow". The kingdom was founded by Ghornma Gravelhide around a crashed **Sky Giant fortress** (mod: 28 AA; wiki infobox: 1 AA), which is still the capital; the mod's title for a ruler of all ogres is *Mengurkha* [eu4: localisation/anb_forbidden_plains_startup_l_english.yml `string_start_ogre_kingdoms_description`]. Ziltagh baits rival Great Clans (Iltklerd, Kvakolba, Olkhajukh, Remlygenjrot, Achtejardon, Garsvulshim) into wasting armies on centaurs and Wild Ogres, then summons the six *Ojkagmas* to a **Nykha Tolutsdi** (ogre New Year) feast ringed by the royal army and demands their lands. From the 1500s: Mossmouth ogre refugees from the Serpent's Vale; the goblin **Web Cleaner** clan arriving on pack-spiders and accepted as the Mengur'm's personal slaves (harming them is destroying royal property; they live in ogre attics); friendship with Grombar; eager industrialisation. Ogres keep **kelaktars** (clan strongholds) with apiaries and flower gardens, and herd **Dire Boars** [wiki: Kingdom of Maghargma; wiki: Forbidden Plains; eu4: history/countries/I90 - Maghargma.txt].

Fauna for encounters: rocs, plains drakes, tlalusk, hyaenodons that hunt centaurs, flute-horned shadhavar, manticores east and griffons west of the Serpent Gift, *kircha ot'laf* ("grabbing grass"), drink-stealing dmarvuk [wiki: Forbidden Plains].

### 2C. AELANTIR

#### C1. Precursor Empire and the Day of Ashen Skies
Aelantir means "Oldhome". **Source status matters here.** On the wiki's Precursor Empire page only two things are firm: the lead ("earliest traced evidence dates the start of precursor elf civilization at 10,000 BA") and the Technology section, which is the part labelled "new canon Jay stuff". The dated timeline that follows sits under the banner "Everything below is notes and not to be taken as canon!!!" and is marked TODO — use it as the best current draft, not as settled fact: **War of Broken Chains** ends c. 10,000 BA; Age of Light from 8000 BA; Emperor Arethon dies 6229 BA, his son **Aldan** issues the **Call for Reflection** (6200 BA) recalling all elves within 1,000 years, while Aldan's brother **Ducaniel** fights on and releases the orcs (6000 BA). What the mod itself confirms: the elves were slaves of the Dragon Scions in the "Rule of Dragons" until **Venaan**, one of the Dragonguard (elite warrior-slaves taught magic), killed his draconic master and set off the revolt; murals in the fragment chain "confirm" the War of Broken Chains [eu4: localisation/anb_precursor_fragment_l_english.yml]. Per the canon tech notes, Precursors were immortal, glowing-eyed, an **artisan, purely magical** society — no guns, no machines; mages power everything. Six **flying cities** ruled regions and collected tribute: Aesadas (capital), Dalaire, Tacenie, Affelai, Harathíl, Lanaiande. Writers' rule: Precursors are "the setting's mythic aliens"; abroad they appear as gods (hence Haless's High Gods) [wiki: Precursor Empire; wiki: Precursor Elf; wiki: Precursor Flying Cities].
The **War of Two** (Ducaniel and his followers versus the Empire) ended with Ducaniel jailed in Aesadas. Its cause, per the mod's fragment chain: the "forced union" of Ducaniel with his niece the **Elfenbride**, which produced a hidden son, Rinetrin "the Changeling Prince" [eu4: anb_precursor_fragment `fragments.303.d`, `tooltip_elfenbride_tablet`; wiki: Rinetrin]. (That she was Aldan's daughter and was kidnapped at her coming-of-age comes only from the wiki's section marked "deprecated and old lore".) On 1 Castanmark, 1 AA his followers crashed the city; the deaths fed **Ducaniel's Wrath**, which carved the circular **Ruined Sea (毁灭之海)** and Cliffs of Ruin, split the continent, tore openings to the Shadow Plane and Feyrealm, and warped survivors. World effects: a century of ash, floods, easier arcane magic, the harimari, the banishment of genies. The **Remnant Fleet** wandered 1,000 years before landing in Cannor [wiki: Day of Ashen Skies; wiki: Remnant Fleet].

#### C2. Ruinborn
"Ruinborn" covers every surviving elven people with a human lifespan; "degenerated elves" are the monstrous ones (Bloodfeeders, Silent Watchers, Cursed Ones, Leechmen, Ice Sleepers, Seedthralls) [wiki: Ruinborn Elf]. Most Ruinborn fear open water.

**North Aelantir**
- **Bloodgroves (血林) / Kwineh (奎内赫).** Trees bleed red sap in winter. The land enforces **Laws-by-Blood** — honour oaths and truth; give back what you take; bring no ruin to the groves — and breakers are cursed, at worst into Bloodfeeders. The spirit **Weeping Mother** speaks through shamans called **Ishtanums**, who also write blood-contracts with the land. Shell-bead money, blood-sap tattoos, yearly gathering at the mouth of the Sella for the festival of Imar'el. 1444 chief: Meyal'e Apolikaypel [wiki: Bloodgroves; eu4: history/countries/G05 - Kwineh.txt].
- **Ynn (洇河).** Castle-and-knight feudalism along a great river that ends at the waterfall **Venaan's Tears**. The **Ynnic Empire** (625–1205; capitals Vareynn, Stanyrhrada, Arverynn) is gone; **Calrodiy IV Vyrekynn** (born 1372) has ruled Arverynn (阿韦洇) since 1386 as claimant emperor. The timeline styles him "the last scion of the Vyrekynn imperial dynasty", but the line does not end with him: he has a capable son and heir, **Carodin II** (born 1414), and a grandson Calrodiy V; he was probably born in exile at Stenurynn (the dynasty had not ruled since 1205), retook Arverynn with Stenurynn's troops and money, and his enemies call him a fake heir planted by the yen Stenur. He is a spendthrift party-giver, not a warrior. In 1443–44 the teenage lord of Trompolere, **Alaran yen Cestor** (born 1427), beat Fadhevych, crushed Arverynn's army at Les Vethrany, drove off Lord Tranvit III of Stanyrhrada, and was bought off by Calrodiy with the whole imperial treasury (the **Peace of Camir's Gate**); in **Alaran's War** he took land from all three and is now planning to attack Vels Bacar. Alaran does not live to restore the empire: his wiki infobox has him "slain in battle by Artoray yen Bacar", and the mod scripts his death for 1 May 1446. Calrodiy dies in 1448 (mod: 1447) [wiki: Calrodiy IV; wiki: Alaran yen Cestor; eu4: history/countries/G35 - Arverynn.txt, G36 - Trompolere.txt]. Faith: **Ynn River Worship** — the river is god; the dead go downstream in coffins on **funeral barges**, and Sarda lords tax them ("Bridge Trolls"). Knights **crop the points of their ears** to fit helmets. Rivals: **Drozma Tur**, the dragon cult of Rzenta (任塔). Its god **Drozmagog**, "last dragon on Aelantir", was found crippled in 1017, made to fight in Arverynn's arena, freed in 1028 (Great Fire of Arverynn), saved Vysamsto (renamed Svemel) in 1154, and was slain in 1371 by the knight Artorean Uma-Zomaj — leaving a hatchling, **Varlengeilt** ("Green Terror"), whose custody Rzentur lords fight over. Canon future: Istralorian Crusade (1622), **Dragon Dominion** (巨龙领域, 1627) [wiki: Ynn; Ynnic Empire; Ynnic Timeline; Drozmagog; Varlengeilt; Dragon Dominion; Ynn River Worship; Sarda; eu4: history/countries/G35, G36].
- **Eordand.** Survivors sheltered inside the **Domandrod**, a forest half in the Feyrealm, and were reshaped by the fey. Five peoples: **Selpheregi** (Spring; flowers grow on their bodies; city Arakeprun), **Caamas** (Summer; sailors), **Tuathak** (Autumn; leaf-coloured skin, disciplined, hate Winter), **Snecboth** (Winter; pale-blue, semi-nomadic), **Peitar** (Eordellon balance; zealots for the Fey Lords), plus the exiled **Fograc**. The archfey who overthrew the **Unthroned Queen** agreed that whichever court's mortals unify Eordand wins leadership of the seasonal fey — the wars are a proxy contest. State of play at the 1444 start (mod): the Tuathak **Hibernal Crusade** against Winter "saw great success in the early 15th century" (founding Jhorgashirr and Slegcal) until the Strutmar Peitar killed its leader Alwaron Winterswrath; the Peitar of Iadth have seized the Autumn Court's capital Dorcurt in a surprise invasion, and island Einnsag claims the Autumn mantle; in 1435 Manaron Goldtree's rebellion tore Elchos and nearly half its domain away from Spring-court Arakeprun before the spellcaster Filnar Dawnfire stopped him; and "the child prodigy **Immarel Winterswrath**" (born 1420, ruling the Winter-court order-state of Gemradcurt since 1431; an evoker with some necromancy) has just taken charge. Her **Red Winter** — the necromantic civil war that splits Gemradcurt — is still to come in 1444, not a past event; in wiki canon it ends with Jahainar Truespear's triumph over her and his Eordellon kingdom of Darbait [eu4: localisation/anb_startup_screen_l_english.yml `string_start_eordand_*`; eu4: history/countries/H16 - Gemradcurt.txt; eu4: Flavour_Gemradcurt_H16; wiki: Early Modern Eordand Timeline]. Later canon: the Final Eordellon Crusade (1559–73) fails and Arakeprun's ruler is named Champion of the Domandrod Fey [wiki: Eordand; wiki: Early Modern Eordand Timeline; eu4: localisation religion string `eordellon`].
- **Haraf (哈拉夫).** Grey-skinned desert elves in **Kinah** (houses); the Third Kinah Federation under House Alas'gasidi (1178–1731; 1444 leader Da'kino Alas'gasidi). House Ch'oosh'marah hunts sandworms. The **Mayte** wear spirit-masks (**Kalun Masks**) made from the faces of Silent Watchers killed bare-handed, and choose kings by wrestling for the King Mask of Xogareil [wiki: Haraf'ne Kinah; wiki: Kalun Masks; eu4: history/countries/G43 - Haraf.txt].

**South Aelantir**
- **Effelai (伊菲莱).** A single hive-mind organism grown from the crash of the flying city Affelai, covering much of the north of the southern continent. It sings; it takes minds (**Seedthralls**); singing calms it; fire drives it back and enrages it. Natives: Effe'i, the giant Lai'i ("troll-elves"), Parura, Thamvoi, Oono'i, and the Dughui'hi who fight it with mushroom artificery. Mod event chain **Effelai Bloom**: every surface flowers, expeditions vanish, and the court must choose between burning it "root and stem" and attunement to "our Goddess" [wiki: Effelai; eu4: localisation/effelai_bloom_l_english.yml].
- **Amadia (阿玛迪亚).** The Precursors' damestear mine, worked by summoning meteorites; craters everywhere. The Ruin left **prismatic damestear** and **wild magic**; every Amadian can cast cantrips and bears scars that glow when magic surges; magic is feared. Old capital Cara Lafquen; faith Rioqay Diú [wiki: Amadia].
- **Taychend.** Most populous region of Aelantir; brown-skinned elves who can grow beards. Ruled before the Ruin by **Precursor Slaver-Nobles**; since Ebenanth the First Emperor it has been trapped in a **Cycle of Warlordism**. Faith: **Hero Cults** — anyone can become a god through glory, chiefly in war; *Periyodhai* is the institutional feuding; **Relic-Lords** wield Precursor weapons. In 1444 **Erankar the Defiant** (Kaonrippavar, "unifiers"; the one member of Urvand's royal house to escape Enlarmai's sack of the city — 1419 on the wiki, "the 1420s" in the mod, when he was nine; then a mercenary in a Gophiran company that he took over) has reunited Larankar (拉兰卡尔) — he retook Urvand in 1433, killed Enlarmai's king, and declared Larankar united in 1435 — and has just won Sheranu's War (late 1444). He takes the Kalavend by 1452; that year Laskaris first marches against him (the battles of Macriavolo); at the ford of **Masialendi** (1455) he gravely wounds Laskaris in the "Duel of the Two Heroes" and Queen Harivilludash of Kattisangamar's cavalry breaks the Ameioni army; Laskaris's last campaign (1460–61) shatters Erankar's field army but ends with Laskaris dead in sight of Nanru Nakar; Erankar is crowned **Amarawor** there in 1465 and dies in his sleep in 1503, aged 92 [wiki: Wars of Two Heroes; wiki: Larankarha Empire]. His enforcers are the **Amapradhana**, including the Orphaned Brotherhood. Larankarha grandees stretch their earlobes with rings and **bells** — the sound warns of sabre-men. The oracle **Fateweaver of Korrimutren** gives prophecies [wiki: Taychend; wiki: Larankarha Empire; eu4: history/countries/G64 - Larankar.txt; eu4: localisation religion string `gods_of_the_taychend`].
- **Kheionai (刻伊俄奈).** Teal-skinned, curly-haired elves of seven **Kheions** on Alecand — Precursor **prisons** (possibly for Ducaniel's supporters) whose **Zopedan / Windwards**, "the most powerful arcane wards known", saved them from the **Kaydhano Deathwinds**. City-republics with senates; outsiders are *bharbhen*. Taboos: wasting food, refusing shelter during a Kaydhano (even to an enemy army, once disarmed), swimming without copper. Naming caution: the wiki calls the home island **Alecand**, the EU4 mod's start text calls it **Deirós**. The mod adds that the Kaydhano is a storm of magically contaminated **blue sand** from a desert the Ruin made (it causes lethal growths and gave the Kheionai their blue-teal skin), and gives the 1444 situation: the western "barbarian" **Eltibhari** have united under one king and prepare to invade; **Kimánis** has just been partitioned between Degakheion and Oktikheion, who watch each other under an uneasy truce; of the overseas colonies (*nékheis*) only Amgremós is fully independent; and rumours arrive of Ameion's Laskaris, "with the goal of taking the great city of Nanru Nakar" [eu4: localisation/anb_startup_screen_l_english.yml `string_start_kheionai`]. The colonial republic of **Ameion (阿墨翁)** is ruled by Kylakas **Laskaris the Conqueror** (born 1399, half-Basobhar bastard), who believes the Fateweaver showed him the year of his death and so cannot die before it — the prophecy as quoted: "Five dozen and one years marks your time upon this world, / No arrow nor sword, no ill nor folly shall take you for your allotted time". Kylakas since 1430/1432 (the page gives both), he has been in Taychend since 1434 (Anakal annexed 1434; a Tirsin–Sthanan coalition beaten in 1435, when he killed the Gophiran king Dhalinar III in single combat and Korrimutren submitted; Kannalulthe taken 1438; cease-fire with the mage-ruled Nagar Vyechei 1439). In autumn 1444 he has just returned from a stay in Ameion and invades Imarchend early in 1445. With his companions the *Simegianes* he conquers southern Taychend and dies outside Nanru Nakar in 1461 — "of unknown causes", found by a tree stump overlooking the city, on his own page; "on the battlefield by Erankar" in one line of the Wars of Two Heroes page. The Ambhen Empire dominates the south for 300 years [wiki: Kheion; Kheionai Ruinborn Elf; Laskaris the Conqueror; Republic of Ameion].
- **Devand (德万) / Mteibhar.** Descendants of slaves led out of Taychend by **Munakles**, who killed his master with the sword he renamed **Saerasyar, the Green-Flamed Sword**. Freedom is their creed; the Chendhyans have bronze-sheened skin and wear at the waist a great feline pelt marking their first victory as an adult [wiki: Devandi Ruinborn Elf; wiki: Ruinborn Elf].

#### C3. Cannorian colonisation and what it sets off
- **1490**: Endral the Explorer, a Redglades elf in Lorentish pay, finds **Endralliande** — lush, empty, no dangerous animals — source of the fog **Uelos' Sigh** [wiki: Rediscovery of Aelantir; wiki: Endralliande].
- **Venáil (维奈尔)**, the island elfrealm of those who stayed behind in 1000 AA: in 1444 an oligarchic republic under **Aldarian I Seawatcher** (born 1144), with admiral **Calasandur Seawatcher**; the old captain **Alariandel**, "born aboard a ship crossing a storm", leads the exploration. Missions: a "100 Years' Loan" from Damescrown, **New Dawn Harbour**, the migration of the whole population, the **Teal Armada** [eu4: history/countries/A21 - Venail.txt; eu4: localisation/Flavour_Venail_A21_l_english.yml].
- **Aelnar**: Venáili "reclaimers" exterminate Bloodfeeders and drive out Kwineh and Sorncósti settlers; Aldarian proclaims the Empire in the 1550s (capital Sella). After his assassination comes the **Rianvisa** ("Enlightenment"). Mod factions: the soldier **Kayd** (wiki: Kayd Obaith), the military reformer **Sicrheior** (Sicrheior Rainsquall), the priest-orator **Dahvar** (Dahvar Siltar), the leveller **Aelthanas** the glass-worker (Aelthanas Freeman), the warrior order **Orda Silblaidd** ("Children of the Blade"), and the people's "Heroine" **Lithiel Oathkeeper**, first Venáili to set foot on Aelantir. Lithiel wins, then becomes **Elissa**: in the mod she falls into a freezing sleep muttering the name, an assassin's blade wakes "the voice of madness" wearing her skin, the palace Arca Ore is shattered in ice, and the **Crystal Queen** uses Ruinborn as "living batteries". The people rename themselves **Star Elves**. Wiki canon: her purification plan is leaked by the Ynnic adventurer **Erandil Poison-Arrow** (1615); the **Trollsbay Concord** and Lorent destroy Aelnar by 1630; she escapes in the flying city **Estadaran**, which still floats over the Dalairey Wastes in 1836 [wiki: Empire of Aelnar; eu4: localisation/Flavour_Aelnar_Z43_l_english.yml].
- **Trollsbay (巨魔湾)**: republics of Cestirmark, Marlliande, Zanlib, Isobelin, Thílvis and Valorpoint found the Concord in 1595 (ratified 1630); plantation economy long built on orc slaves; **Bramash the Liberator**'s orc slave revolt on Endralliande brings Lorentish annexation of the island (1567), and the orcs who escape found free **Ozgarom** [wiki: Trollsbay Concord; wiki: Empire of Aelnar].
- **Jaherian Exemplars**: sun-elf knights fleeing the Jaddari conquest of Bulwar sail the migrant ship **Diranhriá** to Katelperai (1516) under **Ecaris Ecarizuir**. Their talk of abolition and "the last of a group of now-dead gods" turns the Cult of Vherenar into the militant **Oren Nayiru**; the **Scouring of Katelperai** (1519) and the fall of Larankar (1521) follow; they found **Rezankand** (雷赞坎德, 1562) [wiki: Exemplar Expedition; wiki: Rezankand].
- **Harafic Triarchy**: Gommoport (gnomes), Zurzumexia (kobolds), Mestikardu (goblins), settled mid-16th century, united mid-17th [wiki: Harafic Triarchy].

#### C4. Precursor relics and the seven great secrets
Relics are found in bulk deposits (warehouses, cities), not lone chests; the world supply peaks and runs dry within the era [wiki: Ancient Relics; eu4: localisation/anb_precursorrelics_l_english.yml]. The mod's expedition chains [eu4: localisation/anb_precursor_fragment_l_english.yml]:
1. **The Glowing City** — a city carved from one damestear meteorite and deliberately buried; workers vanish or return "deeply changed".
2. **Realm of the Hidden Dragon** (Amadia) — a pocket plane where the last dragon sheltered loyal Dragonguard; raiders are told "You have meddled with our realm for thousands of years… and now you invade? The day after he died?"
3. **The Elfenbride's Manse** (north) — reached by following a rabbit; a preserved garden-palace of "Aelantir-that-was"; she is gone.
4. **The God Fragment** — murals show Castellos was "Castellar, a precursor god" and is dead; under a hatch marked with a diamond symbol floats a fist-sized gem that speaks. **Torrieth** alone understands it: "We may have found the Birthplace of Magic itself." Wiki dates this to 1585 and makes Torrieth Godspeaker first High Rector of the **Ravelian Church**.
5. **The Vault** — an aetheric monitor replays the voices of sheltering Precursors (one named Urion sings *The Secret Domain*); they killed each other long ago.
6. **The Pillar of the Heavens** — a portal nexus to other planets of an interplanetary Precursor Empire (two destinations match the moon and the planet Agradent), sealed from the far side by the colonies themselves. When: the mod's logbook says "many millennia before even the Ruin"; the wiki's Precursor Empire page says "a century before" — the sources disagree, so do not fix a date.
7. **The Jungle's Soul** — Chronicler Teagan Cottersea reaches jewel-like Affelai in the heart of the Effelai.

### 2D. INSYAA AND BEYOND
**Insyaa (隐厦)** lies between Haless and Aelantir behind the **Kiamoa**. Peoples: Anakue, Yanacotle, Utubodi, Yenbenmirra, Taga'ala humans; Charkuchin and Sirimadsik kobolds; Skyfall and Seacrest ogres; **Mechanim** (Precursor-built metal people with a damestear core, the **Kauga**; ageless, cannot cast spells but can use chi; the Insyaan ones reawakened in 1366); and **Dragon Turtles** who came from Yanshen in 6400 BA and serve as living memory. Faiths: **Speaking Memory** (you live while a living being remembers you), **Endless Ocean** (the afterlife can be sailed to), **Volcanic Truth** (volcano-gods Muatlaqua and Quahopilo). Magic is song and story — Flamesong, Wavesong, Stonescrawl, Stonestory — weak but army-wide. Writing is knotted cord (**Okalu**). From the 1470s the hunter-republic Kotli'komhitzi produces **Cuāitlak**, blood-ritual immortals. The mod has ~100 Insyaan tags (N-series) [wiki: Insyaa; Insyaan Magic; Mechanim; Dragon Turtles; Speaking Memory; Endless Ocean; Volcanic Truth; Cuāitlak; Yenbenmirra; eu4: history/countries N00–N99].

## 5. Plot threads with canon basis

1. **The Wolf's Century (1444 Haless spine).** The revolt in Sir (1445) is "the first major instance of the conquered rebelling" and the Command means to make its answer "a clear one". A party can run guns for Siri exiles, carry Bianfang's deniable aid, serve as Gerunanin, or sit in the War Room. Decision points: the Shaman Question, the fate of the Oracle of Tughayasa, whether chi is "magic". [wiki: The Command; eu4: Flavour_TheCommand_R62]
2. **The machine under the temples.** In 1444 the Oni come closest to understanding the High Temples (their Devouring Path lore already calls them bridges that keep the spirits trapped on the far side [wiki: Horned Ogre]); that nobody else does is this dossier's reading, not a quoted fact. Harimar vanished in Tianlou; the heart-door has nine gem slots keyed to other temples; Kongren cuts spells. A campaign can make the Rending something the players prevent, delay, or cause three centuries early. [wiki: High Temples; Great Spirits; Rending of Realms; Azjakuma]
3. **Lotus Throne and Free Land.** An idle Raja, two vizier houses on the road to civil war, a republic of freed slaves on the delta that now buys and drills slaves of its own (and has just been flattened by a typhoon in the middle of a war with Bim Lau), a prophet about to preach, plague in living memory (1414). Whoever wins inherits the Command's invasion. [wiki: Dhenijanraj; Raheni Caste System; Guru Suhan]
4. **One Xia.** Unite the temples by duel or by "humble unity" before the hobgoblins arrive; earn or seize Kongren. [eu4: Flavour_OneXia_Y99; 01_Xiaken]
5. **The Republic of the Lakes.** Three years old, founded on the grave of the Great Incursion. Threads: hunt or protect surviving ice-bridge shamans; the Tyrant Queen's homunculi; Vahjevgiiv's grab for Qarshtuluu; Ziltagh's feast; the sealed Fleshmaw. [wiki: Lake Federation; Forbidden Plains; Kingdom of Maghargma]
6. **The Two Heroes.** Erankar, who rejects fate, against Laskaris, who believes fate has made him unkillable until a known year — with the Fateweaver's Threadbearer holding the truth. In 1444 both are alive and conquering but have not yet met: Laskaris returns to Taychend that autumn, their armies first clash in 1452, the duel at Masialendi is 1455, and the prophecy runs out in 1461. [wiki: Larankarha Empire; Laskaris the Conqueror; Wars of Two Heroes]
7. **Oldhome.** Venáil prepares to return (canon discovery 1490): Reclamation → Aelnar → Rianvisa → the Heroine becomes Elissa; Kwineh blood-contracts; a seventh free republic that never comes to be (Sornicande). [eu4: Flavour_Venail_A21; Flavour_Aelnar_Z43; wiki: Empire of Aelnar; Bloodgroves]
8. **The seven secrets and the dead god.** The fragment chains read as a ready quest line, ending in a talking shard of a god and a church. Links to the Halessi machine: Nomsyulhan's portal fed the same network as the Pillar. [eu4: anb_precursor_fragment; wiki: Nomsyulhan; Torrieth Godspeaker]
9. **Seasons as chess.** Eordan wars are an archfey succession contest; a party can find the Unthroned Queen, or the proof that Iadthi crusaders poach in the Domandrod. [wiki: Eordand; Early Modern Eordand Timeline]
10. **The dragon's ward.** Alaran's War and the scramble for the hatchling Varlengeilt, in a land where river-priests tax the dead. [wiki: Ynnic Timeline; Drozmagog]

Pointers for the other two campaigns: **1022** — Drozmagog fights in Arverynn's arena (captured 1017, freed 1028, when he burns the city for three days and Emperor Camar I launches the Lovdrakan dragon-hunt); the Ynnic Empire (625–1205) is intact and ruled from Arverynn — the sources do not call this its height; Jaher's eastern campaigns into Haless are about to begin (wiki dates vary, 1038–1090s); the Command system does not yet exist. **1836** — Rinonungungen and warlords (Yodashikyu under Mibatsu "the Rat", Nadiraji, Dragon Command), Lingyuk, Kalsyto ending the Egdüqon, industrial Maghargma, the Federal Concord, Aelnar reduced to floating Estadaran [wiki: The Command; Lake Federation; Trollsbay Concord; Empire of Aelnar; vic3: localization/english confirms these country names].

## 7. Uncertainties and source conflicts

- **Founding of the Command**: wiki *The Command*, wiki *Haless* and the mod's start text ("Confederation of Commands, signed in blood in 1392"; "unification of The Great Command in 1392") all give **1392**; only the wiki *Godlost* stub gives 1372 (the mod's Godlost religion text gives no date — an earlier draft of this dossier said otherwise). Wiki *Hobgoblin* dates the "Command system" to 1136–1200, wiki *The Command* to 1136–1392. Use 1392.
- **Moguwon's accession**: wiki infobox 1421 (after the first Grand Marshal's death in 1420); the mod simply lists him as monarch at the 1444 start. Followed the wiki.
- **Azjakuma's capital**: Shinukhorchi (wiki: Azjakuma) vs Chumijemoya (wiki: The Command, Horned Ogre). Best reading: **Shinukhorchi is a shirgrii, not a city** — the mod's start text calls it "the oldest and greatest Shirgrii", the Azjakuma page's epigraph is credited to "an Oni guard of the Shinukhorchi shirgrii", and the Command page has the hobgoblins seize "the libraries Shinukhorchi"; Chumijemoya is the capital besieged in 1489–92. (The mod file's flags `oni_ruler_shinukhorchi` / `oni_heir_ajgriijarul` suggest the heir belongs to another shirgrii, Ajgriijarul — inference.) Verified in the mod: the 1444 ruler is **Tiraga Aromo** (born 1372, ruling since 1425; careful, calm) and the heir is **Akira Surgoshi** (born 1415; "conqueror") [eu4: history/countries/Y01 - Azjakuma.txt].
- **Balrijin's ruler**: wiki "Pax of the Kindled Scale (1397–?)"; mod "Fax Akar of The Kindled Scale". Mod is newer.
- **Harimar's disappearance**: 70 AA (wiki: Harimari), 79 AA (High Temples, Tianlou), "dies 100" (Halessi Timeline). **Jaher in Haless**: 1038 (Haless), 1090–92 (Yanshen), 1094–96 (Tianlou); the Baihon Xinh page is internally inconsistent ("mid 1100s" / 1080). Treat as "11th century; Jaher dies in Tianlou 1127".
- **Rending trigger year**: 1750 (High Temples) vs 1751 (Rending of Realms, The Command). The cornerstone temple is spelled **Smaraya** on those pages while the city is **Sramaya**; I assume one place.
- **Precursor chronology**: neither chronology on the Precursor Empire page is settled canon. The "new canon Jay stuff" label covers only the Technology guidelines; the c. 10,000-BA dated list (War of Broken Chains, 8000, 6229, 6200, 6000 BA) sits under "Everything below is notes and not to be taken as canon!!!" and is marked TODO; below that again is a 50,000-BA chronology (Elfenbride born 14,000 BA and kidnapped; Ducaniel given Dalaire) headed "deprecated and old lore waiting to be revised". - **Ziltagh's accession**: 1440 (wiki; mod start text) vs a monarch entry dated 1438.1.1 in the mod's history file. **Maghargma's founding**: 1 AA (wiki infobox) vs 28 AA, when the Sky Giant fortress crashed (mod start text).

- **Thin sources**: the wiki's Xiaken, Wuhyun, Shamakhad (later ages), Harafic and Devand pages are stubs, and Haraf, Amadia and Insyaa above rest on short or note-form pages — provisional. `anb_aelantir_cannor_interactions` in the mod is placeholder text and was not used. Not covered in depth: Mteibhar politics, individual Kheionai republics, Nomsyulhan's Eagle Hobgoblins and Soulseeker Ogres (isolated until 1697), Gozengun's Shuvüüsh bird-riders.
- **Chinese names** are all from `zh/timeline/dist/data/names-zh.json` (plus 烟州 and 边方国 on the wiki pages themselves). No Chinese form was found there for Harimari, hobgoblin, Godlost, Ruinborn, Precursor, High Temples, Great Spirits or personal names; I left those untranslated.
