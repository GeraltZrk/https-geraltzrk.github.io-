## 1. Overview

**There is one canonical line through the sandbox.** The wiki's main Timeline ends its fixed section at 1444 and then gives a "Jahanic Timeline": because of the *1444 Divergence* "the very existence of the world is believed to have been split into alternate timelines. This is the timeline of the world of Jahan síl Bennon" [wiki: Timeline; wiki: 1444 Divergence]. That Jahanic line is what the Vic3 mod inherits. Its spine is:

Rediscovery of Aelantir (1490) → "Castellos is Dead" (1518) → Corinite schism (1519–20) → War of the Elensbridge → Crimson Deluge (1560–80) → Ravelian Society (1577) and God Fragment (1585) → First Corinite War and Peace of Esmaria → Ravelian Church (1638) → Second Corinite War (1675–84) → Camir Silmuna unifies Escann (1687–89), conquers the Empire (1690–1704) → Mages' Peace (1730–35) → Blackpowder Rebellion (1810) → Álvar sil Armon's expedition and the "Empyreal Age" (1 Castanmark 1820) [wiki: Timeline].

**The four EU4 ages** in the current mod are named *Age of Monsters*, *Age of Unraveling*, *Age of Witch-Kings* and *Age of Artificers* [eu4:ages]. Chinese community names: 怪物时代, 揭秘时代, 巫师王时代, 奇械时代, displayed as roughly 1444–1530, 1530–1620, 1620–1710, 1710–1821 [zh:timeline eras.json]. The localisation *files* are still called `anb_age_of_greentide`, `anb_age_of_unraveling`, `anb_age_of_witch_kings`; "Age of Greentide" and "Age of Reformation/Corinite" in the brief are file-name or vanilla-slot labels, not the in-game names. The wiki also uses "Second Unraveling" for the 17th–18th-century Enlightenment-equivalent [wiki: Second Unraveling], "Era/Age of Witch Kings", and "Age of Artificers/Artificery" loosely.

**Important date finding.** The brief calls the Vic3 start "1836". The current Vic3 mod files point to **1 Castanmark 1820**: historical treaties enter into force `1820.1.1` [vic3: common/history/treaties/anb_historical_treaties.txt]; the Concord's next election is `1822.07.1` [vic3: common/history/countries/B98 - Trollsbay.txt]; Lorent's opening event begins "Castanmark 1st, 1820" [vic3:lorent]; a tooltip says a fact "is unknown in 1820" [vic3:flavor]; most ruler templates carry age comments that only work for 1820 (Castíne "age = 27", born 1793) [vic3:templates]. The wiki agrees: "the Empyreal Age, which started on the first day of 1820" [wiki: Countries at the start of the Empyreal Age]; the Chinese map project ends at an "endpoint-1820" [zh:timeline]. Older material still says 1836 (a "Map of the Trollsbay Concord in 1836" [wiki: Trollsbay Concord]; "By 1836, Aelnar was almost relegated to a footnote" [wiki: Empire of Aelnar]; one template with "age = 73" for a man born 1763; a commented-out child monarch born 1826 [vic3:templates]). **Writers should treat 1820 as the lore date of the third campaign** and decide deliberately whether to label it 1836. See §10.

**Pages named in the brief that fall outside 1444–1836.** *Lanahine War* is a semi-mythical Taychendi war of 271–281 AA [wiki: Lanahine War]. *Damerian Republic* is the ancient republic (288 BA–474 AA) [wiki: Damerian Republic]; it matters here only as the model later republicans invoke. *Decades of Devastation* (Bahar) runs 1413/1418–1454, so only its last decade overlaps [wiki: Decades of Devastation]. There is no canonical "Damerian Republic" revolution; the republican revolution is the Blackpowder Rebellion.

## 2. Fixed canon versus "divergence"

**(a) Fixed canon** (stated by wiki Jahanic Timeline, emperor/monarch lists, and/or Vic3 files): everything in §1's spine; the list of Emperors to 1810 [wiki: List of Anbennarian Emperors]; Lorent's monarchs to Castíne [wiki: List of Lorentish monarchs]; the Jadd Empire (1562) and Deioderan (1723–41); the Command's rise, the Rending (1751) and Great Insubordination; Aelnar's rise and destruction (1550s–1630); the Trollsbay Concord (1595); the Magnate Uprising and Northern League (1772–79); the 1820 map (§3.16).

**(b) Left to the EU4 player** (the sandbox the 1444 campaign can bend): the wiki's own "Countries after the 1444 Divergence" lists states that "scholars suspect may have solidified under different circumstances" — a restored Castanor, Black Demesne (seven Acolyte states are listed), Moredhal, Badakhan, Kaztubar, the Nameless Kingdom, a reunited Aul-Dwarov, a Phoenix Empire, a Gnomish Hierarchy, a Lake Federation with player-chosen constitution, etc. [wiki: Countries after the 1444 Divergence]. In the mod, whoever holds 100 Escanni provinces at the end of the Wars of Consolidation chooses: usurp the Dove Throne, re-form Castanor via the Trials of Castan, declare "a new infernal empire… Moredhal", "Proclaim the Black Demesne", continue Corin's crusade, found the orcish Badakhan or Kaztubar [eu4:witchkings escanni_wars.10]. Likewise the League War can be won by either side, the mural of Castellar can be published or suppressed [eu4:unraveling 1.a/1.b], and Torrieth may hand the God Fragment to a state or steal it [eu4:religion ravelian.7/9]. **Canon picks one branch of each**: the mural leaks, the Adeanic side wins the second war, Núrcestir wins Escann and its king takes the Dove Throne as a Silmuna.

## 3. Facts by sub-topic

### 3.1 After Corinsfall (1444–1500)

- On 11 Nerament 1444 Corin and Korgus Dookanson killed each other at Castonath [eu4:greentide greentide.0]; the detail that it happened "within the great melee in the Imperial Quarter" and that Corin "sacrificed herself to block a killing blow meant for Lothane Bluetusk" comes from the Vic3 concept text only [vic3:concepts concept_corin]. The orc-side event for the same day is titled "Dookansfall", the goblin-side one "Chainbreak" [eu4:greentide greentide.1, greentide.6].
- **Who rules on the 1444 start date** (checked in the mod's history files): Emperor Lothane III síl Wex (b. 1401; as Grand Duke he is Lothane VI of Wex), married to Eilísa Siloriel (1413–1447), heir Caylen [eu4: history/countries/A30 - Wex.txt; wiki: List of Anbennarian Emperors]; King Kylian VI Siloriel of Lorent (b. 1410, r. 1422–49), heir Ruben [eu4: history/countries/A01 - Lorent.txt; wiki: List of Lorentish monarchs]; King Welyam III Gerwick of Gawed (b. 1427) [eu4: history/countries/A13 - Gawed.txt]; the Sons of Dameria under Rogier Silmuna "the Exiled" (d. 1473) [eu4: history/countries/B07 - Sons of Dameria.txt; wiki: List of Rogierian monarchs].
- The 10th Pantheonic Council, held in Escann by adventurer-clerics, admitted Corin to the Regent Court as god of war on the argument of Dominic of Gallowspeak, a priest of Nerat in Corin's Circle [wiki: Corinite].
- Emperor Lothane III síl Wex (1422–1470) ruled a hollowed Empire: "everywhere being a free-for-all when he isn't paying attention". Episodes: the Orston Arbitration, the Mages' Strife (House Vinerick takes over the Magisterium), the War of the Broken Lance (Vincen síl Cestir sends Aucanna Silistra "a broken spear"), Kantin's Rebellion in Arbaran, Esmaria's "smiles and daggers", the Grand Tournament where Aucanna jousts as a mystery knight with a broken lance, and the 1468 divination that showed Lothane lost his duel with Adénn Skylance and was saved by Erlan Silmuna, whom he then betrayed [wiki: Empire of Anbennar].
- 1470–77: Caylen I síl na Trisfer of Pearlsedge wins as a "third option"; Raubin I síl Estallen is murdered at a Lansday feast; Tomás II síl na Trisfer is elected 7–0 at Damescrown; Arbaran annexes Damescrown and Wex is carved up and demoted — "the end of the last Grand Duchy" (the "Chastening of Wex") [wiki: Empire of Anbennar; vic3:broken_empire 6].
- Lorent: Ruben VI "the Restorer" (1449–80) held the Jerdrominar Council at Harascilde (1450), created a permanent royal army, took Wineport and dumped the Council of Seventy Chalices' wine into the harbour, and brought Saloren back by treaty (1456) [wiki: Modern Lorentish Timeline].
- Haless: the Command (three confederated commands — Wolf, Lion, Boar — since 1392) crushed the Revolt of the North in Sir (1445–47), took Xianjie (1450–61) and Shamakhad (1459–66) and made the Oni of Azjakuma a slave state (1489–92) to secure Korashi [wiki: The Command].
- Bulwar: Jaddar Jexiszuir, leading the desert legions, told a defeated gnoll army in 1446: "Take up the cause of Light, or be banished like the Darkness" [wiki: The Jadd].

### 3.2 Aelantir: rediscovery, colonies, Aelnar (1490–1630)

- 1490: Endral the Explorer, a Redglader elf in Lorentish pay, reached Aelantir (landing in Obaithail, then Endralliande) [wiki: Rediscovery of Aelantir; wiki: Modern Lorentish Timeline]. Rival explorer Calbard Fjorhavn "drowns himself in response".
- 1500–1520s "decades of exploration": Lorentish colonies on the Cape of Endral; a Creek Gnome colony near the Cliffs of Ruin; Derannic Derhildia in Dalaire; the Sornic Sons in the Bloodgroves (mid-1510s); the elves of Venáil migrate en masse, seeing themselves as "rightful reclaimers" [wiki: Modern Lorentish Timeline; wiki: Empire of Aelnar].
- The "Green slave trade" in orcs began in the mid-1520s and was abolished in 1589 [wiki: Modern Lorentish Timeline]; Nathalaire's slave market was called "the Krahzate", and after "the 1589 Anbennarian Abolition" its orcs rowed galleys for freedom after ten years [eu4:ideas A74].
- 1533: Reánna IV seized the island of Venáil; in the 1550s (1553 per the Lorentish timeline) Aldarian Seawatcher proclaimed the Empire of Aelnar at Sella [wiki: Modern Lorentish Timeline; wiki: Empire of Aelnar].
- 1564: Aldarian was assassinated → the **Rianvisa** ("Enlightenment") civil war among Calassandur Seawatcher, Kayd Obaith, Dahvar Siltar, Lithiel Oathkeeper and others, alongside Bramash the Liberator's orc slave revolt on Endralliande (1564–70). Lorent intervened (1567) and kept the island; the freed orcs sailed for Soruin/Ozgarom [wiki: Rianvisa; wiki: Modern Lorentish Timeline; wiki: Empire of Aelnar]. 1579: the Compromise of Criodhcel merged the colonies into the crown colony of Endralliande, "the Bride of Lencenor".
- Lithiel won, broke, renamed herself **Elissa** and planned the "purification" of all Noruin. Ynnic adventurer Erandil Poison-Arrow leaked the plan in 1615; the Trollsbay Concord (founded 1595 by Cestirmark, Marlliande, Zanlib, Isobelin, Thílvis, Valorpoint) and ruinborn allies fought the War of the Coalition; Lorent invaded from the sea. By 1630 Aelnar was "entirely destroyed"; Lorent took the Bloodgroves as New Redglades; Elissa fled in the flying capital Estadaran, which still floats by the Delairy Wastes [wiki: Empire of Aelnar; wiki: Trollsbay Concord].
- Consequences: precursor relics taught Cannor "how to create magical objects" [wiki: Artificery]; Aelantiri damestear flooded Cannor [wiki: Damestear]; and the discoveries there destroyed the old religion (next section).

### 3.3 "Castellos is Dead" and the Corinite schism (1518–1553)

- Under a precursor temple, adventurers found a hall of murals; the last, titled "The End", shows the sky god Castellar — one-handed, one-legged, silver-cloaked — failing to contain the Ruin. A ruinborn shrine later yielded a six-foot white staff with a blue orb and a shattered gem: the "Broken Scepter" [eu4:unraveling 1, 4].
- The Order of Chroniclers printed pamphlets with the mural on the back; Tellumite town criers shouted it; apprentice mages seized the Tower of Adráil and projected the mural onto the clouds over the Dameshead [eu4:unraveling 5]. News reached Cannor in 1518 [wiki: Corinite].
- The Corinite Proclamation (1519) declared Corin Castellos' successor; the Eleventh Pantheonic Council (1520) ratified the death and chose Adean. Dominic of Gallowspeak, "wizened with more than nine decades", led Corin's case; priests of Esmaryal and Ryala backed Adean; those of Nathalyne and Falah wavered [eu4:unraveling 6; wiki: Corinite]. Ardor II of Verne declared for Corin in 1519 [eu4:ideas A33].
- The "Red Banners" faction in the imperial military converted the Duke of Istralore; Emperor Galien I called the Istralorians "damnable fools" [wiki: Corinite; eu4:ideas A45].
- War of the Elensbridge → Peace of Elensbridge: princes choose the faith of their lands [wiki: Timeline]. Dates conflict (1535–42 vs 1552–53; §10). Galien I died in it [wiki: List of Anbennarian Emperors].

### 3.4 Crimson Deluge, Floodborn, crusade and conspiracy (1553–1589)

- 1553–57: the Hedine Conspiracy at Konwell — Lord Laucius síl Fencewood's theatre troupe, the "Symphony of Indulgence" (~50 members), tried to summon an entity of the archdevil Hedine; the Blood of Ryala possessed the repentant lord; Magisterium-hired adventurers ended it; Fencewood died imprisoned on Adráil [wiki: 1557 Hedine Conspiracy].
- 1560–1580: the **Crimson Deluge** — "blood" rain and flooding over Escann and "almost all of Cannor east of the Dameshead" [wiki: Crimson Deluge; eu4:religion corinite.2–4]. Design note: "It's not actually blood. Doesn't taste like blood… it should never be confirmed to be a godswar". Lencenor was spared, which Lorent took as Adean's favour [vic3:ig whiterose_court].
- **Floodborn**: Escanni mages born in and after the Deluge have enormous raw power but suffer "the Drowning" — their lungs fill with water and "foul fluids" during heavy rain and they must will themselves to survive. Most die as children; survivors were often noble scions [wiki: Floodborn]. Witch burnings followed [wiki: Corinite; eu4:religion deluge.13].
- 1572–75: the **Anbennarian Crusade**. A petty Adeanic noble of Plainsby executed a peaceful Corinite preacher; peasants seized an Adeanic temple, killed an imperial prince, and founded crusader states under "the Red Council". Artus I offered titles for arms; zealots and opportunists split; several crusader states became Corinite principalities [wiki: 1572 Anbennarian Crusade]. The Orda Aldresia lost its bodyguard role on suspicion.
- Shadowmoon Conspiracy (1567 start, failed 1573): Ellís I Silmuna of Rogieria (b. 1533, married to Laurentíne Silmuna-Wesdam) tried to retake the Empire; Rogieria and Wesdam were lost by 1582 and Madelaire returned to Lorent [wiki: Shadowmoon Conspiracy; eu4:ideas A14, B37]. (Madelaire's return is dated 1573 in [wiki: Modern Lorentish Timeline] and 1582 in [eu4:ideas A14].)
  - Added from [wiki: List of Rogierian monarchs]: the Kingdom of Rogieria lasted 1473–1582. Kings: Rogier "the Young Owl" Silmuna (1473–1517, youngest son of Lothane Bluetusk), Lothane I (1517–18), Devan (1518–56), Ellís (b. 1533, r. 1556–75), Lothane II (r. 1575–82, d. 1619). Under Lothane II the realm was partitioned between Alenor, Núrcestir and Ancardia, while Emperor Artus I Vanbury took Wesdam. Lothane II's family went into hiding "posing as commoners"; **Camir Silmuna is the great-great-grandson of Lothane II through his daughter Marianne Silmuna** — this is what "a lost member of the Rogieran Silmunas" (§3.7) means.
- 7 Nerament 1576: Bisan's Corinites massacred the Adeanic nobility on the eve of Lansday [eu4:ideas A34].
- Adelar síl na Whistlehill of the Order of the Iron Sceptre sketched the **Black Demesne** during the Deluge: a godless magocracy under a Sorcerer-King ("Ascendant Dragon") on a Skull Throne, eight Acolytes, a necromantic Black Host, midnight prostrations toward Castonath. It is an ideology and propaganda bogeyman, not a canonical state [wiki: Black Demesne; wiki: Mages' Peace].

### 3.5 The Ravelians and the God Fragment (1577–1780s)

- 1577: the Ravelian Society — Corinite-born scholars "who sought to fight darkness with knowledge rather than the sword" [wiki: Ravelian Society].
- 1585: captain Vincen "Godsighter", with Evn'do and the Aelnari archaeologist Torrieth Duskstrider, found in Noruin an underground chamber on four trunk-like pillars; the **God Fragment** hovered at its centre — a fist-sized teal crystal with a "fluctuating black, gem-like cubic shard" inside, speaking in high-pitched hums [wiki: God Fragment]. Vincen retired to found Vincenton in western Cestirmark.
- Doctrine drawn from it: the Fragment holds the last essence of the One God, source of all magic, whom the precursor elves robbed; all other gods are aspects or saints; artificery returns the stolen gift to commoners [wiki: God Fragment; vic3:concepts].
- 1590 onward: hospitals, orphanages, banks and arbitration courts — "parallel power structures" [wiki: Ravelian Society; wiki: Ravelian Church]. Emperor Andrel I síl Aranmas (1598–1616) patronised them; Kylian-Tomáis I expelled them from Lencenor.
- 1638: Torrieth emerged from seclusion, asked "What knowledge are we seeking?" [wiki: Torrieth Godspeaker], and the Society became the **Ravelian Church**, seated at Aranthíl. Factions: *Erudites* and *Zealots*. Offices: High Rector, Veridicals, Archprovosts, Provosts, Rectors; Cloakmen (military arm, cloak on the left shoulder); Fractalines (the monastic order holding all the Church's mages) [wiki: Ravelian Church].
- The Four Debates: (1) salvation — Zealots win; expeditions find Hell, and Heaven locked; (2) evangelise by debate — Erudites win; (3) fluid sainthood — Zealots; (4) export artificery (early 1700s) — Zealots. Torrieth abdicated in 1666 and vanished, last seen by angels; a Vernman halfling, Adelmo, succeeded. 1670–92: Galien III's "Heresies of Reason" persecution. 1750s–80s: Zealots outnumber Erudites two to one; the first Inquisitions of Sarhal [wiki: Ravelian Church; wiki: High Rector].
- Added from [wiki: High Rector] (a list marked "Heavily Work in Progress"): only three High Rectors are named — Torrieth Godspeaker (1640–1666), Adelmo (1666–1705, "High Rector during Camiran Wars") and **Bernal II (1801–1828, "High Rector during the Blackpowder Rebellion")**, who is therefore the head of the Church at the 1820 start. The eight holders between 1705 and 1801 have dates but no names; do not invent them without marking them as invented.

### 3.6 The Corinite Wars and their peaces (1620–1704)

- **First Corinite War** (1620–1645): Corinite princes plus Escanni "Crusaders" against the Adeanic Empire; stalemate; **Peace of Esmaria** extends freedom of (Cannorian) religion to all imperial subjects [wiki: First Corinite War]. The mod dates the peace and the first Corinite emperor, Alvar I síl Verne ("The Corinite War has ended!" — "He was wrong"), to 1648 [eu4:ideas A33].
- 1648–1666: the throne alternates between Verne and Pearlsedge amid "intrigue and colonial skirmishes" [eu4:ideas A33; wiki: List of Anbennarian Emperors].
- **Second Corinite War** (1675–1684): Galien III wins; Corinites barred from the Dove Throne [wiki: Corinite].
- **War of Camiran Conquest** (1690–1704): the Corinite princes invite Camir. At Diohmmórin "the Anbennarian army burned as they crossed the chemically choked river at Varainé, and with them Galien III" [eu4:ideas A11]. The wiki words his end differently: "deposed by Camir in 1692" [wiki: Timeline; wiki: List of Anbennarian Emperors] and "overthrown and killed by Camir Silmuna" in 1692 [wiki: Ravelian Church] — so he dies in 1692, but only the Pearlsedge idea text places the death at Diohmmórin. Arannen seceded under the síl Vivins; Vincent I of Lorent grabbed the West Dameshead and Ruben VII lost it again [wiki: List of Lorentish monarchs].
  - Added: Camir "used [the] 1699 comet to help defeat Anbennar during Wars of Camiran Conquest (1690–1704)" — a TODO note, not finished prose [wiki: Oracle's Eye Comet]. A mod unit description has an orcish Ungulan contingent under "Duke Thrak the Second" fighting "Istralorian, Wexonard, and Wesdamerian forces" at a **Battle of Anbenncóst**: "Within the month, Anbenncóst fell and Camir was proclaimed Emperor of Anbennar" [eu4: localisation/anb_units_l_english.yml, orcish_23_camirs_auxilariesDESCR]. The Shadowmoon Conspiracy is credited with sabotaging the Dameshead sea-forts ahead of Camir's invasion [eu4:ideas Z42_seaforts_of_damesear_crossing].

### 3.7 Witch-Kings and the unification of Escann (c. 1600–1689)

- "Witch-King" = a mage who rules as tyrant or defies the Magisterium's limits; Escann lay outside its jurisdiction [wiki: Witch-King]. Listed Escanni witch-kings: Laurens síl Place of Covenblad ("The First Witch-King of Escann", rule given as "1400s"), Varina Escer of Esthíl, the Regent of Wyvernheart (1600s), and (derisively) Camir (1687–1730). The same page lists Lithiel/Elissa of Aelnar among the non-Escanni witch-kings ("Post 1600s") [wiki: Witch-King]. Varina appears in the mod as Esthíl's "Witch-Queen lich" who "rose the dead to fight their perverse war", after an earlier Escanni "War of Treachery" [eu4:ideas I45_war_of_the_black_tower]. Covenblad's infobox gives it as destroyed in 1656, also with the War of the Black Tower as its major event [wiki: Covenblad].
- Núrcestir, founded by the Pioneer's Guild, was a gnomish-led oligarchic republic ("the Luna of the East") with a parliament modelled on the Damerian Republic and the paper mills that printed Escann's "Adventurers Wanted!" posters; the republic fell in the Adenican War of the early 1600s (Floodborn first used in war, 1615) [wiki: Kingdom of Núrcestir; wiki: Floodborn].
- Esthíl (ex-Order of the Iron Sceptre, necromantic) was destroyed in 1663 in the War of the Black Tower [wiki: Esthíl]. In the 1670s Núrcestir moved into the Imperial Palace of Castonath and created the **Splendid Court**, taming nobles with "splendid balls and parlour games" [wiki: Kingdom of Núrcestir].
- **Camir Silmuna** (1660–1730), "a lost member of the Rogieran Silmunas", Royal Wizard, veteran of the Second Corinite War, king in 1687. He captured the Regent of Wyvernheart with companions Verindir and Erelorna; in 1689 the Regent broke out, "killed his way through the Splendid Court", offered Camir his service, exterminated Wyvernheart's nobility — and was then killed by all eight Royal Wizards, five of whom died [eu4:ideas B39]. Escann became the **Royal Demesne**.
- **Camiran Doctrine**: mages as the army's bedrock — *concentrated barriers* cast only at the instant of impact, and the *Covenant of Command* (night rituals linking a mage-officer's mind to the ranks; if the mage panics, so does the army) [wiki: Camiran Doctrine].

### 3.8 The Camiran Empire and the Mages' Peace (1692–1810)

- Camir "unified the Empire of Anbennar into a single state", an absolutist ruling by divine right derived "from magic itself"; he built the palace-town **Arca Alsalar** on the Moonmount's southern foothills to repeat the Splendid Court [wiki: Camir I Silmuna; eu4:ideas A11; wiki: Arca Alsalar].
- 1730–35 War of Camiran Succession: civil war plus invasions; **Lothane IV** (1730–39) wins by instituting a new **Mages' Peace** — the neutrality of Anbennar — and entrenching the "Nobles of the Wand" [wiki: Timeline; wiki: List of Anbennarian Emperors].
- **Madaléin** (1739–1805), "Mad Madaléin": immensely powerful, bred incestuously for a pure Silmuna mage heir; refined the Royal Wizard rite to puberty age [wiki: List of Anbennarian Emperors; wiki: Floodborn]. Marion III (1805–10) dreamed of making his son, "Prince-Ascendant Marion", unifier against an "Empire-In-Shadow" [wiki: Divine Regency].
- The regime kept "a tight leash on novel artificery and political dissent alike" [vic3:flavor A01], hoarded damestear to stop black damestear spreading [wiki: Damestear], and persecuted Ravelians [wiki: Corinite]. In 1805 the Magisterium elected Erlan Silgarion Grand Magister at eighteen — "the sign of magical decadence" [wiki: List of Grand Magisters]. Magical education had "reached stagnation" [wiki: Cannorian Magical Education]. The magical aristocracy were called "bluebloods" [wiki: Magic].

### 3.9 Artificery, damestear, mechanim, airships

- Artificery began with the gnomes of Nimscodd arming mundane troops against the kobolds; three racial traditions (gnomish = safe and empirical; goblin = "it just works"; kobold = body modification and storm-electricity) and three philosophies (Brilliant Inventors — the Sparkdrive Rifle; Techno-Thaumaturgy — Wandlocks; Mechanism — Skyships) [wiki: Artificery].
- **Black damestear / Korashi** nullifies magic; it looks like "a shinier version of gunpowder" and, to a mage, "a void". The Oni made it; the Command chained mages with it; the Gommo obtained it in 1617 after the failed siege of Tianlou. Mass "nullification" in Cannor only came in the Blackpowder Rebellion [wiki: Damestear]. Standardised milestones: the Command's Office for the Regulation of Volatile Resources (1609), the Pikoyhun chain method (1645), the Korashi drill (1675) [wiki: The Command].
- *Blueblood*: in the 1700s Argezvale dwarves showed mage blood can substitute for damestear — "the poor man's damestear", with "Blood Barons" procuring it [wiki: Artificery].
- **Mechanim**: precursor damestear-cored metal people of Insyaa (reawakened 1366), later replicated by artificers; each has a *Kauga* core holding its soul [wiki: Mechanim]. In 1820 Anbennar law treats them as property [vic3:A01 history] and an "awakening"/abolition question looms [vic3: journal_entries/anb_mechanim_l_english.yml].
- **Airships** first flew "in the early 1800s", from Feiten's air-balloons with motors added [wiki: Airship]. Bahari elves already flew mage-piloted "sky platforms" in the 1410s [wiki: Decades of Devastation].

### 3.10 The North: Gawed, the League, Grombar

- War of Eagle and Rose (1508–17): Stovan "Blackboar" killed Kylian VII (1509), stitched a horse's head on the corpse and sent it to Princess Reánna; burned the Redglades (1514) until elves and fey united; was captured at the Second Battle of Red Reach (1515). Gawed lost the Small Country [wiki: Modern Lorentish Timeline].
- The "Gawedi Empire" turned north into the Alenic Reach and Gerudia [wiki: Gawedi Empire]. **Magnate Uprising** (1772–79): four-year-old Rican IV Gerwick died; magnates seized Gaweton; the **Northern League** (est. 1779), "the business of the League was business", destroyed many Great Houses [wiki: Magnate Uprising; wiki: Northern League]. Firms: M. Kingston & Sons (confectioners), Ramsgate Munitions, Alen Steel Conglomerate — the wiki names these as the backbone of the League's economy "moving into the 20th century", so their existence in 1820 is not established [unverified for 1820]. Rival claimants in the uprising were Lan Gerwick (third cousin of Rican IV, backed by most nobles) and Henric Gerwick [wiki: Magnate Uprising]. At the Vic3 start the League holds Bayvek and Vertesk as puppets and the Deranne Trade Company as a chartered company [vic3: common/history/diplomacy/anb_subject_relationships.txt].
- A *Second War of the Northern League* (pre-1810) pitted the League and Busilar against Anbennar and the Vivin Empire and bankrupted the Empire [wiki: Colour Courts; wiki: List of Busilari Monarchs].
- **Grombar**: the Graytide (1420–28) made Brasûr Frozenmaw king of Vrorenmarch with a human princess-wife; half-orc kings followed (Marosh, then Gunk). *Corrected:* it was not "the rebuilt capital" — the Esalder idea text says the rebel city of Esald was razed, resettled by Gray Orcs and renamed **Garok**, and "in 1506 King Gunk chose the renewed city to be the capital of the Grombari state" [eu4:ideas Z23_a_new_dawn]; the wiki infobox still gives the capital as Bal Vroren and ends the "Orcish Kingdom of Vrorenmarch" period in 1505 [wiki: Kingdom of Grombar]; by 1820 a Corinite orc-and-human military monarchy under King Bakram, recently and shockingly beaten by the League [wiki: Kingdom of Grombar; eu4:ideas Z23; vic3:flavor A10]. Reigns from the wiki: Brasûr 1428–69, Marosh 1469–81 (killed in a ritual duel by the orc Thrur; humans and half-orcs crowned Marosh's son Gunk instead). The Frozenmaw dynasty died out "during the Corinite Wars" and the king's son-in-law, admiral Temar Ironsea, took the throne by ritual combat (*Ozarm'Chadash*) [eu4:ideas Z50_ambitions_of_temar_ironsea]; the 1820 ruler is the half-orc **Bakram Ironsea** (b. 1787, "reformer"; a second Bakram, Bakram Ebontusk, leads the industrialists) [vic3: common/character_templates/anb_country_A10.txt].

### 3.11 Lorent's empire (1600–1820)

- Siloriel kings held the Adeanic line (Whiterose Court) until the house died with Reán-Laurens III in 1661 — he "got extremely angry" at a Vernman envoy, "suffered a heart attack and died" at 29. After the succession crisis Vincent I síl Rubenaire took the Ruby Crown in 1666 [wiki: List of Lorentish monarchs].
- Ruben VII bought and conquered ports in Sarhal and near Bhuvauri; Ruben VIII (1744–85), a mage-king, "unlock[ed] a copy of the mind of Ioriel Redrose within the crown" at 32; Reán VI went reclusive and was "found dead in his bed"; Laurens VI (1789–1816) fought the Blackpowder wars; **Castíne** (b. 1793) inherited in 1816 because her elder sister Eilís was declared mentally ill [wiki: List of Lorentish monarchs].
- The **Ruby Crown** carries an echo of Ioriel (Ioriel Redrose, elven founder of House Siloriel and queen of Lorent), whose politics "lean toward absolutism"; most monarchs only receive "subtle suggestions" they take for their own will, never learn the truth, and "often develop mental issues" [vic3:lorent je_the_ruby_crown]. *Corrected:* the earlier draft said she had nudged the monarchs "for centuries". The same file gives two durations — "she has guided Lorent through the Ruby Crown for a long time" and "has quietly guided Lorent's rulers for nearly a century" — and the wiki has Ruben VIII first unlocking "a copy of the mind of Ioriel Redrose within the crown" at 32, i.e. c. 1756 [wiki: List of Lorentish monarchs]. Use "nearly a century" for active guidance. The Vic3 text says she "lived in the 11th century"; the wiki gives her death as 1168 [wiki: Ioriel Redrose].

### 3.12 Haless: rise and fall of the Command

- Expansion: First Dhujat War (1507–08; Bloodsong orcs' charge across Elephant Gorge), Sarisung (1510–11), Tiger Command (1515), Elephant Command (1518), Bianfang (1521–c.1530), defeats by Raja Hemantsen (Second and Third Dhujat Wars, to 1539), Dragon Command at Chatzin (1540), the Jade March goblin slave-state (1557), the Ten Reforms (1569), which fixed the place of the **Wuhyun** [wiki: The Command]. *Corrected gloss:* the wiki defines Wuhyun as "the synchronization of hobgoblin culture and the various cultures of Haless", producing cultures such as Azepyanunin, Kintonin and Ikaniwagain [wiki: Wuhyun] — i.e. the hobgoblin-acculturated subject peoples, mostly human ("the wuhyun of Yanshen… the humans" [eu4:ideas R69]) but the Command page also speaks of "Wuhyuh half-orcs".
- Great Dhujat War (1572–93) against the Triple Alliance of the Jadd, Vanrahar and Bhuvauri; relief of Khiraspid by the "Charge of Phoenix and Tiger" (1580); then a century of attrition, the "Watering of Rahen" — warg riders against Jaddari riders across fortified plains [wiki: Great Dhujat War; wiki: The Command].
- Yanshen: Tianlou Concord wars; Tianlou fell in 1658; "Dragon's Peace"; the Oni were made stewards of the High Temples and began corrupting them [wiki: The Command].
- Decline: Fourth Dhujat War (1701–12); the eunuchs' Bloodless Coup of Tianlou (1706); Second and Third Yansheni Liberation Wars; then in 1751, as peace was being signed at Shengdao, the last temple ward in Tianlou broke: the **Rending of Realms**. The catfish spirit Kharunyana destroyed the Godswall; the Yanhe flood of 1759 killed "upwards of four million" [wiki: The Command; vic3:concepts].
- 1768: the Ninyu Kikun marched into the Oni hills; the Oni returned "a thousand severed heads" and a declaration of independence. **Great Insubordination**: Dragon (Proclamation of Szichcheng, 1783), Tiger, then Elephant (1786) secede; twenty years of war; Faiko Boarborn's invasion of the Dragon Command (1809), "Faiko's Folly", ended at Yuanszi. A black-wolf-headed figure, said to be Moguwon Wolfborn returned, mauls would-be Grand Marshals [wiki: The Command]. Names the same page gives: Hikari Dragonborn issued the Proclamation of Szichcheng; Noriko Dragonborn (Dragon Marshal from c. 1744) died of leukemia in 1782; Elephant Marshal Tyonkun Elephantborn and Tiger Marshal Zurshin Tigerborn were executed after their capitals fell (Sharaajaghal 1805, Xiadao 1806); the ex-general Mibatsu "the Rat" took Xiadao in 1810 and declared Yodashikyu in 1812; the Kusonin warlord Khaburo Ikaninkinon proclaimed Nadiraji in 1817.
- At the Vic3 start the Great Command is only a formable; the map holds Lion Command, Dragon Command, Girakad Command, the Unbroken Guard, the Bureau of Chains, the Soldiers' Republic, Yodashikyu, Nadiraji and others [vic3: localization/english/country_specific_content/anb_countries_l_english.yml; journal_entries/anb_command_splinter_l_english.yml]. Azjakuma has created a "Tamonger Eternal" [vic3:flavor Y09].

### 3.13 Bulwar and Rahen under the Jadd

- Jaddar defeated Zokka Devourer-of-Suns in single combat, took the Upper Suran, and founded the **Jadd Empire** in 1562 as the Raj dissolved; he died in 1563 (a desert elf, born 12 Nerament 1161, "aged 402"; spouse Jasiéne Elaiénna [wiki: Jaddar]). Divine-Heralds: Theladi Elaiénnó (1563–73), Ariathra Jaddarzuir (1573–1612), Tezalindor Erelessazuir (1612–78), Fardeher szal-Jaddar (1678–1741), Kamnaril Andrellzuir (1741–). Capital Dhenijansar, then Jaddanzar from 1612 [wiki: Jaddar; wiki: Jadd Empire].
- The goblin Aqatbar Overclan (est. 1552) converted to the Rite of Jaddar in 1561 [wiki: Aqatbar Overclan].

By the Vic3 start it is "nominally whole again", with rival administrations in Bulwar and Rahen [vic3: journal_entries/anb_sovereignty_under_surael_l_english.yml]. ### 3.15 The Blackpowder Rebellion and Wars (1810–c. 1819)

- **Causes**: failed harvests (partly the "Verdancy" experiments ruining soil), Mages' Peace autarky, bread riots, tax-farming, bankruptcy after the Second War of the Northern League, and the nine **Colour Courts** (Blue, Red, Purple, White, Gray, Orange, Yellow, Black, Green — venal "Nobles of the Robe") blocking every reform [wiki: Colour Courts].
- **1810**: the Emperor revived the Imperial Parliament at Arca Alsalar with a new Fourth Estate of mages — twenty delegates with "the same voting power as the thousand members" of the Third [wiki: Magisterium]. The Oracle's Eye Comet (every 111 years) broke up overhead; for seconds to ten minutes all magic went wild [wiki: Oracle's Eye Comet]. The **Tearfall Teleportation Tragedy** destroyed the Imperial Palace and Madaléin's Tower and was "the inciting incident" [wiki: Anbenncóst; wiki: Oracle's Eye Comet]. Marion III died [wiki: List of Anbennarian Emperors].
- A royalist-reformist parliament opened officer ranks to commoners, abolished tax exemptions and the Colour Courts. Factions: royalist-reformers under the Duke of Acromton; the reactionary Preservation Club under Judge Pírgab; radical **Borandists** [wiki: Colour Courts; wiki: Newspapers]. The Constitutional Assembly, later National Parliament, sat in Anbenncóst's Old Colosseum [wiki: Anbenncóst].
- The Ravelians and the "Blackpowder Barons" seized the confiscated damestear and made **black-damestear bullets** that pierce any ward; the wild-magic moment "gave the rebels their last chance for a counter attack" [wiki: Damestear; wiki: Oracle's Eye Comet].
- "When the great armies of Cannor descended upon the Blackpowder Republic, the Republic defeated them all… fighting to crown a foreign Emperor" [vic3: journal_entries/anb_hakhepra_l_english.yml]. Lorent blockaded Anbennar [wiki: Elfrealm of Moonhaven]. The "ancient magical nobility was purged"; then the petty-noble **Baronites** and moderate Blackpowderists "oust[ed] the revolutionary Borandist regime and put an end to the rebellion" [vic3:ig baronites]. The Magisterium was "absorbed into the Blackpowder state proper", shaped by Fractalines [wiki: Ravelian Church]. Mages "had to work for a living" [vic3: systems/anb_inventions_l_english.yml].
- **Losses**: Escann broke away [vic3:flavor A21]; Vivin took Wexonard land (Bisan, Honderaak) [wiki: County of Bisan; wiki: Republic of Honderaak]; Beepeck passed to the Small Country [wiki: Free City of Beepeck; vic3:flavor A14]; Varionail (Moonhaven's tea colony on the Ravenous Isle) declared independence in "a largely bloodless revolution" because the Lorentish blockade stopped Anbennar sending troops [wiki: Elfrealm of Moonhaven] — **but** in the Vic3 files Varionail (B13) starts as a Lorentish colony, grouped with Torrisheáh under the comment "Smaller colonies seized during the Blackpowder wars, de facto under Endralliande's administration control" [vic3: common/history/diplomacy/anb_subject_relationships.txt]; treat it as Lorentish-held in 1820. Lorent seized imperial colonies (Torrisheáh) and sold Minaria to Neratica (1817) for war funds [vic3:flavor B15, B09; wiki: Neratica]. Wex's nobility "seized their chance to secede from the republicans… but Vivin aid turned to Vivin domination" [vic3:broken_empire 6]; Bisan and Honderaak start as Vivin puppets [vic3: anb_subject_relationships.txt].
- **Who won?** The corpus is not consistent. Hakhepra's event text says the Republic "defeated them all" (quoted above); the wiki's Bisan page says "the Vivin Empire was among the victorious powers that carved away Anbennarian land" [wiki: County of Bisan]; the Vic3 law set has free trade "enforced by powers so Anbennar keeps open" [vic3:A01 history]. The safe reading: the Republic survived and beat the attempt to restore an emperor, but lost its periphery and accepted outside constraints. The Chinese map project, citing Vic3 dev diary 6, adds that the Republic sold Vertesk to the League for help against Lorent, Grombar took Athfork and Ebonmarck, and Lorent gained in Exwes, Carneter and Tretun [zh:timeline] — secondary, not in my corpus.

There is no one "atop the Dove Throne" [vic3:broken_empire, je_broken_empire_reason].

## 10. Uncertainties and source conflicts

1. **Vic3 start: 1820 or 1836?** Files and the Empyreal-Age wiki page say 1820; some wiki pages and stray comments say 1836; a commented-out "Monarchy Anbennar" under a ten-year-old Artur Laurens Silmuna (b. 1826) sits beside the live republic set-up [vic3:A01 history; vic3:templates]. I followed the live files (1820). The wiki's 1821–35 Command chronology was evidently written for an 1836 start.
2. **Blackpowder date.** 1810 in the Timeline, emperor list, Colour Courts, comet and Anbenncóst pages; but "Blackpowder Wars of 1790" [wiki: County of Bisan] and "Blackpowder Empire – Anbennar (1792 AA – Present)" [wiki: Dwarven Hall of Silverforge]; the Isle of Tef page even mentions Blackpowder Wars "in 1870". I followed 1810. When the wars ended is not stated in my corpus; an 1819 treaty (旧港条约, "Old Harbour treaty" — my gloss, no English name found) comes only from the Chinese project's reading of a draft [zh:timeline]. The form of the regime between 1810 and 1820 (the wiki speaks of the Empire becoming "a republic (TODO)") is thinly documented.
3. **War of the Elensbridge**: 1535–1542 [wiki: Timeline; emperor list] versus 1552–1553 with the Counter-Reformation in 1547 [wiki: Modern Lorentish Timeline; eu4:ideas A11, which also has Galien I starting it in 1547 though the list ends his reign in 1542].
4. **Peace of Esmaria**: 1645 [wiki] versus 1648 [eu4:ideas A33]; A11 loosely implies 1627. The mod is newer; the Chinese project also adopts 1648.
5. **Shadowmoon Conspiracy**: 1567 or 1573 — the wiki page itself flags this.
6. **Great Insubordination**: 1784–1808 [wiki stub] versus 1783 onward and 1786–1806 [wiki: The Command]. **Great Dhujat War**: 1572–1593 versus "fifteen-year conflict" [eu4:ideas].
7. **Aelnar**: proclaimed "1550s" or 1553; Lorentish intervention 1567 [wiki: Empire of Aelnar] versus "1564–1570" block [wiki: Modern Lorentish Timeline]. Wiki spells both "Rianvisa" and "Riavansa/Rivansa".
8. **Deranne Trading Company**: founded 1487 at Calbardhaven [wiki] versus 1542 Conference of Columshaven [eu4:ideas].
9. **Blackboar's surname**: Brasslance [wiki: Modern Lorentish Timeline] versus Gerwick [wiki: List of Lorentish monarchs].
10. **Camiran Doctrine** is said to be popularised "in the mid-17th century" though Camir was born in 1660.
11. **Jadd Empire status in 1820**: "nominally whole" [vic3] yet two country tags; the Chinese project describes a personal union.
12. **Not found**: dedicated pages for the Second Corinite War, War of Camiran Conquest, Imperial Parliament of 1810, Tearfall Teleportation Tragedy, Borandists, the two Wars of the Northern League, War of the Black Tower, Adenican War — all known only from mentions. Who "Borand" was is not stated.
13. The Chinese timeline is a fan compilation that mixes attested dates with labelled inferences (推演/推测); I used it for names and for a few flagged claims only.

*Added by the fact-check pass:*

14. **CLSTC founding**: 1502 [eu4:ideas A46] versus 1025 [wiki: Calasanni Trading Company; vic3:A01 history] and a company already operating in 1444 [eu4:ideas A25; wiki: Empire of Anbennar]. Follow 1025 / "ancient".
15. **Length of the War of Camiran Conquest**: 1690–1704, with the throne taken in 1692 [wiki: Timeline] versus "the war, lasting only two years" [wiki: Corinite]. Compatible if 1692–1704 is read as mopping up "rebels, rogue princes and hostile states".
16. **Galien III's end**: "deposed" [wiki: Timeline, emperor list], "overthrown and killed" [wiki: Ravelian Church], burned at Diohmmórin [eu4:ideas A11].
17. **War of the Black Tower**: no date is given anywhere. Covenblad is "destroyed 1656" and Esthíl "destroyed 1663", both with this war as their "major event" [wiki: Covenblad; wiki: Esthíl]; yet a mod unit text has auxiliaries trained "under one of Camir Silmuna's generals" fighting in it [eu4: anb_units_l_english.yml], which cannot be the 1650s–60s (Camir was born in 1660). Either the war ran long or the unit text is loose. Avoid giving the war exact years.
18. **Emperor Tomás**: "Tomás II síl na Trisfer" (Duke Tomás I of Pearlsedge) in [wiki: List of Anbennarian Emperors] versus "Emperor Tomás I" in [eu4:ideas A93].
19. **Ioriel's century**: "lived in the 11th century" [vic3:lorent] versus died 1168 [wiki: Ioriel Redrose].
20. **Varionail in 1820**: independent after a bloodless revolution [wiki: Elfrealm of Moonhaven] versus a Lorentish colony [vic3: anb_subject_relationships.txt]. Follow the Vic3 file for the start date.
21. **Outcome of the Blackpowder Wars**: see the "Who won?" note in §3.15.
22. **No wiki page for the Blackpowder Rebellion itself** exists in the dump (the Timeline says "Papers on this subject are currently unavailable"); everything in §3.15 is assembled from side mentions. This is the thinnest part of the canon and the place where writers will most need to invent — and should label what they invent.

### Changed — errors and one-sided statements

1. **CLSTC "founded 1502"** (year table). True to one idea text [eu4:ideas A46] but contradicted by the wiki (formed 1025), the Vic3 history file (1025), the Damescrown idea text and the wiki's own account of Lothane III's reign, where the company already exists. Rewrote the row as disputed and told writers not to use 1502.
2. **Ruby Crown: "nudged Lorent's monarchs for centuries"** (§3.11). The cited file says "for a long time" in one string and "for nearly a century" in another, and the wiki has the crown's Ioriel-copy first unlocked by Ruben VIII c. 1756. Corrected; also noted the 11th-century / d. 1168 mismatch.
3. **Varionail "seceded bloodlessly"** (§3.15). That is the wiki; the Vic3 start files make Varionail a Lorentish colony "seized during the Blackpowder wars". Both versions now given, with the Vic3 file preferred for 1820.
4. **Wuhyun = "humans who adopted hobgoblin ways"** (§3.12). Too narrow: the wiki defines it as the fusion of hobgoblin culture with the cultures of Haless and the Command page mentions Wuhyun half-orcs. Reworded with citations.
5. **Corin "died blocking a blow meant for Lothane Bluetusk" cited to `greentide.0`** (§3.1). That event says no such thing; the detail is only in the Vic3 concept text. Citation corrected and the scope of each source stated.
6. **Galien III "dies at Diohmmórin"** (§3.6, character table). Only the Pearlsedge idea text says so; the wiki says "deposed" and "overthrown and killed" in 1692. Both now given.
7. **Gunk "made the rebuilt capital the seat of the Grombari state in 1506"** (§3.10). Garbled: the source is about the city of Esald, razed after a rebellion, rebuilt as Garok and chosen as the new capital. Reworded, and the wiki's differing capital (Bal Vroren) noted.
