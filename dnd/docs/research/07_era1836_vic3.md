## 1. Overview

**The date.** The brief says 1836. The current mod files open on **1 Castanmark (January) 1820**: Lorent's first event begins "Castanmark 1st, 1820" [je:lorent]; historical treaties are stamped "entered into force 1819.12.30" [treaties]; Dragon Dominion templates are annotated "age 61 at 1820 start" [tmpl B49]; the Hakhepra chain quotes an observer "c. 1825 AA" as a later voice [je:hakhepra]. A dev comment on Northern League generals ("fixed so that he was of a more reasonable age during the war (i.e. 48 in 1836, not 1820)") suggests that ages were first written against an 1836 start and birth dates were patched afterwards [tmpl A04] — an inference from one comment, not a stated fact. The wiki agrees: "the Empyreal Age, which started on the first day of 1820" [wiki: Countries at the start of the Empyreal Age]. Everything below is the 1820 situation. If the game keeps the label "1836", sixteen years must be added to every age here and the scripted crises should be treated as already in motion.

**The mood line** the mod gives every country: "The time of sword and sorcery has come to a close. In its place, a new era dawns upon all of Halann – one of steam and artificery. Will this new tomorrow bring promise… or ruin?" [flavor GENERIC].

**What just happened.** The Blackpowder Rebellion (1810) destroyed Camir Silmuna's magocratic empire, purged the "ancient magical nobility", and produced a parliamentary Blackpowder Republic that beat "the great armies of Cannor" in the Blackpowder Wars [je:hakhepra event 64; ig baronites]. Black damestear ended the battlefield supremacy of mages; "mages had to work for a living" [inv mages_in_the_workforce]. Escann broke away, Lorent seized imperial colonies, the Wexonard nobility seceded from the republicans with Vivin help — "Vivin aid turned to Vivin domination" (Bisan, Magdalaire, Sugamber and the Wexhills are now divided) — and nationalism now threatens the Republic itself [flavor A01, A21, B15; je:broken_empire event 6]. Note the source of the "beat the great armies of Cannor" line: it is a Hakhepri Nationalist speech, i.e. an admiring foreign view, not a neutral chronicle [je:hakhepra event 64]; Kalsyto's text calls the same revolution "the faltering Blackpowder Rebellion" [je:kalsyto].

**How the Rebellion started (added by fact-check; all wiki).** Before 1810 Anbennar was going bankrupt: bad harvests (partly from "Verdancy" experiments that ruined the soil), the autarky of the Mages' Peace, bread riots, tax-farming, and the costly Second War of the Northern League. To get round the noble Colour Courts the Emperor revived the Imperial Parliament — the **Parliament of Arca Alsalar, 1810** — with four estates: priests, nobles, commoners and a new Fourth, the mages [wiki: Colour Courts]. The inciting incident was the **Tearfall Teleportation Tragedy** of 1810, which destroyed the Imperial Palace in Anbenncóst [wiki: Oracle's Eye Comet; Anbenncóst]. The last emperors were Madaléin Silmuna (1739–1805, "Mad Madaléin", in whose reign the Magnate Uprising happened) and **Marion III Silmuna (1805–1810), who died during the Rebellion**; the last Grand Magister, Erlan Silgarion, had been elected at 18 in 1805, "the sign of magical decadence" [wiki: List of Anbennarian Emperors; List of Grand Magisters]. The magical aristocracy were called "bluebloods" [wiki: Magic]; oppression of Ravelians was an inciting factor [wiki: Corinite]. Black damestear (*Korashi*) was an Oni secret from Haless, obtained by the gnomes of Gommoport in 1617; in the Rebellion the Ravelians, "in league with the rebellious Blackpowder Barons", seized the Empire's confiscated damestear, "nullified" it and made black-damestear bullets that pierce any ward — which also drove up the world price of damestear and renewed colonial interest in Aelantir [wiki: Damestear]. Beepeck was annexed into the Small Country afterwards [wiki: Imperial Halfling]; the Silcalas family renounced its noble titles and kept trading [wiki: Calasanni Trading Company]. The **Oracle's Eye Comet** (a damestear comet returning every 111 years) passed in 1810 and *broke apart* over Halann — a moment of world-wide wild magic — and many now fear that magic itself will dwindle, which is the stated motive for the rush to other planes and planets [wiki: Oracle's Eye Comet]. The wiki's own Timeline says of the Rebellion: "Papers on this subject are currently unavailable" — detailed canon is thin.

**What begins on day one.** The Ravelian scholar Álvar sil Armon, a Busilari half-elf, sailed a small boat over the Root of the Universe — a gigantic underwater branch of the Astral Tree off Sil Berkhen's Island in Uelos' Lament, whose side-effect is a giant maelstrom — struck a damestear tuning fork against the mast, was swallowed, and a little over 24 hours later, on 1 Castanmark 1820, came back from a sea in the Feyrealm "carrying various magical plants and a pixie in a jar" (he wrote "The Theory of All Planes" in 1819; later in life a latent fey curse gave him goat horns). Busilar led the rush that followed and at first kept each plane's tuning frequency in a vault at Port Jaher [wiki: Root of the Universe]. The tech tree's end state is planar and interplanetary portals; the Calasanni Trading Company already plans that "the stars and planes themselves will be monetized" [inv; flavor B03].

**Scale.** The mod places about 1.08 billion people on the map. Largest starting populations: Melakmengi 77M, Nahana Jadd 44M, Anbennar 39M, Lorent 35M, Kalsyto 33M, Northern League 30M, Dragon Command 30M, Vivin Empire 22.5M, Grombar 19M, Tianlou 18M, Busilar 17M, Jaddanzar 16M [pops].

## 2. Facts by sub-topic

### 2.1 Cannor: the great powers

**Blackpowder Anbennar (黑火药安本纳尔 [zh:map])** — capital Anbenncóst on Damesear. Laws: parliamentary republic, census voting, "Blackpowder Militia" ("professional soldiery and local volunteers all driven to defend Blackpowder Ideals by any means necessary"), freedom of conscience, public schools, censorship ("to protect the republic"), slavery banned, anti-strike laws, child labour allowed, free trade "enforced by powers so Anbennar keeps open", no colonial office, **Nation of Artifice** and **Mechanim as Property** [hist A01; laws]. The landowner interest group, renamed the **Baronites**, governs: petty nobles who "filled the vacuum when the ancient magical nobility was purged", then "allied with the moderate Blackpowderists to oust the revolutionary Borandist regime" [ig]. Head of state Reán síl Tirufeld (b. 30 Halament 1789; tactful, cautious, an alcoholic, "expert political operator"; ideology tagged "humanitarian royalist") [tmpl A01]; the Baronscourt in Marionside — a 17th-century palace turned by Horace síl Tirufeld into apartments and the city's first shopping arcade — was a hotbed of Second Unraveling liberalism under Horace's son and then his grandson Reán [wiki: Anbenncóst]. His brother Josep (b. 1792) is a general [tmpl A01]. Primary culture is now only *Cóstine*; Esmari (20% of people), East Damerians (15%), Arbarani, Vernmen, West Damerians, Crownsmen, Beefoot halflings and Pearlsedgers are all outside the nation's core [hist A01; pops]. It holds Damesear, the Damerian heartlands, Wesdam, Istralore, Esmaria (Cann Esmar, High Esmar, Estallen, Konwell, Bennon), Verne, Menibór, Neckcliffe, Seinainé, Telgeir–Calascandar and parts of Wex, Pashainé, Pearlsedge and Moonhaven [states]. Subjects: Silverforge and Moonhaven (puppets), New Erngrove (colony), the Calasanni Trading Company (chartered) [diplo]. Power bloc: the **Blackpowder Front** (founded 1812, "ideological union"), whose only other member is New Uanced in Aelantir [blocs]. Rival: Lorent [diplo].

**Kingdom of Lorent (洛伦特)** — capital Lorentainé; Felicity Palace on its outskirts is still only a royal retreat, rebuilt as the seat of government later in the storyline. Absolute monarchy, state religion (Adeanic), hereditary bureaucrats, mercantilism, **colonial slavery**, **Nation of Magic** [hist A03]. Queen Castíne síl Rubenaire (b. 1793, on the throne since 1816 because her elder sister Eilís was declared unfit; "romantic", "compliant") wears the Ruby Crown [tmpl A03; je:lorent; wiki: List of Lorentish monarchs]. "An unparalleled navy… the greatest colonial empire in the world, cradling the Ruin Sea and dominating the Bay of Rahen", but out-produced by the League [flavor A03]. Home territory includes Rosecrown, Bloodwine, Winebay, Great Ording, Venáil, Sorncóst, Roilsard, Deranne, Darom and the conquered West Dameshead (Carneter, Exwes, part of Pearlsedge) [states]. Subjects: Redglades (puppet elfrealm), Portnamm and Rubyhold (protectorates), Endralliande (dominion), Bloodgroves, Shofa, Torrisheáh, Varionáil (colonies), Lorentish Haless Company (which itself holds Phangban, Taiver, Khabtei Teleni and Bhuvauri as puppets) [diplo]. Bloc: the **Rose Crown** ("sovereign empire"). Rivals: Anbennar and the Northern League; the Trollsbay Concord counts Lorent as its rival [diplo]. A "Continued Blackpowder Wars Tax" is still levied: "Any who refuse… are traitors to the kingdom and agents of the enemy" [amend].

**Northern League (北方联盟)** — capital Gaweton; government "Magnate Council", a presidential oligarchy under a **Grand Magnate**; laissez-faire, protectionism, "poor laws", private schools, slavery banned, Nation of Artifice [hist A04; gov; je:northern_thaw]. "Forty years on, the north lies under the control of a capitalist oligarchy, and stands as the crucible of an industrial revolution" [flavor A04]. Its magnates are nicknamed "Second Sons" [wiki: Magnates]. People: 46% Gawedi, 16% Dalr (Gerudians), 13% Blue Reachmen, 9% Moormen [pops]. It holds Gawed and the lower Alen, Westmoors, the Greatwoods, the Reach and most of Gerudia; puppets Bayvek and Vertesk; the Deranne Trading Company (bought out by the magnates after its bankruptcy) [states; diplo; wiki: Trollsbay Concord]. Bloc: the **Blackford Accord** (1779, trade league). Rivals: Lorent and Grombar [diplo]. It "recently inflict[ed] a shocking defeat on Grombar's vaunted army" [flavor A10].

**Vivin Empire (维文帝国)** — capital Arannen; Arannese and Corvurian. "A mess hidden by a masquerade, a patchwork domain of feudal states stitched together by blood ties… a rural, stagnant realm, an elected monarchy in name only", whose aristocracy "cling to their undying privileges" [flavor A02]. It colonises Daravan's Folly "with the resources of both the Ravelian Church and its more 'nocturnal' benefactors" [je:settle_the_folly]. Emperor Petre síl Vivin (b. 1763; "imperious", "bandit"); heir his grandson Petre Nicolas (b. 1814), half Silmuna [tmpl A02]. Subjects: Bisan and the gnoll republic of Honderaak (puppets), the Ravelian Rectorate (protectorate) [diplo].

**Gnomish Hierarchy (侏儒议会)** — capital Oddansbay; "Supreme Hierarchy" under a **Hierarch** chosen by council; technocracy, racial segregation, state religion (cliff gnomes follow The Thought), colonial slavery [hist A06; gov; common/cultures/anb_cannor_cultures.txt]. It "exploited the grim calculus of colonialism… Kobolds were shipped overseas to secure gnomish supremacy" [flavor A06]. Population 46% cliff gnome, 38% Kobildzani kobold [pops]. Hierarch Torwick Staydampner is 213 years old [chars A06]. Bloc: the **Market Apparatus** (trade league) with the Small Country, Feiten and Kobildzan [blocs]. Subjects: Reveria, Brecc, the Dalaire colonies (Noo Oddansbay, Noo Coddorran, Kobildzexbirrix, Derhildia, Nyr Revrhavn, Ollorland), Themaria, Maamitinaq and the Eordand Administration [diplo].

**Kingdom of Busilar (布西拉尔)** — capital Hapainé, great harbour Port Jaher. Absolute Ravelian monarchy with colonial slavery; citizenship law "national supremacy", beside which a stray dev note reads "human supremacy" [hist A09]. "The Silnaras have spread Ravelianism at rifle-point… the Stone Lions guide thousands of ships through the straits of Tef each year" [flavor A09]. King Ansaro Silnara (b. 1772; sickly, kidney stones, an aesthete); heir Margotta (b. 1818) [tmpl A09]. Bloc: the **Hapiande Conclave** (religious) with Ovdal Tungr, Orenkoraim and Nathalaire. Subjects: Tropaicóst, New Lorincrag, Turtleback, Tvarateram, the Busilari Haless Company [diplo]. It lost Ardimya to a republic and fears a repeat [je busilar content].

**Kingdom of Grombar (格罗姆巴尔)** — capital state Esald (city hub Garok). "Orc and human united under a staunchly Corinite militaristic monarchy"; "from Graytide to Gray Kingdom" [flavor A10]. 33% orc, 30% half-orc, 11% White Reachmen, 6% Mossmouth ogres [pops]. King Bakram Ironsea (b. 1787; half-orc, "innovative", "wrathful", a reformer who "clings to absolutism") [tmpl A10; flavor]. Its landowners are the **Bazrani**, half-orc military nobility turned reactionary [ig].

**Small Country (小人国)** — capital Viswall; parliamentary republic of Redfoot and Bluefoot halflings, 13M people, taxes tobacco [hist A14; pops]. "Through pluck, blood, and a little gnomish aid" it won independence and gained Beepeck and Eaglecrest, but is "a mere cog in the Market Apparatus" [flavor A14].

**Ravelian Rectorate (揭秘会教廷属邦)** — theocracy seated in Pashainé state (city hub Thanallen; Aranthíl of the Grand Summit lies in the same state) plus parts of Asheniande and Wex; **traditional magic banned**; no police; Vivin protectorate [hist A33; states]. Its army is the Order of Saint Aldresia, "arcane knights that act as the martial arm of the Ravelian Church" [ig]; its listed general is a harpy, Sigrun síl Vertesk [tmpl A33]. High Rector in 1820: Bernal II (1801–1828) [wiki: High Rector].

**Lesser Cannorian states:** Portnamm (creek-gnome arts republic); Kobildzan (kobold state religion, parliamentary); Brecc; Reveria; Bayvek (Blue Reachmen and city goblins); Vertesk; Ibevar and Redglades (elfrealms that ban artifice); Moonhaven (elder-ruled, bans artifice); Silverforge (dwarven, "legacy slavery"); Rubyhold (ruby-dwarf monarchy, rosemetal); Nathalaire; Cyranvar (wood elves of the Deepwoods); the **Soyzkaru Cartel** (goblin cartel-republic at Ourdia with secret police, debt slavery and "Makata Companies" — gangs hired out as an army); and the goblin state at Burnoll, tag A12, called Orvitzyen in the mod's country list and Jazhkredu on the wiki [hist files; laws; wiki: Countries at the start of the Empyreal Age].

### 2.2 Escann (东坎诺 [zh:game])

"Neglected by its masters, Castonath's rule has shattered. As fractious adventurer-kingdoms clash with magocratic remnants, Blackpowder stratocracies and foreign invaders, who will emerge as the master of Escann?" [flavor A21].

- **Magocratic Demesne (魔治皇域)** at Castonath (mod tag name Núrcestir; "Demense" in file names): magocracy and autocracy, artifice banned, 49% Núrcestiran, 22% half-orc, 11% iron dwarf. Ruled by Yamina Gracosael, b. 25 Silversight 1730, a Royal Wizard — "Floodborn mages, trained from childhood and augmented through ritual". "Lagging behind rest of Escann in terms of institutions and economy" (dev note). Protects Wyvernheart [hist A30; tmpl A30; flavor A30; pops; diplo].
- **Ancardia (安卡迪亚)**: "presidential republic" with autocracy, national guard, **traditional magic banned**; "most advanced out of Escann". Ruler-general Dalyon of Banwick (b. 1765); rival officer Oto of Moreced, an "ambitious grifter" and "popular commander". Puppet: halfling Newshire. Rivals: Araionn and the Demesne. The **Damescrown Treaty** gives it transit through Anbennar [hist A22; tmpl A22; diplo; treaties]. Roots: the Free Soldiers' Republic of 1575, votes only for those who serve, parliament famous for brawls, the Ancardi War School [wiki: Ancardia].
- **Araionn (阿拉昂)**: Adeanic merchant republic at Oudmere, "the only Adeanic nation in the heart of Corinite Escann", "lost half their territory to Ancardia"; patron goddess Ara, "Lady Luck" [flavor A25].
- **Three Kings Alliance**: mutual defensive pacts among Blademarches ("most tolerant of Escann"), Rósande (still practises the slave trade, bans artifice) and Marrhold [treaties; hist A27, A28]. Marrhold and orcish Unguldavor (serfdom, legacy slavery) are rivals; Rósande and Orvitzyen are rivals [diplo].
- A formable **Escann** exists, with "Escanni Leadership" and "Unify Escann" diplomatic plays [localization/english/diplomacy/anb_diplomatic_plays_l_english.yml].

### 2.3 Aelantir

**Trollsbay Concord (巨魔湾联盟)** — "Federal Concord"; parliamentary, census voting, racial segregation, total separation of church and state, **legacy slavery**: 1.09M of 8.7M inhabitants are enslaved, and 15% are Undercliff orcs [hist B98; pops]. "The Old Concord vot[ed] its own end"; others say the nation was forged "under the fire of the Bloodgroves War during the Blackpowder Wars" [flavor B98]. Next election 1 Teysuren 1822; governing party the Harmonists [hist B98; parties]. Head of state Frederic Thadoch (b. 1755), "head Concord bureaucrat, created most current institutions" [tmpl B98]. It guarantees Spoorland (as does Lorent), holds Daxwell and Saamiršes, and has the **Treaty of Steephaven** (investment, trade privilege, transit) with the three Expanse republics [treaties; diplo]. Old saying: "Concord quick" — a promise never kept [wiki: Trollsbay Concord].

**Sarda Empire (萨尔达帝国)** — Ynnic feudal empire (serfdom, closed borders) under Morvel Vyrekynn, "the Tiger Empress" (b. 1785), who "slew the son of the Bandit King Jahanon". Vassals: Chippengard, West Tipney, Corinsfield; Vyceda in personal union; Argezvale tributary. Rival: the Dragon Dominion [flavor B29; diplo]. **Dragon Dominion (Rzenta 任塔)** — government "Drozmagospos", ruled by Gospodh Aranthiv yen Gozhar for the dragon-god **Varlengeilt**, who is alive, charismatic and "helping modernise things" (birth date 11 Nerament 1444) [gov; tmpl B49]. **Ynnsmouth (洇河口)**: Boek kingdom, a condominium of Sarda and Concord since 1812 [je:trollsbay]. **Bosancovac**: floating river-city theocracy under a Patriarch with a monopoly on Ynn trade [flavor B39].

**Harafic Triarchy (三元议会)** — "Artificer Triumvirate" of tinker gnomes, soot goblins and steelscale kobolds (Gommoport, Zurzumexia, Mestikardu); it owns a slice of Tianlou and puppets Bokguan [gov; states; diplo]. Director Trage Arkan, 46, soot goblin: "invented automated calculus & was involved in making the first new mechanim… fluent in ten languages" [chars B07]. **Neratica–Minaria**: the "grim kingdom of death" of Nerat's Onyx Legion, seized by General Emil síl Onyx, who bought the Lorentish penal colony of Minaria (the **Minaria Purchase**) [flavor B27, B09; treaties]. **Vanburia**: island seat of the Vanbury Guild, far from Telgeir; holds the Devand in the south [flavor B05].

**Lorentish Aelantir**: Endralliande, "the Azure Rose… most prized jewel of the Lorentish crown" (governor Kylian silna Fox; nationalist writer Armand Breac); Bloodgroves; Torrisheáh, suddenly governing "most of the Ravenous Isle and its giant man-eating flora" [flavor B14, B15; chars B14]. **Aelnar** survives as a magocratic "ethnostate" city-state in the Dalairey Wastes under the immortal Elissa Oathkeeper (b. 1374) [hist B17; tmpl B17]. **Eordand**: the Gnomish Hierarchy invaded in 1782, beat Darran Eaglesthorn and the Autumnal League, and runs the Eordand Administration under Hewitt Quintuple, who "sees Eordand as his personal fief"; the free courts formed the Four Seasons Alliance, which the unification storyline already calls a failure [flavor B80, EORDAND; chars B80; je:eordand_je]. **Epednan Expanse**: Plumstead, Beggaston, Tiru Móine, and the new Ranger Republic.

**South Aelantir**: the fallen **Ambhen Empire** of Ameion (collapsed 1780s) with only Sotereion left; the blue-skinned **Klereyen** successor states; Taychendi **warlords** who fight ritual wars (*Periyodhai*) and are courted by Cannorian "weapons testers"; scattered vampires of the **Rakulacan** clan; the federal **League of Kheiós** (united 1787) on wind-scoured Deirós; Rezankand under the Herald of Flame Varpanatal Camnazuir; Amadia; and the **Effelai**, a hive-mind jungle ("Hivespeaker" Kalandu Silversong) [concepts; flavor C30, C39, TAYCHEND, KLEREYEN; chars C01; tmpl C14; gov].

### 2.4 Dwarovar, Bulwar and Rahen, Haless, the Plains, Sarhal

**Dwarovar.** No Aul-Dwarov: separate holds — Khugdihr, Amldihr (with Dûr-Vazhatun), Hul-Jorkad, Gor Bûrad, Ovdal Lodhum, Arg-Ôrdstun (with Orlghelovar), Shazstundihr, Krakdhûmvror, Seghdihr, Verkal Gulan — beside goblin Ves Udzenklan (19.6M people) and Nizhn Korvesto (19M), the freed Jade March (Zerzeko Radin), kobold Kuxheztë, orc Drakonshan, and **Dakaz Carzviya**, a "Dakocracy" under the immortal goblin mage Dak Chaingrasper (b. 1408), styled "Glorious Mage" [hist D-files; pops; gov; tmpl D31]. The **Obsidian Legion (黑曜石军团)** is a colony of the Underempire worshipping the Runefather, with debt slavery and artifice banned; its ruler Thrain az-Thingr is "considered incompetent" [hist D15; tmpl D15].

**The Jadd.** **Jaddanzar (贾德扎尔帝国)** is a theocracy under Divine-Herald Kamnaril Andrellzuir (sun elf, b. 1498), with **artifice banned**; **Nahana Jadd** in Rahen is in personal union, 44M people, serfdom and closed borders [hist F01, R01; tmpl F01; gov]. The Jadd Empire bloc (1562) includes Vanrahar, Babhagama, the Overclan, Kuzaram, Harpylen and a centaur khanate [blocs]. The goblin **Overclan (大氏族)** is secretly run by the **Voltoj** bureaucrat caste, who puppet King Iztuts szal-Perzuk and court Cannor and Ravelianism [je:the_facades_unraveling; tmpl F14].

**The Command's heirs.** The reunification text speaks of "six banners… remnants of the old Great Command" without listing them; the stratocracies standing on the old Command's lands are Dragon Command (龙令; stratocracy, traditional magic banned, debt slavery, 30M people, under Behyakun Dragonborn), Lion Command (狮令), Girakad Command, the Unbroken Guard, the Bureau of Chains (Iztue Wolfborn), Kasardun, Pordhatti, Yodashikyu and Nadiraji, beside the Soldiers' Republic; Lion Command and the Soldiers' Republic are rivals [formation text in anb_formations_misc; hist R-files; tmpl R18, R19; diplo]. **Azjakuma (鬼居)**: the Oni, whose four *Shirgrii* orders corrupted the High Temples, have created the "Tamonger Eternal, an immortal ruler" [flavor Y09; concepts]. **Tianlou (天楼)** owes a crushing indemnity to the Triarchy for rescue during the Rending [je:tianlou]. **Lingyuk (灵域)** is ruled by the great spirit Huszien the Nine-Tailed Fox [tmpl Y04; gov]. **Feiten (飞腾)**, home of the airship, sits in the gnomish bloc [wiki: Airship; blocs].

**Forbidden Plains.** **Kalsyto / Lake Federation (湖地联邦)**: 33M Triunic humans, a "2000 year old democratic tradition", isolationist behind the "Idyllic Shield" [hist E01; je:kalsyto]. **Maghargma**: an ogre kingdom with debt slavery under the young Mughra Gravelhide [tmpl E02].

**Sarhal.** **Konolkhatep (哥诺勒哈特普)**: gnoll "Eternal Empire" on the Sorrow under the immortal demigod Horutep; 2.16M slaves; bloc "Stewardship of the Sorrow" [gov; pops; blocs]. **Hakhepra (哈赫普拉)**: "His Empire", a stratocracy waiting for the bronze dragon Aakhet, shot down at Zerat in 1790 [flavor L17]. **Khatalashya**: the lizardfolk "Final Empire" of prophecy; **Adzalan** with its portal to the Plane of Earth and "crocodilian mechanical battle suits"; **Karshyr** and **Nazhni**, the latter the home of the drug *nazhyn*; **Yezel Mora**, the defeated hags' swamp; **Melakmengi**, the 77M-strong Mengi empire with *Mogzeta* slavery and the *Nadige Monopoly* (foreign trade in two ports only, decreed 1758) [flavor L25–L36; laws]. ### 2.5 Diplomatic map at the start

### 2.6 The scripted storylines (journal entries)

**Cannor**

1. **Broken Empire** (Anbennar). Promised referendums on regional parliaments come due. A bar rises with radicalism and police repression, falls with war and victories. Events: "A Parliament for [state]" (honour it or annul an "advisory referendum, hijacked by separatists"); Peacekeepers fire "three volleys" into an independence crowd; Carnetori druids, lawyers and sprites swear a pact near Woodwell while a Redglader spy takes notes; "purple-clad" Wexonard nationalists revive "Wex must rule"; Lorent and the Vivins may *help* Anbennar repress. Endings: "The Day of the Dove"; "The Breaking Point" (civil war) leading to "Artheta Anbennar" (firing squads at dawn; a quarter of each rebel culture becomes Cóstine) or "United No More" — "The Dove is dead". The slogan: "For Esmaria to unite, Anbennar must divide" [je:broken_empire].
2. **The Ruby Crown / New Courses for an Old Ship / Felicity Palace** (Lorent). The crown holds an echo of the 11th-century absolutist Ioriel, who has "quietly guided Lorent's rulers". Castíne is the most attuned monarch ever; on first contact the voice says "I am Ioriel". A *Conviction* meter: above 75% she is "resolute" and can use the Crown's "Grand Sorcery" (mass enthrallment); below 25% she is "shattered" — drink, opium, abandoned ideals — and Ioriel governs through her (government form "Ioriellan Empire"). Two courses: Ioriel's neo-absolutism ("Face the Storm": autocracy, state religion, secret police) or Castíne's "Ride the Storm" (elected bureaucrats, abolition, women's suffrage, schools, "the Castíne Constitution", the invented tradition of "the Lorenan Principles"). Either way the "Aristocratic Old Guard" must be broken — officials for whom "no document is truly official until it has been copied three times, sealed twice" [je:lorent; concepts; gov; amend].
3. **Northern Thaw** (League): three fronts. *Woodswaller Uprising* — after the dismissal of the old general Tomac Woodswall, foresters with axes and pelts smash machines; a magnate, Walter Soot, is "mauled by werewolves" traced to a Woodswaller hideout; General Oswald Brasslance declares himself "Warden of the Alen and Greatwoods", shoots prisoners, then "crosses the Alen" to surround Rathroost and crown himself Oswald I. *Conversion of Gerudia* — Provost Edward's Ravelian mission; the Last Skald writes down the epics; a "Dalr Anti-Religious League"; Dalr-on-Dalr arson. *Reach Integration* — Blackpowderist pamphlets demand a "united Reachman Republic"; magnates debate provoking pogroms of elves and goblins "to keep the Reachmen divided". ## 5. Plot Threads with canon basis

1. **The Dove's Last Flight.** The Broken Empire crisis as the Cannor spine: referendums, the massacre, Lorentish dossiers and Vivin "hunters", ending in reconciliation, firing squads or six new nations. Player parties can stand on any side [je:broken_empire].
2. **The Queen and the Crown.** A court intrigue in which the party discovers that the monarch's flashes of brilliance are a dead ancestor's voice; they can strengthen Castíne, serve Ioriel, or see the Crown used for mass enthrallment [je:lorent].
3. **Iron Men Wake.** Simultaneous mechanim awakenings on three continents; abolition against property; hungry displaced workers as a third force [je:mechanim, mechanim_awakening].
4. **The Missing God Wears a Republican Uniform.** Hakhepra searches for Aakhet while he commands Anbennarian troops as Rabon Deshird — a secret connecting Cannor to Sarhal [je:hakhepra].
5. **The Open Planes.** Álvar's return starts a scramble for the Root of the Universe, the Sarhali Spires ("teleporters to other planets and planes though this is unknown in 1820") and Adzalan's Plane of Earth portal, with the CLSTC first in line [wiki: Root of the Universe; flavor tooltips, L34, B03].
6. **Masters of Escann.** Witch-queen, soldier-republic, gamblers' republic and three kings; the magic ban against the artifice ban [flavor A21–A30].
7. **The Runefather's Colony.** The Obsidian invasion already under way; Spineborn separatists; the Underempire's demands for slaves and "cleansing" [wars; je:obsidian_colony].
8. **The Beheaded Chimera and the Tamonger.** Six Commands, an Oni immortal, a fox goddess and a city in debt to goblin accountants [je:command_splinter, tianlou; flavor Y09].
9. **Thaw in the North.** Werewolf Luddites, a would-be king on a troop train, missionaries against skalds [je:northern_thaw].
10. **A Free Bay Built on Chains.** The Concord's unification, its slave quarter, its vampire planter clans, and the Ynnsmouth question [hist B98; tmpl B98; je:trollsbay].

## 8. Uncertainties and source conflicts

- **1820 or 1836.** Mod and wiki say 1820; older wiki captions ("Map of the Trollsbay Concord in 1836") and the brief say 1836. I followed the mod. Some templates still carry 1836-era logic.
- **Anbennar's constitution is unsettled in the files.** The live setup is a republic led by Reán síl Tirufeld, yet he is tagged "royalist", the Baronites favour "a monarch subservient to… elected nobility", and a commented-out "Monarchy Anbennar setup" with a child monarch Artur Laurens Silmuna (b. 1826) remains [hist A01; tmpl A01]. His exact title is not given; I have not invented one.
- **Delda Waggleknack**: wiki gives 1636–1786; the mod creates her alive in 1820 with a 1610 birth date. Mod followed.
- **Spellings**: "Demense" (mod) / "Demesne" (wiki); Hapainé (city) / "Hapiande" (flavor, bloc); "Tellum Times" (wiki) / "Tellum Crier" (mod); "Khatalshya" appears once for Khatalashya. Cecile síl Lerosa is "his" on the wiki; the mod template does not mark the character female.
- **Vivin vampires** are implied ("nocturnal benefactors", "undying privileges", rebels screaming "they're hunting us"), never stated outright in the files I read. Trollsbay "vampire clans" in Marlliande are stated only in a dev comment.
- **Elenore Ramsgate** sits in Busilar's character file and Edmund Ramsgate is called the king's brother-in-law; that she is the queen is my inference.
- **Populations, state lists and slave counts** are my sums of game data and should be treated as orders of magnitude.
- **Which six Commands** the "six banners" are is not stated; and tag A12 carries two names (Orvitzyen / Jazhkredu).
- **Unfinished content**: many journal texts are placeholders ("TODO", "Guess who's back", "Damn it really did fall"); the Uneasy Peace, Torrisheáh, Ardimya, Taychend unification, airship tower and Equality of Races entries have little or no prose. *Shadow over Ynnsmouth* is disabled.
- **Not read in full**: the 161 KB Hakhepra file, the Northeast Passage, Kalsyto, Naléni, Telekenid and Karshyr chains (titles and key passages only); South Aelantir character notes beyond rulers; Insyaa (the mod marks its mechanim lore as placeholder).
- The treaty parties in §2.5 marked "inferred from key names" were not checked against the treaty bodies.
